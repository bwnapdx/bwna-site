Traffic deaths over time

People killed on Portland streets, by year

The 2025 figure is from the city’s March 2026 Deadly Traffic Crash Report; 2025 counts are preliminary. The 69 deaths in 2023 were the most in this series.

III

Portland against other cities

Road traffic deaths per million residents, in the US, Canada, Europe and beyond

Years differ (2024–2025) and counting rules vary between places, so read the gaps as rough. Portland’s figure is derived from the city’s own rate of 6.0 deaths per 100,000 residents; Amsterdam’s is approximate. Montréal’s is 29 deaths in 2025 on the island the city police cover, divided by roughly 2.1 million residents, so treat it as approximate too. Among the US cities shown, only Los Angeles and Chicago rate higher than Portland.

IV

Portland against whole countries

Road traffic deaths per million residents: United States, Canada and Europe

Countries include rural highways, where most deaths happen, so a city would normally sit well below its own country. Portland sits above Canada and above the European Union average.

United States: 39,254 deaths in 2024 (NHTSA) over roughly 340 million residents. Canada, 2023, and Ontario, 2023: 4.9 and 3.9 per 100,000. Québec, 2025: 4.1 per 100,000. European figures are the European Commission’s final 2025 numbers, except Spain, which is preliminary. Bulgaria is the highest in the EU and Sweden the lowest.

V

Walking deaths across US metro areas

Pedestrian deaths per 100,000 residents, 2020–2024

From Smart Growth America’s Dangerous by Design 2026, which ranks the 101 largest US metro areas. Portland’s metro ranks 48th, at 2.15 per 100,000 (60 deaths over five years). These are regional rates that include the suburbs, not city rates. A selection of metros is shown. No matching ranking exists for Canadian or European metros.

VI

Getting around by bike

Share of trips to work made by bike, in the US, Canada and Europe

These are not measured the same way, so compare the order rather than the decimals. US cities are city figures from a 2026 ranking of 111 cities built on the American Community Survey. Canadian figures are for whole metro areas in the 2021 Census, which was taken during the pandemic and includes suburbs. Copenhagen counts trips to work and school (2016); Amsterdam counts all trips.

Sources

  • Neighborhood crash counts: Oregon Department of Transportation crash records, loaded live and sorted into the City of Portland’s neighborhood boundaries by this page. High Crash Network streets, traffic calming and neighborhood greenways load live from PBOT. Building ages load live from Oregon Metro’s tax lot records.
  • Traffic deaths by year: City of Portland / PBOT Deadly Traffic Crash Reports (2024 and March 2026). 2025 figures preliminary.
  • Deaths per million, peer cities: official city and prefecture figures compiled by the Hong Kong Transport Department (2024–2025); Amsterdam approximate (Planetizen); Montréal from the SPVM 2025 annual report. Portland derived from PBOT’s 6.0 per 100,000 rate.
  • Deaths per million, countries: NHTSA (United States, 2024); Transport Canada, Canadian Motor Vehicle Traffic Collision Statistics 2023; Ontario Road Safety Annual Report 2023 (preliminary); SAAQ Bilan routier 2025 (Québec); European Commission road-safety statistics for 2025.
  • Walking deaths by metro: Smart Growth America, Dangerous by Design 2026 (five-year average, 2020–2024).
  • Bike commuting: CoworkingCafe 2026 ranking of 111 US cities (American Community Survey); Statistics Canada, 2021 Census; City of Copenhagen Bicycle Account 2016; City of Amsterdam.

The city-to-city and country comparisons are saved snapshots from published reports, not live feeds.
