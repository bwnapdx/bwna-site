This month's agenda includes proposed **bylaws changes** defining BWNA's eligible voters as Beaumont-Wilshire residents and business owners. Voting on most items is limited to board members, but the bylaws item is open to attendees.

Also on the agenda:

- A postscript from the Board Retreat.
- A report from the Northeast Coalition of Neighborhoods (NECN).
- An update from the Ad Hoc Beaumont Crossing Committee.
- A transportation report, including an update on the crosswalk flags.
- A land use report on the Inner Eastside Zoning controversy.
- Reports from the Communication, Events, Newsletter, New Neighbor Welcoming, Crime & Safety, and Friends of Wilshire Park committees.

The meeting is held in person and broadcast via Zoom. As always, all meetings are open to the public — please come join your neighbors.
