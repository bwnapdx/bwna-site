The Beaumont-Wilshire Art & Garden Tour is one of the neighborhood's most beloved annual events. This self-guided walking tour takes you through the beautiful gardens and art collections of your neighbors throughout the Beaumont-Wilshire area.

## What to Expect

- Visit a curated selection of neighborhood gardens, from cottage gardens to urban farms
- See art displays and installations by local artists
- Meet your neighbors and enjoy a leisurely walk through the neighborhood
- Pick up a tour map at any participating location

## Become a Host

Want to share your garden or art with the neighborhood? We're always looking for new hosts! Whether you have a prize-winning rose garden, a creative vegetable patch, a beautiful front yard, or artwork to display, we'd love to include you.

Contact [events@bwnapdx.org](mailto:events@bwnapdx.org) to sign up as a host.
