Oregon Walks is hosting a slow-paced, roughly 1.5-mile walk with all three of our District 2 councilors, **Dan Ryan**, **Elana Pirtle-Guiney**, and **Sameer Kanal**. The route runs through the University of Portland campus to allow a look at the improvements along Willamette Boulevard. Meet in front of the Barnes & Noble bookstore at the Pilot House.

The walk is free. RSVPs are not required, but a note to [info@oregonwalks.org](mailto:info@oregonwalks.org) helps them plan for snacks and beverages. This is one of a series of walks across the city for STEPtember, Oregon Walks' month celebrating walking.
