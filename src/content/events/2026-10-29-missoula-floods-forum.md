Ever wonder why you hit so many rocks digging in your Portland garden? You can thank the Ice Age Missoula Floods for this consternating condition.

BWNA's fall speakers forum features **Jim O'Connor** of the U.S. Geological Survey on the glacial floods that carved the Columbia Gorge and Willamette Valley, and on what they left behind in Beaumont-Wilshire and surrounding neighborhoods. Dr. O'Connor studied geological science at the University of Washington and holds a master's degree and doctorate from the University of Arizona.

The event is free, but donations at the door are welcome. Seating is limited, so reservations are required. [Reserve your space on Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/the-ice-age-missoula-floods-in-your-neighborhood-tickets-1996053424525?aff=oddtdtcreator).
