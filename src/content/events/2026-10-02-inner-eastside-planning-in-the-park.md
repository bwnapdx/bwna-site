A casual drop-in session at the Grant Park picnic shelter where you can learn about the Inner Eastside Zoning Project and share your thoughts and priorities to help shape the future of the neighborhood. Come for five minutes or stay an hour. Rain or shine. BPS staff want to hear what matters most to you.

Learn more on the [City's project page](https://www.portland.gov/bps/planning/inner-eastside-zoning-project/about).
