This permitted, annual, fun-loving ride is also billed as an expression of protest against the fossil fuel industry. According to event organizers, thousands of riders will be participating, and many neighborhood streets will be closed to traffic.

Riders begin assembling at 6:30 p.m. and start departing the park at 8 p.m. Riders do not return to the park after the ride.

For more information visit [pdxwnbr.org](https://pdxwnbr.org).
