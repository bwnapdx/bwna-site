Celebrating its 40th year, this family-friendly street festival sponsored by the Beaumont Business Association will include over 200 vendors offering a diverse mix of local goods, food, and artisan products.

Families are encouraged to stop by the Fun Zone, located in front of The Arrangement, where kids and adults alike can enjoy free mini golf and other activities. Enjoy live music at multiple venues, too!

For more details closer to the event visit [@bbapdx](https://www.instagram.com/bbapdx) on social media.
