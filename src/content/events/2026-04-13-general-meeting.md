This is the annual meeting of the Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association. The business of this meeting includes a report from the board on the state of the association and the annual election of at-large board members and officers.

## What to Expect

- State of the association report from the board
- Annual election of board members and officers
- Updates on neighborhood projects
- Open forum for neighbor questions and concerns

## Location

Sylvan Learning Center is located at 4411 NE Fremont St, in the Beaumont Village shopping area. Street parking is available.
