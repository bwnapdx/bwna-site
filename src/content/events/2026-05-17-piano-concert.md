Northeast Village PDX and Rose City Community Collective, in cooperation with Living Opera, are hosting an afternoon of poetry, lyricism, and brilliance at the piano — featuring music by Liszt, Brahms, and Gershwin, performed by Gyan Singh Maria. Maria is a Portland-based pianist, conductor, and composer. He is a Conducting Fellow at Portland Opera, Music Director of the Muses Project, and Founding Fellow of the Living Opera, Portland Circle.

Event is FREE and open to the public.
