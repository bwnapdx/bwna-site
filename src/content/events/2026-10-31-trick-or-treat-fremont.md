Join the Beaumont Business Association for its annual Trick-or-Treat on Fremont Street! Businesses throughout the Beaumont neighborhood will be handing out candy and welcoming families for a fun, kid-friendly Halloween celebration.

- **Block Party:** NE 44th Ave & NE Fremont St
- **Trick-or-Treating:** Visit participating BBA businesses along Fremont Street for candy, costumes, and Halloween fun.
- **Haunted Hallway:** Sylvan Learning will host a spooky haunted hallway for kids.
- **Trick-or-Treat for Cans:** Bring a canned food donation to Sylvan Learning.

Come dressed in your favorite costume and celebrate Halloween with your Beaumont neighbors! For more information, visit [bbapdx.org](https://www.bbapdx.org/trick-or-treat-on-fremont-street) or follow [@bbapdx](https://www.instagram.com/bbapdx/).
