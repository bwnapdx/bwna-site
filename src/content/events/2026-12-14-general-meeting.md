The board had tentatively planned to vote at this meeting on whether to bring back **Beaumont Crossing** as a permanent public plaza. That vote has been postponed to the **January 11** meeting. PBOT's survey results are incomplete because of the city workers strike, and the board was also concerned about absences during the holidays.

This meeting includes a 15-minute **Open Floor** segment. Residents can speak for up to 3 minutes on a topic of relevance to the neighborhood, such as the pending decision on the plaza.

Also on the agenda:

- An update on the public plaza
- A BWNA-sponsored special meeting presentation for 2027 on the Inner Eastside Zoning Project
- Other BWNA-sponsored events for 2027
- Monthly meeting speakers for 2027
- Ongoing traffic calming concerns
- Incidents of neighborhood crime
- Continued enhancement of the BWNA website

The meeting is held in person and broadcast via Zoom. All meetings are open to the public. Please come join your neighbors.
