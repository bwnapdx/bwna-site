Celebrate the grand opening of Beaumont Crossing, our new community plaza at NE 44th and Fremont! Festivities run from 4 to 7 p.m. and include music bingo, a kids' painting station, a pet photo booth, live music, and more. A few key people will share short speeches. All are welcome — come out and enjoy the neighborhood's newest gathering space.
