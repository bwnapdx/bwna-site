This month's meeting features a presentation on emergency preparedness from **Bec Lawson**, who leads the Beaumont-Wilshire/Alameda Neighborhood Emergency Team. A representative from the Portland Fruit Tree Project will also be on hand to talk about how the non-profit cares for and harvests from the trees that feed and shade our neighborhoods, including your trees if you're interested.

Also on the agenda:

- A proposed revamping of the BWNA website.
- An update from the Beaumont Crossing Plaza Committee.

The meeting is held in person and broadcast via [Zoom](https://us06web.zoom.us/j/86798571365?pwd=ufBoaDGhieyCI3bammVMhbbqzGdZU5.1). As always, all meetings are open to the public — please come join your neighbors.
