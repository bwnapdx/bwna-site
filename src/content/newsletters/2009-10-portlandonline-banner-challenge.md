The City of Portland invites you to contribute to a refresh of PortlandOnline.com, your official city government website! During our Banner Challenge, ideas for new top banners of the website can be submitted online. This opportunity is open to any community member or team of any age who lives, works, or owns a business in the City of Portland!

**WHAT:** PortlandOnline Banner Challenge!

**WHEN:** NOW through October 19

**HOW:** On the website – www.portlandonline.com/refresh/bannerchallenge

**WHO:** Anyone who lives, works, or owns a business within Portland’s city limits

**PRIZE:** Recognition at City Council and in a press release & a $1,000 cash prize

The City of Portland is refreshing PortlandOnline, the city’s primary web presence. With more than two million visits every month and 140,000 different web pages, this is a huge undertaking. So important, in fact, that we are inviting the community to help us! Please help us get the word out and consider submitting an entry yourself.

A 6-member panel appointed by the Mayor and Council PLUS THE PUBLIC AS A SEVENTH MEMBER will judge entries! Whether you send in an entry or not, between October 20-31, you will have an opportunity to vote on a winner.
