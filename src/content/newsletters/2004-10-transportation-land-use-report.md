The Transportation and Land Use Committee has changed its meeting location. We now meet at Java Man Coffee Shop at 4727 NE Fremont St. The meetings are at 7:30 pm on the third Monday of each month. Thanks to Sherry and Ryan Loving for being so kind to let us use their comfortable space for our meetings.

A petition to request a study of neighborhood traffic patterns was signed by nearly 90 people at the annual neighborhood Night Out picnic held August 3, 2004. This shows there is a keen interest from folks to understand how traffic flows through our neighborhood. A traffic study is the first step in the development of an overall speed management plan for the whole neighborhood. Committee members are actively lobbying City staff to complete a traffic study this fall.

The committee will continue to work on transportation related issues this fall and will focus its efforts on pedestrian safety and speed management along Fremont St. Interested neighbors are welcome to attend the monthly land use meetings as well as the general BWNA meetings.
