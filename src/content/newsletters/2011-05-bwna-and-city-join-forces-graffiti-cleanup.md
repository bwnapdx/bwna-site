It’s an ongoing struggle in every neighborhood: the battle against graffiti vandals who deface public and private property alike, spoiling at will, like animals driven to mark their territory. Even works of art, like the lovely community mural in Wilshire Park’s pavilion, are not immune from their mindless destruction. But Beaumont-Wilshire neighbors are poised to strike back. Thanks to a collaborative effort by BWNA and the Graffiti Abatement Director Marcia Dennis (503-823-5860), an army of residents and graffiti abatement contractors will unite on the morning of Saturday, May 21st, to do what they can to repair the damage.

Here’s the plan:

1.) Obviously, the greater the turnout by BW residents, the more effective the cleanup. In order for the City to know how much graffiti cleaning supplies to bring, an approximate count of participants is needed. (Children may join in as well, as long as they’re accompanied by parent or guardian.) If you haven’t notified BWNA already of your intention to participate, RSVP to BWNA President Al Ellis at your earliest convenience. (apelainjoy@aol.com/503.287.0477)

2.) In order to maximize the efficiency of the cleanup, a list of neighborhood graffiti sites needs to be compiled ahead of time. Prior to May 14th (a week before the cleanup), report neighborhood graffiti sightings to Al Ellis, and Al will send the full list to Marcia Dennis.

3.) On the morning of May 21st, meet at Bethany Lutheran Church (corner of 37th & Skidmore, across from the park) between 8:30am and 8:45am for coffee & donuts, followed by instructions and distribution of graffiti abatement supplies. The cleanup lasts from 9:00am to 1:00pm.

Send a message to the bad guys: We care about preserving our beautiful neighborhood!
