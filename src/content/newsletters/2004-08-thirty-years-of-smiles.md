Nancy Tilton of Dee’s Golden Door, a beauty parlor at 4209 NE Fremont (503-282-5554), has been making people smile and feel good about themselves for the last thirty years.

Nancy came to Dee’s Golden Door out of beauty school in March of 1974. After the death of her boss Doris in 1982, Nancy purchased the salon located in the Beaumont Plaza across from Beaumont Middle School. And for the past 30 plus years she has been cutting, dyeing, curling, and styling hair for customers from far and near.

“I’ve been raised by many of my customers,” says Nancy. “I know their spouses. I’ve also seen them raise their children and grandchildren, some of whom have gone on to run their own businesses, and I’ve learned a lot from them”.

The Plaza where Dee’s Golden Door is located is also home to other long time Beaumont Wilshire businesses. Nancy feels lucky to have as her business neighbors people like Pattie Scarpelli of the Beaumont Florist, whose shop has been in its location for 26 years and owned by Pattie since 1988. Dr. Frank Prideaux at Beaumont Chiropractor Clinic has been in his location for 24 years. And of course there is the Beaumont Market, which has been a grocery store since the Dutch Building at 41st and Fremont was built. Another old-timer is George Flesuras of State Farm Insurance, also in the Dutch Building, whose office has been there for 28 and half years. “If it weren’t for such great people, and great customers, I would not have lasted 30 years,” says Nancy.

There is lots of history in the businesses around the area of 41st and Fremont. With the Fremont Fest coming up, stop in and say ‘Hello’ to Nancy and her neighbors and let them know we appreciate them being part of the neighborhood for a combined 100 years and more.
