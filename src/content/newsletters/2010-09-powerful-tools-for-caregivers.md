Powerful Tools for Caregivers is a six week educational series designed to assist family caregivers to take care of themselves while caring for a relative or friend with a chronic illness. You will benefit from the class whether you are helping a parent, spouse, friend, someone who lives in a home, in a nursing home, or across the country.
