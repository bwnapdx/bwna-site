## 1,000 egg Easter Egg Hunt•Saturday, April 11 - 11 am

Bethany Lutheran Church will host a 1,000 egg Easter egg hunt at Wilshire Park beginning at 11 am This event is free to the public. Toddlers and small children are encouraged to bring a parent or guardian to help in their collecting of eggs. Ask for directions to the egg hunt site when you arrive at the park. We look forward to seeing your Easter egg hunter there

## Easter at Bethany

Bethany Lutheran Church will offer two services on Easter Sunday, April 12 - 9:30 am **Traditional**; 11:00 am **Contemporary**.
Bethany Lutheran is located at NE 37th and Skidmore.
For more information, please call 503.284.3836.
