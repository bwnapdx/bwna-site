Beverly Cleary SUN School, formerly Fernwood SUN, will offer two opportunities for adults this fall, located at the Fernwood campus, 1915 N.E. 33rd Ave.

The first is Spanish for Beginners II (Mon. 6:15-7:15pm, Sept 29 - Dec 8). This class will be taught by Nina Frayne, a native speaker with over 15 years experience teaching Spanish to Americans. The class is perfect for beginners with some experience and will continue in the winter and spring as a progression from this beginning term. The cost is $40.

The second offering is AM Adult Yoga, 9 week intro to Vinyasa Style Yoga (Tues. 9-10 am, Sept 30 - Dec 9). Vinyasa yoga is a flowing style where the postures are linked together creating continuous movement. Breathing techniques are also used with each movement to focus the mind, relax and find lightness in each pose. This class is suitable for all levels. The cost is $40.

We are always looking for volunteers to teach a class once a week, based on your professional or personal expertise. You can donate school supplies, snacks or computers to students in need. There are many ways to get involved, just give us a call or send an email to Corrie Heselius at (503) 916-5314, cheselius@portlandimpact.org.
