This spring The Arrangement celebrates 25 years on Fremont St. On **Saturday May 14**, customers will receive a 25 percent discount on all purchases as part of the celebration.

Owner Sue Mautz, armed with a small personal loan, started a flower arranging and wreath-making business in 1980 in a small building next to Hamburger Patty’s (now home of Tuk Tuk.) By 1983 the store had outgrown that location. Sue and two other tenants—Sweeney’s Coffee Shop and Alameda Wallpaper—moved into a remodeled former Mobil service station at the store’s present location on the corner of 42nd and Fremont. By 1993, The Arrangement occupied the entire building, and quadrupled its space.

After its move, the store expanded its inventory, adding greeting cards, stationery, contemporary clothing like Cut Loose and Two Dog Island, and jewelry from local artists and Paris. It now emphasizes seasonal merchandise for Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Easter, Mother’s Day, and Halloween. During Fremont Fest, the store holds its annual “Dog Days Sale,” selling items “under the tent” for a 50% discount.

To what does Sue attribute her success? “First, I’ve had loyal and dedicated staff. Staying in the same location and living in the neighborhood has also helped.” The store has also kept pace with changing times, growing from flower arranging to stationery, adult and baby clothing, jewelry, gift items, and home décor. “Most importantly,” says Sue, “I do something that brings me great pleasure.” Twenty-five years later Sue Mautz is still following her passion.

Hours are Mon.- Sat. from 10 am to 6 pm, and Sun. 10 am to 5 pm, there is off-street parking. You can contact Sue at 503-287-4440.
