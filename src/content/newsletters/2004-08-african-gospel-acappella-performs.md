African Gospel Acappella will perform on **Sunday, September 19 at 7:00 PM** at Bethany Lutheran Church. African Gospel Acappella is comprised of six men who are all blind and originally from Liberia, in Western Africa. They represent five different tribes and sing in several native African languages, and in English (the official language of Liberia).

They have survived a civil war, poverty and many other hardships. The group met many years ago while attending the School for the Blind in Monrovia, Liberia.

The group writes and composes their own gospel songs. Their goal is to share the affirmation of their faith through song and testimony and to focus awareness on the plight of people in Africa, especially the disabled.

They have performed throughout the United States and now reside in Vancouver, Washington. A free will offering will be received and copies of African Gospel Acappella’s CD will be available for purchase. Bethany Lutheran Church is located at 4330 NE 37th Ave, near Wilshire Park.
