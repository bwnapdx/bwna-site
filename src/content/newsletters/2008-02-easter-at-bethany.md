**1,000-egg Easter egg Hunt**
**Saturday, March 22 – 11 am.**

Bethany Lutheran Church will host a 1,000-egg Easter egg hunt at Wilshire Park (rain or shine!) This event is free to the public. Toddlers and small children are encouraged to bring a parent or guardian to help in their egg collecting. Ask for directions to the egg hunt site when you arrive at the park. We look forward to seeing your Easter egg hunter there!

**Bethany Lutheran Church offers two services on Easter Sunday**

March 23, at 9:30 am - Traditional and 11:00 am - Contemporary. From 10:30 am – 12 pm, a continental brunch will be served.

Bethany is located at NE 37th and Skidmore. For more information, please call 503-284-3836.
