A number of Beaumont-Wilshire artists will be in a group show at Blackfish Gallery during the month of January 2011. The gallery is located at 420 NW 9th and is open Tuesday-Saturday 11:00am - 5:00pm. The opening is January 6th (First Thursday) from 6:00pm-9:00pm.

For more information about the art group itself go to: http://www.bwna.us/artspace/index.html
