**BW Community Meeting**
Monday, February 11, 7:30 pm
Beaumont Middle School, Rm 140

The two guest speakers at our February 11th meeting will be addressing issues both close to home and in our city as a whole.

**Robin Plance** is the education and outreach coordinator of the Portland Community Advisory Group (CAG). The Portland Harbor is an Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Superfund Site, and will eventually be cleaned up after years of planning and sampling. The study is expected to take until 2009. The Willamette River may be reasonably clean by 2015. Part of this process is the Portland Harbor CAG. It is comprised of individuals from neighborhoods, environmental, health, recreation, and business groups, and concerned citizens. Learn more about this important project and how you can give your input.

**Apryl Strass**, manager of the Albina Community Bank on Fremont St. and current president of the Beaumont Business Association will also be a guest speaker. The Beaumont Business Association is comprised of the businesses located between 41st and 57th along NE Fremont. Their purpose is to work together to make Beaumont a vibrant business district by providing goods and services the neighborhood needs and wants. Come hear their plans for the coming year and how you can get involved whether you are a business owner or a resident.
