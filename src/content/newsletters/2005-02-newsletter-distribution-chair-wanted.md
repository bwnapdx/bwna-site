Looking for a lot of satisfaction for only 3-4 hours every other month? The exciting world of Newsletter Distribution could be for you! The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association is looking for someone to take over. The newsletter comes out every other month (6 times a year). Once printed, they will be dropped off at your house, you will count them out for each of the ten Captains (who will in turn count them out for each of the delivery people). Once counted, you will deliver the bundles to each of the Captains. You’re done! This takes about 3-4 hours.

Other duties will be occasional recruitment of new volunteers, and maintaining rosters and new volunteer packets. In exchange, you will have the thanks of a grateful neighborhood and the privilege of working with some wonderful volunteers!

If this interests you, or if you have any questions, call Lisa Perkins at 503-282-5816 or lisachiba@hotmail.com
