Beaumont Artspace – the online art gallery featuring works by artists living or working in the BW neighborhood was recently featured in an article in the Oregonian. If you missed the article, you can read it by going to www.bwna.org, click on ‘artspace’, then ‘about’.

This month we welcome four new artists to artspace: Rick Cassidy, Gloria Curtis, Lynn Gandolfi and Carol Sands.

**Rick Cassidy** does illustrations of comic book-like characters. He has been sketching, drawing, painting for the past 15 years. Rick enjoys drawing and finds it very relaxing after a hard day.

**Gloria Curtis** has lived in the neighborhood for over 20 years. She has participated in the Fremont Fest for the last few years. Her paper mache figures of animals, vases and other items are very popular.

**Lynn Gandolfi**, another long time BW resident, enjoys creating tiles of colorful fruits, vegetable, and other items. She has shown at Fremont Fest and her pieces can be found at the Guardino Gallery on NE Alberta at 29th.

**Carol Sands** works in watercolor and other media. Her lovely drawings of flowers, Oregon scenes, and neighborhood haunts can be found on greeting cards, on display at Suzanne’s Restaurant at NE 46th, and Fremont, and in many art exhibits.

Check out their work and that of other neighborhood artists at **www.bwna.org/artspace.html**.
