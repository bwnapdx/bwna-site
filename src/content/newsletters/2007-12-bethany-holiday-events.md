## Thursday, December 13

— 6:30–7:30 PM, Christmas Carol Sing Along ~ Come and sing some traditional as well as many of your favorite Christmas carols and help the Sunshine Division Food Bank of the Portland Police Bureau. Admission to the carol sing is a donation to the Sunshine Division food barrel (suggested donation: two cans per attendee). Light refreshments served following the carol sing along.
