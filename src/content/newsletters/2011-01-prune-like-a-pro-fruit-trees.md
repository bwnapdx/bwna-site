Do you have fruit trees? Is pruning them a random act of violence? Portland Parks & Recreation is offering free workshops on pruning to increase fruit production, train young trees, and restore neglected trees. The workshops take place at the Columbia Children’s Arboretum which was planted by school kids in the 1970’s.

Classes will start with a half-hour of basic fruit tree instruction, followed by hands-on pruning supervised by Portland Parks & Recreation horticulturists. Bring pruning tools and gloves if you have them (they will also be provided).

Workshops Dates are: Saturday, January 15, Saturday, February 19, 9:00am-noon

Classes are free but registration is required.
