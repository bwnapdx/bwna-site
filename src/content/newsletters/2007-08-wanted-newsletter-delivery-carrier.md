If you live near NE Alberta Court and Prescott and would like to volunteer in getting the newsletter out to your neighbors, contact Marsha Mahon, the BWNA newsletter delivery coordinator. She has one open position to deliver newsletters to households along NE 35th Place, 36th and 37th between NE Prescott and Alberta Court.

Delivering newsletters is a great way to meet some of your neighbors and get some exercise and fresh air!! The Beaumont Wilshire newsletter comes out every other month and it should take approximately 30 minutes to do this route, or about 3 hours in one year. If you are interested call Marsha after August 5th at 503-284-1859 or mahonmurrell@comcast.net.
