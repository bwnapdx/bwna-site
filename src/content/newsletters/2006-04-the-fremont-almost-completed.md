The plywood forms have finally come down and Beaumont Wilshire neighbors are getting a first look at their newest retail offering at the corner of NE Fremont and 44th. The architect, Francis Dardis of FBD Architecture, lives in the neighborhood and works just a couple of doors down, so he can see the progression of The Fremont from his office window. Builders Brian Faherty and Lance Marrs anticipate The Fremont will be filled by high-quality, local businesses adding foot-friendly benefit to the neighboring homeowners. “Our goal is to compliment the existing Fremont Street businesses, giving further reason for Beaumont Wilshire residents to shop locally.” explains Faherty.

Slated for completion in late spring of 2006, this single-story, 8000 square feet concrete cast building will have a 1920’s period inspired aesthetic. “We worked with our architect Francis Dardis and project advisor Kevin Cavenaugh to ensure that we created something with a quaint ‘main street’ aesthetic that the community would approve of.” states Marrs. “Our objective at this point is to attract tenants that will enhance the livability of this wonderful neighborhood.”
