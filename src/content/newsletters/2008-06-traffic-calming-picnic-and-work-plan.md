> BW Community Meeting
> Monday, June 9, 7:30PM
> Beaumont Middle School, Room 140

Speaking at the June 9th community meeting at Beaumont Middle School, room 140 at 7:30pm will be **Will Stevens**, *Traffic Calming Program Manager* for the City of Portland, Office of Transportation. We have heard from many residents that traffic patterns and flows on the smaller residential streets as well as the main ones such as NE Fremont are a problem and of concern. Come and find out what kind of plans or solutions the City of Portland Traffic Calming Bureau can offer in the way of alleviating some of these concerns.

We also hope you will join us in planning for the National Night Out Picnic, the big festive event held each year on the first Tuesday in August. This year that will be August 5 at Wilshire Park. The board members would like your input and help.

Also on the agenda is the ‘action plan’ for the neighborhood association for the upcoming year. Hear what the board plans to accomplish and let them know what you think are priorities for the neighborhood in the coming year.

See you June 9th, 7:30 at Beaumont Middle School, NE Fremont at 41st, in room 140.
