**Tuesday, August 3rd**
**6:30 – 8:30 PM**
**Wilshire Park**

The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association invites you to the annual all neighborhood picnic in celebration of ‘National Night Out’. Everyone is invited to this biggest neighborhood event of the year. Bring a salad, side dish or dessert to share. Local businesses and the association will provide hamburgers, hot dogs, drinks, plates and utensils.

There will be door prizes and surprises. Please mark your dishes on the bottom so they can be returned to you if they get left behind. Come meet old friends and make new ones! Don’t miss this great neighborhood get-together on Tuesday Aug. 3 from 6:30 to 8:30 PM at Wilshire Park.
