Bikram Yoga is a warm place to do yoga. Opened in the summer of 1999, Derek and Suzanne Cummings, who live nearby took the helm in September of 2004. Both avid yoga practitioners, they met on Match.com in 2000. Derek invited Suzanne to a Bikram class on their first date. “If a woman could make it through a Bikram class, she might stand a chance with me!” Derek recollects. Suzanne fell in love with both Derek and Bikram yoga. “It is really a dream come true to have a business that fosters community and helps people.”

During a session, 26 yoga postures and 2 breathing exercises are done twice in a hot room. This form of yoga is designed to be challenging to both beginners and experienced practitioners and is very healing. Derek paraphrases the yoga’s founder, Bikram Choudhury: “This yoga is designed to work and restore every system of the body within the 90 minute session. It is like giving your body & mind a much needed tune up.”

They offer 24 classes per week for adults/teenagers. Derek, Suzanne and their young son Sutter are excited about their upcoming children’s yoga program beginning after Memorial Day. The Toddler Yogi program will be half hour classes for 2 & 3 year olds with a parent, and the Preschooler Yogi program will be half hour classes for 4 & 5 year olds with a parent. There also will be a class for kids 6-11 years of age without a parent.

All of these classes will be fun and designed to help children explore their physicality while learning to focus.
