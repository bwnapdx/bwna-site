There is a local effort underway among neighborhood artists to create a monthly art event, “**First Friday on Fremont**,” much like our successful predecessors, “First Thursday” in the Pearl and “Last Thursday” on NE Alberta. If you have *original, hand-made* artwork and are interested in becoming a participant, please contact Pauline Dugas Tait at 503-281-2871 or tait2.1@netzero.com
