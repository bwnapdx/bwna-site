Popina Swimwear, located on NE 42nd Avenue and Alberta Court will be opening a second location in the Pearl district November 4th (First Thursday). Popina’s Pearl location will be located at 318 NW 11th between Everett and Flanders. Once open, the Popina Pearl store will be the largest Women’s swimwear shop in the Northwest. Faithful Northeast Popina shoppers do not worry; the Northeast store will remain open! Owner’s Pamela and Will Levenson, residents of Beaumont-Wilshire hope to have some overlapping brands and styles between the two stores, but each individual store will offer its own brands and shopping experiences. Between the two stores, Popina hopes to offer around 20-25 swimwear brands. “We really hope to create a world class swimwear store in the Pearl, taking the lessons we have learned over the last four and a half years and taking our swimwear retail up a notch or two. We are sincerely grateful for all of our customer support” said Pamela. So mark your calendar for November 4th and come to the grand opening party in the Pearl.
