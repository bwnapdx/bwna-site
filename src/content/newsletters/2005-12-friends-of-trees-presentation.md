**Thursday, January 5, 2006, 6:30–10PM**

Slide show presentation by Friends of Trees at the Wilshire UMC and Native American Fellowship at 39th and NE Shaver St. For those who would like to learn more about their tree choices for the tree planting on 2/11/06 and learn more about Portland’s urban canopy, please attend. For more information or to volunteer for the tree planting, please contact Albert Kaufman at albertkaufman@yahoo.com. We are also looking for local businesses who wish to donate food for the event or prizes for our raffle.
