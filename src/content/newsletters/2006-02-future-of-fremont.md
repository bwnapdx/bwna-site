At writing time, it's the week prior to an Open House to review proposed transportation and parking improvements to Fremont Street between NE 41st and 52nd Avenues. As we have all observed, Fremont Street is evolving into a bustling business center increasing the quality of life in our neighborhood while at the same time causing inevitable growing pains. Our Transportation and Land Use committee in conjunction with the City of Portland has been working to identify issues and solutions that address traffic and pedestrian safety, and parking. This has been an issue for the neighborhood for many years. Through the efforts of the land use committee and the recent involvement by the City of Portland, we are likely to see some positive changes along Fremont. This is a very exciting time for us! If you missed the open house, there are still opportunities to provide your thoughts and ideas—just check the land use committee schedule.

Whether or not you are getting a tree for yourself, come out and help on February 11 at our neighborhood tree-planting event. **Meet at the United Methodist Church between 8am to 9am** and we'll hook you up with a planting team. See the Tree Planting article on the front page for more information.

*—Heidi Blischke*
President, BWNA
