Beaumont-Wilshire will welcome over a hundred new trees to parking strips and yards this year, but they need a little help getting in the ground. Volunteers are needed the morning of Saturday, March 19, when BW teams up with Roseway and Cully neighborhoods for the big planting.

Uncovered pick-up trucks are greatly needed, but there are many other ways to help as well, including joining a planting team, helping with food and refreshments and assisting with mulch and cleanup. If you are available to help or have questions, please email the Beaumont-Wilshire planting coordinators at beaumont-wilshire@plantitportland.org or call 503.467.8545.

Planting trees is treemendously rewarding and a great way to meet your neighbors. Please join us!
