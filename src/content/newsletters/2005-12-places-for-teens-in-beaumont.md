**Bagel Land**, 4118 NE Fremont. Close to Beaumont Middle School, Bagel Land, a family-owned business serving the neighborhood for 29 years, offers a convenient place for students to get their early morning carbs. Bags of day-old bagels for only $1 are especially inviting.

**Ristretto Roasters**, 3520 NE 42nd. This newly-opened coffee spot with beans roasted on the premise offers some of the freshest coffee in the neighborhood and a delicious selection of pastries. The pleasant atmosphere makes it a great place for teens.

**Pizzicato**, 4217 NE Fremont. Part of a Portland chain, this pizza place offers pizza by the slice or pie and salads for hungry teens. Its creative toppings and friendly and prompt service have made it a neighborhood draw for six years.

**Criollo Bakery**, 4727 NE Fremont. Criollo is not only one of the best bakeries in Beaumont, it is one of the best in Portland. From mini-banana cream pies to double chocolate pecan cookies, it has something for every sweet tooth.

**Starbucks**, 4633 NE Fremont. This coffee place has been a staple for teen coffee addicts for many years. With a large variety of drinks and pastries it has something for everyone.
