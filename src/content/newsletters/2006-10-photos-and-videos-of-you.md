The summer was filled with fun events in the neighborhood and photos and videos of those events can be viewed on the neighborhood website

http://www.bwna.org/photos.html

You can see photos and videos of the Zoo visit to Bethany Lutheran in June, scenes from Fremont Fest and of the annual picnic in August. There is also a video of the neighborhood artists exhibiting at the Fremont Fest and meeting at local coffee shops to plan future shows on the ‘artspace’ link. Thanks to BW webmaster M. Caoile for shooting these and making them available for our viewing pleasure.
