Portland Parks & Recreation, City Nature Forestry Division will lead a general tree care walk in the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood on **Tuesday June 12 from 7:30 to 8:30PM**. Bring your tree questions and be ready to learn about Portland's trees. The tree walk will focus on street tree selection, tree identification, general pruning, and urban forestry issues. The walk will also include a hands-on pruning demonstration and tree give away. For more information or to sign up, call Karl Dawson at 503-823-1650 or email kdawson@ci.portlan.or.us.

If you have any questions about your street trees, please call for a street tree inspection at 503-823-4489. City tree inspectors are available to assess your street trees, and issue pruning, planting or tree removal permits.
