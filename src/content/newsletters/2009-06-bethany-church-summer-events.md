5th Annual Day Camp for kids ages 5 -12 years old will take place July 27 – July 31. Four trained camp counselors from Camp Lutherwood (www.lutherwood.org) combine with Bethany volunteers for an exciting, engaging week of camp at Bethany from 9am - 3 pm M-Fri (over before Noon on Friday) where kids engage in crafts, fun activities, worship, and a Bible theme. The cost is $40 and includes 2 snacks, a lunch, and field trip to Grant Pool. Registration forms available on-line at www.bethanyportland.org or call 503.284.3836.

PJ's Purple Peddler will be out on the streets during June and July in the early evenings bringing free frozen treats to the kids in your neighborhood as a way of connecting community together and sharing summer events at Bethany and in the BWNA community. Look for PJ's (Pastor Jeff's) purple bicycle in your neighborhood and listen for the bicycle bell being rung down your street. When you see him, wave at him to stop for a free frozen treat. This event is sponsored by the Bethany's outreach team - connecting community together!
