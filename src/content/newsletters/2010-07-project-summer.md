The end of the school year is fast approaching. That means free food and fun under the summer sun for children and teens! All youth ages 1-18 can have free food this summer with the Summer Food Program. Schools and non-profits around Oregon are providing free meals and fun activities to children in low-income areas. To find the nearest meal site call 1-800-SAFENET (723.3638) or visit www.summerfoodOregon.org.

Do you and your friends have some spare time this summer? Consider volunteering to deliver meals, lead activities, or spread the word for a meal site in your neighborhood. If your community doesn’t have a summer meal program and you want to help start one for next year, call Sally Eliason at Partners for a Hunger-Free Oregon, 503.595.5501 ext. 301. Get involved to make sure that kids in your neighborhood get to eat nutritious meals throughout the summer!

Learning doesn’t end when school lets out. Neither does the need for good nutrition—which is crucial for children to have safe and productive summers. The Summer Food Program provides an opportunity to continue a child’s physical and social development while providing nutritious meals. The summer food program helps lower income children return to school ready to learn.
