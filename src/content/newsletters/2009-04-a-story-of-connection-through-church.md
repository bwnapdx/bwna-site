A year ago, I was walking in our Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood, and took notice again of a quaint brick church with a tall white steeple standing on the corner. I’ve probably passed Bethany Lutheran Church a million times on the way to the Wilshire Park playground with my five and six year old children. Having been loosely raised Lutheran, but not having attended church since my late teens, I decided to “check it out” one Sunday. I wasn’t looking for any big commitment since our family is already busy with a myriad of activities on weekends, but I did want my young children to have the benefit of some exposure to religious and ethical teachings.

That first Sunday, we wandered into the 11 am Contemporary Service (figuring that would be the better of the two services for young children) and were surprised by the warmth of the people who greeted us and the genuine feeling that we were truly welcomed. The service featured great contemporary music played “live” by Pastor Jeff Bethke on bass guitar, Aivars Saukants on lead guitar, and Midori Suzuki joining them on vocals. Needless to say, it was a far cry from the hymns I remembered from my childhood, and the sermon itself seemed relevant and meaningful to our modern lives. Over the next few months, we kept going back and came to enjoy the sometimes very funny input from the kids during the Children’s Message, and always, the very real sense of community we felt there. My six year old has even “joined the band” and now accompanies the adults up front playing the bongo drum. A budding percussionist in the making! (He now even refers to Bethany as “the bongo church, as in, “Mom, do we get to go to bongo church this weekend?)

In order to promote growth and participation, and develop a broader connection and service to the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood and beyond; Bethany has recently re-written its vision statement “We are a growing multicultural mission church with a heart for reaching out to our community with the good news of Christ Jesus”. The church has also received a new grant that will enable them to hire a Community Involvement Director and a Children’s Ministry Director this spring. A slate of ideas for family-oriented programs is in the works - including of course, the 4th annual Easter Egg Hunt in Wilshire Park. The church couldn’t have a more perfect and relaxed atmosphere for parents with children who don’t want to “shush” their way through a solemn and tedious hour. In a nutshell, from this mom’s perspective, Bethany and the 11 am service rocks!

In hindsight, this past year of reconnecting with the church and our community has been a surprising and happy addition to our busy lives. There truly are a lot of hidden gems in this Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood, and I’m glad I stopped that Sunday a year ago, and “checked it out”.
