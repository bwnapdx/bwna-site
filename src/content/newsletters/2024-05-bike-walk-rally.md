**Bike Fun! in Beaumont-Wilshire**

**Sat., June 22**
Start Time 10 a.m. (duration about 1½ hours)
Meet at Wilshire Park

Pedal or stroll and test your inner Sherlock Holmes in this neighborhood-centric event! Put together a family team or grab a couple of friends to compete in solving clues that lead you on a course around the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood. Post-rally gathering with refreshments, prizes, and awards. Space is limited. To register contact John Sandie at SandieFam@gmail.com or text (219) 508-4162.
