Walk into rosabode on NE 42nd Avenue, and you’ll feel wrapped in luxury and comfort.

Touch any item in sight – be it a faux-fur and chenille throw, a silk and velvet wrap or a floaty faux-feather purse – and you’ll find the texture to be, in a word, addictive. You’ll want to pet, to wear, and to snuggle. This is all part of owner Rose Bonomo’s plan. She wants you to have a completely tactile experience.

rosabode is an independent retailer specializing in luxurious home and fashion accessories. Rose says she is proud that everything in the shop is designed and handmade in Portland. Fabric is her inspiration, and the creations that result are diverse and luxurious.

Her new spring line includes velvet and faux fur throws that are the perfect weight for spring and summer evenings.

Another item, the Sassy Skirt, embodies the “fabulous and functional” nature of rosabode’s merchandise. Beautifully crafted from linen, the skirt is functional enough to ride your bike in.

A collection of wraps that pair velvet and silk are fabulous with jeans or for a formal event, Rose says. Elegant pillows and shams are eye-catching accents for a bedroom or living room.

A single mom of two teenagers, a volunteer and breast cancer survivor, Rose also uses her creativity to serve the community.

In the back of the shop, her desk is piled with luxurious throws pattered in small, velvety rosettes. Rose will deliver the blankets to cancer patients in our area.

“I believe that being soothed is part of the healing process,” she says.

The forthcoming “pink” section of her website will allow people to suggest others who might benefit from the healing comfort of a rosabode creation.

**Visit rosabode at 4225 NE Fremont Street or online at www.rosabode.com**
