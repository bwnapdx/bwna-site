“Welcoming” is how Gay Mitchell finds her new neighborhood and fellow artists in the Beaumont Wilshire Artspace, a group of local artists who either live or work in the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood. Gay and Chuck Mitchell moved to the neighborhood in July 2007 from West Linn. Their two children were grown and out of the house, and they were looking for a different lifestyle.

Since moving into the neighborhood, Gay has found a job teaching art at the Portland International Community School (located in the Grant Park Baptist Church), and has shown her paintings in two shows resulting from her affiliation with the Artspace group. In fact, she found the Artspace group through advertisements in the Beaumont Wilshire newsletter.

Gay’s primary medium is oil painting, although she initially studied watercolor painting nine years ago with a West Linn neighbor, Angelina Woolley, now too a Northeast neighbor who shows at the Mark Woolley Gallery. At the time, Gay taught special education in elementary school, but has subsequently earned a degree in Art Therapy from Marylhurst University in Lake Oswego. Gay’s most recent work focuses on Asian themes inspired by travel to Thailand where her son currently lives.

When looking for a new home, Gay, in particular, was looking for a neighborhood with ready access to urban amenities and fellowship with other artists. She is pleased she has found both.

See samples of Gay’s work and that of other Artspace members at www.bwna.org under “Artspace”.
