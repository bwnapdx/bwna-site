Max Bondy is one of our newest neighborhood residents. He is a high school exchange student from Berlin living with the Bruneau family since late August, and attending Wilson High School in SW Portland. The Bruneaus heard about Education, Travel & Culture, Inc.'s (ETC) exchange program from an article placed in the BWNA newsletter.

Before Max arrived in August, his host family enjoyed meeting him and his family last July. Besides being a terrific vacation, this rare opportunity for a prospective host family helped inform them about Max's upbringing and country.

ETC (http://www.edutrav.org) is sponsoring Max for a yearlong stay. ETC is a local, non-profit organization, promoting international understanding and goodwill through educational and cultural exchanges since 1983. ETC provides opportunities for international high school students to study in an American high school while living with an American family.

For Max, the transition has been fairly easy. So far, the biggest thing to adjust to has been American politics. In Europe, no country has the death penalty and abortion is allowed. Gay people are equal and can marry and adopt children. Max finds our way of life very interesting: stuff like supermarkets are different, because everything is full of color and intended for the freezer or microwave, and not fresh. Houses are not so close, with fewer people live in flats like in Germany where everything is closer together. This causes Americans to have more cars (2-5 per family), compared to 1-2 per family in Germany. Max has found the American school system to be a lot different: easier classes and many after school clubs.
