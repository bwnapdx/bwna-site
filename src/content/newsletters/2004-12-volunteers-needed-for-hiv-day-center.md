Be a part of our HIV Day Center community. No experience necessary. The HIV Day Center is a drop-in day center for people living with HIV/AIDS, where clients have access to hot meals, counseling, phones, computers, clothing, TV, laundry facilities, haircuts, massages, and field trips.

We need people to help with meal preparation and clean up. We also need volunteers to deliver meals to those homebound with HIV\AIDS, and pick up hot lunches and bring them to the center. Other creative skills you can offer are welcome.

For more information please contact Mara at 503-460-3822. The Day Center is located in NE Portland and is a nonprofit program of Ecumenical Ministries of Oregon.
