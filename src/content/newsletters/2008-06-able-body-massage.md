Able Body Massage Therapy is fairly new to Beaumont Village—its doors opened at 3515 NE 43rd in June 2007—but owners Henry and Lorraine Tobeck are not new to the massage field. Between them, they have nearly forty years of experience as licensed massage therapists (LMTs).

“Massage is often seen only as a technique that helps the body to relax after physical or emotional stress, such as working too long in the garden or a death in the family,” says Lorraine. “While our practice offers this kind of Swedish massage, we also deal with more serious problems affecting the muscles, joints, soft tissue, and nervous system.”

“Depending on what is needed,” adds Henry, “we alleviate problems in the body that require different therapeutic applications and techniques.” Some of these techniques include:

Deep tissue—gently works through layers of tissue to reach problem areas

Trigger point—releases sustained contractions in muscles

Myofascial release—works on the membranes that cover muscles; lessens spread of scar tissue

Myoskeletal therapy—releases adhesions and realigns skeletal structure

Massage therapy is a second career for both Tobecks. Lorraine worked in banking for twelve years, and Henry in construction for twenty before becoming LMTs. The Tobecks believe they are the only married couple in Portland who has had a full-time massage therapy practice for 16 years.

Henry also specializes in sports massage that relieves tension created by excessive activity or muscle injury and involves focused interactions between client and therapist. “Two years ago I worked with Cirque du Soleil performers during their stay in Portland,” notes Henry.

During her sessions, Lorraine includes Chinese Facial Massage, which uses acupressure points on the face to tone and relax the muscles. She has advanced training and experience working with clients who have Temporal Mandibular Joint (TMJ) problems and fibromyalgia.

Able Body is located in a comfortable, air-conditioned house with two therapy offices. Henry and Lorraine can be reached at 503.239.4785, 3515 NE 43rd (north of Fremont behind Tuk-Tuk restaurant), or visit their web site at www.ablebodymassagetherapy.net
