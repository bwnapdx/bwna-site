Soluna Grill opened its doors to the public at 4440 NE Fremont (Fife’s old location) on September 1, 2009. Dan Straub, Executive Chef, mentions that “a really great meal tends to stop time for a moment and warms my soul. Not just putting sustenance into my body, but feeling someone’s heart and absorbing just a small bit of their craft”. Furthermore, he feels that you can “always tell when someone has put passion into their food, it just tastes better”.

As a chef, it has always been Dan’s goal to let his guests participate in his passion for food. There is nothing more rewarding for Dan than to watch diners pass their plates around the table so others can try all the different flavors. The looks on their faces is the reward that helps to keep Dan doing what he does.

Dan’s restaurant journey started 23 years ago in Southern California, then across to the mountains of Stowe, Vermont, and then down south to Raleigh/Durham, North Carolina (where his predecessor and new friend, Marco Shaw, has opened a fantastic farm rich restaurant “Eno”). Dan eventually traveled back to California where he opened a few restaurants and met the love of his life. All along the way Dan worked the traditional “chef ladder” starting from dishwasher to pantry, sprinkling in some serving positions, and growing his chops down the line until he reached executive status. But, as all chefs know and aspire to, he wanted his own place and was constantly on the lookout for the right location with a quaint neighborhood appeal.

In April of 2009, Dan got a call from my great friend who has been in Portland with his family for the past 15 years. He informed Dan of an available place in his neighborhood that had all of the criteria he was seeking. Who knew? Dan now finds himself living two miles from Soluna. He not only has a fabulous neighborhood to cater to, but a rich and diverse environment to find some of the tastiest ingredients this country has to offer.

Soluna is open Monday - Thursday from 5pm - 9pm, and Friday & Saturday from 5pm - 10pm, with a bar menu served from 3pm - 6pm. Soluna is closed on Sundays. For more information, or to make a reservation, please visit Soluna Grill at 4440 NE Fremont or call 971.222.3433. You may also reach Dan online at info@solunagrill.com.
