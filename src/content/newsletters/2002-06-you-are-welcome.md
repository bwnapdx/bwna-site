If you recently moved into the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood either as a renter or homeowner, and have not received a welcome packet, we would like to provide you with one to say ‘welcome’ to our neighborhood.  Call Liz Johnston at 503-281-8385, leave your name, address and phone number with her. Welcome packets are delivered to new residents quarterly and you will get one in her next delivery.

—Liz Johnston
