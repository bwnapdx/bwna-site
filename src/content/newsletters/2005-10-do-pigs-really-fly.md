One of Beaumont Village’s newest shops, When Pigs Fly, doesn’t just have an unusual name. The shop is also dedicated to stocking hard-to-find gift items for unusual tastes. Co-owners Mark Bergal and Johnathon Guerra both agree, “If you see it anywhere else, we get offended.”

Some “unusual items” include a purse shaped like a watering can, a toaster with a frog design, and a bubble-blowing gnome. “I vetoed the gnomes but Mark insisted,” says Guerra. “Fortunately, I relented. They are very popular.”

Expect the unexpected in a store that customers call the “happy shop.” The store’s eclectic collection is always changing, so browse often and stay as long as you like. Say “hi” to Winter, the store’s doggy mascot.

When Pigs Fly carries seasonal items and right now has a large collection of Halloween stuff including eerie tombstones. It also carries lines such as Department 56 (seasonal), Ann Taintor (vintage), and Pylones (practical things for the home with a funky twist). Of course, there are always pig items—clocks, purses, fancy pins... Also, the store recently added a toy department.

Located in the Fremont Commons at 4605 NE Fremont, #104, the store is open Wednesday and Thursday, 10 a.m.-7 p.m., Friday, 10 a.m.- 9 p.m., and Saturday and Sunday, 10 a.m.-6 p.m. Starting in November, hours will be extended. Questions? Call 503/282-PIGS (7447).

Looking for a gift for someone who has everything? You can bet that you’ll find something just right at When Pigs Fly.
