Quirks of Art will host a show by women artists titled “From a Woman’s View”.  The show will run from May 1-31, 2008. There will be a reception on **May 10 from 3 to 6 p.m.**

The following artists, many of them from the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood, will be showing at Quirks of Art: Carol Sands, Elisabeth Sullivan, Nancy Larson, Judee Moonbeam, Rita Catapano, Brita Gould, Gay Mitchell, and Terry Street.

Quirks of Art is located at 1602 NE 40th Ave. Portland, Oregon (just south of the A-Boy parking lot near Sandy Blvd.) 503-282-4429, website: http://quirks-of-art.com/
