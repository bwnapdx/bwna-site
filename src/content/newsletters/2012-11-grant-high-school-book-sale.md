Donate your used books now then mark your calendars for November 19th and 20th to come shop the sale! This annual event, benefitting our own school Library, is held during conference times.

Any gently used books are appreciated, but please no magazines, textbooks or out-of-date reference books. We also happily accept movie DVDs and books on CD or tape. To donate just drop your books in the yellow bin that will be In the Main Office of the school.

For pickup, contact Jo at 503-284-6642 / jo@jolandefeldarchitect.com or Jennifer at 503-335-0357 / paulxochihua@yahoo.com.

This annual fundraiser is great chance to clean out your bookshelves to make room for all the irresistible books you will purchase to support the Grant library.

Please contact Jo or Jennifer if you have time to help us sort or sell. Thank you!
