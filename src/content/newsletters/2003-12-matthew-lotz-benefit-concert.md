Matthew Lotz returns to Portland for a 4 pm Sunday concert on Feb. 1, 2004 at Bethany Lutheran Church located at NE 37th and Skidmore. Everyone is invited to hear this gifted and energetic musician perform original praise music, including selections from his “Musical Prayers” CD. Proceeds from CD sales and donations go to Northwest Medical Teams.
