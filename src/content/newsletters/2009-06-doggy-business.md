One spring morning, my human Mom (Diane) and I took a walk to Doggy Business on 42nd at Alberta, the new dog training and lodging facility in our neighborhood. You see, I’m an only dog “child” and as much as I love my human and cat family, sometimes I just want to play with other dogs in a fun and safe environment. When we arrived at Doggy Business (4905 NE 42nd Ave), I was a little unsure of the rod iron gates facing 42nd Avenue. But we were warmly greeted by the owner Doug Duncan and his wife Meredith Wilson, who explained they hope to replace the gates with windows to let in even more light. They had yummy dog treats at the front desk and although Doug said the treats were just for me, I bet they give some to other dogs too. I learned that Doug and Meredith gained years of experience training and walking dogs in Seattle where they graduated from University of Washington like my Mom (Go HUSKIES!) before moving to Portland. They both have master’s degrees too and are certified in Pet First Aid, CPR licensed, bonded and insured.

I got a personal tour and saw the private lodging rooms and the wonderful big indoor and outdoor dog play areas where I met other dogs. We did lots of butt sniffing and getting acquainted. I know humans don’t understand that behavior but believe me it’s necessary and fun! Some dogs come for playtime and others stay while their parents go on vacation or travel for business. Doug even sends email reminders after clients make their reservations. If I could use our computer I’d check out their website at www.DoggyBusiness.net. Meredith takes the dogs for 15 or 30 minute walks in the neighborhood. I’ve seen her near our house.

Doug and Meredith opened Doggy Business last December after transforming a vacant building and reused most of the materials from the original building. The recycled rubber tire play area floor sure felt soft under my paws. I give them two paws up for building green! Doug and Meredith are good friends with Mike and Christine, owners of Green Dog on Fremont which is another one of my favorite dog shops – they give out good dog treats too! Doggy Business uses biodegradable poop bags from Earth-Centric, 6017 NE Sandy Blvd. My Mom uses those bags too because it’s the right thing to do. I could go on but I would much rather see you soon at Doggy Business so you can play with me. Woof.
