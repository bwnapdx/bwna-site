If you are an artist who lives or works in Beaumont Wilshire or the adjoining neighborhoods, you are invited to join other local artists at a monthly get together. These happen on the second Sunday of each month (except July and August) at a local café or an artist’s home or studio.

The next meeting will be on December 14 at an artist’s home. Artists come together to share art work, techniques, plan for group shows and show support for each other. The group has had two group shows at Quirks of Art, a small gallery in the Hollywood area and has taken part in the annual Fremont Fest.

Artists in Beaumont Wilshire or the adjoining neighborhoods are also welcome to submit work to appear on the BWNA website www.bwna.us under ‘artspace’. Instructions on how to submit work are listed on that same page. If you want to come to the December meeting contact mc-hk@comcast.net for time and place.
