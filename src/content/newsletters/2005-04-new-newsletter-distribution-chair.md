Jennifer Francis is the new Beaumont Wilshire Newsletter distribution chair. Jennifer is a real estate broker with Prudential. She and her fiancée, Aquiles Montoya, moved here about a year ago because they fell in love with the neighborhood and all that Portland has to offer. She has a lot of great skills and enthusiasm that will make her a great chair.

On a personal note, I’d like to thank all the people I’ve worked with over the past couple of years. It was such a pleasure to be part of a group of volunteers that I could depend on to make my job such a joy. I’ve spoken to other neighborhoods about newsletter distribution and, believe me, we are the envy of the city.

If you have any questions or would like to volunteer, please contact Jennifer at 503-459-8114 or jfrancis8@email.com.
