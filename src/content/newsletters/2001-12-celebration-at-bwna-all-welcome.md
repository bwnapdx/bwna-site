The General Meeting of the BWNA on Monday, Dec. 10, 7:30 pm at Bethany Lutheran Church (NE 37th at Skidmore) will be a festive holiday get together. The board invites all residents, families and friends to join in the celebration.

There will be treats and surprises for all. There may be a visit from a white-bearded man in a red suit! Come and join the fun. Please bring home baked goodies to share and a toy (unwrapped) or non-perishable food items to be donated to agencies in the city that will be distributing the items during the holidays.

Don’t miss this end of the year party. We have many things to celebrate and be thankful for, as Willie Nolan points out in his letter on p. 2. See you on December 10th!
