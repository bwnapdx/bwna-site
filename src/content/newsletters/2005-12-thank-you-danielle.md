I would like to take this opportunity to thank **Danielle Walters** for her five years of faithfully delivering the BWNA Newsletter. Danielle started delivering while an 8th grader at Beaumont Middle School and continued through her four years at Grant. Fair weather or foul, she never missed a delivery schedule in all those years!

Danielle is off to the University of Oregon this fall and we wish her all good things as her college career begins. As for her old route, the search for a replacement has begun. It requires a commitment of about 30 minutes every other month. If you or someone you know (a reliable, 8th grader looking for a community service project) might be interested please contact Jennifer Francis at 503-459-8114 or jfrancis8@email.com
