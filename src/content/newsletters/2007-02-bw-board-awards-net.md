Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency Team (BW NET) leader Liz Johnston and members Chris Boeh and Erika Wolfe Burr presented a proposal to the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association Board in December for moneys to supplement their training, and received a $483 grant.

BW NET has made its presence known in BWNA through regular newsletter articles on safety and information about upcoming trainings. BW NET members have monthly meetings, attend the annual neighborhood picnic, and this year began a tradition of hosting an information table at Fremont Fest. BW NET members train with Fire Station 14 on NE Killingsworth to brush up on emergency preparedness skills. BW NET has been recognized by the Portland Office of Emergency Management for its outstanding contributions, as well as individual member awards to Ms. Johnston as Team Leader and Ms. Burr as contributor from the POEM Speakers Bureau.

The grant from BWNA will go towards supplies and refreshments for an emergency preparedness talk in the spring; a weather-resistant BW NET banner; traffic cones; pop-up canopies; tarps (for patient carries and protection) and a hand truck. BW NET will do an exercise in Wilshire Park during inclement weather with these supplies and use the hand truck to transport some supplies the BW NET received from the Chevron Corporation for its advanced NET experience.
