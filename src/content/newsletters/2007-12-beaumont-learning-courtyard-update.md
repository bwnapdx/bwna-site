This past fall, BWNA board members have held regular bimonthly meetings at Beaumont Middle School with interested parents, faculty and neighbors developing site plan concepts for the Learning Courtyard. So far we have secured over $13,000 in grants from the following: $1,000 BWNA, $5,000 Bureau of Environmental Services and $7,250 from East Multnomah Soil/Water Conservation District Partners in Conservation Grant. We also have submitted a proposal to Office of Neighborhood Involvement for $5,000. The generous grant funds will help transform an expanse of concrete at the core of Beaumont Middle School previously home to four large dumpsters into an inviting courtyard.

Proposed features include integrating rainwater downspouts into infiltration zones planted with native flora and a kiosk and bench seating for outdoor classroom activities. This provides a tangible example of effective, low-tech natural resource management and enhances the school yard environment. Additionally, the space can be used for neighborhood meetings and provide a sample of innovative neighborhood urban renovation

Beaumont Middle School is a particularly attractive site for the innovative storm water management practices due to its unique location on a ridge top between the our two major urban watersheds: Lower Willamette and the Columbia River Slough.
