Bethany Lutheran Church, 4330 NE Skidmore at Wilshire Park, will be holding Taize Service prayer services on the third Thursday, January 17th at 7:00pm. The services last half an hour. Taize prayer is a quiet, simple way of prayer through meditative singing. Short songs, repeated again and again, give it a meditative character. Singing in this manner becomes a way of listening to God, and allows everyone to take part in a time of prayer together. All are welcome!
