On Sunday September 29th, the Cully-Concordia neighborhood will be hosting the Community Harvest Festival: A Multicultural Celebration of People and Food. The primary focus of the event is to promote a more inclusive Cully-Concordia neighborhood and strengthen relationships between community members through food and family fun. The Harvest Festival is made possible through a partnership between many neighborhood groups, including the Cully Boulevard Alliance, Cully Community Market, Native American Youth and Family Center, and Our 42nd Avenue — with support from Central Northeast Neighbors and Northeast Coalition of Neighborhoods.

Come make connections in the community and celebrate the harvest season with friends and neighbors! For more information, please contact Michael DeMarco at michael@ne42pdx.com
