**Monday, June 13, 7:30 PM**
Bethany Lutheran Church
NE 37th & Skidmore

Join us at the BWNA general meeting on Monday, June 13th at 7:30 PM and bring your ideas for how the BWNA board can spend up to $1000 on projects that will benefit our neighborhood. At its last meeting, the board members voted to spend up to $1000 on one or more projects in the coming year. Now we would like to hear from you. Write up your proposal, its costs and benefits, and bring to the meeting for consideration and discussion. In past years, BWNA has funded the installation of 20 bicycle racks along Fremont and provided money to the Beaumont Middle School Band to purchase sheet music and other needed materials. Join us on June 13th, bring your ideas and share your visions for our neighborhood.
