The next time you’re in Beaumont Village, be sure to travel a few blocks east to visit the Turkish bazaar at Ottoman Klasik, 5015 NE Fremont (503.282.2848). Not only will you be able to shop for fine Turkish imports for your home but also experience genuine Turkish coffee, teas, and pastries.

Proprietors Ercihan Dilari and Karen Stahr met in Turkey, while she was on vacation, and where he still owns a horse ranch and riding tour business. In 2001 Karen moved to Turkey, and the couple now divides their time between Turkey and Portland. They opened Ottoman Klasik in February 2008.

“Erichan has a good eye for quality merchandise and for the unusual,” says Karen. “He visits villages where he selects one-of-a-kind and vintage hand-crafted house wares that display the artisans’ personalities.”

The shop has a wide selection of unique Turkish kilims, flatweave rugs, and Turkish carpets, known for the durability of their double-knot construction (Persian rugs are single-knot). They carry hand-painted and hand-thrown classic and contemporary ceramic tiles and table wares. “So far, ceramics are the most popular item we carry. People love the colorful designs,” notes Karen. Other items include natural soaps, cushion covers, lamps and lanterns, inlaid wood boxes, large terracotta jars, antique wooden chests, copper cauldrons, pots, and trays, and a variety of Turkish food products.

You can enjoy Turkish coffee, similar to espresso in taste but made from a fine powder, a coffee drink from Illy’s Coffee, or traditional Turkish tea served indoors or in a secluded Mediterranean-type garden with young fig and olive trees and Turkish poppies.

Hours are 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. every day except Tuesday. Free Wi-Fi is also available, making this shop a lovely combination of the old and new worlds.
