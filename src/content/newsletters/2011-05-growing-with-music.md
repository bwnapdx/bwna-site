Celebrate 12 years of bringing music and movement to at-risk children.

Support Growing with Music Spring fundraiser
Where: Garden Fever Nursery
Located at 3433 NE 24th, Portland, Oregon
Thursday, May 5th 2011 6:00pm to 8:00pm

An evening to benefit Growing with Music. No ticket fee but a great opportunity to buy your spring plants – flowers or vegetable. For more information about the evening or about Growing with Music, please call Anne Clark 503.282.1839 or growingwithmusic@yahoo.com.

Great selection of tomato starts. Purchase raffle tickets for a basket of garden related goodies and a $100.00 gift certificate to Garden Fever. Growing with Music is a small non-profit, doing big work with young children and their moms.

Anne Clark leads weekly classes in music and movement with teen moms and moms in recovery and their young children. Anne brings enthusiasm and her instruments to the women and children where they live. Through the structured music and dance, the mothers and their children learn and have fun together. The activities have been selected to enhance brain development, strengthen parenting skills as well as attachment and bonding. The activities in the classes offered by Growing with Music can also reduce child abuse. The fundraising event will focus on gardening, plant information, raffles, music and food.
