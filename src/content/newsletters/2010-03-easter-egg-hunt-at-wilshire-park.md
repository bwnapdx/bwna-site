Saturday, April 3rd 10:30am

Children, toddlers through 10-year-olds, are invited to bring their Easter baskets and join in the fun for this annual community event. The egg hunt will take place in the center of the park , just west of the covered buildings. There will be hundreds of eggs waiting to be found. Bring the whole family. It’s GREAT FUN and it’s FREE! Sponsored by Bethany Lutheran Church, 37th and Skidmore. For more information call 503.284.3836 or go to www.bethanyportland.org. The Easter Egg Hunt will take place rain or shine!!

## Easter Service at Bethany Lutheran Church 10:30am

Experience a rich, traditional Easter service, with exceptional music and a wonderful children’s program.

**Everyone is welcome.**

Corner of 37th and Skidmore. 503.284.3836.
