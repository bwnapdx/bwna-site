## Picnic at Wilshire Park

Tuesday, August 2nd, 6:30pm

The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association invites you to the annual BW picnic in celebration of National Night Out. Everyone is invited to this biggest neighborhood event of the year.

Bring a salad, side dish or dessert to share. Local businesses and the association will provide hamburgers, hot dogs, drinks, plates and utensils. There will be door prizes and surprises. Please mark your dishes on the bottom so they can be returned to you if they get left behind. Don’t miss this neighborhood get-together on **Tuesday Aug. 2 from 6:30 to 8:30pm at Wilshire Park**.

## Fremont Fest

Saturday, August 6th, all day

The **parade starts at 10:00** at Beaumont Middle School. All children are welcome to participate—prizes for best-decorated bike, tricycle or wagon. Enjoy the horse drawn wagon rides—all day up and down Fremont. Listen to live music at the three stages. Auction items for a silent auction will be on display at stage areas. Foot Traffic & Albina Bank are co-sponsoring a used shoe drive to benefit the Nightwatch Homeless Shelter. There will be food, art, give-aways and fun for everyone. Don’t miss it!
