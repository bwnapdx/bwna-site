During the month of December, members of Beaumont Artspace will exhibit at the Quirks of Art, **3602 NE 40th Ave** (south of Sandy Blvd next to the A Boy parking lot).

The show runs through December 2007 with an opening reception on **Saturday, December 15, from 3-6 PM.**

The featured artists include Keith Buckley, Mario Caoile, Rita Catapano, Elaine Falbo, Barry Frankel, Brita Mae Gould, Jude Moonbeam, Carol Sands and Suzie Spaggiari.
