On April 10 at our general meeting, we will elect a president, treasurer, and up to 7 ‘at-large’ positions (out of the 16 member board). Most of the positions are for a 2-year duration although 2 of the ‘at large’ positions have a 1-year duration. Some of our board members have agreed to run again for their current position or for another position.

We are looking for volunteers with the energy and time to help coordinate neighborhood activities and come up with ideas to improve the neighborhood whether through activities, land-use or transportation, or community services. To join the board one must either live in the neighborhood or own a business within the neighborhood boundary (which can be found on our website bwna.org). If you are interested in helping shape the future of the neighborhood, please come to our meeting and join the board! The positions can take a little bit of your time or a lot depending on your level of participation. Although even if everyone gives only a little, a lot can get done.

Although I have enjoyed being the President, I will not be putting my name in the hat again for a couple of reasons: I have already served 6 years on the board which is the maximum allowed according to our bylaws and it is time for me to spend more time volunteering at my son’s school. Participating in, organizing and helping out with many of the BWNA activities has given me the opportunity to meet neighbors, get to know some of the business owners, watch neighbors work together to plant trees, collect bulky waste, mingle at the picnic, and give a little back to the neighborhood of which I am glad to be a part.

Another very good reason, outside of joining the board, to come to our April 10th meeting is to hear the Beaumont Jazz Band and get a taste of many of our local neighborhood restaurants at the “Taste of Beaumont”! More information on the “Taste of Beaumont” is provided on the front page in this newsletter.
