In the event of a citywide or regional emergency such as a severe winter storm, flood or major earthquake, households need to be prepared to be on their own for the first 72 hours. In large-scale disasters, firefighters and police officers may be overwhelmed with requests for help and could be slowed down by impassable streets and other damage.

Neighborhood Emergency Teams (NETs) are groups of citizens trained by the Portland Office of Emergency Management and Portland Fire & Rescue to provide emergency disaster assistance within their own neighborhoods. NET members receive basic training on how to save lives and property until the professionals can arrive. Through this training, they become:

- Prepared for self-sufficiency for 72 hours in any emergency.
- Able to provide emergency assistance to their family and immediate neighbors.
- Able to work as an emergency response team to save lives and property in their neighborhood.
- Able to guide untrained volunteers who will want to help others when disaster strikes.

The Beaumont Wilshire NET is one of the most active of Portland's 82 teams. Monthly meetings and skill refreshers keep the team at the ready for potential emergencies. Look to future newsletters for "NET News" updates on our team.

For more information about NET training, contact Rachel Jacky, Office of Emergency Management, 503-823-4614.
