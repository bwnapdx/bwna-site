The “arctic blast” has past, and spring is just around corner. Just as the seasons change, so must life. Mario and Helen (see page 1) have provided our community with this amazing newsletter for the last nine years. They are a true testament to the value of community and the importance of caring for each other. Thank you so much for your efforts. They will never be forgotten nor go unnoticed.

In the same spirit of community involvement, I encourage each of you to reach out to your friends, family and neighbors and make the Beaumont Wilshire community even better in 2009. As a full-time Financial Adviosr and part-time group exercise instructor, I know that change cannot happen without your involvement. Even if we all gave just 5% more...imagine what a difference it could make! Moving forward, I hope that this newsletter will connect and inspire you to do more in your life and with your community. The articles this month are based on action.... what can you do right now to make a difference. At the present time, there are two open board positons available. Is one of them for you? For more information about the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association or to see how you can help make a difference, please visit our webiste at www.bwna.us.

Moving forward, I want to hear from you. Please take a moment and share with others why the Beaumont Wilshire meighborhood is so special to you by submitting a small article (75-100 words) that we can share with others. We want to know what unique qualities this community provides that meets your needs. Please include a photo if possible. This is an amazing community, with great shops, restaurants and very kind people. I encourage you to share your journey with others, as well as shop locally! Please send all articles to dkjohnson@wradvisors.com by the editorial deadline (see “calendar” on page 1). Would you like to recieve a PDF version of the newsletter via email? Just send me your email address and I will add you to our distribution list (a list that is only used for newsletter distribution). I look forward to hearing from you.
