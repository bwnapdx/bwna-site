The 2nd Annual 42nd Avenue Street Fair is scheduled for Sunday, August 28, 12pm-6pm. This year we’re adding a community parade from NE 42nd Ave and Killingsworth southbound to NE 42nd Avenue and Wygant. The fair will take place on NE Alberta Court between NE 42nd and NE 43rd.
