Everyone is excited to see our own Farmer’s Market coming to the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood. Spread the word and tell your friends. Come out this Sunday and see what 19th Street Farm is bringing for your pleasure. Along with fresh produce and fruit there will be local-neighborhood artisans, crafters and collectibles. Enjoy brunch on the patio at Soluna and hit the market for some tasty treats to take home.

Every Sunday from 10:00am to 3:00pm in the Soluna Grill Parking lot at 4440 NE Fremont. For more information or for vendor booth questions, please contact Rebecca Lehman 949.395.3305.
