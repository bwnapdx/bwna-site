In these difficult times, it’s easy to stop buying, stop going out to eat, stop worrying about anyone but ourselves. But with a little planning, each of us can do small things that make a difference for our neighbors and our neighborhood.

As the mega stores struggle to stay afloat, reducing their workforce as they go under for the third time, we can make sure to buy the necessities and gifts needed from stores in our immediate area. Keeping the Northeast vibrant benefits us all.

That goes for eating out. Let’s not let our wonderful neighborhood restaurants suffer. We may need to go less frequently, skip dessert, cut corners in one way or another, but we should try not to stop going entirely. You deserve it, and we have great restaurants, cafes and wine bars that need your support.

And we need to remember those who have trouble putting enough food on their tables or, even worse, no longer have tables on which to put it. If you’re planning a birthday party or other get together, if you own a business, or if you are a member of a group that meets regularly, put out a box or barrel in which to collect food for the Oregon Food Bank. Then ask folks to bring donations. It’s fun and it helps. For information, call Allison Hoffman at the Food Bank, 503.282.0555 x 245. Together, we can make a difference.

Five or ten years from now, when the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood is still a great place to live, we can be proud that we help to keep it that way by our actions today!
