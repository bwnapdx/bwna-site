A lot, I learned when talking recently to Ristretto Roasters owner Din Johnson. For instance, did you know that “ristretto” in Italian means the first burst of espresso, or the intense essence of the bean’s flavor? Roasting coffee beans combines listening to the “crack” of the beans as they roast, as well as a sense of the visual, or look of the bean. These are skills Johnson employs when hand crafting coffee on the premises of his business located at 3520 N.E. 42nd Avenue.

Din Johnson has long had an interest-turned-passion in fine coffee. In fact, he started roasting beans on a small scale while working in Los Angeles in his contracting business. As his passion grew, Din began taking roasting workshops offered in the Bay Area. His disenchantment with Los Angeles and the contracting business joined forces with family matters to draw Johnson and his wife to Portland, where Johnson was born and raised.

Back home in Portland with the decision to launch his coffee enterprise, Johnson decided to locate in northeast Portland. Ristretto Roasters & Cafe opened for business in September 2005. Ristretto is a traditional espresso bar and cafe. Espresso drinks, pastries and freshly roasted beans are available. All beans are medium roasted to ensure maximum flavor. Because the flavor is in the oil, when beans are over-roasted, the flavor is obliterated. Johnson buys beans from growers throughout the world trying to work through local families with ties to coffee growers.

Business has grown steadily by word of mouth. This past summer, the business expanded by setting up shop at the Hollywood Farmers Market and supplying beans to several local establishments including Siam Society and Stacatto Gelato. Johnson is currently expanding his wholesale business as well and for certain would like to have a second roasting facility. For now, however, he is pretty satisfied with how things are going.
