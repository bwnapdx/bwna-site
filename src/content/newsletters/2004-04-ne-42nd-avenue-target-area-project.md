> **Monthly meeting every 4th Tuesday at 7pm — St. Charles, 5310 NE 42nd**

The 42nd Target Area Project is a geographically focused community-based initiative. The Target Area is located along NE 42nd Avenue from NE Prescott to NE Holman intersecting Cully, Concordia, and Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhoods. The main goals established by community members are to improve and promote a viable neighborhood-serving business district, to provide opportunities to build community, and to retain and promote diversity and unity.

A community steering committee has developed action plan strategies to accomplish these goals. Community members successfully orchestrated several neighborhood improvement projects including: a 42nd Avenue Market Study, the new 42nd Avenue Business Association, a business directory, street trees, planters/benches, 24 street banners in partnership with local students, involvement in the Whitaker-Adams redevelopment process, advocacy for community services, Spring clean-ups, the Youth Photo Project, the 42nd Fiesta event, the Latino Youth Network, quarterly bilingual newsletter, PDC Storefront Improvement outreach and more. The projects are based on community collaboration in partnership with local groups. 42nd Target Area goals for 2004 are:

1. Continue NE 42nd Ave. Business Association activities; 42nd market study strategies.
2. Support the Whitaker-Adams School site redevelopment, define our role in the process
3. Support the development of social services in the 42nd Ave. community; connect with School-Aged Policy Framework implementation, local schools, Schools Uniting Neighborhoods (SUN) programs
4. Promote youth development activities, specifically
   - Latino Youth Network, connection to other youth groups
   - August event (42nd Ave. Celebration)
5. Provide staff for technical assistance, organizing materials, progress meetings and other resources to support community organizing efforts

If you are interested in getting involved contact Sandra Lefrancois, 42nd Target Area & Neighborhood Involvement Specialist, Central Northeast Neighbors (503) 823-2780, cnnoutreach@teleport.com
