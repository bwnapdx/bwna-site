Bethany Lutheran Church will offer two services on Easter Sunday **April 11th**: 9:30am Traditional, and 11:00am Contemporary. Visitors are welcome! Children are invited to the Easter egg hunt following the 11:00am service. Bethany is located at NE 37th and Skidmore. For more information, call the church office at 503-284-3836.
