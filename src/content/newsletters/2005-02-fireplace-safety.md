Many of our Beaumont homes have lovely fireplaces and this is the time of year that we enjoy the comfort of a nice warm fire. Unfortunately, many of us don’t think much about the condition of our chimney before doing so. You can walk down almost any block, and you won’t have to go far before you find a chimney that’s in sad shape. Hopefully smoke isn’t also coming out of it.

In the United States, there are an average of 18,300 residential fires each year, which originate in chimneys. Hundreds of people are injured and millions are paid out in damages. The majority of fires are preventable with proper chimney maintenance.

First and foremost, have your chimney inspected and cleaned every one to two years by a professional who is certified by the Chimney Safety Institute of America (www.csia.org). Wood burning fireplaces in particular accumulate creosote, which is highly combustible and can result in a serious chimney fire. Despite products on the market that claim otherwise, manual scrubbing is still the only way to effectively get the job done. A chimney sweep will also inspect your chimney and fireplace for signs of deteriorating mortar, cracking or settling of the entire chimney, and blockages caused by debris or animal nests. Only burn seasoned hardwoods, which produce much less creosote. Never burn paper or cardboard as sparks and ash can fly up the chimney and cause a creosote fire. Also, never burn wood in a gas fireplace.

These simple tips can help keep you and your family safe and warm, and prevent a really bad start to the New Year!
