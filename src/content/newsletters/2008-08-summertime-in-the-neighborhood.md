With the official arrival of summer in late June, the weather finally changes for the better and our neighborhood comes alive. July, August, and September showcase the natural beauty of Wilshire Park and the colors of our home gardens. Predictably, the sidewalks, streets, jogging paths, play areas, and commercial enterprises are filled with people taking advantage of this special time of the year--all of which underscores how fortunate we are to live where we do.

Summer is also a good time to get to know our neighbors better, and two early-August events—Fremont Fest and the BWNA Picnic—provide ideal venues for it. Besides the fun activities and delicious food at both celebrations, there’s the opportunity to interact with local merchants and neighbors from various parts of the community. Accordingly, the BWNA Picnic is organized as an all-neighborhood potluck. With BWNA supplying the hotdogs, hamburgers, drinks, and entertainment, neighbors provide the bulk of the feast—including chips, dips, salads, entrees, and desserts. So mark your calendars -Saturday, August 2nd, for Fremont Fest, and Tuesday evening, August 5th, for the BWNA picnic. Partake in the fun and meet your neighbors!

Meanwhile, the BWNA Board is busy behind the scenes planning for fall, winter, and spring. Some of the issues on our lengthy agenda are traffic safety, a possible re-routing of Bus #10 to connect with MAX, crime prevention, littering in and around the park, and enhancement projects at Wilshire Park and Beaumont Middle School. I encourage you to get involved and let your voices be heard. Go to the BWNA Web site (www.bwna.org) and click on the links. Information is provided in both printed and video formats. And feel free to contact me personally with questions, concerns, and suggestions.

*–Al Ellis*
