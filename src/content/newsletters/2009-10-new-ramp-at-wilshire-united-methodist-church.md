The congregation of the Wilshire United Methodist Church, Native American Fellowship, was given a challenge by a member to build a ramp and improve accessibility to the church. The member, Lloyd Wagner, was not able to come to church because of the inaccessibility into the sanctuary. Lloyd graciously donated up to $10,000 and challenged the church to match these funds within one year and build the ramp. With some money already collected, construction began. Once the ramp was complete, the congregation gathered outside the church for the dedication. After a brief history of the church’s journey towards accessibility, a purification ceremony was completed. Next, Dean Wagner, father of Lloyd Wagner, cut the ribbon to the ramp. Once the ribbon was cut, Boy Scout Troop 107, which is sponsored by the church, led the way up the ramp and into the sanctuary. A plaque that honors the Wagner family, which now rest in the sanctuary next to the door from the ramp, was presented by Helen Stewart. Unfortunately, Lloyd Wagner passed away before the ramp was completed. Along with Lloyd’s generous gift, the church held many fund raisers including a weekly collection of pennies (and other denominations) as well as an ongoing effort to collect pop bottles and cans. The consistent effort and dedication of the members to reach this goal will now provide accessibility to many people for years to come.
