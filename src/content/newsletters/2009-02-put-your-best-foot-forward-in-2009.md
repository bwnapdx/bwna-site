Ours is one of the most walkable areas in the city, thanks to our bright shops, big trees, friendly neighbors and yes, calorie-burning hills. Winter drizzles are no match for the walking and jogging faithful of our neighborhood. Those stepping into 2009 with a new fitness routine can take advantage of a number of close-by resources.

Take a lap around Wilshire Park: We’re biased, but we have one of the best walking and running parks in the city. Two laps around Wilshire Park is just over a mile. Its wood-chip trail is easier on joints than pavement, and large trees can provide shelter from the rain.

Find a route that works: Here’s a popular route to get your heart rate up: Take the winding incline up Wistaria Drive, which turns into 41st Ave. Turn right onto the Alameda Street ridge, and take one of the staircases back down to Wistaria. Build into your routine staircase climbs as you gain endurance. For a more extensive walking map of the Alameda ridge and stairs, visit http://tinyurl.com/7rmojd.

Train for a half marathon: Out of your reach? Maybe not. Local fitness retailer Foot Traffic is partnering with Alameda Fitness to put on Foot Traffic University, the only half marathon-specific training program in Portland. Starting at $85, entrants get experienced and fun coaches for both runners and walkers of all ability levels. Training begins February 2, and sign-ups are open until the first week of April. Groups meet every Saturday morning through July 4, with an optional weekly strength training at Alameda Fitness Center. Foot Traffic Manager Lara McCulloch says varied training locations around Portland help runners not get bored of the same course. For more information, visit www.foottraffic.us or call (503) 284-0345.

Form a walking group: It doesn’t cost a thing to call up a neighbor, plan a regular meeting time and keep each other motivated.
