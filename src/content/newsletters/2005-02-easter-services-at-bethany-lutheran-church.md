Bethany Lutheran Church will have two services on **Easter Sunday, March 27, 2005**. Traditional, 9:30 a.m. Contemporary 11:00 a.m. Visitors are welcome. Children are invited to the Easter egg hunt following the 11 a.m. service. Bethany is located at NE 37th and Skidmore. More info: 503-284-3836.
