Bethany will once again host a Camp Lutherwood Day Camp to be held **July 31 – August 4, Monday – Friday, 9am – 3am.** This camping experience, for children ages five through twelve, is an excellent way for children to experience a little taste of camp right in their own neighborhood community.

Staff from Camp Lutherwood along with dedicated adult volunteers from Bethany Church guide the week as the children sing, play, discover, touch creation, do crafts, act, participate in Bible Adventure Time, worship… Did we mention HAVE FUN!

This day camp allows children to experience, in an outdoor setting, what a Christian summer camping program is like without leaving home. Cost for the week is $40 and scholarships are available to make it affordable for those in need. For registration information, contact Bethany Lutheran Church at (503)284-3836 or print a registration form from:
www.bethanyportland.org
