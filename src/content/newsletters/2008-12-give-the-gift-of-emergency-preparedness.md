Surprise a friend or college student with an emergency preparedness kit! At a minimum, a 72-hour emergency preparedness kit helps one to be self-sufficient in the event of an emergency or disaster, if services are limited. Visit www.oregonredcross.org for samples of kits already assembled and ready for purchase at your local Red Cross. Visit your local hardware store for emergency supplies as well as home improvement stores.

If you need an extra boost to preparing your own emergency kits, two members of the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency Team are willing to mentor you through the preparation. You can contact either member by email: Sally Sincic, sincicsj@mindspring.com, or Aaron Bertrand, netinfopdx@gmail.com.
