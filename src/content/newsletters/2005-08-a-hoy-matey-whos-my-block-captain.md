Portland has a long history of community involvement in neighborhood based crime prevention. One of the best ways to decrease crime in a community is for neighbors to be connected and communicate with each other. Neighbors need to know who and what activities belong in their neighborhood, what is unusual, and how to document and report suspicious activity. Criminals are less likely to go into an area if the community appears to be organized and united. Getting people to be watchful and involved not only reduces crime, but also increases the pride in making their neighborhood a safe and healthy place to live.

The *Neighborhood Watch* program increases security on neighborhood blocks by involving as many households as possible in a united crime prevention effort. This program teaches citizens:

- To be familiar with neighbors and activities
- To recognize and report suspicious activity
- To communicate with neighbors on a regular basis using block maps and telephone trees in order to increase home security.

Neighbors interested in organizing a *Neighborhood* or *Apartment Watch* should contact Beaumont Wilshire’s local Crime Prevention Coordinator, Preston Wong, 503-823-2781, Pwong@ci.portland.or.us. You can read about establishing your Neighborhood Watch by visiting http://www.portlandonline.com and put in a search for “neighborhood watch.”

In a business district or a large office building where topics such as shoplifting and robbery prevention, dealing with difficult people, workplace violence prevention and money handling security may be a concern, a Business Watch may be needed. Business Watch participants often develop a communication system through a fax or e-mail network to share information about potential problems and keep in touch with other local businesses.

Businesses interested in organizing a Business Watch in Beaumont-Wilshire, should contact **Preston Wong, 503-823-2781, Pwong@ci.portland.or.us**
