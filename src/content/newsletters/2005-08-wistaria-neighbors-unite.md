Neighbors in the vicinity of the traffic island at NE Wistaria Drive and NE 41st have united to replant and maintain the island. Plans have been submitted to the city for approval. The proposed plants are drought tolerant and require low maintenance. The overall effect will provide color and structure year round. Upon city approval, a date will be set for neighbors to remove the debris and prepare the island for planting. Volunteers are needed to help. An account has been opened at the Hollywood Branch of WAMU for fund raising. Checks can be made to ‘Wistaria Traffic Island’ and mailed to 4114 NE Wistaria Dr., Portland, OR 97212 or call Judy Rice at 503-281-3792.
