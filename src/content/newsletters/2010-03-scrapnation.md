Groups and/or classes can have an amazing learning experience on a fun-filled field trip to SCRAP! From art to zippers, SCRAP is a wonderland of thousands of creative reuse materials. Located in Northeast Portland, our organization inspires environmentally sustainable behavior by offering education programs and materials needed to spark creativity while learning about waste reduction and reuse. SCRAP field trips provide a hands-on experience including interactive group projects, individual take-home projects, a scavenger hunt, tour of our facility and reuse art gallery, educational chats, and more!

Further information can be found at http://scrapaction.org/creative-reuse-center/fieldtrips or by contacting John at jon@scrapaction.org or though www.scrapaction.org

Slots are starting to fill up presently for 2010, so please contact us today with any questions or to reserve your space. We look forward to seeing you and your group for a great field trip!
