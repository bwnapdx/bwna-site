There’s a new shop on Fremont that sells bikes just like the one my wife, Diane, rode to Beaumont School many years ago. Too bad she didn’t keep that Schwinn. Luckily David and Brenda Volonte have opened **Beaumont Bicycle Club** at 4225 NE Fremont, and buy, restore and sell vintage bicycles. It’s a perfect little bike shop and a wonderful resource right in our neighborhood. Call or email them at (503) 572-0231, dave@beaumontbicycle.com with your questions or comments. The shop is open Wednesdays 4-8pm, Saturdays 10-5 and Sundays 9-4. They donate a portion of their sales to the Oregon Humane Society, Dove Lewis Animal Hospital, Lance Armstrong Foundation, and BTA (Bicycle Transportation Alliance). Beaumont Bicycle Club is a dog friendly business so bring your pooch in for a fun visit too. The Volonte’s newest addition is a cute little King Charles spaniel puppy named Bentley.
