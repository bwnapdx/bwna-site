## Blackbird Wine Shop

Blackbird Wine Shop is located at 3519 NE 44th Ave (44th & Fremont), and sold their first bottle of wine on September 15, 2007. It was a Côtes du Rhône, produced bio-dynamically by a small producer. Small producers are the key to Blackbird's success. Too often, big producers add sugar and acids to "fix" their wines, as well as sulfites to preserve them. To avoid this, Blackbrid specializes in great wines from small producers that are "made on the vine."

Owner Andy Diaz might look familiar. He recently appeared on "Anthony Bourdain: No Reservations," the Puerto Rico episode. Andy is a food lover from New York – and wine is an extension of that passion. "After I did the show, I caught up with Bourdain at this bar in Chelsea. I told him about my plans for the wine shop. He had just finished shooting the Northwest episode of the show and said that Portland was the perfect city. So, I did it," Diaz said.

So why the name Blackbird? "Have you seen how many blackbirds there are in the neighborhood? Have you seen how big they are? We just wanted to be part of the community" said Diaz.

Wine tastings are available every Friday from 5:00pm - 7:00 p.m. You can also find out more information from their website, www.blackbirdwine.com or contact Blackbird at (503) 282-1887.

## Vicinato Wine Bar

Vicinato Wine Bar is located at 4605 NE Fremont. Vicinato is Italian for neighborhood, so come as you are. They offer a Happy Hour every day and all day Monday with $1.00 off all beers and wines. They also offer live music every Friday and Saturday from 8:00pm to 10:30pm, and feature a laid back and fun atmosphere with a very eclectic selection of music.

"We try to be inexpensive, with no glass of wine from the 40 glass option over $10.00 as well as featuring as many organic and bio-dynamic wines as possible" explains owner Tim Martens. They also offer many beers from around the world by the bottle and large wine glass pours. "We pour each wine in a glass large enough to let the wine open up and do what it is supposed to do" explains Martens.

With great service and a neighborhood atmosphere, as well fresh homemade foods and soups, Vicinato is becoming a popular neighborhood location.

For more information you can contact them at (503) 288-8487 or at www.vicinatowinebar.com.

## Vinideus Wine Shop & Bar

Part of the success at Vinideus Wine Shop & Bar, located at 4759 NE Fremont (and also at NW 11th & Lovejoy), is the cozy, warm but fun atmosphere. Vinideus has become a popular neighborhood gathering place since it has opened. It is a home away from home for many area customers.

"We specialize in exclusive wines from France and Italy, as well as offering great choices from Oregon, Washington and Spain" explains Diane Pierce, co-owner. No wine is over $8 a glass, and any bottle can be opened and enjoyed right off the shelves.

Along with an impressive wine selection, Vinideus also offers a menu of the dishes you might enjoy in France. This menu includes some wonderful dessert selections such as decadent chocolate and various fruit options. Menu prices are extremely affordable ranging from $3.00 -$10.00.

Vinideus is not your average wine bar, with ongoing events such as French movie night on Wednesdays, live music every Friday and Saturday evenings, plus lessons in "parlez Francais" on Sundays with co-owner Patricia Raclot.

For more information and to review a list of upcoming special events, such as tango lessons, or find out how you can host your own private party, contact Vinideus at (503) 284-2795 or on their website at www.vinideus.net.
