While crime in general has decreased this past year in the City of Portland by 6%, burglaries have increased by 10%. In one SE Portland neighborhood burglaries increased by 36%. Some of the reasons for the rise are the current high rate of unemployment and the increase in drug usage among teenagers. Do not allow yourself to be a burglary victim by following these helpful tips:

1. Keep your doors and windows locked when you are away from home. A large number of burglars enter through unlocked and open doors and windows.
2. Pick up your newspaper and mail as quickly as possible. When you are on vacation or gone for a day or two, call and stop mail and newspaper deliveries.
3. Close your blinds and/or curtains when you are away. This prevents the burglar from looking inside to see if anyone is home or what you have.
4. Keep your garage door closed at all times. An open garage door with no vehicle tells a burglar no one is home. Even if you are home, an open door makes theft or entry easy.
5. At dusk or night leave several lights on including a porch light, but not the living room light. The living room light makes it appear that you are away and left the living room light on. The kitchen or bedroom lights are better because it looks more natural. Timers are a great tool for turning lights on and off.
6. Let your neighbors know if you are gone for an extended period of time so they can watch your home. Organize a block watch for long-term prevention.
7. Notify the police bureau if your house is going to be vacant for a long period of time. Leave the police and your neighbors with emergency phone numbers.

These measures are not a guarantee that you will not be the victim of a burglary, but they will reduce the chances considerably. If you have any questions regarding burglaries call me at 503-823-2781.
