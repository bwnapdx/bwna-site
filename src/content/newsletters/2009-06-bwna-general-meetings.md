Summer is synonymous with outdoor gatherings, when people from all walks of life intermingle amidst the beauty of nature and warmth of the sun. In the BW neighborhood, picturesque Wilshire Park is a magnet for these activities. Accordingly, BWNA's June 8th General Meeting (the second Monday of the month) will focus both on events in the park and community diversity.

With respect to park activities, discussion will center on two July events--a concert and Sunday Parkways--and one August event--National Night Out Neighborhood Picnic. There will also be to an opportunity to air concerns about park usage (e.g., liter, noise, parking, etc.) and suggest ways to make improvements.

On the diversity issue, a representative from Basic Rights Oregon will address the topic of legal safeguards in Oregon aimed at equalizing rights and minimizing discrimination based on sexual preference and gender. The presentation will be followed with a question/answer session.

At the beginning of the evening, local police officers, as always, will provide a crime update and answer questions. BWNA business will immediately follow, including committee reports, the 2009-2010 BWNA Action Plan, and organization for the August picnic.

Meetings are held in the Fellowship Hall of Bethany Lutheran Church, on the corner of 37th & Skidmore, and begin at 7:30pm. (A sign will indicate where to enter on the Skidmore side of the church). Become informed and lend your voice to the shaping of our neighborhood.
