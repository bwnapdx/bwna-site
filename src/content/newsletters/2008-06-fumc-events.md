**Neighborhood Summer Solstice Celebration**: Join us on the front lawn, Sunday, June 22nd, 11AM-1:30PM for a barbecue and family activities to celebrate the beginning of summer. All are welcome! Sponsored by Fremont United Methodist Church (FUMC).

Fremont United Methodist Church is taking a different approach to Vacation Bible School this year. “Fourth Sunday: Growing With God” will be an inter-generational gathering immediately after the 9:30 worship service, from 10:30 to noon on the fourth Sundays of June, July, and August. Bring your family, or come by yourself, for some music, games, crafts, and discussion about creation and stewardship. Our first gathering coincides with Fremont’s Summer Solstice Party on June 22, food provided. Fremont UMC is located at 2620 NE Fremont St., 503.284.4647.
