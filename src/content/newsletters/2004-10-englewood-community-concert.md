Englewood Christian Church, 3515 NE Killingsworth
Friday **October 15**, 7:30 pm (free will offering)
Enjoy an evening of ballads, gospel songs, old American favorites, and sing-a-longs in four-part harmony. Headlining the event will be the Rose City Timberliners and Northwest Harmony.

A reception will be held following the concert. For information contact the church office at 503-287-1241.
