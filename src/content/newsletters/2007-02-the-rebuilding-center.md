The ReBuilding Center located at 3626 N Mississippi (one block north of Fremont) is a building materials thrift store which accepts and carries Portland’s largest variety and volume of used building and remodeling materials. Everything from wood-framed windows, to support beams, to chandeliers are accepted as donations, if in reusable condition. We even come by to pick stuff up. “When upgrading - taking out cabinets, for example - people don’t realize that there is a lot of life left in these materials,” said developer and Beaumont Wilshire resident Tim Ray, who donated excess materials to The ReBuilding Center when building the Fremont Commons.

Materials are sold to the public at 50 to 90 percent off retail value. Grants of building materials are available to projects that enhance the local community. The ReBuilding Center also offers DeConstruction Services, a sustainable alternative to demolition, and ReFind Furniture, which makes functional furnishings from castoff building materials and milled salvaged lumber. The ReBuilding Center keeps 5 tons of reusable materials out of the landfill every day; it is anticipated that within three years this will increase to 10 tons per day. Now in its eighth year, The ReBuilding Center was initially operated by four dedicated volunteers. Today, it employs 50 people who earn a living wage with full benefits. For more information on The ReBuilding Center, a non-profit program of Our United Villages, please visit the web site: www.rebuildingcenter.org or call 503-517-0761.
