In the June/July issue of our newsletter, we asked you to help us spend $1,000 dollars for neighborhood projects. We had 3 proposals for use of the money and supported all of them. Max Cowan, a member of the Beaumont Middle School Band, was awarded $500 towards drum line equipment and chair racks for the band. The Beaumont Wilshire Land Use and Transportation Committee was awarded $100 for sign-making materials asking drivers to slow down and $200 for signs that they have placed in local businesses asking their customers to be courteous and not park in or block residential driveways. Be watching the newsletter for other opportunities to help us spend neighborhood association money but you don’t have to wait for the next solicitation for projects, you can pass along ideas to the BW board to enhance the neighborhood at any time.
