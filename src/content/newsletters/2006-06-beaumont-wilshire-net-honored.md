On April 29, members of the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency Team (BWNET) received three awards from the Portland Office of Emergency Management during their “Ready. Safe. Go.” training conference and recognition event.

The Star Volunteer Awards reflect exceptional initiative, enterprise, creativity, dedication, and an extraordinary attitude in service to the Portland community. Our neighborhood is fortunate to have two NET members individually recognized with this prestigious award: Liz Johnston and Erika Wolfe Burr.

Liz completed her NET training in 2001 and has been our team leader since 2002. She’s served on several committees, assisting in everything from budget recommendations for the Portland Office of Emergency Management and Portland Fire & Rescue to giving City Council testimony regarding the effectiveness of the NET program.

Erika has been a member of our NET for three years and just completed her EMT training at Portland Community College. In addition to this busy schedule, she volunteers as a disaster preparedness presenter for the Portland Office of Emergency Management and a first aid provider for Event Medical Services.

The Beaumont Wilshire NET received a team award for its outstanding participation in and commitment to the program. Our team has 13 certified members who keep up with neighborhood issues and refresh emergency preparedness techniques by training regularly with Station 14 in NE Portland.

NET membership is a great way to meet your neighbors while learning how to keep your friends and family safe during a disaster. We’re always looking for new members, please contact Lawrence Behmer at the Office of Emergency Management at 503-823-4421 for more information. The next free NET training is scheduled for fall 2006.
