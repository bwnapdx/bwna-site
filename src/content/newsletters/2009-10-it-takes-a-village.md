The focus of the BWNA General Meeting on Monday, Oct. 12th, will be on community teamwork as a basis for improving and enhancing the neighborhood. Appropriately, the two featured speakers represent very different institutions, but they network in ways that make our community a more attractive and welcoming place to live. Marsha Dennis is Graffiti Abatement Coordinator for the City’s Office of Neighborhood Involvement, and Liz Casson-Taylor is the new Principal at Beaumont Middle School. Marsha and her staff work closely with neighborhood schools in combatting the spread of graffiti (which has been on the increase in the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood), while Principal Casson-Taylor, along with her staff, promote positive values and responsible behavior in their students. Each presentation will be followed by a brief question-and-answer session.

“It takes a village” community input is the focus of the second part of the meeting. Neighbors in attendance will be asked to share their ideas for projects and activities that they would like to see BWNA support in the coming months through volunteer efforts and/or funding. These suggestions will form the basis for BWNA Board decisions concerning which community teamwork programs to pursue in the near future.

The meeting begins at 7:30 at Bethany Lutheran Church, on the corner of 37th and Skidmore, and is held in Fellowship Hall, a basement activity room that is most easily accessed down the walkway on the Skidmore side of the church. (There will be a BWNA sign indicating the entrance.) We encourage you to come and learn about the city’s graffiti abatement program, welcome Principal Casson-Taylor to our neighborhood, and help shape the future agenda for BWNA.
