Not to be outdone by Iowa or New Hampshire, the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association will hold their annual general elections at the Community Meeting, April 14. You can be the next president of the association, the treasurer or a board member. The following positions are open:

- Five member-at-large positions (one of these is the chair of the transportation/ land use committee)
- Two officer positions – President and Treasurer

All positions are two year commitments. If you are interested in getting involved and helping with neighborhood projects, now is the time! Let Bill Markwardt, acting president, know. He can be reached at president@bwna.org 503-282-4610. Or just come to the April 14, 2008 meeting.
