*The annual Taste of Beaumont was held on April 9 this year and featured Beaumont Middle School's Jazz Ambassadors, conducted by band director Cynthia Plank. Attendees sampled an eclectic feast prepared by local restaurants. Other local restaurants and businesses provided door prizes and raffle items. Plan to join us next year for this fun event.
