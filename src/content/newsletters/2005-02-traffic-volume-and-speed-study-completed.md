The transportation and land use committee continues to meet at Java Man, 4727 NE Fremont at 7:30 pm on the third Monday of the month. The committee is in the process of developing a mission statement and a series of transportation and land use related goals. These will be presented in the April/May issue of the newsletter.

The City of Portland Department of Transportation (PDOT) performed a study during December and January, to determine the traffic volume and traffic speed at a number of locations within the neighborhood. This is the study that many of you requested by signing the “Safe Streets” petition at the picnic last summer. The results were presented by PDOT at the January meeting.

The Beaumont Wilshire web site (www.bwna.org) has added a new section that provides information on transportation and land use issues that affect our neighborhood. Look on the web site for results of the traffic study.
