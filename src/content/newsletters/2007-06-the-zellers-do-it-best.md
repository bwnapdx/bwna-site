Most of us in the neighborhood cannot remember a time when there was NOT a hardware store on Fremont. Beaumont Hardware was started in the 1950's at NE Fremont and 42nd. It moved to its present location at **4303 NE Fremont** in the 60's. Other tenants in the building were bought out to make room as the inventory expanded and since it opened, it has had many owners.

Tony and Kim Zeller bought the business in June 2005 from Mike and Connie Eggleston who were getting ready to retire. The Zellers have made every effort to make a smooth transition and have retained many former employees who were knowledgeable about the business. Tony grew up in Portland and is the fifth generation of the family who owned the Zeller Chapel of Roses at NE Broadway at 21st. He met his wife Kim while she was employed at the Zeller funeral home.

Tony and Kim are committed to maintaining good customer service at Beaumont Hardware. They were one the first small local businesses to establish a store website where people can shop online at:
http://beaumont.doitbest.com/

If an item you want is not in stock, they can order it from the warehouse in just a few days. The neighborhood is lucky to have this friendly, locally owned small business catering to all the 'do it yourselfers' and gardeners in Beaumont Wilshire. Thanks, Tony and Kim, for continuing an important community tradition. Visit Beaumont Hardware at 4303 NE Fremont, open seven days a week or call 503-281-4406.
