Saturday, April 23rd 10:30am

The annual Neighborhood Easter Egg Hunt will take place this year beginning on the front lawn of Bethany Church and Neighborhood Center at 37th and Skidmore just across from Wilshire Park. Children (toddlers through 10-year-olds) are invited to bring their Easter baskets and join in the fun for this annual community event. There will be hundreds of eggs waiting to be found. Bring the whole family. It’s GREAT FUN and it’s FREE! For more information call 503.284.3836 or go to www.bethanyportland.org. The Easter Egg Hunt will take place rain or shine!!
