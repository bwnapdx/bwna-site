Beaumont Middle School, located in the heart of the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood (4043 N.E. Fremont St.), invites local families, friends, neighbors, businesses, and all current and past Beaumont students and staff, to join the celebration of Beaumont's Centennial on Sunday, May 22, from 2:00-4:00 p.m. The fun kicks off with Beaumont's 200+ student Marching Band and flag team strutting its stuff on the Beaumont field, then expands into the school gym and cafeteria for games (with prizes!), races, a staff vs. student free-throw contest, birthday cake, an exhibition of student work, and much more.

There is no admission to this event, but anyone who would like to donate to Beaumont's Food Pantry is welcome to bring non-perishable food, which will be distributed to Beaumont families in need.

Come celebrate 100 great years—and help launch Beaumont's next 100 years of educational excellence!
