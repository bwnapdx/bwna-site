Reduce your chances of being a victim of a car prowl. Some suggestions from your police bureau:

- Never leave valuables in your car (no purses left under the seat)
- Don’t leave important identification papers in the glove box.
- Park in your garage.
- Park in well lighted areas.
- Avoid leaving your car in unattended parking lots for long periods of time.
- Keep your keys with you; avoid putting keys in ‘hiding places.’
- Organize a neighborhood watch program.
- Report all crimes to the Portland Police Bureau.
