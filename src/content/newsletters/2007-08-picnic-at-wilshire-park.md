Tuesday, August 7th, 6:30 – 8:30 PM

The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association invites *YOU* to the annual BWNA picnic in celebration of National Night Out. *Everyone is invited to this biggest neighborhood event of the year.*

Bring a salad, side dish or dessert to share. In the spirit of making the picnic ‘green’ we ask that you bring your own non-disposable utensils–forks, knives and spoons. Local businesses and the association will provide hamburgers, hot dogs, drinks, plates and biodegradable utensils. *There will be door prizes and surprises.* Please mark your dishes on the bottom so they can be returned to you if they get left behind. Don’t miss this neighborhood get-together on **Tuesday Aug. 7th from 6:30 to 8:30pm at Wilshire Park.**
