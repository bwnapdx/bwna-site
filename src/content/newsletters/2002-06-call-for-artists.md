We are planning to create an online art space at **www.bwna.org. Artists** who live or work in Beaumont Wilshire are welcome to submit 5–10 digital images (jpeg files) of their work. Include title, media, dimensions and date. Each image must be less than one megabyte. A bio of exhibitions, education, awards, etc. is optional. This is a free service. Sales transactions are between artist and buyer—include contact information. Email to **mc-hk@msn.com**, Mario 288-2568.
