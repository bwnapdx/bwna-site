Beaumont Wilshire has long been home to a coffee shop or two, but it’s just recently that the neighborhood can boast its own coffee drink,” the Fremont Latte”. The delicious honey vanilla concoction is the creation of Owner/Manager Sherry Loving. Sherry and Ryan Loving opened for business in February at 4727 NE Fremont Street. They renamed the shop”Java Man on Fremont” from plain Java Man thanks to the suggestion of customers Lee and Daniel Brixius.

Sherry grew up in the coffee business working for her parents and Starbucks. Ryan brings product/ marketing expertise to the partnership. The Lovings chose this location, because they like the community and its sense of family values. They also live nearby with their 5-month old daughter Madison. Their goal is to be the neighborhood coffee shop that offers consistent quality and community focus. They view Starbucks down the street as “healthy competition”.

On the menu are freshly brewed coffee from Allann Brothers Roasters, a variety of special recipe coffee drinks including the Fremont Latte, Mighty Leaf Tea, Fruit & Yogurt Smoothies, Bubble Tea and biscotti. The Lovings will work closely with the owner of the Criollo Bakery next door to offer a complement of lunch and dessert. “We’re going for a Tuscan, European theme,” according to Ryan. The warm colors and sunny ambiance speak to the look. Customers can move freely between the French doors connecting the two shops.

Future plans include last Thursday of the month art shows featuring local artists, quarterly used book sales with proceeds donated to local schools, and free internet access.

Java Man on Fremont is open 6am–7pm Monday to Thursday; 6am–11pm Friday; 7am–11pm on Saturday; and 7am–4pm on Sunday. Parking is available daily from 6am–4:30pm in Amalfi’s parking lot.
