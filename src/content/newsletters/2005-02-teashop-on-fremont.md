To some, it may not seem obvious, but those of us who have lived in Beaumont Wilshire for a while know that our business area, the Beaumont Village, has grown by leaps and bounds in recent years. The demolition of Rose’s Ice Cream started an evolution that continues to change the face of NE Fremont. From those ashes arose a ‘green building,’ designed by Kevin Cavanaugh and built by Siteworks Construction.

Across the street a couple blocks east, developer Tim Ray continued this renaissance with the recently unveiled Fremont Commons. Its neighborhood-friendly design features a courtyard, inviting passers-by to stop and well… smell the tea brewing at **Foxfire Teas**, a cozy, organic tea house located in the northwest corner of the Commons. With no shortage of coffee shops, this tea emporium is a welcome addition to Beaumont Village.

Husband and wife team, Quinn & Katherine Losselyong opened Foxfire Teas in October 2004, growing slowly but surely with the best advertising there is: word of mouth. Up early every day to bake “Portland’s best scones”, Katherine, an epicurean artist, whips up the softest scones I have ever tasted.

Quinn created the open-air design at Foxfire Teas. With secluded corners and a central fireplace, its ambience is at once comfortable and easy, intimate and spacious—a difficult, fine-line to achieve. The log-stump stools give it a twist, reminding us we live in the land of timber.

The tea list is extensive. One should arrive with enough time to peruse the list of organic teas available here. For the true tea aficionado, Foxfire Teas is nirvana and for area residents, Fremont has never looked better! **Foxfire Teas 4605 NE Fremont**. Open Wednesday to Thursday 9am–8pm; Friday & Saturday 9am–10pm; Sunday 9am–6pm.
