Brian Faherty and Lance Marrs were guest speakers at the November 15th BWNA land use and transportation committee meeting. Mr. Faherty, a Portland resident who grew up in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood, and Mr. Marrs are planning a new development project at 4323–4339 NE Fremont.

The project site currently holds two older houses that contain the retail tenants Bellamar Salon and the recently closed Savory Restaurant. In a conversation with Mr. Faherty, the Committee learned that the project is still in the early planning and feasibility-study stage. Mr. Faherty expressed his intention to build a building that is sensitive to the scale and character of Fremont St. He explained that he has a close connection to the neighborhood. His first job was at Amalfi's restaurant on Fremont and his grandmother, who still lives in the neighborhood, will be watching the development plans closely.

Mr. Faherty was able to share some specifics of the project. He and Mr. Marrs intend to build a one-story retail structure with up to four tenant spaces. He is anticipating that construction will consist of an approximately 8,000 square foot building on the 10,185 square foot site. This will allow the building to be set back from the sidewalk slightly to soften the urban edge. Mr. Faherty said that building a two story building with office space on the second floor is not in character with the neighborhood and would create too much parking pressure in area. Parking on the property is not likely, he said, because it would require roughly half of the lot to create code conforming parking spaces.
