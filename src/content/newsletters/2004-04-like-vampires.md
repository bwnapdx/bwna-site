> **BWNA General Meeting**
> **Monday, April 12, 7:30 PM**
> Board Elections ~ Tax Assistance ~ Crime Prevention
> Bethany Lutheran Church, NE 37th & Skidmore
> All Beaumont Wilshire residents welcome!

Like vampires, the board of directors of the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association is always looking for fresh blood. Our April General Meeting will provide us with just such an opportunity—as we hold our annual board elections.

In even numbered years (like 2004) we will elect a **President** and a **Treasurer** to our 17-person board. There will also be **six** open positions for **members at large**. All elected terms will last for two years and will require attending no more than one meeting a month.

We invite you to join us as we proactively work together to enhance the livability of our neighborhood and establish and maintain open lines of communication between our neighbors and the agencies, officials and institutions we rely on. The Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood is already a great place to live, work and play—and the unstated goal of the BWNA is to keep making it even better.

If you would like to be nominated for a board position, but are shy about raising your hand, please feel free to email me at vicepresident@bwna.org and I’ll make sure to add you to our slate of candidates.

Our meeting will convene in the basement of Bethany Lutheran Church (at NE 37th and Skidmore) on Monday, April 12th at 7:30 PM. We have a fairly light agenda and a fast-talking President, so we should be out of there by 8:30.

Dave Austin, the new Crime Prevention Specialist at Central Northeast Neighbors, will be a guest speaker. He has been assigned the responsibilities of providing public education, problem solving and resource sharing in enhancing the efforts we all share towards creating safer and more livable neighborhoods. We look forward to meeting him and sharing our concerns and ideas with him.

Also, for extreme procrastinators, we will have employees of Multnomah County’s Finance Department on hand. They will be available to answer questions, provide forms and improve our understanding of the new county tax, which is due on April 15th for the 2003 tax year.

Hope to see you there!
