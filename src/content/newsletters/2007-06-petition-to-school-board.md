Residents of the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood north of Skidmore have been circulating a petition to have school district boundaries redrawn. Shannon Berlant, one of the signature gatherers, knows it can be difficult to affect this sort of change, but after researching enrollment patterns and figuring out which schools were closest to her home, she decided to take action.

"All of our assigned schools (Vernon, Tubman and Jefferson) are significantly farther away from our homes than schools in the adjoining district. Alameda, Beaumont and Grant are much closer. I feel like the school district has divided our neighborhood with these boundaries."

The petition highlights these discrepancies and also states that current district boundaries force some children to cross high-traffic streets to get to school bus stops. Residents who support the petition believe that sending their children to the schools closest to their homes will create a greater sense of community and foster a greater connection to their schools, as well as allow children to attend the same schools as their friends who live nearby.

Neighbors have collected over 200 signatures and gathered enrollment data. There is a pattern of declining enrollment which seems directly related to dissatisfaction with schools within the existing boundaries. For more information please email:
