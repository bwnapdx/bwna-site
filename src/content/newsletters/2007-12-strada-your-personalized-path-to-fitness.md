“Strada” is the Italian word for “path” or “way”. It’s also the name of the “training, adventure, and discovery” center located at **4815 NE Fremont**. Strada is not your typical fitness center. There are no joining fees, memberships, open gym times, or one-size-fits-all training packages. Executive director Steve Schulz says Strada offers more of lifestyle coaching, as opposed to personal training. Lifestyle coaching is customized to meet the goals and needs of each individual, and may include strength and endurance training, nutritional guidance, functional training to experience day-to-day activities efficiently, and outdoor adventure. It may even include goal setting for individuals recovering from chemotherapy or surgery.

Schulz moved to Portland in 1994 from Colorado where he earned a degree from the University of Colorado with a minor in exercise science. He initially worked as a personal trainer for large corporate fitness clubs, but did not find the work fulfilling. In 1999, he and a colleague opened Tri Level Fitness at the current location. The name of the club changed a couple years ago to better convey the commitment to working with individuals to find their personal way to fitness.

Strada draws clients from the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood, as well as the broader Portland area and beyond. Offerings include individualized programs addressing personal goals and group goals, as well as early morning classes on Mondays and Wednesdays. Among Strada’s more unique services are multi-day and multi-sport events. For example, Strada has teamed up with the Bike Gallery and Cycle Oregon since 2003 to prepare cyclists to enjoy the 500-mile, seven-day adventure ride across Oregon. During the winter, Strada offers outdoor trips such as snow shoeing for all skill levels. Seasonally, Strada staff work with elementary schools to offer “Explosion 4 Kids”, an after-school, at-school exercise program for kids in kindergarten through grade 5.

The vision for Strada is to be unique in the fitness arena. It is also about you and having fun. For a more complete description of offerings and fees, check the website at www.stradalife.com, or call 503-493-2413 with questions or for an appointment.
