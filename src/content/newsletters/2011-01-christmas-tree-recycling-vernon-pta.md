**Fundraiser for Vernon PTA**

We’ll be on the west side of Vernon School, on NE 20th Ave. Please remove ALL decorations, tinsel, etc before bringing your tree down. All trees will be taken to Wood Waste Management, where they will mulch the trees. All proceeds will go to the Vernon PTA, to help provide free events to our families and community. Suggested donation: $10 if you drop your tree off at the school, $20 if we come pick it up from you.

Please contact Kris Woolen at 503.422.7866 or kcbw@comcast.net or Megan Lynch at 503.929.5691 or heya.megan@gmail.com if you have any questions, or would like to help. Pass this info along to your friends and neighbors, too!

**Sunday, January 2, 12:30pm - 4:30pm**
**Saturday, January 8, 10:30am - 4:30pm**
