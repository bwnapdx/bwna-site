All the wonderful gardens in our neighborhood prompted a few of us to think about a **Beaumont Garden Club**. We could have garden tours, trade plants in the fall when we divide our perennials, collect and exchange seeds, etc. If you are interested in this idea, please give me a call at 503-288-3441 (evenings only please) or email me at **janbak@pacifier.com**.
