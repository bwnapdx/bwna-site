**PJ's Purple Peddler** will be out on the streets on Tuesdays and Wednesdays during June and July around 6PM bringing **free** frozen treats to the kids in our neighborhood as a way of bringing community together. Look for PJ's (Pastor Jeff's) purple bicycle in our neighborhood and listen for the bicycle bell being rung down your street. When you see him, wave at him to stop for a **free** frozen treat. This event is sponsored by the Bethany's outreach team.
Web: www.bethanyportland.org or call (503) 284-3836.
