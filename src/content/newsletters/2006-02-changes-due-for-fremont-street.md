> Monday, February 13, 7:30pm
> Bethany Lutheran Church
> NE 37th and Skidmore

Are you frustrated trying to cross Fremont Street? Have difficulty pulling onto Fremont Street from a side street? Sometime before the beginning of summer, you will start to see fresh paint on the pavement that will help address your concerns. David Whitaker, the BWNA transportation and land use chair, will speak at our general meeting on Feb. 13th. He will discuss Fremont St. improvement projects that were presented at two open houses in January by the City of Portland. See the land use article written by Dennis Lisk on page 5 for a description of some of these exciting, new projects. **Please join us at this important meeting.**

*—Dave Whitaker*
