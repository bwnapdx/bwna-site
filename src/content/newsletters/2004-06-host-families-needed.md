Since 1983, Education Travel & Culture, Inc. (ETC, http://www.edutrav.org) a local, non-profit organization, has promoted international understanding and goodwill through educational and cultural exchanges. ETC provides opportunities for international high school students to study in an American school while living with an American family. Host families provide room and board, a quiet place to study, and an American family experience for guest students during their semester or academic year. Foreign exchange students provide J-1 visas, transportation, personal health insurance & a monthly allowance

ETC’s Community Coordinators (CC) are advocates for both the student and host family. The CC calls and visits regularly to assess student progress & assist with the adjustment process. The CC submits monthly evaluations to the ETC main office, the student’s natural parents and overseas sponsoring organization. The CC also periodically checks on the student’s school progress, providing assistance as needed.

If your family is interested in being a Host Family for a student from Korea, Japan, Vietnam, Brazil, Germany or Kyrgyzstan, please call ETC Community Coordinator Julia Haykin at (503) 249-1094.
