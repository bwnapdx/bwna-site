This year’s show “Adornment and Provisions for Life with Baby” is on **Saturday, October 6th from 10-4** at the Oregon Stamp Society building on NE 33rd Avenue & Alberta Court and is a benefit for the Oregon Food Bank. Attendance is free but shoppers are asked to contribute a non-perishable food item (no glass containers) for donation at the door. As well, many of the vendors will be donating a portion of the day’s sales to the Oregon Food Bank.

Cat in an Apron, a cooperative venture, was established in 2006 by three NE Portland residents: Anne Gaber of Firecracker Baby, Marne Nordean of OHenry and Shannon Swartley of Splendid Wares. It was formed to provide an outlet for small, high quality local designers. Their mission is to focus on designers who put their time and energy into their design and product, but may not want to create large quantities of goods that are required for retail sales. This allows the items to be unique, limited editions.

Products for the show are previewed and juried based on the integrity of the design and the inherent beauty and quality of materials. Don’t miss this exiting showcase of local designers.
