I became acquainted with Ralph Bateman and his son, Jay, while enjoying the rehearsals of the Rose City Timberliners Barbershop Chorus at Wilshire United Methodist Church. When I visited with them, I learned that Ralph and his family lived just around the block from me. Ralph and his wife Ginnie are both retired. Ralph worked as an air traffic controller at the Portland airport for 37 years. His career spanned the early Boeings 247’s, the later mammoth 747’s, the arrival of Air Force One to Portland and the well-publicized hijacking by DB Cooper in 1971. *(See Cooper story p. 4)*. Ginnie worked as a medical technologist and real estate agent before retiring.

Originally from Utah, Ralph moved to California when he was 19 and worked for the Lockheed Corporation. Later he obtained a pilot’s license and became an air traffic controller. He did similar work when he was in the Navy for three years. He moved to Portland in 1942 where he met Virginia at the Old Portland Ice Rink. Ginnie, as she is called, learned to figure skate when she was growing up in North Dakota. They were soon married and settled in Beaumont Wilshire, where they raised son, Jay, and two daughters, Jean and Julie. Jean majored in music, Julie became a drama teacher at Wilson High School and Jay studied electronics at Benson High School.

Over the years, the Batemans have enjoyed special family traditions and get-togethers. Every year for the past 52 years, the whole family rents a cottage at Hood Canal, Washington, so they can vacation together. I enjoyed becoming acquainted with Ralph and his family and when I visited his home, I discovered that Ralph is a talented wood carver and has a splendid collection of exquisite birds, which he carved by hand. Family and hobbies keep life interesting for Ralph in his retirement years.

*Ralph’s works can now be viewed on Beaumont Artspace. To see Ralph’s carved figures, including ducks, fish and birds, click on ‘artspace’ at www.bwna.org.*
