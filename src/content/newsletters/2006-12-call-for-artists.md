Artists who live or work in the Beaumont Wilshire area are invited to join Beaumont Artspace, http://www.bwna.org/artspace/ an online gallery sponsored by the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA). There is no cost to artists to participate. For info on submitting work go to http://www.bwna.org/artspace/submit.html
