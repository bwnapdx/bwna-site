Beginning Sunday, July 27, Bethany will once again host Camp Lutherwood Day Camp which will be held Monday – Friday (July 28 – Aug. 1) from 9 AM – 3 PM. This camping experience, for children ages 5-12, allows children to experience, in an outdoor setting, what a Christian summer camping program is like without leaving home. Staff from Camp Lutherwood along with dedicated adult volunteers from Bethany Church guide the week as the children sing, play, discover, do crafts, act, worship and HAVE FUN! Cost for the week is $40 and includes lunch, two snacks, and a field trip to Grant Pool. Scholarships are available to make it affordable for those in need. Contact Bethany Lutheran Church for registration information at (503)284-3836 or go to our website at www.bethanyportland.org and download the registration form.
