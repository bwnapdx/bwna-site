November 5th, 9:00am - 3:00pm
Bethany Church: corner of 37th and Skidmore

The community is invited to Bethany’s Annual Christmas Bazaar the first Saturday in November. This annual event is a favorite among the neighborhood to usher in the Christmas season. The ladies of Bethany have spent the year making homemade craft items, and have gathered many treasures and vintage items for the White Elephant Sale. Refreshments will be available, as well as baked goods to take home. Join your neighbors and friends for this holiday event.
