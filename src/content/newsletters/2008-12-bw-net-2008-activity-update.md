The BW NET is active year-round with meetings on community outreach, trainings with teams in the Fire Management Zone (FMZ 14), regional trainings, and monthly team meetings. BW NET has had meetings with Fire Station 14 as well. Here are some of the highlights: Community outreach at Hollywood Farmers Market, Fremont Fest, and presence at National Night Out picnic in Wilshire Park. FMZ meetings covered Building Size-up, Radio Communications, Earthquakes in the Pacific NW, and Incident Command System. Participation by BW NET members (with two family members serving as “victims”) in the Emergency Response Team Rodeo at Tualatin Valley Fire and Rescue Training Center that featured emergency skill drill and demonstration stations.

The fall NET training (free, 8-week training) has begun and includes sections on disaster awareness, hazard mitigation, utility control, fire suppression, hazardous materials, disaster medicine, light search and rescue, and team response and management. Expectations are that the winter training could begin in January 2009. Visit www.pdxprepared.net for general NET information, including future trainings. A partial list of active BW NET members include, Aaron Bertrand, Chris Boeh (amateur ham radio operator), Keith Carlson, Mary Copenhaver (Alternate Team Leader), Ellen Karas, Marian McDonald, Sally Sincic, and Erika Wolfe-Burr EMT-B. Liz Johnston is the team leader for Beaumont-Wilshire NET and can be reached at liz@keithcd.com.
