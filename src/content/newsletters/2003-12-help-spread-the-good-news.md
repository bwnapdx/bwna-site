How would you like to get a little exercise while helping out the neighborhood? We need volunteers to be Newsletter Delivery people. We have several routes open, and most take less than an hour.

We are also in need of one Newsletter Captain. The Captain receives a bundle of newsletters and counts them out and gets them to each delivery person. Newsletter delivery is six times a year.

If you would like to sign up, or if you have any questions, contact: Lisa Perkins, 503-282-5816 or lisachiba@hotmail.com.
