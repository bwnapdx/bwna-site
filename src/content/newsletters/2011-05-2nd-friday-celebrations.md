The Beaumont neighborhood re-launches its 2nd Friday celebrations beginning Friday May 13th. For the Beaumont Business Association these have been years of immense growth, and they are set to resume 2nd Fridays on Fremont complete with an official re-branding effort. That is not to say that the events that have occurred thus far should be ignored, but the BBA has decided it is high time to become more official in their ventures.

They started off with a website last September (www.BeaumontBBA.com) and can be seen regularly via Facebook (Beaumont Business Association) and Twitter (@BeaumontBizPDX.) This August will be the 25th anniversary for Fremont Fest, with a budget that doubled from last year.

2nd Fridays are a chance for local vendors to rent sidewalk space, and businesses stay open later than usual. Also included are food and drink specials from restaurants, live music, and local artists. As warmer weather nears, additional farmer’s market booths are expected to join in.

As stated by Cornelius Stewart at The Sentinal:

> Beaumont Village is gaining a reputation for its bustling, unique assortment of businesses, including custom and digital print shop services, auto servicing, and optical sales and repair. Locals point to their favorite boutiques, card and gift shops, live jazz bistro bars and wine shops, premier cuisine, and family friendly vendors. Many will offer refreshments and markdowns with colorful sidewalk displays. Also in May a veteran Beaumont vendor will celebrate its 31st anniversary in the neighborhood: The Arrangement.
>
> Beaumont Village boasts a hardware store, market, florist, and children’s bookstore on its friendly, small town-style main street. With trendy shops and eateries, it’s carving out its own upscale and family friendly atmosphere, suitable for strolling or exploring by bike. Coffee lovers enjoy cult Portland coffeehouses while window shoppers browse through a handful of antique shops and gift stores.
