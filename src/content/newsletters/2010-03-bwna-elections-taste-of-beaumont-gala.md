The Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association Board of Directors is the leadership cadre that keeps our neighborhood association afloat.

From organizing social events to responding to issues of importance to the Beaumont-Wilshire (BW) community, Board members function as decision-makers and implementers for the neighborhood. But over a year’s time--due to conflicting work schedules, home situations, and moves out of the neighborhood--Board vacancies occur, and new volunteers need to be found. To this end, elections are held every year on the second Monday of April--this year April 12th--and in an effort to attract the largest crowd possible, the General Meeting is combined with one of BWNA’s showcase events of the year--

## “Taste of Beaumont.”

The evening’s festivities get off to an early start at 6:30pm in the Bethany Lutheran Church basement activity room, known as “Fellowship Hall”--where all BWNA General Meetings are held. Local restaurants and cafes set up around the room, offering free cuisine samples (bottled water will be available as well). At the same time, entertainment is provided by the talented student musicians of Beaumont Middle School’s Jazz Ensemble, led by effervescent band director Cynthia Plank. It’s a veritable feast amidst a lively neighborhood-friendly beat!

The local merchants who have graciously contributed to this event include Alameda Cafe, Alameda Brewhouse, Ambient Prints (responsible for the banner), Beaumont Florist (responsible for the table floral arrangements), Beaumont Market (responsible for the cold beverages), Delphina’s Bakery, Doc George’s Jazz Kitchen, Papa Murphy’s Pizza, Pizzacato, Rose’s Ice Cream, Roundtable Pizza, Soluna Grill, Starbucks--Beaumont, and Tuk, Tuk, Thai (subsequent responders will be acknowledged in the May/June issue.)

A General Meeting follows, beginning around 7:15pm with a crime report from a neighborhood police officer and a brief Q & A session. Then begins the culminating activity of the evening--nominations to fill vacant Board and officer positions and subsequent voting. (We urge you to consider volunteering to serve!)

In the end, you will have sampled some of the finest cuisines in the neighborhood, been entertained by gifted young musicians, and participated in the selection of your neighborhood leaders. Not too bad for one Monday evening in April.
