Please join us April 10 (during the BWNA general meeting) for the third annual Taste of Beaumont. It starts at 6:30 pm and will be held in the basement of Bethany Lutheran Church at NE 37 and Skidmore. Many restaurants located in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood will bring samples of delicious treats from their menus. There will also be two gift certificates from the Alameda Brewhouse that will be raffled at the meeting. While we are enjoying the food, musicians from Beaumont Middle School Jazz Band will serenade us. This is a great opportunity to try out food from these restaurants, and taste what our neighborhood businesses have to offer. Mark your calendars, and don’t miss it! Here are just some of the participating restaurants:

- **Alameda Café**
- **Blue Olive**
- **Fife**
- **Foxfire Teas**
- **Parisi’s**
- **Round Table pizza**
- **Tuk Tuk** and others!
