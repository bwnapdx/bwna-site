Members of the neighborhood association's Artspace group will unveil new works, as well as some new takes on traditional media, from June 1 to June 28 at Blackbird Wineshop, 3519 N.E. 45th Ave.

Rita Catapano and Keith Buckley will show photographs, including her black-and-white “Invocation.” Mario Caoile, David Friedman, Sally McKeon, Gay Mitchell, and Suzie Spaggiari will show paintings that range from abstract to representational. Brita Gould will present “Swirling” and “Inspire,” sculpted and painted polymer masks. I will debut my latest limited-edition letterpress book, “A Tango with Ataturk.”

To see the results and breadth of your neighbors' creativity, drop in anytime during Blackbird’s usual hours and stop by for a visit and some vino with the participating artists at a reception from 7 pm to 9 pm Thursday, June 11.

The Artspace group maintains a gallery on the neighborhood association’s Web site (www.bwna.us) and meets monthly to brainstorm, inspire, and present our work.
