The very first Umpqua Bank opened in 1953 in a small town called Canyonville, Oregon by four loggers who wanted to ‘be in charge’ of their money. By 1994 it had grown to six branches still in southern Oregon. In the same year, Ray Davis became CEO and President and launched the ‘store’ concept, as a way to differentiate Umpqua Bank from other banks. If you have visited an Umpqua Bank, you will see why it is called a store. Each site has the feel of a boutique, with a neighborhood slant. Today there are 144 Umpqua Banks stretching from Seattle to Sacramento. And one of the newest stores is the one in Beaumont Wilshire at **4335 NE Fremont** which opened in November 2006.

Besides offering all the regular services of a bank, Umpqua Bank is unique in so many ways. It provides free classes for first time home buyers and classes for small business owners on such topics as bookkeeping and marketing skills. Recently they sponsored a beer tasting class in conjunction with Alameda Brewhouse! According to manager Jeff Goodman, bank staff are encouraged and supported in participating in community services. Staff members Nathan and Crystle volunteer at the Hollywood Library, Angeline and Jeff help out at Habitat for Humanity, Crystal at Meals on Wheels and Dusty at Children’s Relief Nursery.

Umpqua Bank also shows its support for local neighborhood businesses by displaying and making their merchandise available on the ‘local spotlight’ table. CDs of music by local musicians are available for sale or can be downloaded for 75 cents per song off the bank’s website. At the ‘my booth’ stations anyone can access information online about their accounts or browse the Internet. They have recently created a site called ‘Local Space’ for individuals and businesses to post their services. This is a free service for all local businesses.

In an attempt to be ‘green’ and cut down on paper, most bank information is in electronic form, which can be printed, downloaded or just viewed. If your idea of a bank is to wait in line to complete a transaction or ask a question, you will have a totally different experience at your neighborhood store, the **Umpqua Bank on Fremont**.
