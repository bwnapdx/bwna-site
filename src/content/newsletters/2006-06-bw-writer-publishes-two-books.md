I am a happily ensconced resident of the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood (about thirteen years now on NE 39th Ave), and am delighted to announce that in April 2006, the first two books in my young adult series called The Short Division of the World Secret Service–Case One: Bobblings, and Case Two: Stolen Children were printed.

The series follows thirteen year olds, Lucie and her best friend Rodney, who work for Lucie’s father’s company in the Short Division of the World Secret Service. Wit, intelligence, and inventiveness are required to solve each case and dispatch the villains.

I was born and bred in New Zealand, a country known for its strong literary heritage, penchant for travel, and offbeat sense of humor - which probably influenced the tone and content of the stories. Each book features code breaking, sports (often extreme), travel and a healthy dose of fun.

The books are available at many local bookstores including –A Children’s Place bookstore at 4807 NE Fremont, and Broadway Books at 1714 NE Broadway.
