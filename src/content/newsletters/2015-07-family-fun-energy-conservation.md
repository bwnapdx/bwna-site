As temperatures climb, Portlanders head outside—typically sporting a big grin—because the best season of the year is here. It's finally summer in Portland!

This year, Clean Energy Works is sponsoring Portland Parks & Recreation's Summer Free For All: Movies in the Park, a beloved Portland tradition, bringing fun, free movies to the whole family. When it comes to Wilshire Park on July 10th, stop by the Clean Energy Works table to say hi and learn how our nonprofit can help keep your home cool and your utility bills down this summer.

We'll see you at the movies in the park!
