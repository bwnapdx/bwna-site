**Monday, April 10, 6:30–8:30pm**
*One hour earlier than usual*
Bethany Lutheran Church
NE 37th and Skidmore

As former board member Ted Perkins once said, the neighborhood association is always looking for ‘new blood’. And again this April, people are needed to fill various open board positions : vacant this time around are the positions of president, treasurer, and seven at-large board seats. Positions are for a two year duration.

If you have been thinking of getting involved, want to help out with the annual picnic (coming in August) or other neighborhood events, or if you have ideas for projects, want to work on traffic, school related or other issues, — here is your chance. Come to the meeting April 10, volunteer for a position — help make Beaumont Wilshire an even better place to live.
