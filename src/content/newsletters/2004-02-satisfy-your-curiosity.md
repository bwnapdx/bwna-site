How much did the house down the street sell for? What are the zoning regulations on that property along Fremont Street? How much and what type of crime has occurred on my block for the last 12 months? You can satisfy your curiosity and answer these questions and more by logging on to **www.portlandonline.com**. On the official website for the City of Portland, there is a box on the left hand side titled ‘***PortlandMaps Search***.’ Type in your address and you will be linked to a wealth of information about your property and the neighbors around you. It’s fun and informative. Give it a try!
