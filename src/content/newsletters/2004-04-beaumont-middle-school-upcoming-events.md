Beaumont Middle School will hold a Spring Concert on **Tuesday, May 11 at 7pm** in the West Gym. Admission is free, but donations are gratefully accepted.

In addition, look for the Beaumont Middle School Marching Band in the following two local area parades: St. John’s Parade, Saturday, **May 8** ; and the Jr. Rose Festival Parade, Wednesday, **June 9**.
