Do you consider schools and children’s programs a priority? Are you concerned that children will suffer in this tough economic time? If so, then come give children a voice at the Presidents Day Rally to Protect Kids and Schools on February 16th at noon on the Capitol Steps in Salem. To keep from losing ground, legislators need to hear that Oregonians support reasonable revenue increases and targeted investments in education. By standing together, we will make it possible for legislators to stand up for our children and other vulnerable members of our communities.

Register today at www.stand.org/or/rally or to reserve your seat on the Alameda Bus call Heidi Blischke at 503-280-0133. Bring your children and grandchildren and a homemade sign. We need to show our strength through numbers. It also makes for a fun and educational day for your kids.
