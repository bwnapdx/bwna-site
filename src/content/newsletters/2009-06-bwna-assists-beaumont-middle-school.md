In an interview with Beaumont Middle School Principal Sherie Knutsen last fall, it was brought to BWNA's attention that numerous classrooms at the school were, and had been for years, equipped with chalkboards that were no longer functional. Knowing how basic a board to write on is for a classroom teacher, the BWNA Board was interested in helping to rectify the situation. Principal Knutsen responded that she had been trying to replace decrepit blackboards with white boards for years, but a tight budget had prevented her from doing so. However, she continued, if BWNA could contribute money toward the purchase of white boards, then she would be able to match it with funding for their installation--partly from the school's budget and partly from PTA contributions. The BWNA Board acted promptly and donated $600 for that purpose, and the white boards were ordered through the school district. That was last fall. Finally, in March, the long wait was over. District workers arrived to install the white boards, five of which were purchased with BWNA funding.

Fred Fox Beaumont Middle School Core Teacher

If you've been out of the classroom for awhile, perhaps you're not familiar with white boards. In place of the messy and, at times, irritatingly-noisy chalk, special colored pens are used to write effortlessly and quietly on a smooth white surface. While overhead projectors and computer-projected equipment are used as well, teachers interviewed for this story unanimously agreed that a board to write on in the front of the room is an essential element in virtually all their lessons. Core teacher Fred Fox was enthusiastically thankful for the new board in his room: *"The old blackboards were totally unusable. What a huge difference this white board has made to my teaching! Please let everyone in the neighborhood know how much this means to us on a daily basis."*

Obviously, the wait was worth it, and BWNA couldn't be more pleased with the results.

If you would like to take a look at the white boards being used during the school day, contact Vice Principal Greg Crabtree for a guided tour. You'll be impressed--both with the boards and the teaching!
