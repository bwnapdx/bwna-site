Crowds of neighbors and visitors celebrated a new monthly Beaumont Village event on Friday, May 14. Many said it was a “shot in the arm” for the Fremont area, which was coordinated by the Beaumont Business Association (BBA). Festivities centered on Northeast Fremont Street between 40th and 52nd Avenues from 5pm to 9pm.

Participants included 15 vendors, as well as artists, musicians, and a non-profit SCRAP collection. BBA organizers expect to nearly double the number of vendors the June 11th event. Music and festivities will go from 5pm to 9pm and will offer a “walking fashion show” featuring ShopAdorn, Rosabode & Bonnie’s clothing and hair by various local stylists.

The May vendors offered jewelry, art, children’s wear, purses, handmade soaps and garden décor. Beaumont Village boasts a hardware store, market, florist, and children’s bookstore on its friendly, small-town style main street, suitable for strolling or exploring by bike. Coffee lovers enjoy cult Portland coffeehouses while window shoppers browse through a handful of antique shops or gift stores. For more information check with the Beaumont Business Association Chair Person, Rebecca  Lehman, 1.949.395.3305, or at: bba.vpweb.com.
