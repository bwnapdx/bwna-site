A number of local artists will be showing and selling their handmade artworks in the **Fremont Commons courtyard** at 4605 NE Fremont ***every first Friday of the month***, weather permitting, from 5 to 9 PM. The artists and Tim Ray, property owner, sponsor this event.

As the spring months turn into summer, Beaumont Village will be brimming with pedestrians and patrons who will have a new venue to see fresh works of art every month brought directly from artist to you. Besides beautiful jewelry, ceramics, fabrics and fused glass, there will be guitar music in the background.

Many local businesses will also be participating by staying open till 9 PM. **Bella Flora**—4439 NE Fremont, will have cast concrete sculptures of gnomes, birdbaths, and planters. **flea**—4419 NE Fremont, will host a mini-flea market featuring a handful of other dealers.

Residents of Beaumont Wilshire have much to cheer about. With a myriad of cuisines to choose from between 41st & 52nd, there is something for everybody on NE Fremont. So, come see the magic unfold, ***First Friday on Fremont!***

Call 503-281-2871 for more info.
