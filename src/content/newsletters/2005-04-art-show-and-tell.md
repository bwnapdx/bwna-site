April 8–9

Judee Moonbeam, aka Jude Cornwell, a longtime BW resident, is one of 106 artists in the 15th annual *Art Show & Sell* at Buckman Elementary School. Judee creates beautiful wearable art works, including hats, purses, scarves, and pins, in what she calls “a fiber walk along the silk road.” Buckman Elementary School is located at 320 SE 16th Ave. (between SE Pine and SE Stark) Portland. 5 to 9pm Friday, April 8, 2005 and 10am to 6pm Saturday, April 9, 2005 Admission: Friday: $5 per person, Saturday: free.
