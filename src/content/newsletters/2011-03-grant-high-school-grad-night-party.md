Grant High School has an All Night Party Committee comprised of parent volunteers who plan and execute the Grad Night Party that immediately follows graduation. In order to help raise funds for this event, we have partnered with Papa Murphy’s Pizza at 41st and Fremont. On the **first Wednesday of every month** Papa Murphy’s will donate a portion of their sales to the All Night Party. Patrons must mention Grant High School when ordering!
