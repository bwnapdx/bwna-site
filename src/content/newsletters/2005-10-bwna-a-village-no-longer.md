One of the great challenges facing the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood is the balance between reasonable growth and protecting the special and historic character of our “village community”. While we like to perceive Beaumont Wilshire as a “village,” the reality is that a number of pressures currently challenge the future of that character and image: parking concerns are growing along Fremont and adjoining residential streets; traffic congestion has burgeoned; and the current trend is towards increased population density which inflates business and residential rental costs and property values at an unusual rate. Our neighborhood land use and transportation committee chaired by David Whitaker does a great job representing the neighborhood in public input towards land use decisions. If you are interested in participating, they meet on the 3rd Monday of every month at 7:30 PM at Fremont Coffee, 4727 NE Fremont.

We had over 250 people turn out for the National Night Out picnic last August. The kids had fun exploring the police car and fire truck along with learning about how our local police and firemen protect us. Food was plentiful and over 100 door prizes were given away! I want to thank the many organizers and contributors to our annual picnic as this event would not happen without you!
