Saturday and Sunday, October 13 & 14, Studios East of the Willamette, 10-5
Saturday and Sunday, October 20 & 21, Studios West of the Willamette, 10-5

Two members of the BWNA artspace group, Jenelle Kathan and Jude Cornwell (aka Judee Moonbeam) will open their studios in Beaumont Wilshire for the annual Portland Open Studios eastside tour, October 13-14th. Jenelle works in glass and Jude in fibre. Their work can be viewed on the BWNA website www.bwna.org, click on “artspace.”

You can purchase a Tour Guide and tickets for $15 for two adults (kids free) which include a map, pictures of artists’ work and contact information in a calendar format at the following: Art Media stores, New Seasons Market, Powell’s Books, Weir’s Cyclery and other outlets listed on www.portlandopenstudios.com.

There isn’t a better way to discover the vitality of Portland than to visit the studios in the neighborhoods that make Portland so vibrant. Discover your own neighborhood and explore new ones. Artists will share with you the secrets of their art--the materials, the tools, the techniques and the ideas that contribute to the creation of paintings, prints, sculptures, textiles, glass, ceramics, and photography.
