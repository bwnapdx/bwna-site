*A treasured family recipe from Susan Trabucco (see accompanying article on page 14)*

Serves 6 to 8

**Ingredients:**

- 1 lb chopped or ground beef
- 3 Tbs grated Romano or Parmesan cheese
- 2 Tbs chopped parsley
- 6 Tbs olive oil
- 3 eggs, slightly beaten
- 3 slices stale bread
- 1 clove garlic, chopped (I always use more!)
- salt and pepper to taste (if you don't want to eat raw meat, 1 tsp of salt and ½ tsp of ground pepper is good)
- Flour

**Instructions:**

1. Soak the bread in water for 5 minutes; squeeze dry. Mix thoroughly with meat, slightly beaten eggs, cheese, garlic, and parsley. (Yes, use your hands to mix.) Add salt and pepper to taste.
2. Shape into balls about the size of a small egg; roll in flour; fry in hot olive oil about 10 minutes, crisping each side.
3. Serve very hot; add sauce of your choice if you wish, but plain with grated Parmesan is darned good, too!
