BWNA artist Carol Sands will have a piece in the Watercolor Society of Oregon “Aqueous Media Exhibition” at Columbia Arts Gallery, 215 Cascade Avenue, Hood River, Oregon during the month of April 2008. Opening is April 5th.

Also in the “Senior Expo” at the Hilton in downtown Vancouver, Washington on Sunday April 13. Look for five paintings by Carol Sands. Hilton Vancouver, 301 W. 6th Street, 1-360-993-4500.

You may view work by Carol and other BWNA artists on the BWNA website at:
http://www.bwna.org/artspace/index.html
