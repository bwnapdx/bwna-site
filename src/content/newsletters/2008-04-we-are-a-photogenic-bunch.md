Many neighbors have contributed photos of neighborhood events over the years. These can be viewed at the BWNA website at **http://www.bwna.org/photos.html**

We have photos from events going back to 2001 - annual neighborhood picnics, annual Fremont Fests, the Beaumont Middle School 75th anniversary celebration, among others.

See if you and your kids are at these events and if you have changed at all since 2001. We want to thank the neighbors who have contributed photos to our ‘scrapbook’ over the years and look foreword to more in the coming years: Ted Perkins, Bill Markwart, Helen Koba, Mario Caoile and Tyler Dean.
