**Monday, December 13, 7:30 PM — Bethany Lutheran, NE 37th & Skidmore**

The above are the agenda items for the Dec. 13, 2004 general meeting. Paul Morris of the Bosco-Milligan Foundation will speak about the work this organization is doing to research, preserve and restore old architecture in Portland (see article about Bosco-Milligan on page 6). He will also speak about the soon to open Architectural Heritage Center and its education opportunities and its collections. If you like old houses (and who in Beaumont Wilshire doesn't) and want to know more about 'preserving our building heritage', this is the meeting for you.

Also Brain Hoop of the Office of Neighborhood Involvement (ONI) will speak about how proposed ONI guideline changes will affect our neighborhood association.

We will have treats from Criollo Bakery. Don't miss our last meeting of 2004!
