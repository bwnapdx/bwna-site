The beautiful summer evening was picture-perfect for the annual National Night Out Picnic in Wilshire Park on the first Tuesday in August. It was a chance for neighbors to come together in celebration of the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood and to join with police and firefighters in community solidarity.

The estimated crowd of 300 Beaumont-Wilshire residents, including many with young children, delighted in sampling each other’s picnic fare. Complementing the potluck dishes were barbecued burgers and hot dogs (courtesy of BWNA), watermelons (courtesy of Parsons Farms), ice cream cups (courtesy of Umpqua Bank), and two scrumptious cakes (courtesy of Whole Foods). Additionally, veggie burgers and garnishes were on hand (thanks to a hefty $100 donation from New Seasons), along with ice-cold refreshments (courtesy of Beaumont Market, Justin’s Market, and BWNA).

But while the deliciously-eclectic cuisine served as a main attraction, it was far from the only crowd-pleaser. Under the shade of a large tree massage therapist Johannes Venghaus provided soothing massages, free of charge, in the comfort of a recliner. Not far away, suspended by a large ring attached to a sturdy tree limb, was aerial artist Petra DeLaRoche, who performed a series of complex gymnastic routines to traditional Americana music provided by the Eccentric Force “Kapansk” Band, featuring Petra’s dad (former BWNA Treasurer David DeLaRoche) on tuba. Meanwhile, kids continuously made their way to the BWNA Table, where a “grab basket” of free playthings awaited them, including pinwheels, bubble blowing kits, and assorted other fun gadgets (courtesy of the Portland Police Bureau and BWNA).

The kids also received free raffle tickets at the BWNA Table, as did the adults.

There were two separate raffle drawings–beginning with the kids, then the adults. Groans and cheers emanated from the crowd, as little ones gathered closer and closer to the microphone in hopes of hearing their lucky number called out. Dozens of gift certificates, apparel, and miscellaneous handy items (for dogs and people!) were distributed–a result of generosity shown by the following businesses (in alphabetical order): A Children’s Place, Alameda Brewhouse, Alameda Café, Amenity Shoes, Bagel Land, Blackbird Wineshop, Bottles, Delphina’s Bakery, Fire on the Mountain, Green Dog Pet Supply, Jim & Patty’s Coffee, Johannes Venghaus Massage, Nike, Pizzicato Pizza, Ristretto Roasters, Rocket Pizza, Rose’s Ice Cream, Soluna Grill, and Yo Choice Self Serve Frozen Yogurt.

Lastly, BWNA is especially appreciative again this year for the use of a dozen long tables and over 100 folding chairs from Wilshire United Methodist Church Native American Fellowship and for the indispensable assistance provided by the dutiful Boy Scouts of Troop 107 and their can-do Scoutmaster, Chris Labuhn.

The National Night Out Picnic is a testimony to what can be achieved through neighborhood spirit and teamwork toward a common community goal, and it’s all good.
