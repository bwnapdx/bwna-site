Monday, June 11, 7:30 PM
Beaumont Middle School
Room 140
NE 42nd & Fremont

There will be two speakers at the **June 11** general meeting:

**Howard Patterson**, a volunteer with the Climate Project, will present a slide show on the science and impact of global warming: promised to be educational and 'pithy'.

**Mark Wahlers** from Concordia University will describer CU's proposed changes for the old Whitaker Middle School. He will have photos of the proposal, explain the process and take questions.

Two at-large board positions remain open for those interested in getting involved in shaping neighborhood events and projects. In addition, the position of President has opened up again *(see p 2)*.

Volunteers are needed to help prepare for and coordinate the annual National Night Out Picnic which will be held **Tuesday Aug 7 at Wilshire Park**. Come and find out more about this event and how you can get involved.
