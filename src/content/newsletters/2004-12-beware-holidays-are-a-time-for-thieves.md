The holiday season is normally a joyous time of the year, unless you become a victim of crime. This is one of the two times of the year (the other is summertime) when criminal activity is at its highest. The main reason is gifts: they are everywhere. Some are brightly wrapped, still in shopping bags and with receipts. The two places thieves look for presents are in your vehicle parked in a mall and inside your home. Protect yourselves and your purchases.

**Your VEHICLE:**

- Do not leave purchases visible in your car. Put items in the trunk, but avoid doing so when people are around.
- Keep receipts separate. Many thieves return items to a store and get 100% of the value.
- Always lock your car
- Park in a well lighted or heavily traveled area whether it is in a lot or on the street.
- Try to shop with another person. This will help you and your car be less of a target

**Your HOME:**

- Give your home the appearance that someone is "at home". Use lights and timers that turn the lights on and off every hour. Leave on a radio or TV.
- Keep blinds and curtains closed.
- Use your alarm system.
- Let your neighbors know or alert your block watch person if your house is vacant for more than one day
- Keep your garage door closed whether you are home or not.
