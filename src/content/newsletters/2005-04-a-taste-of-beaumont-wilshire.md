**Monday, April 11, 6:30–8:30 PM**
Bethany Lutheran, NE 37th & Skidmore

Please join us as we celebrate the wonderful restaurants in our neighborhood and be serenaded by musicians from Beaumont Middle School. On Monday April 11, owners and managers of many of the fine eating places along Fremont will be sharing samples of their cuisine. This is a great opportunity to try out these restaurants for free, and learn what our neighborhood has to offer. We will meet at a special time this month only— 6:30 to 8:30 PM in the basement of Bethany Lutheran Church, NE 37th and Skidmore. Treats that will be provided include pizza from Pizzicato and Roundtable, specialties from Alameda Brewhouse, and Stanich’s famous hamburgers. Mark your calendars, and don’t miss it! Here are just some of the participating restaurants:

- Alameda Brewhouse
- Criollo
- Java Man Café
- Parisi’s
- Pizzicato
- Roundtable Pizza
- Stanich’s and more!
