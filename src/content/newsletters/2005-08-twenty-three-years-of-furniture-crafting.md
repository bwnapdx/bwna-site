Walt Heck, a skilled craftsman, has been building custom furniture specializing in ‘Arts and Crafts’ styled furnishings at his present location at 4507 NE Fremont, since 1983. He first began working with furniture at the age of twelve under the guidance of his father. He then went on to work for several other furniture shops before deciding to open his own business on Fremont St. where he became one of a handful of furniture makers who helped bring about a renewed interest in the ‘Arts and Crafts’ style of furniture in the Portland area.

This style of furniture, also referred to as the ‘Craftsman’ or ‘Mission’ style, was first produced in the New York and New Jersey area by the Stickley brothers in the early 1900’s and soon spread to the western and Midwestern United States.

Arts and Crafts furniture is distinctive in its design and is not easily confused with other furniture styles. It emphasizes clean lines and functionality, achieving its appeal through simplicity and durability. It came about in creative response to the over-ornamentation of Victorian furniture and architecture. Walt employs many of the traditional methods of construction and design elements used at the turn of the century. Northeast Portland with its many bungalow styled homes is a natural location for Walt’s talents. His work ranges from commercial fixtures, to residential furnishings and ‘period built-ins’, to a small line of mirror and art frames.

Walt believes in keeping skilled craftsmanship alive in the form of small, local businesses and sees his work as a reflection of his commitment to the greater community. By remaining small and staying local, he is able to keep a focus on quality, one-of-a-kind pieces from the traditional to the contemporary giving much attention to hand worked detail. He works closely with his clients and always tries to implement their ideas.

If you have ever visited Skamania Lodge in the Columbia Gorge, you have probably sat on one of his chairs or at one of his tables. Walt is busy building heirlooms daily. Visit Walt at his shop, Furniture Craft, 4507 NE Fremont, check out his website www.waltheck.com or call him at 503-287-5685.
