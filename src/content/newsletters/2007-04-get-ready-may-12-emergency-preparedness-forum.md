Are you prepared in the event of an emergency to be on your own for up to 72 hours while authorities respond to the most serious situations?

Take an hour on Saturday May 12 to learn some simple ways to prepare yourself and your family. Join Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency Team (NET) member Erika Wolfe Burr as she leads this FREE forum with topics such as:

- Easy steps to prepare a 72-hour kit,
- The importance of having out-of-state contacts,
- How to survive without utilities, and
- General tips on how to ready our families and houses in the event of an earthquake, flood, ice storm, or other disaster.

Stop by to meet your Beaumont Wilshire NET and learn how they have been trained to help neighbors in an emergency. Saturday, May 12 at 10:00 a.m.; Bethany Lutheran Church, 4330 NE 37th Ave.; Open to the public.
