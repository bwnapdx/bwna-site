The 4th annual Antique and Artisan Faire will be held at Rose City Park Presbyterian Church (44th and NE Sandy) on Saturday and Sunday, November 14th and 15th. Hours are 9 a.m.- 4 p.m. on Saturday and 11 a.m.- 3 p.m. on Sunday. Handcrafted items, food, antiques, and collectibles, as well as raffle tickets for a handmade quilt, will be available for purchase. A special feature of this year's faire is the participation of the Zimbabwe Artists Project: a connection between rural African women artists and markets in the United States.

The building is wheelchair accessible. Bring a copy of this article for a free raffle ticket.

For more information, please call 502-231-3814.
