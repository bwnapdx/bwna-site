Wilshire United Methodist Native American Fellowship at 3917 NE Shaver Street is sponsoring a rummage and yard sale, plus a concert, to support Mission projects. The rummage and yard sale take place on July 26th (10:00am to 3:00pm) and July 27th (9:00am to 3:00pm), and residents are encouraged to bring beverage cans/bottles to be recycled, with refunds benefiting the fundraiser. Indian tacos will also be for sale at 11:30 on both days.

Then on Sunday, July 28th beginning at 7:30pm, a benefit Native American Flute concert will be performed by Geri Littlejohn. A "love donation" will serve as your ticket, and if you arrive at 6:00pm, you can spend some time with Geri prior to the concert.

For additional information about Geri or the concert, call 503.846.1755 or email alaria@syrynx.net.

The Wilshire United Methodist Church building is wheelchair accessible. For additional information about the fundraising events, call Helen at 503.282.6431.
