## BW Community Meeting

Monday, April 14, 7PM
Cafeteria at Beaumont Middle School

Please join us April 14 for the ‘ Taste of Beaumont’. This great food tasting event will start at 7 pm and will be held in the cafeteria of the Beaumont Middle School (entrance to cafeteria is on NE Fremont at 41st). Neighborhood restaurants will bring samples of delicious treats from their menus. While we are enjoying the food, musicians from Beaumont Middle School Jazz Band will serenade us. This is a great opportunity to try out food from these restaurants. Mark your calendars, and don’t miss it! Some participating restaurants are:

- Bumblekiss
- Cha Cha Cha Taqueria
- Ottoman Klasik
- Parisis
- Rose’s Ice Cream
- Round Table Pizza, and others

We will also have elections for officers and board members during this meeting. There are five vacant positions on the BWNA board: president, treasurer and five board members-at-large. Become part of the board which meets every other month (January, March, May, July, September and November) and is in charge of heading up projects for the neighborhood, the newsletter, preparing the annual night out picnic and other events. The board needs some new blood!
