The beautiful colored autumn leaves are all raked away. Thanksgiving Day meals are just a memory. We look forward to more holiday meals, Christmas trees, Hanukah candles, last minute shopping trips, and a rumored visit of Santa Claus.

We look back at a very good year in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood complete with new buildings, new businesses, upgraded homes, and new neighbors.

You are invited to our December 10 general meeting for a time of holiday celebration with food and beverages and to hear our guest speaker. Our crime prevention coordinator, Stefanie Adams, will also be there with copies of ‘Holiday Safety Tips’. Here is one shopping safety tip: Avoid leaving valuables in your car in plain sight – and if you must put a purchase into your car trunk, move your car before going back to shop!

Do you have a particular topic or issue to discuss, or a suggestion for speakers for upcoming meetings? Your neighborhood board wants to hear from you!

Looking forward to seeing you at the meeting!

–Bill Markwart
Acting President, BWNA
president@bwna.org
