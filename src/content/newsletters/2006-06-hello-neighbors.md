I’m excited to be your new neighborhood association president. Heidi Blischke served on the board for the past six years and I want to thank her for the great work she has done on everyone’s behalf! I moved to this community a year ago and became the coordinator for our tree planting. For the past eleven years I have been an environmental and neighborhood activist in Seattle and Portland working on population growth; turning Mount Hood into a national park; airport noise; Freecycle and protecting forests and other species. By day I develop software, practice yoga and study Pronoia, a fantastic new book by Rob Brezsny.

I love building community and look forward to sharing the ideas I have for Beaumont Wilshire. I envision a place where people take the time to get to know one another, traffic is calmed, and we feel comfortable biking and walking to great local cafes, restaurants and services. I want to live on tree-lined streets full of birds singing surrounded by organic flower and vegetable gardens. Think I’m dreaming? Look around!

Contact me with any visions you want to manifest and I hope you will join our neighborhood e-mail discussion list at bwna.org *(see URL inset left)* to keep current on what’s up in the hood!

*Albert Kaufman,*  
BWNA President
