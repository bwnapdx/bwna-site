It is a pleasure to talk with someone who is genuinely enthused about their work. Aubree Swart welcomed me to **Therapeutic Associates** at NE Portland for an interview to learn about her services and I found her to be confident, inspiring and enthused about her profession.

Aubree has directed Therapeutic Associates at 4040 NE Fremont since December 2006. She is a graduate of St Mary’s College in California and Pacific University in Forest Grove where she received a doctorate of Physical Therapy in 2003. She has been working for Therapeutic Associates, Inc. for the last five years.

The Therapeutic Associates clinic offers a broad spectrum of services for many different conditions. Injuries following accidents, surgery or athletics and problems related to stress, arthritis, imbalance disorder, and others, all receive skillful attention. Aubree’s approach is to enhance the patient’s quality of life and treat the whole person, not just the injured area. Her goal is to help each person achieve balance and well-being. Being an athlete herself, she especially understands the needs of dedicated athletes who sustain an injury.

When you drop by the office, you will be greeted by her receptionist Lisa whose friendliness reflects her great customer commitment and experience. Both Aubree and Lisa told me that they were very impressed with the quality of life in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood and look forward to giving back to the community by providing superior and timely services.
