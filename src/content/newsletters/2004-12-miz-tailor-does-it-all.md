Don't throw or give it away. That loved item—a favorite blouse or suit that you've worn almost to shreds—Miz Tailor can recreate it for you.

Claudia Al-Amin, owner, and Cati Thomas, a recent addition at the shop, do custom tailoring, alterations and contract sewing (making samples of a new pattern for a designer) at **Miz Tailor at 4425 NE Fremont**. They sew formal and every-day wear—suits, coats, costumes—using usual fabrics as well as leather, sequins, denim…. You name it, they can do it.

"No job is too small," says Claudia, who began hand sewing at the age of five. A native of the Big Apple, Claudia learned from her mother, who studied all aspects of the garment industry: design, clothing manufacturing, and sewing techniques.

When she was 12 years old, Claudia graduated to machine sewing, and she has never looked back. She opened her first custom tailoring store in upstate New York. She came to Portland in 1992 where she continued sewing for family and friends and worked part-time in a high-end interior design shop. She founded Miz Tailor in August 2002.

Cati says she takes on the "wild stuff." Originally from London, England, Cati started sewing for herself, then her friends, and finally began making costumes for theaters in London. In her twenties she immigrated to the United States. She lived in Prineville, Oregon for nearly 20 years where she did custom sewing in her home.

Miz Tailor at 4425 NE Fremont is open Tuesday through Friday, 10 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and on Saturdays by appointment. Walk-ins are welcome.
