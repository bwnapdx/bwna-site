On Sunday, July 10th from noon to 2 PM a van full of animals from the Oregon Zoo will come to Bethany Lutheran Church, NE 37th at Skidmore. Parents and children of all ages are invited to pet and see ducks, rabbits, snakes, walking sticks, and Madagascar hissing cockroaches!! Handlers will be there to answer questions, and there will be animal related crafts for the kids.

This is a free special education program of the Oregon Zoo called ZAP (Zoo Animal Presenters). The ZAP team is a group of teens that present, educate, and reach out to the community by delivering a message of respect for nature and wildlife.
