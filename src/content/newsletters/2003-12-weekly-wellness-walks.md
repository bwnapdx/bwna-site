Starting Saturday Dec. 6th

Having trouble getting out to exercise as the days grow shorter and wetter? Join Andrine de la Rocha LMT as we walk away winter inertia with “Weekly Wellness Walks.” Start and finish at Beaumont Health Care Clinic, 4445 NE Fremont St.

Meet a few minutes prior to start time and walk for 30-45 minutes at a brisk but gentle pace. Wear good walking shoes and bring a water bottle. We’ll walk rain or shine, so dress accordingly. Days & Start Times: Monday 1:00 p.m. Thursday 8:00 a.m. Saturday 2:00 p.m.

As always, check with your physician before starting any new exercise program. Listen to your body and go at a comfortable pace. For more information, contact Andrine: 503.249.7752
