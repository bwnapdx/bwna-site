It’s a new year, the days are getting longer and it will soon be springtime! As the weather warms up, we will see more of our neighbors out for a walk, a bike ride or working in yards and gardens. We are indeed fortunate to live in a neighborhood blessed with wonderful people and with convenient access to an ever-growing number of neighborhood businesses.

Do you have a new neighbor on your block or perhaps just met someone new to the area? Take a moment to invite them to join you at the upcoming BWNA community meeting on February 11 at Beaumont School (see page 1). We will hear about projects and developments in the neighborhood. We will have a speaker to talk to us about Willamette River cleanup plans. A Beaumont Business Association representative will update us on changes and new businesses coming to our area. A neighborhood police officer will be here to talk about public safety and answer any questions you may have.

Are there other topics or issues you would like to discuss or hear about, or do you have a suggestion for speakers for upcoming meetings? Please let me or board members know. We want to hear from you! Looking forward to seeing you at the February 11 meeting!

*–Bill Markwart*
Acting President, BWNA
president@bwna.org
