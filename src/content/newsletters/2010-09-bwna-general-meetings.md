Over the summer, the Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) Board of Directors made a decision to alter a long-standing BWNA tradition--moving back the starting time of meetings from 7:30pm to 7:00pm. The change goes into effect with the September 13th Board Meeting and October 11th General Meeting and will continue on after that. The Board’s unanimous decision was based on the fact that meetings had been adjourning as late as 9:00pm, and too often residents were having to exit before the meeting’s conclusion to get back home to their families. It was also noted in Board discussions that the vast majority of other neighborhood associations begin their meetings at 7:00pm for the same reason: earlier start = earlier adjournment. That’s the goal, and BWNA leadership will do its best to make it happen.

Now that you know when to arrive at the meetings, here’s a peek at what’s on the respective agendas:  
The focus of the September 13th Board Meeting is on three traffic calming concerns--the Wistaria curve, the vicinity of Alameda and Klickitat, and the corner of Alameda and 37th. The Board will also review developments at Clearwire’s residential site for a cell tower (near the corner of Alameda and 37th) and consider proposed BWNA Bylaw revisions. (Board meetings are open to the public, and there are still two vacant Board positions remaining to be filled.)

The General Meeting on October 11th features three local speakers with expertise in three different areas of health care: GreenField Health, a unique out-patient option in primary care for residents of the N.E. area; the Multnomah County Gatekeepers program, an outreach effort to aid at-risk older adults and people with disabilities; and Oregon Action’s Healthy Kids program.

Additionally, a local police officer will provide a neighborhood crime update and answer questions. The remainder of the agenda is open to residents’ concerns and suggestions (which the Board will review and consider at its November 8th meeting).
