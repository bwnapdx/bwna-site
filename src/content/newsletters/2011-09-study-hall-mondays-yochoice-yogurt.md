Starting this fall students can do their homework and enjoy a treat at YoChoice Self-Serve Frozen Yogurt at 4941 NE Fremont on Monday evenings. “We call it Study Hall Mondays,” says YoChoice Yogurt owner Andre LaGrande. “We provide free wi-fi and 10% off on yogurt and toppings every Monday from 5:00 to 9:00 pm for students who come in to do their school work.” LaGrande says one of his regular customers suggested the shop would be a comfortable and social place for kids to get together to study after school.

Another new feature at YoChoice Yogurt is “Double Punch Tuesdays.” Everyone gets two punches on their customer appreciation card for yogurt purchases any time on Tuesdays. “This also applies to everyone using our new Perka system, which enables customers to keep track of their punches on their iPhones or cell phones, using a free app,” LaGrande explained.

YoChoice Self-Serve Frozen Yogurt is beginning its second year of business at NE Fremont and 50th, in the heart of Beaumont Village. The shop opens every day at noon. For more information: www.yochoiceyogurt.com
