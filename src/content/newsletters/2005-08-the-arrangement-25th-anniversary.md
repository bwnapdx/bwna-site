On May 14th, Beaumont neighbors were treated to live music by the Swizzle Chicks, a vibrant jazz trio and children’s entertainment by Noodles the Clown – all as a part of the 25th anniversary celebration of The Arrangement, a shop owned by Sue Mautz at 4210 NE Fremont that features gifts for life’s special occasions.
