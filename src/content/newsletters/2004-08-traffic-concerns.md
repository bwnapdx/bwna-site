The Transportation and Land Use committee continues to meet at the Alameda Brew Pub the **third Monday of the month**. At the June meeting there were a number of new neighbors who expressed concern with high traffic speeds on their streets. In particular the concerns were about: the dangerous intersection at 38th and Wisteria, cars speeding on Failing St. between 42nd and 47th and the broad intersection that is dangerous for pedestrians to cross at 37th and Alameda.

Brian Ray was a guest speaker from Kittelson and Associates, a traffic engineering company. He listened to neighbors’ concerns and then presented ideas for addressing those concerns. He spoke about the need to work together on an overall speed management plan for the whole neighborhood. As a result, the committee has decided to request that the City complete a traffic study of neighborhood traffic this fall. A petition to request that this study be completed will be available for all neighbors to sign at the annual picnic on **August 3rd** at Wilshire Park.

Please join us at **7:30 pm every 3rd Monday of the month** at the Alameda Brew Pub for a lively discussion of Land Use and Transportation issues in the neighborhood!
