Recent geologic studies show that Oregon is due for an earthquake upwards of magnitude 8. Massive earthquakes in the Pacific Northwest occur in long cycles—every 350-500 years—but experts at the U.S. Geological Survey and Oregon Department of Geology say that the next one is due any time. An event like this would cause considerable damage in Portland and much of Western Oregon.

The key to surviving any disaster is planning. The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency Team (NET) meets every month to review procedures and skills that would assist firefighters, police officers, and citizens during such an event.

You can make a difference, too, by following these easy tips—before disaster strikes:

**1. Create an escape route.** Every member of your household should know the quickest and safest exits for each room. Note all potential hazards that could be in your path.

**2. Store a flashlight and a pair of shoes near each bed.** A major earthquake will likely disrupt power service. Shoes are helpful in case you encounter broken glass.

**3. Know where your utility shutoffs are.** Locate your gas, electrical and water shutoff and learn how to operate them.

**4. Practice your plan.** This is especially effective if it is done at night with the electricity off.
