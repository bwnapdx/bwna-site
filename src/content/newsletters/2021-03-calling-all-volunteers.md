BWNA has volunteer opportunities to suit every interest! From serving on the board or a committee to delivering the newsletter to reporting/article writing to offering your neighbor a hand or helping to maintain and beautify Wilshire Park, there's much work to be done to keep our neighborhood strong. Want to know more? Contact Al Ellis (editor@bwnapdx.org) and get involved!
