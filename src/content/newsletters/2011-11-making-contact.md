Did you know that Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) has an email list for anyone wanting to communicate with other neighbors to review and discuss general interest topics? This list is used to discuss and share information like traffic calming concerns and developments such as the Clearwire cell tower site.

Don’t let this valuable resource go unused. To subscribe to the BWNA Community List, simply do the following:

1. Go to the BWNA Web site at www.bwna.us
2. On the bottom right of the home page click, “Subscribe to BWNA email discussion list”
3. Type your email address, name and password

When someone sends an email to the list, you will automatically receive a copy of the email. This is a great way to communicate with several neighbors at one time, stay current on the issues that concern our community, as well as keep our community safe and vibrant.

We want to hear from you! Your voice counts.
