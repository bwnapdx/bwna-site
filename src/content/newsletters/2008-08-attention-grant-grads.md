A Grant High School Alumni Association has been formed and is looking for your membership and participation. “We’ll never let that old Grant spirit die” through newsletters, social events, scholarships and special projects for the school.

Grant High School alumni are invited to the first public event sponsored by the U.S. Grant High School of Portland Alumni Association. A no- host luncheon and social hour at the Sextant Bar and Restaurant, 4035 N.E. Marine Drive from 1 to 3:30 p.m. on Friday, September 12, will be followed by attendance at the Grant vs. Barlow high school football game at 4:30 p.m. in the Grant Bowl on NE 33rd and U.S. Grant Place.

There will be special prizes as well as Grant sports items available. Many have received notice through the recently started alumni newsletter according to President Rodger Eddy. The alumni group was formed in 2005 and is still reaching out to Grant alums for membership.

The Grant bowl for many years was the site of Grant General’s football games during several state championship years. Use of the field was recently reactivated. An admission fee is charged for the games. Alums who register will be provided with a name tag and a group cheering section, said John “Buddy” Leonard, vice president of the alum group and a former championship team guard. All who attended or graduated from Grant are cordially invited.

Hope to see alums at these events. For more information about the alumni association, call President Rodger Eddy at 503-223-3606 or email at grantalumni@aol.com or mail to PO Box 13291 Portland, OR 97213.
