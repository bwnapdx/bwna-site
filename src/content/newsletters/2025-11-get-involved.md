## Love the Skidmore Block Party?

**We're Looking for two new event organizers!**

It's time to pass the torch! In 2026, work with BWNA's experienced event co-organizers to learn the ropes about the planning and execution tasks necessary to put on this rockin' annual block party. They will be with you every step of the way; you won't be thrown to the proverbial wolves!

To be considered for this rewarding volunteer experience, we're asking for a minimum two-year commitment after going through "training" (2027-2028).

Please contact patricekoehler@gmail.com if you have questions or want to jump in!

## Love Your Garden?

**Consider signing in for the 2026 BWNA Art & Garden Tour**

Beaumont-Wilshire streets are filled with interesting and beautiful gardens. If yours is one of them, being a host for next year's Art & Garden Tour is a great way to meet other gardeners and neighbors. It's fun!

Ticket sales help BWNA fund many free community events throughout the year.

To learn more, visit BWNApdx.org, choose Get Involved! and click on the pink garden host button. Contact patricekoehler@gmail.com if you have an interest!

## Do You Have Ideas for Our Speakers Forum?

- Birds, bees, seeds? History or herstory? City politics? Aging in place? Downsizing? While there are a host of potential topics for our BWNA speakers forum, we'd like to hear from YOU.
- What are you interested in? Or do you have a subject you'd like to present?
- In 2025, BWNA logged talks on our neighborhood's history, beneficial bugs, and going all-electric, and on November 13 we're hosting a presentation on the Cascadia Subduction Zone earthquake. What's in store for 2026?

If you have ideas for us, contact sandiefam@gmail.com.

For more information visit bwnapdx.org and click Get Involved!
