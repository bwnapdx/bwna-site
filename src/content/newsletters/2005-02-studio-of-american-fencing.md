Zach Holzman and Miwa McRee are two people who are enthusiasts about fencing. Together they run the Studio of American Fencing located at 4048 NE 42 Ave. (south of NE Mason). This is a non-profit organization started in 1986 by Nancy Doty, who owns a business next door.

Zach retried from post office vehicle maintenance and Miwa (pronounced Meewa) is a licensed massage therapist. Zach started fencing at the Portland Recreation Department in 1977, and both were students of Len Carnighan, a local fencing master.

Modern fencing evolved over many years from dueling in medieval times. During those times, many died from wound infections rather than from the wound itself. After Louis Pasteur made his discoveries, participants would dip their swords in wine to kill the bacteria! A code of conduct for fencing as a harmless sport came out of France. Participants execute precise moves to impress judges who determine the outcome of a bout. Today, a ‘touch’ is determined electronically.

The studio is open weekday evenings and Saturdays. Lessons are available from beginner through advance skill levels. Zach and Miwa offer both group and private lessons. Zach notes that Beaumont Middle School also offers fencing classes for kids after school. He says that some of his students are very serious about the sport. Most simply enjoy it and have a good time. One of his students is over 80 years old. Located at 4048 NE 42 Ave. 503-249-2884.
