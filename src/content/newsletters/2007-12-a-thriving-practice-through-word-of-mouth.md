When you enter Alameda Family Chiropractic, located at **4410 NE Fremont**, you feel like you’ve entered someone’s parlor, not a waiting room. “There are many ways to structure a practice,” notes Dr. Ann Wilson, a former fine arts and drama teacher from Pennsylvania. “I give thoughtful and comprehensive care in a nurturing environment that helps patients to heal faster.”

Ann never advertises because her practice grows through referrals. “I treat my patients well and they show their appreciation by telling family and friends.” Dr. Wilson began working as a chiropractor in 1995 after graduating from Western States Chiropractic College in Portland. Several years later, she realized that she wanted to have her own practice. She has been located at 4410 N.E. Fremont since 2000.

In 1985, Ann injured her neck, back, and shoulders, in a head-on car collision. After six months of daily physical therapy, she was still in debilitating pain. “I had given up hope,” says Ann. Then, a friend urged her to visit her chiropractor, and that action turned Ann’s life upside down. Ann recovered and returned to work. A year later, she decided to become a chiropractor herself. With her husband, Steve, and daughter, Adrianna, she relocated to Portland.

“A good chiropractor uses skill, finesse, and speed to accomplish precise adjustments,” explains Ann. Her methods include gentle spinal stretching on a Leander table, electric muscle stimulation, deep muscle relaxation with percussive massage, and low-back traction on an inversion table.

An extraordinary piece of equipment that Ann employs is an AquaMed table that naturally increases the body’s healing processes using dry hydrotherapy. “It’s similar to sitting in a hot tub with the jets all in the right places,” says Ann, who is the only chiropractor in Portland that has one.
