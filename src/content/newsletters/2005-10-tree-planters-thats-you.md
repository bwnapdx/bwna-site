The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association and Friends of Trees are sponsoring a tree planting on February 11, 2006 and are seeking homeowners who want trees and volunteers to help organize and carry out the planting! If you are interested in participating and haven’t submitted an interest form, please complete and send the form in this newsletter. **All interest forms are due by November 1st, 2005**. *(See form on page 6.)*

Those who complete an interest form will receive a free inspection from the City’s Urban Forester who will advise on tree types and placement. Trees cost different amounts from about $50 to $75 and range from 6 to 14 feet (full grown). This fee covers the cost of the tree, hole digging, assistance on planting day, stakes, twine, and follow-up monitoring.

A slide show of available trees will be held this Fall. Please keep an eye on the neighborhood website at www.bwna.org for further details. Both street and yard trees will be available.

If you are able to volunteer to help with outreach, graphic design, t-shirt design, or in other ways, please contact Albert Kaufman at albertkaufman@yahoo.com or mail the form to him at 3244 NE 44th Ave. Portland OR 97213. We’re looking for everyone to be involved in this year’s tree planting - whether you are receiving a tree or not, hopefully, you see this activity as a way of improving your neighborhood and you’ll jump right in and help out!

For more information about Friends of Trees and the great work they are doing to increase the urban canopy in Portland, please visit their website at www.friendsoftrees.org See you on **February 11th, 2006**!
