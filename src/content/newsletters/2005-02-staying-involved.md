It’s a new year, and with it we can expect to continue to see changes as Portland and our neighborhood grows. We are very fortunate to live in a treasured neighborhood. A large part of what makes our neighborhood so special are the people who live here, the care they take of their houses and yards, their volunteerism and involvement in neighborhood decisions, and the friendly words that are spoken as we pass on the street.

Another very special part of our neighborhood are the wonderful businesses that contribute to neighborhood activities and provide great places to eat, sip coffee or tea, shop, get things repaired, provide a place to exercise, and many more services. I would like to give a special thanks to Java Man on NE Fremont for helping to entice people to fill in the neighborhood survey by offering free lattes for those who completed one.

These surveys will help us identify and prioritize the 3 most important issues in the neighborhood. We will provide the results in the April/May newsletter or you can view the results earlier on the BWNA website. These results will also help us prioritize what we focus on as a neighborhood association. I hope one of your New Year’s resolutions is to continue to help us keep this neighborhood as special as it is by staying involved in community decisions as growth and changes occur to our neighborhood.
