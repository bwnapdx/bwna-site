Graffiti is a citywide problem and Beaumont Wilshire has not escaped. The Beaumont Business Association and all the businesses along Fremont are committed to removing graffiti as soon as it appears. They are offering a $100 reward for information leading to the arrest and conviction of those responsible.

Many businesses have signs in their windows announcing this. Also the association met with Preston Wong, Crime Prevention Specialist, to get ideas and suggestions on how to deal with this problem. Here's what we all can do: call the Graffiti Hotline 503-823-4824 to report graffiti. Remove it yourself if possible. Call Brandon Wooters, president of the Beaumont Business Association, at (503) 493-1390 if you have information on anyone defacing the businesses in the neighborhood.
