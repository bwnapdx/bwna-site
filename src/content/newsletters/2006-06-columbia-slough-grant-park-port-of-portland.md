**Monday, June 12, 7:30 PM**  
Bethany Lutheran Church  
NE 37th and Skidmore

Three speakers will address these topics on June 12. **Bonnie Shoffner** of the Columbia Slough Watershed Council will speak about the Slough, its recreational opportunities, volunteer opportunities and some fun upcoming events.

**Lloyd Lindley** of the Grant Park Facilities Improvements Project will describe how the improvements will be funded (private funds) and how we can get involved and what the benefits will be to NE Portland.

**Chris Corich**, Port of Portland, General Manager of Long-Range Planning and Community Affairs will address long range planning, airport noise management and possible purchase of the Colwood Golf Course. Come and share your concerns and get your questions answered.
