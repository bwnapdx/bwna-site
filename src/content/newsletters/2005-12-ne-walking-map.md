As I write this message, the holiday season has already begun with Halloween. My 6-year-old son believes that Santa Claus is going to bring him a fire-breathing two-headed snake; he also told me he would train it to be nice. So, whatever you believe and celebrate, enjoy the season.

At our December 12 general meeting, we will be collaborating with the City to produce a walking map of NE Portland.

Our neighborhood is at the center of the target area for the NE Walking Map. It will be a great project to bring neighbors together and further enhance the livability of our neighborhood. I hope that you can help out by giving input on the walking routes, leading scheduled walks or bike rides, or participating in the scheduled walks/rides. The end product will be a NE Walking Kit which includes a map of NE Portland with the walking/riding routes, a Ten Toe coupon book from local businesses on the Walking Route, a pedometer, walking logs to track your walking progress, wellness brochure, schedule of escorted walks, schedule of Smart Living Classes and “Walk to the Store” note pads. These will be distributed in the neighborhood.

Other volunteer opportunities include our neighborhood tree planting in February and after school tutoring at Beaumont Middle School. It was great to have Sherie Knutsen, the Beaumont Middle School Principal and Donna Bestwick, the Sun School Program Coordinator for Beaumont at our last meeting. The school could also use donations towards or snack foods for the after-school program. This newsletter has more information about each of these projects and volunteer opportunities, so read on!

—Heidi Blischke
