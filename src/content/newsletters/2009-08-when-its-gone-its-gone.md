Growing up, my mother would buy an eight-pack of Coca Cola in the 16oz glass bottles. Being one of four siblings often led to a “first come, first serve” distribution schedule. One day I arrived home from school and saw my brother drinking the last bottle from the eight-pack. I ran to mother for help and proclaimed “Mark drank the last one and it isn’t fair”. In her calm and controlled parental voice, she responded “when they’re gone, they’re gone”. At 14, I was angry. That was not the answer I wanted to hear. Where was the punishment for not sharing? Why did I have to suffer while Mark enjoyed the last Coca-Cola? It didn’t make sense to me until I was an adult. I often ask my clients today “what would you do if X happened”? This simple question illustrates the need for action today to protect from the unknown. As you read the articles and ads in this newsletter, you will recognize a common voice of the community, a voice of compassion and admiration. Take action today and share your story with someone, just like I’ve shared with you. The National Night Out Beaumont-Wilshire Picnic on August 4th in Wilshire Park is a great opportunity to share your story. This is our community, and we need to enjoy and protect what we have today since it might be gone tomorrow, and “when it’s gone, it's gone."
