For those skilled at the fine art of thrift shopping and treasure-hunting, NE Fremont–between 41st & 48th–is resale nirvana. Looking for that special something–one of these shops along ‘resale row’ is sure to have what you want.

Starting at the west end at 4110 NE Fremont is **Full Circle** owned by Gazelle’s Robin Ady and managed by Patti Smith, formerly of the Patti Smith Shop in Royal Oak, Michigan. You will find quality clothing, both current and vintage, curios, jewelry and accessories.

Two doors down at 4120 NE Fremont is the recently opened **Saavy-Urban Vintage**: a fine collection of assorted antiquities, furniture, clothing & jewelry. Owners Lonnie Henry & Nicole Carlon have created a warm & welcoming place with an eye for the eclectic.

A couple blocks down at 4419 Fremont is **flea**–treasures & junk. From crystal chandeliers to mid-century modern furniture, this two year-old local haunt opened by Astrid Guldenmann and Jim Van Eaton, is never the same place twice. If you see it, like it and want it, you better buy it. Don’t “sleep on it” too long, it will be gone!

At 4443 NE Fremont the long-time women’s resale shop formerly known as Bonnie’s has been reincarnated into **My Girlfriend’s Closet**. Owned by sisters Kimberly Makris & Bunny Scalf, this upscale resale shop specializes in classic clothing with all the accessories to match: shoes, purses, jewelry and hats; a rack of vintage too, for those who love the timeless styles of gone by eras.

A trip down resale row ends at **Vestiges and the King’s Ransom** with “something old and something new”. An antique mall with 30 dealers on two floors at 4743 NE Fremont, Vestiges has been owned & operated by Pam Stadick since 2000.

If you need a break, easy to do at one of many eateries along the route. From brew pub to bento to gelato, there’s a cuisine for every taste and budget. Just another reason to love our neighborhood, not that we need one!
