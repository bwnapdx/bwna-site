I want to make sure people know that coyotes are here in Beaumont Wilshire. Unfortunately I did not see the BWNA June/July 08 issue in time to save my cat who was killed on NE Shaver in June 2008. I was not aware of the presence of coyotes and what a huge threat they are to pets. I want to confirm what was stated in the article.

Coyotes are here and people need to keep all pets in from dusk till dawn. I also have been warning my closest neighbors and anyone I see with cats that are out because I do not want anyone else to have to experience the tragedy of losing a pet.
