With Haberdashers, located at the tail end of the Beaumont Village at 4937 NE Fremont Street, owner Chris Osaka has created a men’s store that provides modern and European fashions, and infused it with an ambiance overflowing with testosterone. If you’re in need of the vestiture and accoutrements necessary to manufacture a smart casual look but don’t want to miss a minute of the game, Haberdashers has got you covered with the latest styles and a fifty-five inch flat screen television.

Haberdashers specializes in high quality denim from names like Chip and Pepper, Joe’s Jeans and Rock & Republic, and carries a wide spectrum of polos, button downs, jackets, swim and underwear from the likes of Lacoste, Ben Sherman, Robert Graham, Kangol and 2(x)ist. In addition to clothing, Haberdashers provides the materials necessary to spruce up, preen, primp and smell great. An array of fine products from The Art of Shaving and Jack Black will render your follicles trim and sculpted while conditioning and exfoliating the skin. There are even neat little gift sets that include all the tools needed to pamper that deserving male.

Chris is a true son of the neighborhood, growing up on Alameda Terrace and attending Portland State, where he studied business. The idea to open a men’s clothing store started as a light hearted joke, but soon began to crank the gears of Osaka’s sagacious, economically oriented mind.

After attending a trade show and armed with new knowledge, Chris discovered his niche. The space is anchored by an eclectic instillation where modern forms are lacquered to a deep mahogany, reminiscent of men’s clubs of yore with a modern twist. Resting on the black micro fiber sectional that dominates the back of the store, Chris explains, “we have the TV on non-stop ESPN, the pool table over there and the fridge is always stocked with drinks. We wanted to create an environment where guys can hang out, feel comfortable and actually enjoy the shopping experience for once in their life.”

Chris has recently opened a sister store in the Pearl at 428 NW 11th & Glisan. Come check out the Beaumont or Pearl locations from 11-7 Tuesday through Sunday, or the website www.shophaberdashers.com for more info and pictures of their clothing and accessories.
