## Free Pancake Breakfast

**Oct 10th and Nov 14th, 7–8:15 AM,** Bethany Lutheran Church will host a pancake breakfast including pancakes, juice, coffee, and milk. Gluten-free, sugar-free, and non-dairy options will also be available. This is a FREE EVENT for our neighborhood community and is sponsored by Bethany’s Outreach team. For more information, please call 503-284-3836.

## Bethany women’s annual bazaar

**November 3rd, 9 AM – 3 PM**, Bethany Lutheran Church, 4330 NE 37th Ave – crafts, baked goods, white-elephant sale, and delicious lunch. Money collected will benefit charities around the Portland area. 503-284-3836.
