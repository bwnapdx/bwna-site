At the April BW general meeting, we were serenaded by the Beaumont Jazz Band under the direction of Cynthia Pitts and got to sample treats from many fine eaters located along Fremont. Thanks to the Jazz Band for some fine finger-snapping music. Next time you’re dining or imbibing in one of these places, tell the owners you appreciate their support of neighborhood events: **Alameda Brewhouse** (4765 NE Fremont), **Alameda Café** (4641 NE Fremont), **Blue Moose Café** (4936 Fremont), **Blue Olive** (4627 NE Fremont), **Foxfire Teas** (4605 NE Fremont), **Parisi’s** (4831 NE Fremont) and **Round Table Pizza** (4831 NE Fremont). Gift certificates donated by Alameda Brewhouse were won by Jean Shevland and Deborah Ham. Photos from this event can be viewed at:

http://www.bwna.org/photos.html
