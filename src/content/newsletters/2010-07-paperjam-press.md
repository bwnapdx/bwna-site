**Paperjam Press**, your neighborhood digital print shop, is staying open late on 2nd Friday on Fremont, July 9th from 5pm-9pm to celebrate their new location on Fremont street-- even though they have been in this location since October 2009 , they haven’t officially had a Grand Opening Party...so they invite everyone to stop by and say hi. They will be celebrating with food and beverage, a special gift item, a free drawing for product, music and fun.
