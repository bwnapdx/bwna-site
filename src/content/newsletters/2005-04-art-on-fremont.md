Several businesses along Fremont will display artwork by Beaumont artists during the month of May. Over a dozen Beaumont artists will have works in about ten businesses, as part of the first *Art on Fremont* group show. Works will include photographs, watercolors, paintings, mixed media pieces, fiber art, basketry, jewelry, and works in glass.

Shop, dine at or visit these businesses during May and enjoy the local art: **Albina Bank, Cool Runnings, Bella Flora, Fife, Red Fig, Foxfire Teas, Parisi’s, Alameda Café, Criollo, Java Man**, **Jim and Patty’s** and others.

On May 17, there will be a fiber art fashion show featuring work by Judee Moonbeam, and a Mediterranean fare dinner and music at Alameda Café. The event begins at 7pm, by reservation.

The idea for the group show grew out of the monthly meetings of artists who live in Beaumont Wilshire and whose works are featured on the ‘online’ gallery, *Beaumont Artspace*, found on the neighborhood’s website, www.bwna.org. The artists have joined with business owners to showcase local art, promote the businesses along Fremont, and create a fun event for the community.

Maps of businesses participating and artists showing at different venues are located at each site and can also be accessed on the website, www.bwna.org/artspace. See you at ‘Art on Fremont’, May 1 – 31, 2005!
