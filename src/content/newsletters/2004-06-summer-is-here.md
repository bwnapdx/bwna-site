It’s June and summer weather is here. Everyone is outside walking, skateboarding, biking, or driving with the top down and windows open. My five year-old son Andy and I ride our bikes every morning along Alameda across Fremont and into Hollywood to his preschool. Crossing at Fremont and Alameda can be tough and we usually need to wait before we can cross. I am always impressed with the number of people who stop for us as we make our way across Fremont. This is the place on our ride where Andy often has trouble getting back on his bike to start across the street. Thank you for your patience with the little ones in our neighborhood!

Our neighborhood transportation and land-use committee has identified traffic along Fremont as one of the main concerns, primarily for cars pulling out of side streets and pedestrians crossing. We are working with Debbie Bishoff at the City of Portland’s Bureau of Planning to address traffic concerns in our neighborhood. Debbie will attend our general meeting on June 14th. She will explain how the City is prioritizing traffic concerns and is interested in your input. You can also give feedback into the process by calling our neighborhood transportation/land-use chair, David Whitaker at 503-281-2176. As we are in the process of searching for solutions to make Fremont safer, please be aware of the many people crossing the street and cars entering traffic. We are lucky to live in a neighborhood with so many great businesses where we can shop and enjoy strolling down our neighborhood’s main street, Fremont.

I had the honor of being elected president at our BWNA Board elections in April. I am looking forward to serving you for the next couple of years and look forward to lots of great input from the neighborhood. I would like to welcome back our current members, all the neighbors who deliver newsletters and everyone who has been contributing to planning neighborhood activities and seeing neighborhood projects through completion. I am excited about our newly elected board members who bring new ideas and renewed energy. And finally, I would like to give a special thanks to Helen and Mario who put together this very successful newsletter every other month and maintain the BWNA website. These both require a tremendous amount of volunteer time. Thank you Helen and Mario!
