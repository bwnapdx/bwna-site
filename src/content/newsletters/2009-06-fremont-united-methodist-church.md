Fremont United Methodist Church is taking a new approach to Vacation Bible School this year. "God's Green Garden: Caring for Creation" will be from July 27-31, 9 am to noon. Children ages 4 through entering 4th grade are invited to explore how we can care for the earth and for others. We'll have daily music and movement, crafts, gardening, and mission projects. Fremont UMC is located at 2620 NE Fremont St., 503.284.4647, www.fremontumc.org.
