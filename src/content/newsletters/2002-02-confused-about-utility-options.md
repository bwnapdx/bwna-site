As part of the next phase of Oregon’s electricity restructuring, residential customers are being asked to choose from a portfolio of options for their service. The utilities have done a mass mailing explaining the changes and asking customers to choose between their existing service, three “green” power options, and a time-of-use option to encourage using power at off-peak times.

Debra Kennedy, BWNA resident, is working with the Fair and Clean Energy coalition to help present this information all over the state so customers understand the changes and can make choices based on their own circumstances and lifestyle preferences. Customers can choose to do nothing and keep their existing service, or select a new energy option. Most choices have an open enrollment, where customers can choose to make a change at any time. Some of the programs have enrollment deadlines, and limited enrollment. Come with your questions.
