## Get ZAPped!

**October 16, from 12 PM – 2 PM**, the ever-popular Zoo Animal Presenters (ZAP) will be back at Bethany Lutheran Church, 4330 NE 37. Toddlers, kids, and parents have all thoroughly enjoyed this event at Bethany for the past several years. Look for sidewalk signs directing you to the viewing area.

ZAP is a program in which high school students are trained by members of the Oregon Zoo to be animal presenters, enabling them to teach both young and old distinct features of each animal. Not only is this an excellent opportunity for our neighborhood families to learn about these animals, but it also serves as excellent training for our young student leaders who are continuing to enhance their public speaking capabilities. An Oregon Zoo representative is always there to answer any questions viewers may have about this as well as other opportunities that the zoo offers.

## Be Your Own Supernanny

**October 20, 7pm** at Bethany Lutheran Church 4330 NE 37
“Parents Unlimited: Our Role as Supernanny” is designed for parents of active children who seek more information in dealing with ongoing, frustrating situations. Gena Minnix, a trained counselor who holds a Masters degree in Marriage and Family Counseling, is specifically trained in communicating and implementing positive reinforcements for both parents and children. Classes will meet each Thursday evening at 7 PM beginning Oct. 20 – Nov. 17. Seating may be limited. Call Bethany at (503)284-3836 to reserve your spot.
