I hope you’re all enjoying our crisp, winter weather! As you know the Beaumont Business Association (BBA) has spent most of this year rebuilding itself to over 56 members currently. We enjoyed a very successful Fremont Festival in August and our ambition to build name recognition for “2nd Fridays on Fremont” is working out just fine. Our efforts have been recognized by both the business community and the neighborhood and our desire is to keep the momentum going into 2011! Our focus is two-fold; 1) To create a positive response from the businesses in our area to help create a vital and thriving business area; and 2) to bring people to our businesses by creating an atmosphere where people want to shop, dine and play.

With that, the BBA has just been awarded a $3,000 grant from the Alliance of Portland Neighborhood Business Associations. The grant application was contingent on specific plans and must be accompanied with matching funds. Together, with your input we have come up with two plans for the winter of 2010 and we are excited about both of them!

The first was the Fremont Festival of Lights where we lit 65-75 trees along NE Fremont from 40th to 50th. The tree lighting ceremony, held on Friday, December 10th was a hit.

Next, is to place 30 (23”x36”) flag pole banners on the street poles on NE Fremont from 40th to 50th. These flags will welcome visitors to our business district and add an appeal to remind visitors to stop and enjoy our street. Each flag will be designed to enhance our village main street and welcome visitors passing through. We are currently working with a designer to come up with several ideas for our banner pole flags on Fremont. Have an idea you’d like to see? Get involved on our banner committee! We hope to have three designs to choose from in the next few months and plan to hoist the banners on the poles as soon as we can.
