Fremont Fest 2008 will be held on Saturday Aug. 2nd, 2008 with festivities beginning at 10AM and continuing well into the evening! Fun for the whole family – check out what we have planned for you:

Gather for the annual Fremont Fest Parade – pets, families and families on bikes welcome (helmets required!) **Parade kicks off at 10:30AM**. Gather in the parking lot of Beaumont Middle School at 10 AM . Sponsored by Green Dog Pet Supply who will host a pet costume contest before the parade – judging begins at 9:45AM.

**Music** — For your listening, dancing and singing enjoyment, hear The Retrofits, playing at Fremont Court (41st and Fremont). At the courtyard at Amalfi’s Italian Restaurant: What’s That Noise?, the Ultrapants and SFOS, a Latin Jazz Trio. There will be more music all along the street from 10AM through the evening hours.

**Art** — Local artists and jewelers will show case their work between 11AM and 5PM at the Fremont Court Art Fair behind Foot Traffic, on the corner of 41st and Fremont.

Sparkle Beauty Bar will also be hosting a Children’s Art Gallery showcasing and selling local children’s artwork. 100% of proceeds will go to the Make a Wish Foundation.

**Foods of Fremont** — Tempt your taste buds with wine tasting at Blackbird Wineshop and Vinideus, free Turkish coffee served at Ottoman Klasik, Bar-B-Q at Beaumont Market, roasted corn on the cob, grape slushies from Blackbird Wineshop, pizza at Amalfi’s and Papa Murphy’s and more!

Test your **Fremont Facts** with the *first* **Fremont Fest Scavenger Hunt**. Pick up your maps and clues at Alameda Fitness Center (4016 NE Fremont) and win a prize when you complete the hunt. This map will also provide a guide to activities and vendors for Fremont Fest.

Take a free carriage ride sponsored by Gazelle Natural Fibre Clothing, Alameda Brewhouse and Fremont Veterinary Clinic, starting at the corner of 41st and Fremont. **Rides will continue from 11AM to 4PM**.

Your favorite Fremont retail stores will have sales and discounts galore! Check out tent and sidewalk sales in addition to in-store discounts! Balloons and face painting will be available for the young and young at heart!
