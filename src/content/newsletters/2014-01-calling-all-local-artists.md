For the last ten years a group of local artists has been meeting occasionally to discuss ideas for local shows, encourage and support each other and just have a good time. This group is known as Beaumont Artspace. In 2012 we had a group show at Ristretto Coffee celebrating 100 years for the Steel Bridge.

Artists of all media are invited to join our group. No fees or obligations.

If you are interested you may contact me at mc-hk@comcast.net.

We can be found on the Beaumont Wilshire website: http://www.bwna.us/artspace and on Facebook www.facebook.com/artspacers.
