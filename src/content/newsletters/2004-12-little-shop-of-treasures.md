If you're like me, you've probably noticed a grand transformation taking place in our wonderful neighborhood. Much to the delight of area residents, lots of new shops have opened recently along NE Fremont. One bright spot in particular, is a little shop of treasures called, quite simply, **flea**.

Longtime Beaumont Wilshire resident, Astrid Guldenmann and partner Jim Van Eaton are co-proprietors. Together they have created a veritable ongoing garage sale, filling flea with an eclectic mix of antique and kitschy collectibles from furniture to lighting, jewelry to kitchenware. Primitive, Shabby, Retro or Mid-Century Modern—it's all there!

Having a knack for knick-knacks, Astrid has been known in the neighborhood for putting on great garage sales over the years, while Jim has been in the collectibles business for a decade. Since September they have taken their talents for the "art of the deal" to the next level by opening this little shop of treasures, restocked every week with new finds. One never knows what might be selling there, making a weekly stop at flea—for people like me who "brake for garage sales"—a must.

But shopper - beware: if something catches your eye, you'd better act quickly. Alas, I discovered lots of things at flea sprout wings and fly out the door. Consider yourself warned! **flea, 4419 NE Fremont Wed – Sat 12 to 6, Sun 11 to 4**.
