The third concert in the series June 22, 7pm features Matthew Weiers, (see story left).

The final concert in this series, on Saturday, July 20 at 4pm, features the Bethany Lutheran Praise Team, singing upbeat contemporary music.  Last year’s outdoor concert at Bethany tells us that this will prove to be a fun and exciting event for people of all ages—especially kids who may enjoy popsicles or ice cream in the July heat! For more information about this concert or the concert series, please visit www.bethanyportland.org.
