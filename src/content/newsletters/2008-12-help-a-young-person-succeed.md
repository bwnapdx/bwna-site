Teachers strive to give their students the individual attention and instruction they need to succeed in school. But with class sizes what they are and special needs increasing each year, the task is daunting. This is where adult residents in Beaumont Wilshire can help by volunteering to tutor special-needs youth. There are two successful Northeast Portland tutoring programs that will be discussed at upcoming BWNA General Meetings:

1.) Beaumont Middle School Mentor Program: Volunteer one hour each week to tutor during the school day. Last year 30 adults made a huge difference in the lives of 30 kids. Contact Principal Sherie Knutsen at 503-961-5610. Principal Knutsen will be speaking at BWNA’s December 8th General Meeting.

2.) Youth Employment Institute GED Preparation Program: Volunteer once or twice a week during the school day in either writing or math. The focus is on GED attainment in an alternative school setting, which can then lead to employment opportunities with participating businesses. This nonprofit organization is also interested in finding community members to mentor youth in a program for young parents. Contact AmeriCorps Member Madeline Lockwood at 503-280-1058. Ms. Lockwood will be speaking at BWNA’s February 9th General Meeting.
