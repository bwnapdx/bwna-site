With Easter and Passover observances scheduled for the same week in April this year, it’s only fitting that BWNA’s own spring gala add to the week’s festivities. Held annually on the second Monday in April, the “Taste of Beaumont” event, featuring a variety of cuisine samples from neighborhood restaurants, is not so coincidentally planned for the same evening as BWNA elections. In addition to the gastronomical and political activities, entertainment will be provided by band leader Cynthia Plank and her talented musicians from Beaumont Middle School. Mark your calendars now: April 13th, 7:00 p.m., in the Bethany Lutheran Church’s “Fellowship Hall,” which is a basement activity room, located on the corner of 37th and Skidmore, across from Wilshire Park. (To get to the activity room, enter from the Skidmore side of the church. There will be a sign indicating the pathway to the room.)

A wide range of businesses have contributed this year to insure the Taste of Beaumont is a success. This year’s participants include Alameda Brewhouse, Amalfi’s, Beaumont Florist (providing floral arrangements for the tables), Beaumont Market (providing soft drinks, in celebration of their 20th year in the neighborhood), Bumblekiss, Cha! Cha! Cha! Taqueria, Ottoman Klasik, Parisi’s, Ristretto Roasters (supplying freshly ground coffee), Rose’s Ice Cream, Round Table Pizza, and Tuk Tuk Thai Restaurant.

The food, drink, and entertainment are all free of charge, and there’s no obligation to volunteer for BWNA positions. With that being said, the expectation is that a few dedicated souls will emerge from the crowd to allow their names to be put into nomination for newly-vacated Board positions. A Board member serves a two-year term (with the possibility of being re-elected up to 6 consecutive years) and is obligated to attend Board and General Meetings on the second Monday of each month. The Board responds to residents’ concerns, sets the agenda for General Meetings, and directs BWNA activities and projects. Along with the Newsletter and Web site, the Board of Directors is the lifeblood of our neighborhood association.

Thus, with a unique blend of dining, music, and community activism, this is one BWNA General Meeting you don’t want to miss!
