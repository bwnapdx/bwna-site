## Balto Bumps Into Buttigieg

In June, U.S. Secretary of Transportation Pete Buttigieg came to the area for President Biden's Investing in America Tour. Sam Balto, Portland bicycle transportation advocate and neighborhood bike bus leader (see Creative Kids article on page 14) said he had one purpose to speak with the secretary: "To spread the joy of bike bus."
