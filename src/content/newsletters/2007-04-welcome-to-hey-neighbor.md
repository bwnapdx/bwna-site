Now available on the front page of the Beaumont Wilshire website, http://www.bwna.org/ is a link to a new service called ***Hey Neighbor!*** I developed this website because I believe, today more than ever, we need opportunities to interact with our neighbors.

There may be an item you are looking for that is sitting around the corner in someone else’s garage waiting for a new home. Or a local group’s next volunteer project is across the street from your home. Hey Neighbor! is dedicated to the exchange of goods and services, volunteerism and to community-building.

We will all be enriched by reaching out to our neighbor and the site offers many opportunities for neighbors to interact. It may be through the exchange of goods or services. It may be announcing a mom’s or dad’s group meeting at the park. It could be by connecting potential participants to existing walking groups or volunteers to repair a neighbor’s garage, or just the exchange of ideas and helpful hints. Local businesses can advertise and offer specials. The opportunities are limitless. We can discuss neighborhood news, check out local businesses, look for volunteer projects, and announce garage sales, neighborhood meetings or just say “Hey Neighbor”. Check it out!
