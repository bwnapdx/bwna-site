Wednesday, October 27th  
6:00pm – 8:00pm

The Annual Community Pumpkin Carving and Pizza Party will be held on:  
**Wednesday, October 27th from 6:00pm – 8:00pm** at Bethany Lutheran Church across from Wilshire Park. Kids of all ages and their parents are invited to bring pumpkins and join in the Halloween fun as we create masterpieces and messes!  
**Costumes are encouraged, pizza is free, and a good time is guaranteed!**

Bethany is located at:

**4330 NE 37th Avenue**

For more information call 503.284.3836 or visit us at www.bethanyportland.org
