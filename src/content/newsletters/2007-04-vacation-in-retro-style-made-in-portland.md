When planning your next vacation (spring, summer, fall, or winter), consider how great you will feel and look in a stylish, individually fitted swimsuit. Poppi Swim and Sport, a neighborhood boutique features locally designed and sewn retro swimwear and is just the ticket for you. Owner/Designer Pam Levenson first conceived of the idea of the boutique when she needed a swimsuit for an October vacation. The swimwear business was not a huge stretch for Pam; she has a degree in apparel design and did an internship in a bikini shop. She has also worked for designers Guess and Norm Thompson.

Poppi Swim and Sport celebrated its first anniversary this March 17th. Business has been good and is growing. So much so that Pam would like to expand her offerings and space. She designs and fits her line of one and two-piece suits for women which are stylish yet comfortable. One-piece styles include ‘Shirley’, ‘Paula’, and ‘Sadie’, names that go back to the 1940’s and 1950’s. Two-piece styles, including the ‘Wendy Tankini’, are also available. You have a wide choice of fabrics, most of which are a nylon/spandex blend. You can also find a line of 100% chlorine-proof suits for water aerobics.

In addition, Pam carries Havaianas flip flops (‘the best rubber sandal in the world’) and oil cloth tote bags to complete your ensemble. All suits are moderately priced, and for a small additional fee, Pam will custom fit a suit just for you. Most sales, however, are “off the rack” already made suits. By summer, Pam will offer men’s swimming shorts. Also coming soon will be a vacation board on the boutique’s website and e-commerce. In fact, you can preview some of the designs on the website. Better yet visit the store so Pam can assist you in person.
