PDX made its community service debut on Saturday the 10th of January in support of the Portland Public Schools PTA Clothes Closet and the Oregon Food Bank. The clothing and food drive was an exercise in social networking that resulted in the donation of 70 large bags of clothing, shoes and winter coats for needy school children, and 309 pounds of food to help prevent hunger.

About a dozen people showed up at Willow Cottage, a Beaumont-Wilshire residence, to sort food and clothing that group members collected from their friends and neighbors. Representative-elect Michael Dembrow (pictured above) was on hand to work with his new constituents on this exciting project, and to take advantage of an opportunity to take questions concerning the upcoming legislative session.

CHANGE TAKES ACTION – PDX was inspired by President-Elect Obama’s call for community involvement in our government, and is one of over 4,000 groups that met in homes across the country in December. The goal of the meet-up was to develop a grass-roots method to discuss issues, make recommendations to public officials, and to plan and carry-out community service actions. The group expects to meet monthly to help ensure that government at the federal, state and local levels is engaged in a two-way communication to enhance our sense of community.
