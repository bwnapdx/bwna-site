The Transportation and Land Use committee meets on the third Monday of each month at the Java Man Coffee Shop, 4727 NE Fremont St at 7:30 PM . The committee is actively working on the two top neighborhood concerns that came to light in the neighborhood survey.

Improving pedestrian safety is a main concern. We are working with the Portland Department of Transportation (PDOT) and the Portland Bureau of Planning on the ***Fremont Main Street Study***. The results of this study will provide a framework for the City and the neighborhood to develop projects that will meet our desires for a more safe and enjoyable Fremont Street. We are in the beginning stages of the study and have recently provided the City with a list of specific issues that we want addressed. These issues are listed on the BWNA web site (www.bwna.org) under the transportation and land use heading.

Commercial development along Fremont and its affects is another concern. Two new commercial developments are proposed for our neighborhood. Many of you have probably seen the picture board that shows the new commercial building proposed for development at 44th and Fremont. There is also a new mixed use building proposed for development at NE 50th and Fremont. The committee has met with both developers and has expressed the neighborhood’s desire for the design of buildings that “fit in” with the neighborhood character. We will continue to work with the developers during the design review and city permitting process.
