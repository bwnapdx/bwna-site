We would love your help in distributing the BWNA newsletter. Distributing entails walking around a short route assigned to you and putting a newsletter on your neighbor’s doorstep. The newsletter is published every two months and walking the route is an excellent way to meet your neighbors and to get those muscles in action especially after the wet winter we’ve had. We are lucky to have such a great newsletter to keep us abreast of what is happening in our neighborhood and I encourage you to be a part of this wonderful publication. Please contact Jennifer Francis at 503-459-8114 to find out more information.
