Neighborhood Waves is a program sponsored by United Cerebral Palsy of Oregon and S.W. Washington. Program goals include:

- Educating community businesses about disabilities and support programs at United Cerebral Palsy.
- Indirectly educating our neighbors about disabilities through exposure and positive interactions.
- Developing relationships with community businesses and neighbors who may be interested in supporting our programs through donations or volunteerism.
