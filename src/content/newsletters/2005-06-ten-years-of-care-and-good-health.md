Beaumont Health Care Clinic (BHCC) is celebrating 10 years on Fremont, and to celebrate, it is offering new patients $10 off the regular fee of any practitioner.

“The neighborhood has changed a lot in the past 10 years,” says Dr. Lori von der Heydt, clinic founder and naturopathic doctor. “The number of people who visit Beaumont Village has surged. We are no longer a sleepy little village.”

As traffic has increased, so has business. “We have more walk-in clients and “walk-bys”—people who visit the area because of the interesting shops and events, see our clinic, note the name, and then call us later,” says Lori.

The clients have also become more educated about alternative medicine. Ten years ago clients mostly requested to see a naturopath. Now they call up and ask for a specialized service, i.e., treatment for allergies, upper respiratory infections, migraines, arthritis, cancers, and fibromyalgia.

The clinic has added several new staff. Carla Welker, a licensed clinical social worker and certified alcohol and drug counselor, provides individual and group counseling. Among her specialties are anxiety disorders, depression, life transitions, and crisis intervention. She also holds a group for women in transition through loss.

Dr. Marianne Marchese, a board-certified naturopathic doctor, is licensed in Oregon as a primary care physician trained to diagnose and treat both acute and chronic disease. Marianne specializes in assessing environmental risk factors for certain health conditions. She also teaches at the National College of Naturopathic Medicine in Portland.

In general, the BHCC is open 9 AM to 5 PM , Monday through Friday and by special appointment. Check the Web site at www.beaumonthealth.com for the hours, specialties, and fees of individual staff. The clinic is located at 4445 NE Fremont, and staff may be reached at 503-240-7752.
