“Past – Present – Future”

“An Evening of Music for the Community”

*Featuring Pianist* Matt Burnett

Saturday, October 16th at 7:00pm  
Bethany at Wilshire Park

The community is invited to a special concert of music for all ages. Pianist Matt Burnett will perform original compositions, tributes to artists from the past and homage to more modern music. He will be joined by local guest singers and musicians. This is a community event you won’t want to miss.

Burnett began studying classical piano at the age of three. With an early aptitude for improvisation in classical and romantic music, at age nine he took on the role of accompanist in the ballet studio of his mother, Lynn Marchant, who is a Grand Master of Russian classical ballet. Burnett has spent more than 25 years in the field, performing classical, contemporary, spiritual and pop/rock music as well as recording many of his own. He is now a composer, producer, arranger, recording artist, teacher and mentor.

Bring your neighbors and friends to an enjoyable evening of music for people of all ages. Refreshments will be served.

The concert is a gift to the community from
