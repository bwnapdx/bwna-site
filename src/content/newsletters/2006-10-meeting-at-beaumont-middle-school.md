**Monday, Oct 9th, 7:30 PM**
**Beaumont Middle School**
NE 42nd & Fremont

The first general neighborhood meeting of the fall season will take place **Oct 9th at 7:30PM** at Beaumont Middle School located at the corner of 42nd and Fremont. The board has decided to move its neighborhood meetings to this more centrally located spot, with plenty of on site parking. The focus of the meeting will be ‘Schools’.

Speakers will include **Sherrie Knutsen**, Principal of Beaumont Middle School. She will share with us how the school is doing and what is planned for this school year.

Also speaking will be **Debbie Gordon** of ‘Portland for Schools’. She will speak on the Portland Public Schools Local Option Property Tax Levy, which will be on the November ballot.

BWNA board member **Colin Rowan** will give a short presentation on the spring tree planting and how you can get involved.

***Refreshments will be served***. Anyone who lives, works or owns a business in the Beaumont Wilshire area is a member of the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood and everyone is cordially invited to this fall kick off meeting.
