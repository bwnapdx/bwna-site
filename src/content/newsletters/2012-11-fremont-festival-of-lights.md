## Begin the Holiday Season

**Friday – December 7th, 5:00pm – 9:00pm**

*Bring your family to the 3rd Annual Fremont Festival of Lights for a visit with Santa, music, shopping and entertainment.*

## New this year…

*Parents can sign up for a 30 minute time slot for their children's turn for photos and a visit with Santa. Santa's Village will be by The Arrangement and Santa will arrive at 5:00pm Friday. No more waiting in long lines.*

*Details at www.businessonfremont.com*

*The Shop Hop is expanded to three days – Friday, Saturday and Sunday, December 7th – 9th, with stores and shops in Beaumont Village offering holiday bargains and specials.*

*Everyone who participates in the three-day Shop Hop is eligible to enter the drawing for three wonderful prizes.*

## The tradition continues with…

*Santa's arrival on NE Fremont at 5:00pm on a Portland fire truck with lights flashing.*

*Four villages along NE Fremont which will include Santa's Village with his elves and Mrs. Claus, live music, a children's craft center, and street vendors. Locations by The Arrangement, Paperjam Press, Grand Central Bakery, and Foot Traffic.*

## Music by:

*Innisfree Celtic Band, Dickens Christmas Carolers and more.*

*Mark your calendar. Bring your family and friends to the 3rd Annual Fremont Festival of Lights, starting Friday, December 7th.*

*Get in the holiday spirit! Enjoy the good cheer and enchantment of the Fremont Festival of Lights!*

*More information at: www.businessonfremont.com*
*Sponsored by The Beaumont Business Association, Venture Portland and the many businesses on NE Fremont Street.*
