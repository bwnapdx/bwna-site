**On Friday, April 8th**, Alameda Elementary will host its fifth annual auction to support staff funding. Come join this celebration of the spirit of Alameda. Contact the school at 503-916-6030 for ticket information. This year’s auction will be at the Tiffany Center downtown, and will give you a chance to relive the movies, the music, the television of the 1980s. The most highly coveted items every year are the classroom projects. Every classroom, from kindergarten through 5th grade, creates a class project up for sale. Come to the auction and see the talent and the creativity of the students and teachers of Alameda.
