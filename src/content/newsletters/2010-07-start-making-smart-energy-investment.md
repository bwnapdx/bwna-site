Did you know 60 percent of energy used to heat and cool homes is lost due to leaky ducts, inefficient equipment, poor insulation and air leaks? By increasing your home’s energy efficiency, Energy Trust of Oregon can help you reduce energy costs and save money, while making your home healthier, safer and more comfortable. Schedule a Home Energy Review at a convenient time for you. During your review, an Energy Trust energy advisor will conduct a one-hour walkthrough of your home, visually assessing typical areas of energy loss. These areas include Insulation levels in the attic, walls, floors and ducts, Heating system, Air sealing and windows, Ventilation and Moisture problems. Your energy advisor will also give you the information you need to make your home more comfortable—while using less energy—and provide free instant energy-saving products. After your Home Energy Review, you will receive customized recommendations and information on how to receive Energy Trust cash incentives for qualifying, energy-saving home improvements. Energy Trust cash incentives can often be combined with state and federal tax credits—giving you even more savings.

With your advisor’s recommendations, you will have all the information to make your improvements. When you’re ready to install energy-efficiency upgrades, contact an Energy Trust trade ally contractor—or any other contractor with an Oregon Construction Contractors Board-license, or a current Washington contractor’s license—to discuss and schedule installation of eligible energy-saving improvements for your home.* Washington customers heating their home with gas from NW Natural are eligible to participate. Contractors are licensed and insured, and trained on the latest energy-efficiency standards and offerings from Energy Trust. After work is completed, they can help you submit Energy Trust forms to receive cash incentives on qualifying improvements. *Attic or floor insulation can be self-installed with pre-approval from Energy Trust. Interested in solar energy improvements?

If you are interested in installing a solar electric or solar water heating system, attend a free Basics of Going Solar workshop. Learn how solar works in Oregon, identify the right system for your home and find out how solar can fit into your budget with Energy Trust cash incentives and state and federal tax credits. Register for the workshop at www.energytrust.org/solarforhomes. To schedule your no cost energy review call 866.368.7878.
