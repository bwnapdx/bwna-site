## Intro to Northeast Village PDX

**November 16 (Monday) 11 a.m. – 12:30 p.m.**

**Location:** Rose City Park United Methodist Church, 5860 NE Alameda St. (conference room)  
Use glass-door entrance on NE Alameda; free parking in church lot

Want to learn about a vibrant and engaging community of older adults (60+) in northeast Portland? At this informational session you'll learn how you (or someone you love) can become a member and/or volunteer at NE Village PDX. The Village creates connections, shares skills and expertise, and provides services to members such as rides, help with yard work, tech support, and rightsizing advice, along with a variety of social activities and educational programs. The event is free and open to the public. Northeast Village PDX is a program of the Northeast Community Center. For more info call (503) 895-2750 or email [info@nevillagepdx.org](mailto:info@nevillagepdx.org).

## Fremont Holiday Fest

**December 4 (Friday) 4 p.m. – 7 p.m.**

**Location:** Beaumont Village from NE 41st to NE 50th Avenue

Join in the seasonal spirit at Fremont Holiday Fest, sponsored by the Beaumont Business Association (BBA). This festive neighborhood celebration is a chance to see friends, support local businesses, shop for unique crafts and gifts, and kick off the holiday season together.

Santa and Mrs. Claus will be at Nectar Frozen Yogurt Lounge for photos starting at 4 p.m. An appointment is required; sign-ups begin November 21 at [bbapdx.org](https://bbapdx.org). The celebration will also feature a festive tree lighting, bringing the community together to officially light up the holiday season on Fremont Street!

## Weihnachtsmarkt German-Style Christmas Market

**December 5 (Saturday) 10 a.m. – 6 p.m.**

**Location:** German American Society, 5626 NE Alameda St.

Enjoy holiday warmth and Gemütlichkeit (cozy comfort) at this annual event hosted by the German American Society. Find Local artisans, authentic German crafts, fresh-baked holiday sweets, traditional German food, and warm Glühwein, a classic German mulled wine. (See ad on page 12.)
