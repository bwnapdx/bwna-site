Crossing the border into California, inspiration struck.

Some 20 years ago, when Robin and Byron Ady were driving to San Francisco for their first trade show to stock their soon to open clothing store, they were struggling to come up with a name. Robin knew she wanted “natural fibre clothing” as the tag line for the store, which they planned to open in Portland’s Old Town, but beyond that, they had no idea.

Passing Azalea in southern Oregon got them started looking at road signs for inspiration. They were considering using ‘Azalea’ when they passed the turn off for Gazelle in northern California. There it was: a tiny town nestled in a sweet valley, with Mount Shasta rising handsomely behind it: *Gazelle*. The Adys ran with it, and the name not only recalls a cute outpost in the Northern California landscape but also garments that drape, flow, and exude naturalism.

After close to a decade in downtown Portland, the Adys opened a second store at the current Beaumont Wilshire location, a move that Robin calls an “instinctual win.” The family lives off NE Fremont St. and frequently passed by the Dutch Village building, where the For Lease sign tipped off something of a Gazelle franchise. For four years, the Adys ran the two stores, and then consolidated at the present location when Robin decided to simplify her life and business.

“I felt like I was coming home,” Robin says of the Beaumont Wilshire move. “It’s a gorgeous building, and I feel so loved here.”

Robin, who got her start working as a shipping clerk—and then “handling everything”—for a local wholesale clothing manufacturer, enjoys spending more time on the floor, and her “mathematician wizard” husband, Byron, keeps the books. Along with their three employees, they offer a wide range of clothing and gifts for women and men ranging in ages 20 to 70.

This June, Gazelle celebrates its 20-year anniversary. They will hold their annual June anniversary sale from Friday, June 11th to Sunday the 20th. Gazelle will be hosting a reception with live music open to the public from 7 p.m. to 9 p.m. Friday, June 11. Gazelle, 4100 NE Fremont St., 503-288-3422.
