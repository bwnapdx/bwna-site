Husband and wife Vincent Chavez and Lisa Laing-Chavez have blessed Beaumont Village with a scrumptious dining experience called Bumblekiss. The tiny space (formerly Suzanne's) lies just one block off of NE Fremont on 46th at 3517 NE 46. You can't miss its persimmon and matte black exterior. The restaurant offers a fresh, vibrant and unique dining experience.

Everything is made in-house from scratch and with love. Lisa and Vincent along with chef Clay Card really care about what goes into people's bodies. This means using organic, nutritious, free-range, and veggie-friendly ingredients.

The couple moved to Portland from the Bay area two years ago. Lisa, an artist, and Vincent, an architect, decided to merge their creative energies and the outcome was Bumblekiss. The restaurant has just celebrated its first year and now offers breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Breakfast boasts specials like salmon cakes with poached eggs, and regulars such as cinnamon French toast, tasty scrambles, biscuits and gravy, and dynamite veggie and meat benedicts. Lunch offers a wide selection of soups, salads, 'free-range' burgers, pasta and gourmet sandwiches. Dinner while similar to lunch includes entrees such as grilled flank steak with marsala cream sauce and wild salmon with fresh thyme, capers and lemon butter.

The restaurant offers beer and wine, is kid-friendly, has an outdoor patio, encourages sidewalk art and has a ping pong table. Bumblekiss is open Tuesday through Sunday from 8AM until 3PM, and Wednesday through Saturday from 8AM until 9PM.
