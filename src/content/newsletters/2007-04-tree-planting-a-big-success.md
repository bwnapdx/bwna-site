On Saturday, February 17, over one hundred people gathered at the Wilshire United Methodist Church to support the Friends of Trees neighborhood tree planting. The tree planting included the Beaumont Wilshire, Vernon and Concordia neighborhoods. Over 170 trees were planted in our neighborhoods, adding shade and beautifying our urban forest.

Special thanks to the Wilshire United Methodist Church for hosting the event, Umpqua Bank for financial support, Albina Bank and Ristretto Roasters who teamed up to provide much needed coffee to start the day, Bagel Land and Delphina’s who provided breakfast treats and, of course, all the volunteers who came out to support the planting.

If you are interested in supporting Friends of Trees or curious as to when the next planting in the neighborhood will be, check in at http://friendsoftrees.org/.

Videos and photos from the event can be seen at http://bwna.org/videos.html.
