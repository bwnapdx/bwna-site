A Neighborhood Emergency Team (NET) is a group of at least 20 citizens trained by the Portland Fire Bureau (PFB) to provide emergency assistance in their own neighborhood for 72 hours following a disaster such as a severe winter storm or major earthquake.

Be a part of Beaumont-Wilshire’s team and attend this free 8-session training by the PFB. You can receive advance notice and a registration form by calling the NET info line at the PFB (**503) 823-1260**, and leave your name and mailing address.
