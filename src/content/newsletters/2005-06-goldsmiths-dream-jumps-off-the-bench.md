Alexander Berzl may have opened his shop recently—the first day of winter, Dec. 21, last year—but he’s been living in Northeast Portland and practicing his goldsmith trade for much of his life.

After completing a four-year apprenticeship program in Augsburg, Germany, not far from his home village, Berzl moved to the United States in 1978, settling into a house two doors away from Suzanne’s on NE 46th Ave.

After two-plus decades working on the wholesale level, that is, catering to customers via other jewelry shops around town, Berzl decided it was time to make a move. “I turned 50,” he says. “I had to do something. Some people buy red sports cars ... “

Lucky for Beaumont Wilshire residents, Berzl kept an eye out for a storefront along NE Fremont St. When he saw the vacant space in the building near Java Man and Criollo, he hit the brakes and signed up. He says, “I’ve seen the progress on Fremont. I’d been looking for a location, and it paid off to wait.”

While Berzl builds his inventory, repairs and custom work are the driving force behind his business. Custom work can include the meltdown and remanufacture of old pieces into something new and dazzling, as he just did for a couple celebrating a milestone wedding anniversary. Berzl works in silver, gold, and platinum, and suggests allowing two weeks for the completion of custom pieces.

Customers are impressed that he works on-site. He says, “Northeast Portland feels like a little village,” reminiscent of his hometown in southern Germany.

Designs on display at Alexander’s Goldworks include Berzl’s inlay work with opal, diamonds, and gold as well as strung precious and semiprecious stones. Berzl also displays glass by a couple who work out of a studio in Cannon Beach under the name Icefire Glassworks. Coincidentally, they grew up in Northeast Portland.

Shop hours are 10 AM to 6 PM Tuesday through Friday and 11 AM to 5 PM Saturday. 4727 N.E. Fremont St.; 503-282-1330.
