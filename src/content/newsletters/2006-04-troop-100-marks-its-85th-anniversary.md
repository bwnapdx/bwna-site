Charter applied for on February 23, 1921, approved on April 21, 1921 – Troop 100 has been in continuous operation ever since. Through the years, countless young men and volunteer adults in the Alameda, Beaumont, Hollywood, Irvington and other neighborhoods of northeast Portland have been members of Troop 100.

Please join us to celebrate and reminisce. Everyone is invited to the
85th Anniversary Open House
Tuesday, April 11, 2006, 7 p.m.
Fremont United Methodist Church
Fellowship Hall (basement) 2620 NE Fremont St.
