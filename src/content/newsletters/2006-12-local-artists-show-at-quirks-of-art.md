Beaumont Wilshire artists will be exhibiting work December 1–30 at Quirks of Art, 3602 NE 40th Ave (south of Sandy Blvd next to the A-Boy parking lot). *Artist Reception* on two days, **Saturday Dec. 9, 4–7PM** will be a wine tasting and **Sunday Dec 10, 1-4PM** will be a cheese tasting.

The featured artists include Cathy Brown, Keith Buckley, Mario Caoile, Rita Catapano, Barry Frankel, Adrienne Fritz, Brita Mae Gould, Nancy Larson, Jude Moonbeam, Carol Sands, Suzie Spaggiari and Pauline Dugas Tait. For more information go to:

http://www.bwna.org/artspace/news.html
