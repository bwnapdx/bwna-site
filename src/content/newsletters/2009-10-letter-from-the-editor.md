As I walked home recently from the Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) Board Meeting, I listened closely to the sounds of our neighborhood.  In one house, the sound of laughter from friends and family gathered around the dining table. In another, the tones from a large wind chime set free from a fall breeze. And at another, the unmistakable sound of a dogs bark to guard the residence. In each issue of this newsletter, we bring you ample visual interest so that you know what is happening around our city and community. As you enjoy this issue of the newsletter, consider the sounds associated with the Wilshire-Riverside Little League game on page two, or the BWNA Annual Neighborhood Night Out on page seven. These are the sounds of your community. The sounds of our neighborhood represent comfort and security to me. What do they mean to you? This newsletter is more than just words and images. This newsletter is created, published and delivered from a team of more than 40 volunteers, and each issue is hand delivered to over 3000 residents and businesses every other month. Starting in 2010, the newsletter will become bi-monthly consistent with the calendar year (i.e. Jan/Feb, Mar/Apr, etc.) to inform our neighbors in advance of upcoming events like the Taste of Beaumont each June. Just as John Carson remembers the passing of his parents on page five and the contributions they have made to our community, I challenge each of you to consider where and how you spend your time and what noise creates your since of community. I also believe that giving back to your community is not an option, it is a necessity, and it is the necessity that requires each of us to take action. As you walk our neighborhood, spend time in our parks and chat with our local merchants, please remember to listen. As my Grandmother told many years ago, “you have two ears and one month and you should use them in that order”. Grandma is now 95, and a great listener. Have a wonderful holiday season.
