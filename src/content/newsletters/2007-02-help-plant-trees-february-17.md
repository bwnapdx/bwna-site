On Saturday, February 17th, volunteers from the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood and Friends of Trees will plant street trees throughout the neighborhood.

Property owners paid just $ 75 per tree, a significant discount under the $150 tree cost. Along with adding beauty to the neighborhood, these trees will help reduce storm water runoff, sequester carbon dioxide and provide shady sidewalks in the summer. If you would like to help plant trees, meet at Wilshire United Methodist Church, 3917 NE Shaver St at 8:30am on February 17th.

This event is a wonderful way to meet your neighbors, support Friends of Trees and contribute to the ongoing improvement of the neighborhood.

Trees can no longer be added to this planting, but if you’d like to have a tree planted in front of your house next winter, visit www.friendsoftrees.org this summer for additional information.
