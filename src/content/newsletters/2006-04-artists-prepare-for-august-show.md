Artspace artists met at Foxfire Teas in February to plan a group show to coincide with the annual Fremont Fest which takes place each year on the first Saturday of August. Artspace is the online art gallery of artists who live in the BW neighborhood. If you are interested in joining, please check the guidelines for submission of works on the neighborhood website www.bwna.org/artspace. New artists are welcome.
