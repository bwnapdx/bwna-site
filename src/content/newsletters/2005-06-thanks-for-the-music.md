As Heidi mentioned above, the second Taste of Beaumont, which took place on April 11 at the BWNA general meeting was a big success because of the delicious treats provided by the following neighborhood businesses: **Leaf and Bean, Staniches. Round Table Pizza, Alameda Café, Alameda Brewhouse, Parisis, Criollo** and **Java Man**. Select members of the Jazz Band at Beaumont Middle School provided music for the event. To view photos from the event go to www.bwna.org click on Photos and ‘Taste of Beaumont.”
