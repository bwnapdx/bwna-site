This street party will be Sunday August 29th. Alberta street will be closed between The Spare Room and US Bank with a beer garden and food vendors from NE 42nd Avenue businesses. There will also be a Classic Car Rally and Contest as well as great blues music with Duffy Bishop headlining with the Ladies of the Blues from 2pm-10pm. All proceeds will benefit breast cancer. Together we can make a difference! For more information, please contact Will Levenson (Popina Swimwear) at 503.282.5159.
