Trilliums, ferns, Oregon iris and more! Spruce up your garden with Oregon native plants. Fremont United Methodist Church Youth are hosting a native plant sale on Saturday April 26, 9 am to 4 pm through Boskey Dell Natives, Inc. (www.boskeydellnatives.com). Proceeds support the Youth on their summer mission projects. Fremont UMC is located at 2620 NE Fremont St., 503.284.4647, www.fremontumc.org
