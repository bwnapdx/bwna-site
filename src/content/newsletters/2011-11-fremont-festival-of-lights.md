*Friday - December 9th, 5:00pm - 9:00pm*

What better way to kick off the holiday season! Join the festivities at our 2nd Annual Lighting of the Trees in Beaumont Village Bring out the whole family to an evening of holiday cheer and enchantment!

**Don’t miss Santa’s trip down NE Fremont from 52nd to 41st on our own Portland City Fire Truck! Arriving at Foot Traffic Court Yard**

We’ve added three venue tents for your enjoyment in addition to:

**Dickens Christmas Carolers

Innisfree Celtic Band**
