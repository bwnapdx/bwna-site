“It was uplifting” , said Benny Dockery. It was “mind boggling” said Henry Houghton. “I can’t believe we’re going to state”, said player Carson Running. These were a couple of player reactions to the success of this year’s Wilshire-Riverside Little League All Star team. For the first time since 1984, Wilshire-Riverside won the Oregon District #1 Championship Tournament. The tournament held in early July was hosted by Scappoose Little League. Wilshire-Riverside went unbeaten, knocking off Rose City, Hollywood, St Helens, and finally Parkrose.

Most of the players on this year’s team began play at the T-Ball level. A combination of natural talent, hard work during practice, parental support, and the support of volunteer coaches over the years can all be attributed to this year’s success.

The District win qualified the team for the Oregon State Tournament held in Eugene. In Eugene, the team went 1-2 against strong competition, with a victory over Pendleton, and losses to Parrish and Klamath Falls. Congratulations to Parrish for winning State and continuing on to the Western Regional Tournament in San Bernardino, California.

This year Wilshire merged with Riverside to become Wilshire-Riverside. The combined league serves over 500 players ranging from T-Ball on up to Juniors. Games are played at Riverside Field, Lakeside Field, Beaumont Middle School, and Wilshire Park. The league’s website can be found here: http://wilshirelittleleague.org/.

Hats off to the fellas for giving  their best, and to all of the volunteers who helped make this achievement possible!
