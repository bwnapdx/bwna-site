**BWNA General Meeting**
**Monday, June 14, 7:30 PM**
**Bethany Lutheran Church, NE 37th & Skidmore**

Nine neighbors met at the Alameda Brew Pub last month to discuss land use and transportation issues in Beaumont Wilshire. The impetus behind the meeting was to develop a list of neighborhood issues around bike and pedestrian safety, fluid auto movement and the desire for a financially viable business district along Fremont. The list was presented to Debbie Bishoff, the Bureau of Planning’s new NE Portland District Liaison Officer. Debbie will speak at the June 14 meeting about her new position, and will describe the connection between the Bureau of Planning and our neighborhood, and the methods for working with the Bureau to accomplish the goals we have for our neighborhood. She will be joined by Sandra Lefrancois, the neighborhood involvement specialist for the Central NE Neighborhoods.
