For the first time ever, please join us as we celebrate the wonderful restaurants in our neighborhood, both new and old! At our next general meeting, owners and managers will be talking about their menus and sharing samples. This is a great opportunity to try out cuisine from restaurants for ***free***, and learn about what our neighborhood has to offer. We will meet at a special time for this month only at **6:30pm**. *Free childcare will be provided.*

> **BWNA General Meeting**
> ***A Taste of Beaumont Wilshire***
> **Monday, April 14, 6:30 – 8 PM**
> **Bethany Lutheran Church**
> **NE 37th & Skidmore**

Some of the treats that will be provided include pizza from Pizzicato and Alameda Bars from the Alameda Café. Mark your calendars now, and don’t miss this event! Here is a list of some of the participating restaurants:

*Alameda Baking Company*
*Alameda Brewing Company*
*Alameda Café*
*Beaumont Bar and Grill*
*Cool Runnings*
*Fife*
*Pizzicato*
*Savory*
*Suzanne’s*
*and more!*
