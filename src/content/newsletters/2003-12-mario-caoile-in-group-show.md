Beaumont Wilshire resident and artist, Mario Caoile, will be one of many artists participating in the Mark Woolley Gallery’s 10 year anniversary show, “10 years: One Foot After Another”. The show runs from December 2, 2003 to January 31, 2004. The gallery is located at 120 NW 9th Suite 210 Portland 97209.

Openings will be on Wed. Dec 3, 6pm to 10pm, preview party, and First Thursday Dec. 4, 6-10pm. Over 100 artists will be participating in this great celebration of the gallery’s 10-year anniversary. As described in the Oregonian, “If there’s one gallery in town to expect to see the unexpected, it’s the Mark Woolley Gallery”.
