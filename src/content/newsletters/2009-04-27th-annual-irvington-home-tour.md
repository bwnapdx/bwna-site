Each year, on the 3rd Sunday in May, a thousand plus people flock to North East Portland to par take of one of the city’s premier events—The Irvington Home Tour. Founded in 1967 when the residents of Irvington were faced with the continuing demolishment of the areas historic houses, this very first “tour of homes” that Portland was to experience led to the coalescing of a neighborhood and the awakening of the value of preserving the grace and the grandeur, as well as the humility and the heart of the past.

Once again this spring Irvington opens its doors and celebrates the importance of the rich legacy of historic neighborhoods with eight homes of architectural distinction offered to tour, inviting visitors to see for themselves how the past informs the present and provides direction for the future. In addition, a lecture on home restoration will be held in the afternoon at the Central Lutheran Church-(Lecture details available on the IHT web site below).

The 27th Annual Historic Irvington Home Tour will be held on Sunday, May 17th, 2009 from 11am to 5pm. The tour ticket price is $20 with proceeds going to fund local charities, programs and neighborhood projects.

Tickets will be on sale after April 1st directly from the web site at www.IrvingtonHomeTour.com, as well as many local merchants after April 10th like Broadway Books, Caffe’ Destino, New Seasons Market as well as the The Architectural Heritage Center.
