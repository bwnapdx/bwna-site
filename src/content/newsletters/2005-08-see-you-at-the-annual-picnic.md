Summer is in full swing. Although one wouldn’t know it from the rainy weather, I can tell because of carting my son between summer camps some of which are half day and squeezing work in between the time spent shuttling. There are lots of opportunities for our children in the summer many of which are in Beaumont-Wilshire such as sport camps in Wilshire park and summer camps at the neighborhood churches.

Join us for the Neighbor’s Night Out picnic in Wilshire Park on Tuesday, August 2 from 6:30 until 8:30 PM. We’ll have a fire truck, local police, raffle give-aways, and free hot dogs, hamburgers, garden burgers, and beverages. Bring a side dish or dessert. It’s always loads of fun. At our last general meeting, we heard proposals for the $1,000 we have decided to put to use in the neighborhood. We’ll be announcing the winner or winners of the $1,000 for a neighborhood project at the Neighbor’s Night Out picnic. Thanks to those who volunteered to help organize the picnic. For those who can make it a bit early or stay a bit late, help will also be appreciated with the set-up and tear down for the picnic.
