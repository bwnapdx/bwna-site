WB Wells and Associates, Inc. is a long time neighborhood business that specializes in surveying, engineering and land planning for both residential and commercial customers. They have been located in the 2-story building at 4230 NE Fremont Street for the last twenty-seven years. The thirty-four employees working at WB Wells and Associates provide a range of services with a major emphasis on surveying work. As the survey crews leave the parking lot each day they may be on their way to work on a residential lot partition, boundary survey, topographic and tree survey, a mortgage foundation inspection or perhaps a parking lot layout for a new commercial development. The professional surveyors, civil engineers, land planners and support staff who remain behind design roads, sanitary systems, retaining walls, plats and and draft plans using electronic data crews bring back from the field and input from City and County planning staff.

Willard B. Wells first established his surveying and engineering business in 1924 so the business has been in operation for over 80 years. With a track record like that, they must provide a good product at a fair price. If you are interested in having some surveying or engineering work done, you should contact your neighbors at WB Wells and Associates. For information, call or drop by 4230 NE Fremont, 503-284-5896, website: www.wbwells.com.
