April is election time for Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association. We have a number of board positions open, and I urge you to consider becoming involved in your neighborhood association! This is a great opportunity to help shape the future of your neighborhood and a good way to learn about and participate in interesting projects related to livability, land use, public safety, transportation, parks, school activities, tree planting, etc. Board members serve two-year terms and meet monthly on the second Monday. At the general meeting on April 14 we will elect a president, treasurer, and five board members-at-large.

I have enjoyed the opportunity to serve as BWNA ‘acting president’ this past year, and wish to thank all of those who have worked faithfully throughout the year to help make this great neighborhood what it is. Please join us April 14 at 7:00pm in the Beaumont Middle School cafeteria for our general meeting and elections and for the ‘Taste of Beaumont’ (see more information in this newsletter) and to enjoy music provided by the Beaumont Middle School Band.

Looking forward to seeing you at the April 14 meeting!

–*Bill Markwart*
Acting President, BWNA
president@bwna.org
