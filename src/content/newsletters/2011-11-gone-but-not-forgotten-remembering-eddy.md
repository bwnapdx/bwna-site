My husband saved a news segment on the DVR last Friday night for me. When I came home, he said “ I recorded something I know you’ll want to see. He was right, it was about a cold case – a segment about Eddy. You may recall Eddy “the weatherman” Morgan. A retired homicide detective is reviewing his unsolved murder case. The news segment interviewed the owner of the Wilshire Tavern. In the interview Shar Fisher said Eddy was trustworthy, harmless and child-like. If you needed help, he was always there to help you.

If you lived in our neighborhood during the 60’s to early 90’s there isn’t any way you can’t be familiar with Eddy. Eddy was someone special in our neighborhood, mentally challenged and perfectly innocent. Eddy’s fixation was the weather, all the local weathermen knew him. During the late 70’s and 80’s he would walk with determination up and down 42nd Street between Holman and Hollywood with a huge smile on his face reporting the weather that was on the way to whoever would listen. He was known in every business along 42nd and would boldly come in and tell you the weather. On Memorial Day in 1994, a car pulled up and someone inside shot Eddy for no reason. He died right where he fell on 42nd and Alberta. It had to be a random killing, a drive-by is what it is referred to these days. Our neighborhood was beside itself. There’s a small memorial plaque that marks where he died. Eddy will be in our hearts forever. I feel a sense of gratitude to the retired detective who hasn’t forgotten Eddy either.
