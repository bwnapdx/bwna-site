My husband Mario and I have been the editor/publisher of the BWNA newsletter for many years. We feel it is our time to ‘step down’ and give others the oppotunity to take on this task.

Board member Dan Johnson has volunteered to take on the duties of editor. He will need someone who has page layout and publishing skills to assist him. The software we use is Adobe Indesign for page layout and Photoshop for managing images. The newsletter comes every two months and we spend about 15–20 hours getting it ready to go to press.

If you have these skills and are interested in getting involved in putting out this outreach tool to the neighborhood, contact Mario at 503-288-2568.
