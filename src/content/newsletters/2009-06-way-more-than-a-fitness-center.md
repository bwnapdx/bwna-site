The little community center that could, the Northeast Community Center in Hollywood, is now four years old and still gathering steam. Started by a group of volunteers who formed a nonprofit, the NECC took over the building that originally served as the Northeast YMCA for 80 years.

The big yellow building just west of Starbucks was extensively renovated by volunteers and donors who wanted to save the facility and maintain its longtime heritage of service to the community. That heritage is now being expanded. The NECC has developed new programs and classes that go far beyond the fitness activities once offered by the YMCA.

Beyond swimming, basketball, volleyball, circuit training and aerobics, the NECC offers other programs such as dance classes, arts and crafts, film production, guitar lessons, chess and book clubs, after school programs for kids, and hikes and travel adventures for active older adults. There’s something for everyone at the NECC, including child care.

In these tough economic times, the NECC has struggled like other organizations. Still, there is a vibrant spirit in the building that is evident the minute you walk in the door. People come here for exercise and to work out, but also to meet friends and share good times together. Their mantra: Smile, laugh, exercise – repeat daily.

For more information on programs and classes, please contact the NECC at 503.284.3377. Or visit www.necommunitycenter.org. The Northeast Community Center is located at 1630 NE 38th Avenue. Stop by for a tour and ask for a Program booklet.
