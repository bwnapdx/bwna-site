Get your car washed and help Grant High junior class students raise funds. **Saturday, Sept, 18,2004** at the Chevron Station on NE 47th and Sandy Blvd. The class of 2006 hopes to raise as much money as possible during the coming school year and the next school year to go towards their senior prom. By doing a car wash early in the summer we hope to get us out there early, (before school even begins) to let people know what the Grant kids are about.
