BWNA resident John Wolfe, a social work manager by day and fiction writer by night, has published a novel for young adult readers. In *Night of The Moray Eel: A Novel in Short Stories* (Publish America), Keoni (kee-o-nee), an African American child, takes the reader, along with his family and friends, on an island journey of adventure, danger, and romance with plenty of life lessons. From a mysterious discovery of old Hawaii, to a close encounter with a shark, from being chased out of a rainforest by a swarm of bees, to a last-second shot to try to win the game and keep the girl, Keoni discovers the value of family, friendship and a lesson to listen to his young heart. This exhilarating novel is filled with excitement in every chapter.

Targeted for youth age 12-18, *Night of the Moray Eel* is available at The Children’s Place and Broadway Books. On Saturday, January 5th at 11:00 am John Wolfe will present a reading of one of his stories at The Children’s Place, 4807 NE Fremont. Come and support your local author!
