Are you looking for an opportunity to get involved to make your neighborhood an even better place to live? Well, good news! The Transportation and Land Use Committee is looking for new members to help guide transportation and development decisions in our neighborhood. We meet once a month on the **3rd Monday of the month at 7:30 PM**.

Since our last meeting place recently closed, we are in the process of trying out a new location. For the next few months, we will meet at Vicinato, which is a new wine bar that recently opened across from Green Dog Pet Supply in the building at **4605 NE Fremont St**. It is a very comfortable environment and the owners are excited about us meeting there.

The committee is currently working on a host of exciting issues to improve the neighborhood and our region. Please join us at our next meeting to find out how you can get involved in making the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood and even better place to live.
