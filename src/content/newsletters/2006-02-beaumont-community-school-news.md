Thanks to the generous donation of the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association to Beaumont Middle School, our kids now are enjoying fruit snacks to "curb that after-school hunger" before they attend their favorite activity.

If any BWNA member would like to continue feeding our hungry after-school kids, they can bring boxes of fruit snacks (in individual packets so we don't have to get a food handlers license) to the Beaumont Middle School office 9:00 am – 4:30 pm.

For Winter Term at Beaumont SUN School I have opened up all of my evening classes to families to include middle schoolers if they would like to join their parents. We have Yoga on Mon., Tae Kwon Do (martial arts) on Tues., Rag Rug Crafting on Wed. and Fencing (all gear provided) on Thurs. All classes begin the week of Jan. 9 – 12 and all you have to do is call Donna Bestwick 503-916-5615 to register.
