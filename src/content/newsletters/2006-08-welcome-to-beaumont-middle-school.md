BWNA has decided to hold its general meetings at the Middle School ***beginning October 2006***. We are glad to share our facilities with the neighborhood and look forward to having you meet here. I plan to attend several of the meetings to visit with you, share what our school is doing, and learn more about BWNA.

One of our goals the past three years was to have our students create permanent art projects that would enliven our walls. We will share their work with you at our first meeting or you can drop in anytime for a tour.

When I visited with you last fall, I told you about the mentoring program that we were beginning. I am thrilled to be able to report that we had over 40 adults who mentored students all or part of last year. The purpose of the program was to help close the achievement gap between our European American and African American students. Although we did not completely close this gap, we did make good progress. Not only did the students benefit from the mentoring experience, the mentors were extremely positive about their experiences. We will continue the program next fall and there is definitely a place for more volunteers!

You may have heard about the many reorganizations and closures taking place in Portland Public Schools.
