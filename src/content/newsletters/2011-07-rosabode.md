Rose Bonomo opened her shop, rosabode, in 2005 after a bout with cancer. She wanted to provide women with products that make them feel good, and feel good about themselves. “Since my brush with adversity, creating things that comfort people became a directive. I am certain that being soothed is part of the healing process,” says Rose. Although everything within the store is functional and easy to care for, Rose provides her customers with clothes and accessories that break away from the mundane and provide style for any occasion. Part of what sets rosabode apart is the service experience and individual attention customers receive. Rose aims to help her customers break slightly out of their comfort zone towards fun and stylish clothing that makes them feel as great as they look.

Owning her own business allows the store to grow along with Rose; as she morphs and changes, so does rosabode. Initially selling high-style throws and baby blankets (still top sellers), the store has grown to include clothes, jewelry, and items for the home. rosabode has been able to expand its line of products in part due to its strong customer base. “The people in our neighborhood are so loyal; they really appreciate and support small business owners.” Rose also appreciates the sense of camaraderie shared among the local businesses. “We are all very supportive of each other,” she says.

Originally from the east coast, Rose moved to Portland in 1993 and has raised two children here. Besides running her business, she is active in the community and regularly does volunteer work.

rosabode is located on Fremont at 42nd (4225 NE Fremont Street). Please visit the website for hours of operation and additional information (rosabode.com) or call 503.493.9391.
