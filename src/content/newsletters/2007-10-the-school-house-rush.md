The Transportation and Land Use Committee is beginning to work with neighbors along NE 37th to address their concerns about vehicle speeding along the street and also the high number of vehicles that travel down the street each day. There have been a number of incidents during the past year where speeding vehicles have hit parked cars.

Now that school is back in session, these neighbors are seeing an increase in the number of vehicles driving along the street. I ride my bike to work each day along NE 37th and a few of the other streets in the vicinity of Beaumont School and am amazed by how many drivers speed down these streets in order to deliver their children to school on time.

It puts a smile on my face to see all of the kids that are walking or biking to school and having such a good time along the way. However, there is concern about their safety around somewhat frenzied parents that are speeding to get their kids to school. I strongly encourage all of you that are driving around the school to slow down and even drive slower than the speed limit. I know it’s hard, but you can do it.

Isn’t it great to live in such a wonderful neighborhood!
