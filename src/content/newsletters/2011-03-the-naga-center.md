The Naga Center is a small school of Traditional Thai Massage and Medicine as well as a community Thai massage clinic, located in the Beaumont neighborhood at 44th and Fremont. Thai bodywork is an integral component of the ancient system of traditional Thai medicine. As such, it differs vastly from the western, or Swedish, massage that is commonly known in the U.S. Thai massage is performed on a fully clothed client on a mat on the floor, and is a deeply therapeutic, intensely physical form of bodywork that employs deep compression, point work, passive stretching (like having someone do yoga to you), hot herbal compresses, balms and liniments, percussive beating, fire cupping, and many other tools that encourage healing, opening and soft tissue release.

In addition to teaching Thai massage on a uniquely in-depth level, The Naga Center serves as a clinic where Thai massage is performed by therapists who have studied extensively both at The Naga Center and in Thailand. The clinic offers massage in the traditional manner of clinics in Thailand; a large open room, with several mats, where multiple massage sessions can occur in the same space. In the U.S. massage is generally experienced in small private rooms in a sedative atmosphere. Conversely, in Thailand, massage is expected to be about intensive healing in a social environment.

Every month The Naga Center hosts sliding scale massage clinics for those who may otherwise be unable to schedule sessions, as well as donation based open houses that raise money for local and Thailand-based charities. Those interested in classes or Thai bodywork treatments can find out about open houses, sliding scale clinics, flash specials, Thai movie night and more by visiting their website nagacenter.org, following them on Twitter, or checking out their Facebook page.
