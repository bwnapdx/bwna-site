Share your love of reading. Become an OASIS Reading Tutor. No experience is necessary. Free training, supplies and monthly tutor support meetings are provided.

Work one-on-one with a child grade 1-4 in a participating school. Make a difference in the life of a child. New tutor training will be held in Fall 2004. For more information call, Colleen 503-833-3636.

OASIS is a national non-profit education center for adults 50 years and over. The tutor program celebrates 15 years this fall.
