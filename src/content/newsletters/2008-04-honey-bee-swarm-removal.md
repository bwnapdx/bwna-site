The old trees in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood are home to perhaps hundreds of feral honey bee nests. Every spring, most of these nests divide themselves in two when the old queen bee leaves along with thousands of her offspring. She typically lands on a nearby branch or mailbox *(see photo)*, where she is then surrounded by all the other bees. This is called a swarm.

Although swarms are not very dangerous, you still may wonder what to do if you find one on your property. One option is to call a local beekeeper who will remove the swarm without killing them and take the bees to an apiary, where they will continue to live out their gentle pollinating lives.
