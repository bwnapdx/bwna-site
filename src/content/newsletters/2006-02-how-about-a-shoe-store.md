That was the suggestion to Amenity Shoes owner Liz Page from Robin Ady of neighboring shop Gazelle when pondering the type of business to launch in the Beaumont Wilshire district. Liz had been a frequent customer along Fremont and knew she would love to open a business in the neighborhood. She and husband Zaki Hafid live nearby and were thrilled to learn of the potential vacancy in the Dutch Village Building.

After weeks of discussion with landlord John Rumpakis and considerable research, Liz opened Amenity Shoes at 3430 NE 41 Ave. in June 2005. As the name implies, Amenity Shoes offers an array of shoes and accessories with excellent service. Liz's goal is to offer stylish and attractive (not necessarily trendy), yet comfortable shoes at a range of prices for both women and men. Some of the brands include Pikolinos (a gorgeous line of women's shoes and boots from Spain), Naot, Keen, and Rogue (a new line designed in Lake Oswego). In addition, Amenity Shoes carries accessories including jewelry, handbags, wallets, scarves and more. Most of accessories are designed and made by northwest artisans.

Once she feels a bit more established, Liz might think about expansion to another part of town, or even designing her own line of shoes. Liz is an artist and jewelry designer. She studied metal smithing in college and has operated Liz Page Designs, her own jewelry company, for the past eight years. For now, however, Liz is pleased with how things are going and is happy to be in a neighborhood with other unique independently owned businesses.

If you have not yet visited Amenity Shoes, stop in and feast your eyes and treat your feet to some fine footwear. 3430 NE 41 Ave (just south of Fremont). Store hours are Monday – Friday 10am – 7pm, Saturday 10am – 6pm., and Sunday 11am – 5pm.
