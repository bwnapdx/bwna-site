***Dear Editor,***

Please put this letter in the BW newsletter. This is to acknowledge the two young men Shawn and Beau. They are employees of Foot Traffic on NE Fremont at 42nd. I was shopping in the store and I dropped a bank envelope with my cash withdrawal. I wasn’t aware of my loss until I received a call from one of them. He told me he found some money he thought belonged to me at Foot Traffic. They had to search through my Starlight Race application form to find my name and phone number but they tracked me down! So again, thank you guys for being so honest and thoughtful.

Eddie Querner
NE 47th Ave.

Foot Traffic is located at 4020 NE Fremont.
—*editor*
