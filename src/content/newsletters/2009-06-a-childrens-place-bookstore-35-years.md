There is one bookstore in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood that is truly unique - it is Portland's only independently owned children's bookstore.

A Children's Place Bookstore, located at 4807 NE Fremont, will be celebrating 35 years of "raising readers" in June 2009.

It opened in 1974 on NE Broadway in a small storefront space. Its present location on NE Fremont is spacious and filled to the brim with books for kids aged from newborn to young adult. Who of us does not remember the first book we 'read' or our first trip to a place with lots of books, such as the library or a bookstore. The current owner, Pam Erlandson, and her knowledgeable staff are eager to help young and old readers find the right book for themselves or for a child.

A Children's Place also has a section for "Mom & Dad" books. In addition they carry parenting and teacher resource books, children's music, toys, games, and cards. Check their website for hours and for upcoming events.
