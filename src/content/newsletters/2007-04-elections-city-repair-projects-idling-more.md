**Monday, April 9th, 7:30 PM**
*Beaumont Middle School*
NE 42nd & Fremont

In April BWNA holds board elections. The vice president, secretary and a few at-large board positions are open. If you are ready to ‘step up to the plate’ and help make BW even a better neighborhood, come to the Aril 9th meeting and get involved!

**Ankist Zadeyan** will brief us on the City Repair Village Building Convergence projects in BW. One of the three projects is the 37th and Shaver Intersection Repair which will transform a high-use intersection by autos and pedestrians into a ‘user friendly’ intersection by installing street and sidewalk art, planter boxes with native plants and flowers, and a kiosk for neighborhood info. Volunteers are needed for the three projects. *(See page 2 for more)*

**Brian Heron** will share his experience of having a positive adult in his life and the rewards of being a foster parent. ‘One positive adult relationship is all it takes’ says Brian whose mother abandoned his family when he was three. He is with the Foster Family Care Network.

**Donna Green** will discuss the unpleasant affects of vehicle idling on our health. *(page 6 for more)*

**Jillian Detweiler** will speak on the City Charter Review proposal.
