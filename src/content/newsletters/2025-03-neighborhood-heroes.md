Last fall, Beaumont-Wilshire neighbors Dennis and Carol Breeling were caught in the act of doing good work by another B-W resident, Jeff Littman, who thought (rightfully so) that they deserved a spot in the newsletter.

On that fateful fall day, the duo cleared the street gutters and drain for the whole block. Neighbors helping neighbors, for sure.

Thank you, Dennis and Carol, for your generosity of time and spirit. You are indeed neighborhood heroes! (Photos by Jeff Littman.)
