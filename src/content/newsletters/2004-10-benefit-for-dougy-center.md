A benefit matinee showing of the classic “A Christmas Story” will be sponsored by Portland area businesses on Sunday, **November 14**. The Dougy Center for Grieving Children and Teens will receive all of the proceeds from this one-time showing, beginning at 2:00 p.m. in the Hollywood Theater. Brian Faherty and Lance Marrs of Hasson Company Real Estate are co-chairs and main sponsors of this afternoon event.

Tickets are $20 a piece or $50 for a family and may be obtained by calling 503-473-8707.
