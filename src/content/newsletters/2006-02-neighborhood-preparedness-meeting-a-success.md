On Saturday, November 12, the Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency Team (NET) held a well-attended presentation for the general public. Team member, Erika Wolfe Burr led the discussion on disaster preparedness with topics that included:

- Easy steps to prepare a 72-hour kit,
- The importance of having out-of-state contacts,
- Sample household escape route plans,
- The role of firefighters, law enforcement, and city officials in the event of an emergency, and
- General tips on how to ready our families and houses in the event of an earthquake, flood, ice storm, or other disaster.

A team of firefighters from Station 14 also spoke during the session, reinforcing the importance of taking on emergency preparedness as a personal goal and answering any questions that came in from the crowd.

Are you prepared? In the event of an emergency, citizens like us could be on our own for up to 72 hours while the authorities respond to the most serious situations. Visit www.redcross.org or www.fema.gov to learn how you can better prepare yourself and your family.
