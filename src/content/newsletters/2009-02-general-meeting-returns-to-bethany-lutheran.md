Over the past year or so, the BWNA Board has experimented with conducting General Meetings--which are held on the second Monday of even-numbered months (except August)--at Beaumont Middle School. It was reasoned that the school is both centrally located and a valued institution of learning in the neighborhood. What was not anticipated, however, were unexpected changes in room assignments, room arrangements, and, occasionally, inadequate seating.

Consequently, at the November Board meeting, the vote was unanimous to return General Meetings to their previous venue--the basement activity room at Bethany Lutheran Church--beginning with the February 9th meeting. A sign will be set up on the church lawn as a reminder to residents of the meeting, and arrows will indicate the walkway leading to the activity room, which is on the Skidmore side of the church. (Board meetings will continue to be held at the church in a conference room--best accessed from 37th Ave.--on the second Monday of odd-numbered months.) The BWNA Board thanks the Beaumont Middle School administration, staff, and custodial crew for their hospitality and assistance during the Association’s use of their building.

Our General Meeting begins at 7:30 pm and will shine the spotlight on two entirely different, but quite timely topics for neighborhood residents: 1.) the concerns about Coyotes and other wild animals roaming our neighborhood; and 2.) NE Portland GED and mentoring services available for student dropouts. Addressing the issue of wild animals in urban residential areas will be experts Kevin Christiansen and Mike Foster from the USDA, and explaining how young dropouts can find the help they need to carry on and hopefully succeed in life will be Madeline Lockwood, AmeriCorps Member for the nonprofit Youth Employment Institute. Also on the agenda will be a question-and-answer session with local police officers, reports from committee chairs on BWNA activities, and suggested projects or programs for the Board to consider at its March meeting. Come join your fellow neighbors for an interesting and informative evening of give and take.
