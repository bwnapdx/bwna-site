On Saturday April 23 from 11am to 2pm at the Fremont Court property at the Corner of NE 41st and Fremont, there will be a youth run & walk to the Northeast Emergency Food Program located at 4800 NE 72nd. The youth groups will go straight up Fremont on the sidewalks to 72nd and turn left for 8 blocks to the food bank location. They will drop off $2 or 2 cans of food and return to Foot Traffic for a party. The party will feature a band, free pizza and hot dogs and fruit and drinks. Youth groups from throughout the city are invited.

**Sports Teams, Church Groups, Chess Clubs: All Groups Welcome.**

The sponsors include:

- Adventure Boot Camp for Women
- Albina Community Bank
- Foot Traffic
- Mountain Shop
- Papa Murphy’s
- Pilot Wealth Management
- Pride Financial Partners
- Rip City Skate
- Therapeutic Associates
