**Red Lodge Transition Services Native American Prisoner Art Show**

Northeast Community Center
1630 NE 38th Ave
Portland, OR 97232

The 2009 Native American Prison Art show is opening in Portland on April 1, 2009 and runs, through April 30, 2009. Each Sunday from 1-4 there will be Native American speakers, drumming and Red Lodge community members there to share information about Red Lodge. Over 50 artists, whose work will be displayed, are incarcerated in Oregon prisons. They have donated their artwork in support of the Women’s Transition House Fund. Nine Oregon prisons have participated in this project. Specialty crafted artifacts made within the prisons will be offered for educational and display purposes. Approximately 100 pieces of original art will be displayed for over a five week period. Reproductions of the art work will be offered at affordable prices. Admission is free of charge. This is a great family opportunity to experience Native American culture.
