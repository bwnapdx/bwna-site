Urban coyotes are all around us. Researchers at Portland State and Alameda Elementary are beginning a study of these coyotes and would welcome your help. If you see a coyote, please report the sighting and share your stories at browerb@pdx.edu . For more information www.projectcoyote.com. You should also report it to the Audubon Society of Portland at 503.292.0304 or bsallinger@audubonportland.org.
