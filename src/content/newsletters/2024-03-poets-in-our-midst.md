## The Powers That Be
*by Chris Rauber*

Portland is run by crows.
They strut, jump, jabber,
convene, converse, cavort

and famously swoop.

More effectively than the City Council they patrol the streets,
digest trash

Scare off owls and other predators, make decisions on the fly.

Debate is frequent
and never behind closed doors.

Like other city leaders, though,
they are known to squawk

At times too frequently.
