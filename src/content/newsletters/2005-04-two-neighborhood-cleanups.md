**Cully Neighborhood Cleanup**
**Saturday, April 30,** 9am to 4pm at Trinity Lutheran Church 5520 NE Killingsworth. $5 per car, $10 per van and $15 per truck.

**Concordia Neighborhood Cleanup**
**Saturday, May 7**, 8am to 12:30pm at Whitaker School Parking Lot, corner of NE Jessup and NE 39. $25 per pickup truck or trailer load, $10 per car load.

Accepting metal, plastic, furniture, yard debris, appliances, clothes, lamps, old computers and other “stuff” to the cleanup drop boxes in the parking lot.

*Please no household or food waste. No hazardous waste, tires, batteries, paint or oil. An informational flyer will be available at the cleanup with the closest Metro locations for proper disposal of those items.*
