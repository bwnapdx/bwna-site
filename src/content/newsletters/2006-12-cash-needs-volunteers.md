CASH (Creating Assets, Savings, and Hope) is a community partnership of government and non-profit agencies dedicated to connecting low-income families with the tax benefits they deserve. CASH is currently seeking volunteer tax preparers for the up-coming tax season. Anyone can volunteer; no experience is necessary, but computer skills are a plus. Volunteer tax preparers will receive free training on state and federal tax returns, as well as instruction on filing returns electronically. CASH is also seeking greeters, interpreters, and childcare attendants. Openings are available in Tax-Aide sites throughout the tri-county area. Training for the tax season starts soon!
