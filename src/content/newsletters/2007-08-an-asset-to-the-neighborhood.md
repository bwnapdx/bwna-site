Earlier this year I was driving by Justin’s Market on NE 42nd at Beech and I did a double-take. “What’s this?” I thought. The storefront just north of the market that had been boarded up for years was now an attractive business—an asset to the neighborhood!

Luis and Emily Urueta opened Community Appliances at 3746 NE 42nd in January of 2007. Their main service is buying used appliances with potential and refurbishing them, aesthetically and mechanically. Luis is highly skilled at this kind of work and has many years of experience at another appliance company before opening his own business. He also provides repair services on all household appliances and sells parts for major brand-name appliances.

Their intention is to provide a valuable service and also to do their part in improving the neighborhood. You will find quality refurbished refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers and washing machines at the store at reasonable prices. They also sell kitchen utensils, small household items and other gift items in the attractive store space.

Emily has lived in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood almost all her life. She and Luis met and married six years ago. Luis is originally from Mexico. They recently bought a house in the Concordia neighborhood.

Emily shared with me the mission statement of their business: *We are committed to improving our community by providing quality appliances and customer service and improving the environment by repairing and recycling.*
