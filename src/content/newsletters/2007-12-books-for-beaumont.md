Barnes & Noble at Lloyd Center will donate a percentage of sales on **Sunday, December 9, between noon and 5:00 PM**, to the Beaumont Middle School Foundation. Shoppers must mention Beaumont when they check out. Stock up on books, CDs, DVDs, calendars and more. You can even enjoy beverages and food from the in-store café—it all supports Beaumont Middle School. Beaumont students will be on hand with vocal and instrumental performances. There will be a few activities for children throughout the day too.
