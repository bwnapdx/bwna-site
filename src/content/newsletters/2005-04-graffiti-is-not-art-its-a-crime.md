Graffiti is more than just vandalism; it’s a property crime and a blight that sends a message that our neighborhood tolerates such destructive behavior. To be part of the solution check out the website: http: //www.portlandonline.com/oni (select the “Neighborhood Services” tab, then under the “Neighborhood Livability Services” section, click on “Graffiti Abatement”). It’s important to file a police report for the vandalism by calling Portland Police Bureau’s Graffiti Hotline: (503) 823-4TAG (4824). When taggers (vandals) are arrested, the police and district attorney use police reports to substantiate their criminal cases.

Everyone can join in the efforts to report and clean up graffiti in our neighborhood. Be sure to report the exact address of the building or give the street intersection if the graffiti is on a sign, traffic box or other property without a physical numeric address.

Thanks to Matt Steadman, Concordia neighborhood, and Marcia Dennis, Graffiti Abatement Coordinator, for the information in this article.
