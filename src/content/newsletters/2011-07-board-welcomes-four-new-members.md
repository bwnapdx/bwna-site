For the first time in several years, the neighborhood association has a full board with the addition of four new members who ran and were elected in April. They include a lawyer, a salesman, a land-use consultant and a computer technician. Three of them are new to the neighborhood, while one has lived in his Beaumont-Wilshire home for 24 years.

## Serah Breakstone

Breakstone, 37, moved to a house on Northeast Fremont Street in January with her husband Aaron from the Mount Tabor area because this area is more walkable and has good schools.

“This neighborhood is a lot more neighborly than where we were living before,” she said.

Breakstone is a project manager for a land-use consulting firm. She brings expertise on the city’s zoning code, permitting, transportation and public outreach. She said one of her goals is to find a balance between competing interests, including density and livability.

Breakstone is originally from Montana and lived for seven years in North Carolina before moving to Portland to earn a Master’s Degree in urban and regional planning from Portland State. She and her husband have a yellow Lab, but no children. She enjoys backpacking, hiking, cross-country skiing, gardening, biking and cooking.

## Larry Freeborg

Freeborg has lived near Wilshire Park for 24 years. He doesn’t have a specific agenda in joining the board, but said he wants to be an advocate for residents.

“I wanted to be able to be open to a lot of issues,” he said.

Freeborg works for Hewlett-Packard in technical services and also has a background in commercial printing.

Freeborg was born in Portland, but grew up in Yakima. He enjoys Beaumont-Wilshire because it’s quiet yet near a lot of things and good for walking.

Freeborg, 58, is married to Laurel, a nurse and professional musician. They have an adult daughter. He enjoys running, biking and skiing. About 25 years ago he got involved in Japanese flower arranging and now teaches and puts on shows.

## Michael Gray

Gray said he joined the board because he met so many friendly neighbors within a week of moving into his new home across from Beaumont Middle School in October and said he feels in tune with the neighborhood. Gray said his top priorities are schools and children. He volunteers as a mentor at St. Mary’s Home for Boys.

Gray, 34, was born and raised in the Portland area. He has worked in sales, most recently for Securitas, an international security company. He’s now between jobs and focused on improving his house.

He and his wife Sara got married a year ago in Italy. Gray enjoys sports, including basketball, tennis and running. He and Sara also enjoy traveling. They combined interests on their honeymoon, when they traveled to Paris and watched some of the French Open. They don’t have children, but a dog: Charlie.

## Alex Wall

Wall is the second lawyer on the board. He works as in-house counsel and project manager for discover-e, a company that processes data for litigation. He said his background will be helpful on municipal law and tech issues.

Wall, 34, is originally from Maine and lived in Manhattan before moving to Portland. He likes that the neighborhood seems to share the values of Maine and a bit of the cosmopolitan sense of Manhattan.

“I just got this sense of community that I liked,” he said.

Wall’s wife Marie grew up near NE 42nd and Klickitat. They bought a house on Fremont Street a year ago. They also have a dog, but no children.

As a bike commuter, Wall is concerned about transportation issues and cooperation between cars and bikes. In his spare time, Wall likes to play guitar, travel and do yard work. He also volunteers for a group that encourages environmental entrepreneurs and smart environmental policy.
