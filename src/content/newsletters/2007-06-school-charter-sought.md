A group of parents, educators and community members are applying for charter school status within the Portland Public School system for The Ivy School, a 1 – 8 grade Montessori elementary school. The school will be located in NE Portland and will open in September 2008 with 60 first to third graders. It will grow to a total enrollment of 240 students.

Montessori schools have a 100-year tradition of world-class education of allowing children to make their own educational choices in a thoughtfully prepared classroom environment. Students study math, language, physical education, science, art, gardening, and community and peace education. The Ivy School will also have a Spanish bilingual element in every classroom. Classrooms are multi-age, allowing students the natural opportunity to become mentors and leaders. There will be an informational meeting Tuesday, June 5th at 7pm at Augustana Lutheran Church – 2710 NE 14th Avenue. Childcare will be provided.
