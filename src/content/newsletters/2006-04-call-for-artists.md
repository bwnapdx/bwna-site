For the second year, neighborhood artists will show and sell their hand-crafted items from April - October (weather permitting) every First Friday on Fremont in the courtyard of Fremont Commons at 4605 NE Fremont, from 5 - 9 PM. If you are an artist with handmade work and are interested in joining, call Pauline Dugas Tait: (503) 281-2871
