Bethany Luheran Church will offer two services on **Easter Sunday April 20th**: **9:30am** Traditional, and **11:00am** Contemporary. Visitors are welcome! Children are invited to the Easter egg hunt following the 11am service. Bethany is located at NE 37th and Skidmore. For more information, call the church office at 503-284-3836.
