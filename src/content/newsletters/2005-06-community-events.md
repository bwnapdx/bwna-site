## Day Camp at Bethany Lutheran Church

July 31 – Aug 5 , from 9am to 3pm
NE 37th at Skidmore

A camping experience, for children entering grades one through four. A taste of camp right in our own neighborhood community. Staff from Camp Lutherwood along with dedicated adult volunteers from Bethany Church guide the children as they sing, play, discover, touch creation, do crafts, act, and participate in Bible Adventure Time. Cost for the week is $40 and scholarships are available. Contact Bethany Lutheran Church for information at (503) 284-3836.
