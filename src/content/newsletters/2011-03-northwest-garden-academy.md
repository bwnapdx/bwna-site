Northwest Garden Academy, a non-profit project of Parkrose Community Foundation, will be hosted at the historical Rossi Farms located at 3839 NE 122nd, corner of NE 122nd and NE Shaver. Its mission is to teach people how to garden so they can turn open spaces at their churches, schools, businesses and homes into gardens that can provide access to healthy food for themselves, their community and to create more sustainable neighborhoods.

Offerings will include: How to garden in the dirt with a master gardener; workshops on subjects related to gardening, but not gardening specific (i.e., soil testing and proper amendments, etc.), and on-site consultation services. Although a class schedule has not been finalized and published, classes will start mid-April and continue into Fall. Watch for grand opening news soon!
