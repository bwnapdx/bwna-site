For a nominal fee, come taste the chili entries and vote for your favorite. Or find a good book to read. It's all part of a fundraiser on Saturday, September 7th, at Wilshire United Methodist Native American Fellowship: 3917 NE Shaver Street—corner of Shaver & Caezar Chavez.

Empty bottles and soda cans for recycling are also appreciated.

Books go on sale from 10:00am - 4:00pm, and the chili cook-off takes place from 11:00am - 1:00pm.

(The church is wheelchair accessible.)
