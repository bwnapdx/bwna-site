A festive celebration of wine and art was held at the Blackbird Wine Shop on June 11. Owner Andy Diaz poured a variety of European wines while over 50 guests viewed the artwork of nine NE Portland artists. Called Artspace, the group meets monthly at local artists’ homes and businesses planning support, events, art shows and fun under the direction of Mario Caiolle. Art pieces include oil, acrylic and watercolor paintings, photographs, scratchboard three painted metal sculptures, masks, and handmade books. Many other showings are planned for the future. Your support is always appreciated, so we look forward to seeing you at our next event.
