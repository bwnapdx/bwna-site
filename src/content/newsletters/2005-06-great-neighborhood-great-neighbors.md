Over 100 people showed up for the “Taste of Beaumont” on April 11. I was very impressed by the Beaumont Middle School Jazz Band who provided the music. You can check with the school to get a schedule of their performances. The food, provided by eight neighborhood restaurants, was delightful. We should make it an annual event.

At the same meeting we elected four new board members, and three returning members for the seven slots available. Just in time for planning next year’s activities. Upcoming events include the Neighbor’s Night Out picnic, Fremont Fest, and the next winter’s tree planting. Read the newsletter for details, as the events get closer.

Enjoy the summer, be aware of the kids playing as school lets out, and I hope to see you at our neighborhood events.
