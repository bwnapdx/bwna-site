As the new year unfolds, we thought looking back at the many happy moments life brings us was just the ticket. So, we asked our readers about the good times they had last year. Here's what our neighbors had to say.

**My favorite memory from 2024:**

"It's hard to pick just one! I really loved watching fireworks together as a family on the 4th of July."
*(Eryn, 11 years in B-W)*

"…is going to Hawaii. I got to kiss a dolphin! And when the Dodgers won the World Series."
*(Conner, 8 years in B-W)*

"...was finding out we were going to be grandparents and seeing our first grandchild through an ultrasound image."
*(Patty, 33 years in B-W)*

"...is our oldest daughter and her husband buying their first home."
*(Jeff, 33 years in B-W)*

"...is going to Sunriver and riding our bikes as a family. We rode over 60 miles!"
*(Mike, 11 years in B-W)*
