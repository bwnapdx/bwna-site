The Beaumont Business Association (BBA) has been working both to improve the look of Fremont and encourage more people to use local businesses. We want to serve you—please let us know how we can help.

I hope by now you have seen the Beaumont Village Walking Maps. These have been an outstanding success. If you haven’t seen them, stop by one of the local businesses and pick one up. They are a great resource, both for locals and for people from outside the neighborhood interested in what Beaumont Village has to offer.

You will soon see banners identifying Beaumont Village as the place to shop, eat, and play along Fremont between 40th and 51st. This was done, in part, with grant money from the city through the Alliance of Portland Neighborhood Business Associations (APNBA). Special thanks also go to Sonia Jones of Sonia Jones Design and Lori von der Heydt of the Beaumont Health Care Clinic. Sonia created our new logos and designed the banners. Lori shepherded the banners through the permitting process (and it was quite a process).

The BBA meets on the first Tuesday of every month at 8:00 am at the Alameda Brew Pub. Meetings are open to the public. If you would like to present information to the Association, please call my office at (503) 493-1390, and I will add you to the agenda for an upcoming meeting. The information should be relevant to business along Fremont.
