Artspace, the online art gallery of artists living or working in BW, has added another Moonbeam to its collection:

Jenelle Kathan / Moonbeam Glassworks, joins Judee Moonbeam, a fiber artist who was featured in the Dec/Jan 04 issue. You can view Janelle’s fused glass panes, plates, coasters and other items on artspace.

Adrienne Fritze has new sculptures in her exhibit. Also, her installation, “Razor Wire Phoenixes: Through Prison Walls” opens April 3rd at the Urban Grind Coffeehouse 2214 NE Oregon St. The theme of the month long exhibition is ‘imprisonment and freedom’ with artworks by women inmates from the Coffee Creek Correctional Facility as part of the larger installation by Adrienne. Children of current and former inmates of the facility will display work in the Children’s area of Urban Grind. There will be a public forum discussion on April 24th from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.

New photos by Rita Catapano of autumn trees, neighborhood yards, Bishops Close and other familiar places in Oregon are also available for viewing at artspace.

View the works of Beaumont artists at www.bwna.org/artspace.html.
