Beaumont Wilshire (BW) board members are elected on a staggered basis with each office term being 2 years. On **Monday, April 11** at our general meeting, we will elect a vice president, secretary, treasurer and several member at large positions. Are you interested? Please come, and if not interested join us for a “Taste of Beaumont” **from 6:30 to 8:30 PM**.

This will be my last year serving as your neighborhood association president. In the next 12 months, we will be focusing on the following key projects: National Night Out Picnic on August 2, Fremont Fest, Winter 2005/2006 Neighborhood Tree Planting, and Crime Prevention. We continue to have a great transportation and land use committee with David Whitaker’s leadership and super neighborhood involvement. The committee has worked with the City to improve safety on Fremont Street by blocking a few parking spaces on Fremont so that cars entering and exiting side streets have better visibility. Our transportation committee continues to look for additional ways to improve pedestrian crossings in ways that will also benefit our neighborhood businesses. We also have a great website and bimonthly newsletters which Mario Caoile and Helen Koba make happen – thanks!

Board members balance family and work responsibilities while making a contribution to sustain and improve the livability in Beaumont Wilshire. There are always improvements that we can make to our neighborhood, but we need your help. We need you at our bimonthly meetings so you can give your input. Less than 30 people usually attend our general membership meetings.

We know that you take pride in this great neighborhood as evidenced by home improvements, attendance at the August neighborhood picnic, as well as support of the businesses in our neighborhood and local schools. Thanks to all who help as block captains, deliver newsletters, and either are serving or have served on the BW board. There are countless neighbors making a difference on their block, in their school, and in the community at-large. Thank you and I hope that you consider joining the BWNA board.
