After so many consecutive soggy months, the arrival of July and August is certainly reason for celebration, and if you’re looking for family-friendly, admission-free outdoor festivals, some of the best may be just down the street from you! Mark these on your calendar: BWNA’s Movie in the Park in Wilshire Park (Sunday, July 10th), BWNA’s National Night Out Picnic in Wilshire Park (Tuesday, August 2nd), Beaumont Business Association’s Fremont Fest on Fremont Street (Saturday, August 6th), and 42nd Avenue Business Association’s 42nd Avenue Street Fair at 42nd & Alberta Ct. (Sunday, August 28th).

Movie in the Park is sponsored by BWNA and organized by Portland Park & Recreation’s “Movies in the Park” staff. It’s a premier event for Wilshire Park and tailor-made for families with children. Beginning around 5:30pm, pre-recorded jazz music will fill the park, and by 6:30pm live entertainment kicks in, with two hours of soft rock favorites performed by the River City Trio. You can bring a picnic dinner or opt to purchase food and drink from two on-site vendors. But leave some room for the popcorn, because it’s free from 7:00pm until the end of the movie. Showing from 9:00pm to around 10:30pm will be Shrek 4—Forever After.

National Night Out Picnic is a longtime BWNA tradition, with hundreds of individuals and families participating each year. The Picnic substitutes for the August General Meeting and is totally organized and implemented by BWNA Board members and neighborhood volunteers. And while the underlying purpose of “National Night Out” is to bring residents and public officials together in the fight against crime, the event doubles as an all-neighborhood potluck family picnic. Beginning around 6:30pm, neighbors bring picnic dishes to share (chips, dips, fruit, salads, entrees, desserts, etc.), with BWNA providing barbecued hamburgers, veggie burgers, hot dogs, and refreshments. Local merchants contribute food as well as prizes to be raffled off free of charge at the conclusion of the evening around 8:00pm. In addition, enjoy live music, performing acts, free massages, and special treats for kids—including a real fire engine to climb up on courtesy of the Portland Fire Bureau. Contact BWNA President Al Ellis (apelainjoy@aol.com) if you have a barbecue we can use for the evening and/or are willing to volunteer to assist with various tasks.

Beaumont-Wilshire’s two business associations follow up with their own August festivals—Fremont Fest on the Saturday following the National Night Out Picnic and the 42nd Ave. Street Fair at the end of the month. You can find out more about scheduled activities and volunteer opportunities by contacting Beaumont Business Association President Becky Lehman (rebeccalehman90@gmail.com) for Fremont Fest and 42nd Avenue Business Association President Bill Dickey (bill@morelink.biz) for 42nd Avenue Street Fair.

Celebrate locally this summer!
