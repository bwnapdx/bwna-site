Monday, February 14, 7:30 PM
Bethany Lutheran, NE 37th & Skidmore

That was a recent headline in a local paper. The BWNA general meeting on Feb. 14 will focus on break-ins in our neighborhood and give neighbors an opportunity to discuss what we can do to prevent them. Preston Wong, the crime prevention specialist with the Central Northeast Neighborhoods will speak. (See his tips on page 6). Also, if you are interested in starting or revitalizing a block watch program for your block, this would be a good meeting to attend.

The second guest speaker will be Megan Slack of the American Cancer Society speaking on the ‘Relay for Life’, a 24-hour relay walk/run to be held this summer to raise funds for cancer research and to promote community spirit. This will be the first time that one will be held in the N/NE community.

Two important issues, two good speakers. See you on Feb. 14.
