It’s the season of the sun, and also the time of year when the residents of our community are most active. As Diane Charlton mentions in her article on page two, let’s work together and keep our neighborhood free of pollution. This includes noise pollution. As we spend more time outside, please remember to respect your neighbors personal space and keep noise to a minimum. As you enjoy the warmth of the sun, please try to do so peacefully. In this time of economic stress and rapid change, it is more important than ever that we take time to enjoy the beauty and peace which makes Beaumont-Wilshire such a great neighborhood to call home. With this in mind, as you take Rover for a stroll, please remember to take all of Rover home with you. Why should your neighbor have to remove Rover’s waste from their front lawn, parking strips or in the park? Please be a responsible pet owner. My personal wish is that you enjoy the warmth of the sun over the next few months in peace and without poop. Is that too much to hope for?
