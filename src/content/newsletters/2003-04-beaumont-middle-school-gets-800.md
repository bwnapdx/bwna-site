At the BWNA general meeting last February, the members voted to donate $800 to the Beaumont Middle School band. BWNA President Melissa Ritter presented the check to principal Sherrie Knutson on March 3, 2003. The band director sent us the following letter:

*Thank you for your generous donation to our school and band program. In times like these, when news of shortages and cutbacks dominate the press, it’s encouraging to find a bright spot in the clouds. Your contribution to Beaumont will help to purchase t-shirts for the band to march in, and to maintain creative options for other students next year.*

*Active neighborhood associations like Beaumont Wilshire are powerful members of the local community, and when schools and the neighbors work together, everyone wins!*

*Thank you again,*
*Cynthia Pitts, Director*
*Beaumont Middle School Band*
