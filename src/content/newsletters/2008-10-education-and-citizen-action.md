**BW Community Meeting**
Monday, October 13, 7:30PM
Beaumont Middle School, Room 140

Given the large number of families with children in our neighborhood, school in full swing and a highly-charged political season—education issues and taxes are never far from the front burner of discussion. BWNA's Oct. 13th general meeting will focus on both. Additionally, the BWNA board will unveil its proposed "Action Plan"—a statement of priorities for the next year. Those in attendance will be encouraged to evaluate and make suggestions for the plan.

Keynote speaker at this meeting is one of the leading education experts in Portland—veteran Beaumont Middle School principal, Sherrie Knutsen, who is in her last year as principal at Beaumont. In a recent interview for the newsletter, Ms. Knutsen spoke candidly about the inadequacy of her school budget, shortsightedness of No Child Left Behind expectations, and the challenges of running a 21st-century educational program in an aging 84-year-old building. Nonetheless, there are many bright spots as well: Beaumont's top-notch staff and neighborhood citizen volunteer efforts. With only two staff tending all the schools' grounds in the Portland District, volunteers provide a great service to the school and community. Contact Mary-Jaron Kelley at 503-249-9306 who coordinates the beautification and clean up efforts. Ms. Knutsen also spoke enthusiastically about 30 citizens from our neighborhood who volunteered last year to tutor students with learning needs. Call the school if you want to help out.

Public education is dependent on tax revenues, and this November's Ballot Measure 26-94 is a property tax levy that, if renewed, will continue funding for early childhood education, mentoring, abuse prevention and intervention programs. A proponent from the "Campaign for Safe and Successful Children" will make the case for a "yes" vote, followed by questions and comments from the audience.

Lastly, Emily Gardner of the Bicycle Transportation Alliance will be on hand to encourage BWNA members to endorse a bike boulevard on N.E. Going Street.

The meeting starts promptly at 7:30 at Beaumont Middle School. Signs to rm. 140 will be posted.
