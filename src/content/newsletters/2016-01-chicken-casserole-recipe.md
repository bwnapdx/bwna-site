- 4 cups wide egg noodles
- 2 cups chicken (cooked and chopped)
- 1 small onion
- 1 T chopped garlic
- 1 cup chopped celery
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 can cream of celery soup
- ¾ c milk
- grated cheddar cheese

1. Saute celery, garlic, and onion in olive oil in Dutch oven. Season with Italian seasoning, tarragon, dill weed, salt and pepper. Put soups in a bowl and add ¾ cup of milk. Whisk together. Add to celery, garlic and onion mixture. Cook the noodles, drain, and add them to the Dutch oven mixture. Stir together.
2. Put the mixture in a greased 9x13 glass pan and bake at 350° degrees for 30 minutes.
