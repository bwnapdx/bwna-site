The Transportation and Land Use committee meets at the Java Man Coffee Shop at 4727 NE Fremont St at 7:30 p.m. on the third Monday of every month. Will Stevens from the City of Portland Department of Transportation spoke at the October meeting. Getting Mr. Stevens to the meeting was one of the first steps in the development of an overall speed management plan for the whole neighborhood. Mr. Stevens expressed his desire to work with the neighborhood to complete a traffic study this fall or winter.

With the holidays arriving, everyone will be driving more and the shops along Fremont will be full of your friends and neighbors looking for just the right gift (perhaps for you). Please be patient and kind when driving. Have respect for those who are trying to cross Fremont. Remember that if a pedestrian steps off the curb at an intersection, he or she has the right of way. It is the law that all drivers must stop for those pedestrians crossing the street. Keep your cool, let them cross, and then carry on – slowly and safely. Thanks, and have a fun and a safe holiday season!
