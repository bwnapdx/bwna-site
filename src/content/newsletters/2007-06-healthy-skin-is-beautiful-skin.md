"Having beautiful skin is a sign of good health," says Ariel Fauley, owner of Ariel's Advanced Skin Care in Beaumont Village.

Her salon features Eminence Organics, a product line of masques, toners, serums, and moisturizers that are freshly made and shipped express from Hungary. They are two weeks old, contain 99 percent active ingredients and "are so fresh you can eat them," notes Ariel. For the past four years, Eminence has won first place at the annual International Aesthetics Conference.

Most skin care products on the market today are 68 percent water and only 8-12 percent active ingredients. To keep costs down, they are made in large quantities and stored for a long time. "The brands on the shelves today are usually three to five years old," states Ariel.

Basic skin care services that the salon offers include:

- *Microdermabrasion* for resurfacing the face, fine lines, and pigmentation, using diamond and sapphire dust
- Micro-Current with French organics for removing old paraffin and plastics with sponges and greens and for tightening facial muscles and improving elasticity (an "electrical fountain of youth")
- European and organic body wraps
- Salt scrubs, micro-current detoxifying foot baths, and massage

In 2001 Ariel started her skin-care business in Sandy, Oregon, after taking botanical studies at Ashmead College in Portland. She moved to her present location in 2004.

"I have always loved botanicals and the use of touch to heal," says Ariel.

Two women join Ariel at the salon: Andrea Johnson, who specializes in deep massage, and Karel Redeker, who is studying with Ariel to be an aesthetician.

Ariel has been involved in healing of all kinds for more than 20 years. She currently works part time as a chaplain for Multnomah County Corrections.
