National Night Out With Your Neighbors

**Tuesday, August 4th, 2009**
**6:30-8:30pm**
**at Wilshire Park**

Regular or Veggie Burgers & hotdogs provided! Please bring Chips, Salad, Entrée or Dessert. Door Prizes, Kids Activities, Music and More

If you have questions or want to help, email president@bwna.org

*We want to thank our neighborhood retail stores for donating door prizes*

BWNA providing barbequed burgers (both meat and veggie), hotdogs, drinks, & entertainment and neighbors preparing an assortment of potluck appetizers, salads, entrees, and desserts.
