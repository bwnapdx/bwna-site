## Chili cook-off & antique appraisal

**Oct 13th, 11-1 pm**, *Chili cook-off* and baked potato feed by the Native American Fellowship. Taste and vote for your favorite chili $1; bowl of chili and baked potato with condiments and beverage $5

**Oct 13th, 1-3 pm**, *Sixth annual antique appraisal* by the Native American Fellowship Women. $3 for each item of $8 for 3 items and each additional item $2.

## Halloween hospitality

**Oct 31st, 5:30-8:30 pm**, Hospitality table on the front porch. Hot spiced cider and coffee along with goody bags. Happy Halloween!

## WUM Women’s and Native American Fellowship Bazaar

**Nov 3rd, 10-4 pm**, featuring hot turkey luncheon, holiday gifts and crafts.

Wilshire United Methodist and Native American Fellowship, 3917 NE Shaver, 503-287-6526/ 503-281-0324 .
