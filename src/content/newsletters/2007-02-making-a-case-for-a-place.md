‘This neighborhood is mine; this neighborhood is yours’. But what is it exactly? Our homes, our businesses, our parks...yes! But there’s more! It’s the nooks and crannies that we don’t see or notice that offer the most untapped potential. With the help of the City Repair Project, I propose that we seize the opportunity to improve our neighborhood.

The City Repair Project is group of local citizens creating public gathering places and helping others to creatively transform the places where they live. There are two specific BW locations that I believe we can transform for the benefit of many:

**Beaumont Middle School Learning Garden** – Creating a small, sustainable garden on school grounds for student education outside of the classroom has garnered great support thus far. It would require the support of the school, its PTA, the neighborhood association, the business association and of course local residents to maintain the garden during the summer.

And though the Garden would be the focus of this project, some other ‘Placemaking’ features could be “cobb” style benches for walkers, public art and sculpture from our artist population and tables to host community meals perhaps savoring the wonders from the new garden.

**Picnic Shelter at Wilshire Park** – Imagine nurturing and energizing the shelter that commands such a prominent position in Wilshire Park.

Underused and consequently lonely, we could give it some mural art, nature-scaping, water harvesting capability, solar-powered lighting, a re-stained floor, cobb benches and climbing structures for the “little ones” (the kids play area is adjacent), apply some sheer shades or awnings, so that residents could use it for discussion groups, playgroups, warm-season yoga and movement classes or as the rainy season shelter it was intended!

Caring for this structure and transforming it into a location where all could congregate and enjoy its hospitality is the essence of Placemaking and community-building and, I believe, provides untold benefits to the richness of our lives.
