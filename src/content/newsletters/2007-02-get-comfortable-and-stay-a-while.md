Fremont Coffee House has recently renovated its space, expanded its hours, revamped its menu, and become a venue where local artists, authors, and musicians can showcase their talents.

Last November, Sunny Andersen resigned from her job as a sales executive with Boly:Welch and joined her husband Brian in operating the family business full time. The Andersens have three daughters, Tess, 18, Rianna, 14, and Lilly, 10, who sometimes help out by either waiting on customers or cleaning up.

Transforming the coffee shop into a coffee house required closing down for a couple of weeks. To give the space a more relaxed atmosphere they repainted the walls in warm colors, added bookshelves, books, and antiques, provided a workstation and free WiFi service, and furnished plenty of overstuffed chairs. “We want to offer an inviting environment,” says Sunny, “that encourages customers to get comfortable and stay a while.”

Friday and Saturday evenings now consist of either a monthly wine tasting or artist reception, and weekly live music. “In December, the Kings Raven Winery offered a tasting and ten musical groups performed,” notes Sunny. Carlyle, a band that features Sunny as lead singer, has also performed several times.

French pastries from La Provence French Bakery now supplement selections from Annie’s Donuts. “We are making major changes to the menu,” says Sunny, “including sandwiches during the day, after-dinner desserts, and serving beer and wine during evening hours.”

Sunny and Brian have a strong commitment to supporting local business, and they “taste test” everything to enable them to provide the very best at reasonable prices.

Fremont Coffee, located at 4727 NE Fremont, is open seven days a week: Sunday, 7 am-4 pm; Monday-Thursday, 7 am-8 pm; and Friday & Saturday, 7 am-10 pm. To receive the latest schedule of events, call 503.287.5456.
