**Monday, Dec 11th, 7:30 PM**
***Beaumont Middle School***
NE 42nd & Fremont

Come join us and meet some neighbors on December 11th for the year-end holiday potluck get-together! Bring some deserts. Drinks and music will be provided. Besides the social stuff and reports from BWNA officers, you shall hear from three speakers, not necessarily in this order:

**Sandra Lefrancois** of Central NE Neighbors (CNN) Coalition will present CNN programs; update on community involvement activities and objectives.

**Yveline Wilnau**, coordinator of the Village Building Convergence (VBC) will answer general questions about the steps required in hosting a community project site for the upcoming VBC in May 2007. There will be Placemaking Guidebooks on hand for sale as well, a resource and “how to” manual.

**Jackie Dingfelder**, State Representative of House District 45, most likely will update us on what was accomplished at the last legislative session and what her hopes are for the upcoming one. Beaumont Wilshire is one of many neighborhoods she represents. Bring your questions or concerns. *See ya!*
