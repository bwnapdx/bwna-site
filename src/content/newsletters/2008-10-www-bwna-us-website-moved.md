The Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood website has a new URL. If you have been trying to log on with old '.org' site, you would have not gotten to it. Recently it was moved from a server provided by neighbor Rob Vaughn to a new server. We want to thank Rob for hosting the website on his server for many years at no cost to the association. Rob is having health problems so if you know him, send an email or call and wish him a speedy recovery.

The website has some minor changes. But it still provides unique and useful neighborhood info. The events page now has a **calendar** and a link to Central Northeast Neighbor's calendar of events so you can see what other neighborhoods are up to. On the 'photo' and 'video' pages, you will find photos from recent events, the 2008 picnic, Fremont Fest and a video of the new mural at Wilshire Park. Phone numbers for board members are on the 'contact' page and numbers of other city organizations are on the 'links' page. For all your neighborhood info go to **http://bwna.us**.
