Middle school kids are at the in-between point-- still children, yet developing into young men and women headed off to high school.

It’s a time for them to be aware of the importance of community service in their neighborhood, and they want neighbors to know they’re good kids with good hearts!

The Beaumont Middle School 8th graders are offering to develop a list of needs in the BW neighborhood: needs of the elderly, such as cleaning up yard debris, general help for home owners such as painting a fence or assisting local small businesses with tasks such as washing windows or folding flyers.

We invite your requests! You’ve heard of “Craig’s List”? Well, we’ve got the “Bobcat List”--and you can be on it! (The Bobcat is the BMS mascot)

These tasks will be offered during school hours, within walking distance of the school, supervised by parent and teacher helpers. Requests for service need to be coordinated with them through Beaumont Middle School and condoned by standards set by Portland Public Schools. This service will take place from January through spring 2007. Help these kids help our community!
