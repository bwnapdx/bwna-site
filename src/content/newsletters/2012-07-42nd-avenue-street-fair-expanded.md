This summer's 42nd Avenue Street Fair promises to be livelier and more diverse than ever. Come join the fun on Sunday, August 26th, from noon till 5:00 pm. Head to NE 42nd and Alberta Court for live music, kids' activities, beer garden, vendor booths, and the Classic Car & Motorcycle Cruise-In-For-A-Cure.

Registration for the Cruise-In begins at noon, with $5 donations benefiting the Komen Foundation's Race for the Cure.

Families are also invited to participate in the Street Fair Parade, which kicks off from NE 42nd and NE Killingworth at noon. Dog-owners are welcome to march with their pets as well!

This family-friendly festival is brought to you by the NE 42nd Avenue Business Association, working to support your growing 42nd Avenue small business district. For more information about NE 42nd Avenue or the 42nd Avenue Street Fair, please visit www.42ndba.com or call Marie Larkins @ 503.287.5258.
