Garden Fever, 3433 NE 24 (south of Fremont) April 20th 6:30-8:30 pm

Garden Fever will open its doors for an after hours event full of music and shopping. A portion of the evening’s sales will benefit Growing with Music, an organization that brings music and movement classes to young children and their teen-age parents. Music will be provided by students of the classes and the Salvation Army. Tickets can be purchased at the door for $10 each. For more information, please contact Anne Clark at 503-282-1839.
