Katherine Anderson, former CNN Crime Prevention Program Coordinator, has informed us that the Block Home Program was discontinued in April 2002. The Portland Police Bureau sent letters to Block Homes notifying them of the fact. Please disregard the information in the last newsletter about contacting CNN to become a Block Home.

According to Katherine, people can still join the Neighborhood Watch and Block Captain Programs. Katherine also informed us that she has been moved to the East Portland Neighborhood Office and is no longer at CNN. **Dave Austin** is our new Crime Prevention Program Coordinator. He can be reached at the same number, **503-823-2781** or daustin@ci.portland.or.us. Neighborhood Watch is a good way to get involved and build a sense of ‘family’ on your block. If you are interested in forming a Neighborhood Watch for your block, contact Dave Austin to get more information.
