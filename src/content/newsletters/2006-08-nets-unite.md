Spring ’06 presented another opportunity for the city’s commitment to safety—Neighborhood Emergency Teams (NETs) in the surrounding Fire Management Area (FMA) of Fire Station 14 on North Killingsworth gathered for a refresher on utility management. Captain Craig Funk discussed protocol for utility safety in the event of a catastrophe. Teams in attendance included: Cully, Rose City Park, Beaumont Wilshire, Woodlawn, Sabin, and Roseway. In addition, Lawrence Behmer, Portland Office of Emergency Management (POEM), spoke about the critical need for interaction between fire stations and NETs in the event of a citywide emergency.

NETs are groups of citizens trained by the POEM and Portland Fire & Rescue to provide emergency assistance within their own neighborhoods, should police and firefighters become overwhelmed in the event of a disaster. NET members receive basic training on how to save lives and property until the professionals can arrive. Through this training, they become:

- Prepared for self-sufficiency for 72 hours in any emergency.
- Able to provide emergency assistance to their family and immediate neighbors.
- Able to work as an emergency response team to save lives and property in their neighborhood.
- Able to guide untrained volunteers who will want to help others when disaster strikes.
