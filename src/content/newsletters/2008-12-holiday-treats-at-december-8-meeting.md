> **BW Community Meeting**
> Monday, December 8, 7:30PM
> Beaumont Middle School, Room 204

BWNA general meetings are held on the second Monday of even-numbered months (except for August, when the Wilshire Park neighborhood picnic takes place), and the normal venue for these meetings has been room 140 at Beaumont Middle School. But at October’s general meeting, it was discovered that room 140 had been converted into a drama rehearsal room with several tables, no student desks, and just barely enough chairs to accommodate the number of people attending the meeting. As a result, Beaumont principal Sherie Knutsen has arranged for this month’s meeting to be moved to room 204, spacious with chairs and tables. Signs will be posted with directions to this new meeting room.

December is a time for winter holiday celebrations, and BWNA is no exception. In the spirit of the season, refreshments will be made available before, during and after the meeting. The agenda for the meeting includes a “state of the schools” update by Principal Knutsen, a presentation by Cyd La Luz of Basic Rights Oregon regarding the impact of state laws banning discrimination on the basis of sexual orientation and gender identity and consideration of proposals for future BWNA community activities and projects. Starting time for the meeting is 7:30PM, but the holiday treats will be set up by 7:15PM, so everyone is encouraged to arrive a little early for snacking and conversation.
