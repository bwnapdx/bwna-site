With summer and warm weather quickly approaching, more and more families will be using Wilshire Park located at NE 33rd Av and Skidmore St. The following are some park rules that will help everyone’s visit to the park be safer and more pleasurable.

1) Every park in the City of Portland park system has operating hours. The hours for Wilshire Park are 5am to 10pm seven days a week

2) Alcohol is prohibited in the park except by permit. Permits are issued to groups by the City’s Parks Bureau.

3) Wilshire Park has a designated “off leash” dog area. The area is clearly marked with signs. If you walk your dog in other areas of the park, your dog must be on a leash.

4) Your group or family can reserve space or tables in the park. This can be done through the City’s Park Bureau. Groups with reservations will be given priority.

5) Littering is against the law. Please bring a bag for litter or deposit you litter in the cans provided.

6) City park rules are enforced by the Portland Police Bureau and Park Rangers. Park Rangers are assigned to a number of parks and wear green polo shirts that are clearly marked. Mark Warrington is in charge of the Rangers and can be contacted at 503-823-5459. For emergencies call the Portland Police at 911.

Being aware of and following the above rules will help to make your visit to Wilshire Park an enjoyable experience. For questions call Preston Wong, Crime Prevention Specialist, 503-823-2781.
