If one of your New Year’s resolutions was to give back and be more active in your community look no further than Ride Connection! Become a volunteer driver for those who need assistance in your neighborhood! Help get older adults and people with disabilities to doctor appointments, grocery stores or other destinations.

Each day many requests for rides are being denied due to the lack of volunteer drivers. Call today to take the first step in becoming a volunteer driver! Ride Connection knows and appreciates how precious your time is so we have created many volunteer options to fit around your schedule! Please contact Trink Easterday at 503.528.1738 or teasterday@rideconnection.org to learn about the varied opportunities.
