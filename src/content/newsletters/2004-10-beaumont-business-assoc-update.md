Fremont Fest was a huge success this year. Thanks to the marketing efforts of Matt Schumacher, owner of the Alameda Brew Pub, and others, we had the biggest turn out I’ve ever seen. And thanks to Deena Spang of The Arrangement, who organized all the musical entertainment, people stayed longer. We had lots of people from the neighborhood and from all over the city. I even met someone from Norway at the Festival!

You may have noticed the recent graffiti problem in the Beaumont Village Area. Businesses are committed to removing any graffiti as soon as possible. In addition we are offering a $100 reward for information leading to the arrest and conviction of those responsible. You will soon see signs to that effect in the windows of local businesses. If you have any information, please call my office at (503) 493-1390.

I am also happy to announce that we will be working more closely with the neighborhood association. Chris Copp and E. John Rumpakis have agreed to work as liaisons on local traffic and other issues.

The BBA meets on the first Tuesday of every month at 8:00 a.m. at the Alameda Brew Pub and is open to the public. If you would like to present information to the Association, please call my office at (503) 493-1390, and I will add you to the agenda for an upcoming meeting. The information should be relevant to business along Fremont.
