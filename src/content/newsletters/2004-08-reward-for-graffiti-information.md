You have probably noticed the graffiti that defaced many businesses along Fremont over the Memorial Day Weekend. In response, the Beaumont Business Association is offering a $100 reward for information that leads to the arrest and conviction of anyone defacing member businesses. If you have any information about anyone who has defaced any of the businesses along Fremont, please call Java Man Coffee at (503) 287-5456 to report it immediately.
