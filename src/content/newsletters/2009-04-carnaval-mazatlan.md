On March 7, over 370 guests and volunteers came together at Montgomery Park for the 9th annual school auction. This event celebrates the Alameda Elementary community and raises much needed funds to ensure the continuation of a quality and enriching education for the children of Alameda and for other schools under the Portland Public Schools umbrella.

The theme was Carnaval Mazatlan – In Celebration of Children. Carnaval Mazatlan is a celebration of artistic and literary talent of adults and children. Guests embarked on a virtual trip to Mazatlan, complete with metaphorical sunshine, vibrant colors, and a break from the cold, gloomy Portland weather. What a nice treat, right before it snowed that night.
