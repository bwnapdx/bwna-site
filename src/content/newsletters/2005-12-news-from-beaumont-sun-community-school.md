Winter before and after school activities for Beaumont Middle School students include Chess, Volleyball, Cartooning, Homework Assistance, YuGiOh, Dance Team, TaeKwonDo, Newspaper Production, Babysitter Training, When I’m In Charge (Home Alone), MESA (Math Engineering Science Academy) and Basketball teams for 6, 7 & 8th grade boys and girls.

Sherie Knutsen and I have decided the best snack for our after school activities is fruit snacks. They store and distribute easily and the kids love them. Beaumont Wilshire neighbors who want to help our kids can purchase boxes of “individually wrapped” fruit snack packets (we need them for the rest of the year) and drop them in the Beaumont Middle School office from 8:30-4:30.

Thanks again for your wonderful support,
