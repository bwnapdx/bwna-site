***Perspectives on Being Female*** **group show being planned**

A meeting to gather women artists for a May show at Quirks of Art & Furniture is scheduled for Sunday, February 17, 2008. The meeting will begin at 1:30 p.m. at 3045 NE 45th Avenue. The intention of the meeting is to provide a forum for the artists to talk about their works and how they will express the theme of the show, to meet other women artists and to hear about and discuss the show logistics with Quirks co-owner Kerry Tomlinson. Please call or email host Brita Gould to confirm your interest or if you have questions: 503-335-0479 denybrit@easystreet.com
