The last four months of the year are upon us, and there is still time to make a difference. As a Financial Adviser, I have the privilege to help people make positive change in their financial and personal lives. This change is usually directly associated with the amount of control and happiness they experience. I’m often asked, “why are you so happy”. In general, I am because I want to be….who wants to “Debbie Downer”? But the truth is I’m not. The economy is still struggling and, upon my suggestion, my clients continue to alter and adjust their financial and personal lives as necessary. These are tough times, so let’s be honest. And let’s be honest about our commitment to the people we love and the neighborhood we value so much. Have you truly given back to your community lately? With four months left in 2010, come to one of the upcoming BWNA Board or Community meetings (see page five) and see how you can make a difference. If board meetings don’t excite you then delivering this newsletter is a great place to start. Call Marsha Mahon (see page two) and spend 20-30 minutes every other month and deliver this newsletter in our neighborhood. Do you have children? Delivering this newsletter with them is a great way to teach them about community involvement. Before the holiday season overwhelms and distract us, and before the cold weather sends us back into dormancy, I challenge you to use the next four months and make a difference in your personal, professional, social and community activities. If we all do a little, it adds up to a lot (see page one regarding the success of Neighborhood Night Out….. it “takes a village”)!
