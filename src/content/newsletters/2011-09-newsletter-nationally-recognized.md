The BWNA Newsletter has been a neighborhood favorite for years, both in terms of community news coverage and journalistic style. But this year a milestone was reached: recognition of excellence on a national level. In a competition involving electronic newsletters submitted for evaluation from around the country, the online (color) version of the BWNA Newsletter (which can be accessed via www.bwna.us ) was among six recipients receiving highest (“Gold Level”) honors. Awards were announced in Anchorage, Alaska at the 36th Annual Conference on Neighborhood Concerns, sponsored by Neighbors, USA (NUSA). Entries were evaluated based on criteria that included layout, consistency, relevance, and overall appearance.

In a fitting conclusion, the congratulatory letter and award certificate were sent by NUSA’s Awards Program Committee Chair Robert Gibbons to former BWNA Newsletter Editor Dan Johnson–the one whose initiative it was to enter the contest in the first place and, for the past couple of years, a prime mover behind newsletter quality, innovation, and organization. For Dan and his team of hard-working volunteers–led by stalwart assistants Deb Schwing (Graphic Design) and Ethan Derner (Copy Editor)–this professional acknowledgement of journalistic achievement couldn’t have been more gratifying, and, from the neighborhood’s point of view, more deserving.

Although no longer Editor, Dan continues to play an important role on the Newsletter team, both through his duties as BWNA Bookkeeper and as contact person for business ads. And, thankfully, Deb and Ethan have continued on in their positions, with Dan’s editing and reporting responsibilities divided up among BWNA Board members Bob McKibben, Dave Anderson, Serah Breakstone, Michael Gray, Alex Wall, and Al Ellis.

Newsletter by committee? Perhaps–but well worth the effort to keep this award-winning publication alive and vibrant in our neighborhood and beyond.
