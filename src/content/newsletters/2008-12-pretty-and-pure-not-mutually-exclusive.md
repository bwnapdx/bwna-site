While working as a research analyst for the Environmental Working Group in Washington, DC, Emily Headen became aware that “…an average consumer uses up to 25 different cosmetic and personal care products containing about 200 different chemicals every day” (Campaign for Safe Cosmetics). After further personal research, she thought, “Someone should open a shop that provides only organic and toxin-free products.” Emily returned to Portland in 2000 and realized her dream in August 2007 when she opened Camellia Pure Beauty at 4941 NE Fremont in Beaumont Village.

To Emily “pure beauty” means body-friendly products that are plant and mineral-based (biodegradable), contain no phthalates, parabens, petroleum products, artificial fragrances, or harsh detergents, and aren’t tested on animals.

Camellia Pure Beauty provides a wide range of personal care products: skin care, hair care, cosmetics, artisanal soaps, spa body scrubs, bath bombs, salts and oils, natural sun screen, and more. As Emily puts it, “Anything you can put on your body, Camellia has it, and because all merchandise is toxin-free, customers don’t have to waste time reading labels, unless they have a specific allergy.”

Most product lines are manufactured by west coast companies. Some brand names include Alchemilla—exceptionally pure, antioxidant-rich skincare products; Talulah—handmade skincare products; and Alima Pure—100 percent mineral makeup. She is also always looking for new products.

Camellia has been selected as a “Best of Portland Picks”—perhaps because her products are of superior quality, and the boutique is spacious, the air aromatic, the atmosphere harmonious, and the service personable.
