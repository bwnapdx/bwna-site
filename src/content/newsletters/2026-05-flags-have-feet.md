BWNA pedestrian flags are appreciated by the community. Alas, they have been "walking off" at a fast pace lately. They cost money, and volunteers spend time making them.

We need your help wrangling them back! Specifically, if you see:

- A flag where it doesn't belong, please return it to any flag receptacle along NE Fremont.
- A kiddo or adult carrying a flag nowhere near a receptacle, please encourage them to return it to a receptacle.

THANK YOU for helping us keep our flags so we can keep folks safe!
