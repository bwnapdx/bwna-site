On March 13, nearly 90 volunteers, including nine young people, from Beaumont Wilshire, Alameda and Concordia neighborhoods planted 94 street and yard trees as part of this year’s Friends of Trees (FOT) neighborhood tree planting.

Thirty seven trees were planted in the Beaumont Wilshire area.

Planning for this event began last spring. Board member Barbara Warren-Sams worked with Reed Hall (Alameda) and Lisa Johnston-Smith (Concordia) to organize the tri-neighborhood event.

Interested neighbors received information about the planting through the bi-monthly BWNA newsletter, posters located in strategic locations around the neighborhood, and information provided at several community events. They accessed information about available tree species on the FOT web site (www.friendsoftrees.org), from brochures provided by FOT, and by attending a slide show in January where Jim Gersbach, local tree expert, gave detailed information about tree species.

The following local businesses supported this year’s planting: Umpqua Bank was the official corporate sponsor; Trader Joe’s, Delphina’s Bakery, Noah’s Bagels, and Marsee Baking donated food items. Figaro’s and Java Man on Fremont provided pizza and coffee at reduced prices. Elizabeth Shepherd coordinated the effort to feed hard-working planting crews.

A special thanks to Wilshire United Methodist Church, which hosted the slide show and served as the staging area for the planting. And a warm thank you to everyone who purchased trees, and especially to the volunteers who gave up their Saturday morning to continue Friends of Trees’ fifteen year local-action effort to keep Portland’s urban environment healthy and beautiful.
