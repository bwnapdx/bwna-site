Isn't there an easier way? Those who have had occasion to laboriously memorize songs, poems, or scripture verses could well ask that question.

Try this to take the pain out of the process: Last thing when you retire at night, read the verse out loud at least three times. Then lights out! Don't think about it or use any effort; just sleep on it. Do this for three or four consecutive nights, and you might be surprised how it sticks.

Share this method with your children and get compliments from their teachers!
