Fremont Holiday Fest, proudly sponsored by the Beaumont Business Association (BBA), returns to Beaumont Village on December 6. The village will be bustling with carolers, activities, warm beverages, and a village tree. For little ones with an early bedtime, Santa and Mrs. Claus will be extending their hours this year! They will take appointments from 1:30-6:30 p.m. And thanks to Portland Fire & Rescue Station 28, the little ones can marvel at Santa and Mrs. Claus arriving in a shiny red fire truck! Reserved spots are the only way to ensure a photo with Santa and Mrs. Claus, and they fill quickly. Visit www.beaumontvillagepdx.com to access the Santa and Mrs. Claus reservation link as well as activity updates.

The Beaumont Village business district loves hosting neighbors near and far, and Holiday Fest is the perfect time to take in all of the variety the street has to offer. Come join in on the holiday fun and explore festive Fremont while you're at it.
