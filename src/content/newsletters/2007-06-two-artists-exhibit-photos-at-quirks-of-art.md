During June BW resident and artist **Keith Buckley** is showing photographs called "Imagos", a new series of artistic nudes exploring the visual organic transformative possibilities of the human form.

**Barry Frankel** will be showing photographs from his recent trip to Thailand. Both shows can be seen at Quirks of Art, 1602 NE 40th Ave, south of Sandy Blvd. next to the A-Boy parking lot.
