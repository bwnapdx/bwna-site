One of several new shops opening along Fremont in the past few months is Laylahma at 4759 N.E. Fremont. Laylahma offers natural fiber clothing for men and women. I first noticed the store one day in July when I was taking inventory of the new shops in the neighborhood. I had not done my “new business walk” for a while. I came upon Laylahma and decided to check it out, thinking I might find just the right birthday present for a friend whose party was that very evening. Upon entering the store, I was promptly engaged by owner Joshua Brownstein who gave me the complete tour of the merchandise, as well as the physical space.

Most of the offerings are blends of organic cotton, hemp, tencel, and bamboo materials. Yes, that’s right “bamboo”. I had never heard of bamboo clothing. I was surprised to find that it both looks and feels good. In fact, all the products look and feel good. What makes them even better is that the source materials are not grown using pesticides and the clothing is fabricated in sweat-shop free environments. For now, men’s and women’s styles are available. Josh plans to add a line of children’s clothing by the holidays.

The same eco-consciousness that characterizes the clothing carries through in the shop design. The floors are bamboo. The walls are painted with earth-clay dye. Even the ceiling incorporates natural fabric and bamboo poles to create the overall look and feel of a natural environment. The end result is a lovely light and airy space.

By the way, I did find a birthday present for my friend and a little something for me too. I recommend you stop by and take a look. I also recommend you take the opportunity to talk with Joshua about the mystery of “Laylahma”, the desert planet in a fantasy story he has written. 4759 N.E. Fremont 503-282-5731 open Tues – Sat 11 am – 6:30 pm, Sun 12 pm – 5 pm, closed Mondays.
