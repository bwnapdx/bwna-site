As temperatures cool and leaves fall, summer seems distant. But the fact is that it was only a couple months ago that neighbors gathered together at Wilshire Park in record numbers on a picture-perfect 80º August evening to celebrate as one. It was BWNA’s annual National Night Out Picnic in support of crime prevention, and an estimated 300 neighbors--many with young children in tow--delighted in the wide assortment of potluck dishes and fun activities. Contributing to the feast were Pizzicato with salads and pizzas, Umpqua Bank with ice cream bars, and BWNA with hamburgers, veggieburgers, hotdogs, and condiments. Lively entertainment was provided by former BWNA Treasurer David De La Rocha and his fellow musicians of the “Guerrilla Contingent of the Fighting Instrumental Karma Marching Chamber Band Orchestra,” which performed a series of traditional toetapping standards. For those seeking relaxation from a stressful day at home or at the office, local massage therapist Johannes Venghaus returned for a second straight year to offer free ten-minute massages. And for the kids, there were free pinwheels to play with and an opportunity to climb aboard a genuine fire engine (driven into the park and staffed by Portland firefighters).

After the eating, relaxing, playing, and socializing had run its course, it was time for raffle ticket holders to pay attention to their numbers. The free door prizes ran the gamut--from tote bags & table runners to gift certificates & wine tasting--and by the end of the evening over 20 winners had been called up by BWNA Board member Ankist Zadeyan to receive their lucky gifts.

Response to the picnic was overwhelmingly positive. Liz Johnston, a veteran BWNA picnic volunteer, summed it up simply: “A+!” As always, teamwork and volunteerism were the keys to success, and special thanks go out to the following neighbors, organizations, and merchants for their invaluable contributions:

- The Storer family--providing barbecue grills and transportation to get them to and from the park.
- Boy Scout Troop 107--assisting in setup and cleanup, as well as distribution of flyers to neighbors. (In gratitude for an outstanding effort, the BWNA Board voted to contribute $100 to Troop 107.)
