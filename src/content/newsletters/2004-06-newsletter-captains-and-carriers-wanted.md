Beaumont Wilshire is looking for captains and carriers to help distribute the bi-monthly newsletters. The Captain receives a bundle of newsletter (usually 200-300) and counts them out and delivers them to each Newsletter Delivery Person (usually about 4-6 people). This should take less than an hour. We have two Captain Sections open.

If you are interested, or would like more information, contact Lisa Perkins 503-282-5816 or lisachiba@hotmail.com.
