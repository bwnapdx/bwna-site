The Beaumont Wilshire newsletter welcomes articles on any subject that is of general interest to the neighborhood. We invite BWNA residents to submit articles such as interviews with neighbors, business focus articles, upcoming events and announcements. These are just some suggestions. You may have other ideas. Please submit articles either by email to mc-hk@msn.com (preferred method) or mail to 4025 NE Shaver, 97212. Articles should be no longer than 350 words in length and are subject to editing.
