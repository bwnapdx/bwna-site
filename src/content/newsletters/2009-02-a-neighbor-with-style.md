Julie Feinberg’s entire career has been in the hair styling industry. It's been an exciting journey moving through all of the fashion trends and changes over the years, and still today she is excited to stay on top of the very latest techniques. Julie is passionate about hair color! “How lucky we are to be able to enhance or change our looks so easily, with the many techniques and beautiful products available today” says Julie. Haircutting is also something she takes great pride in. Julie loves a challenge! One example would be, taking someone with fine hair and creating the illusion of fullness and movement. The end result is the appearance of a full head of hair.......with a great cut.

After years of working in the hustle and bustle of a full salon, Julie decided to build a private salon inside her home, right here in your neighborhood! As Julie explains, “I am thrilled with the results and feel I have met my goal of having created an artistic and relaxing space. My time and energy is concentrated on each individual in a comfortable and relaxed environment”. Come warm yourself by the fireplace while enjoying a fresh latte as you discuss with Julie the style that is perfect for you.
