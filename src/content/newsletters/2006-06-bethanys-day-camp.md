**Monday – Friday, July 31 – August 4, 2006 from 9am– 3pm**

For Kids ages 5-12 years old to experience a Christian Day Camp without leaving home. Staffs from Camp Lutherwood and dedicated Bethany volunteers guide the children in song, play, discovery, touch creation, crafts, Bible Adventure Time, worship and FUN! Cost for the week is $40 and includes lunch and two snacks each day and a field trip to Grant Pool. Scholarships are available for those in need. Contact Bethany Lutheran Church for registration information at (503)284-3836 or go to our website at www.bethanyportland.org and download the registration form.
