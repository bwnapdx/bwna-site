With flowers in bloom and temperatures on the rise, spring is a symbol of renewal and hope. And so it is with Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) on the second Monday of April, when the best-attended general meeting of the year takes place. The impressive turnout, however, is due more to cullinary and musical artistry than changes in the natural environment. It’s the annual Taste of Beaumont/Elections General Meeting, beginning at 6:30pm on April 11th at Bethany Lutheran Church (corner of 37th & Skidmore, across from Wilshire Park) in the basement activity room (“Fellowship Hall”).

So what’s the excitement about? How about a free meal, free entertainment, and the opportunity to participate in the selection of BWNA’s leadership for the next 12 months?!

The buffet-style dinner consists of cuisine samples from some of our neighborhood’s finest eating establishments, with refreshments provided by BWNA and local businesses. Enjoy musical entertainment during dinner from a group of gifted Beaumont Middle School musicians, under the direction of dynamo director Cynthia Plank. Following the meal, a Beaumont-Wilshire-beat police officer reports on the incidence of crime in our neighborhood (usually among the lowest in NE Portland) and fields questions from the audience. The remainder of the evening focuses on business agenda items--first and foremost being the nomination and election of BWNA officers and Board members. With Board vacancies to fill and a vacated Treasurer’s position as well, the hope is that neighbors will step forward and volunteer to be nominated. (To qualify as a candidate, one must be a Beaumont-Wilshire resident, available to attend meetings on the second Monday evening of most months, and have a desire to improve and enhance the neighborhood.) Lastly, committee reports concerning ongoing issues and projects are presented, followed by door prize drawings just prior to adjournment.

With limited seating, a 6:15pm arrival to secure your chair(s) at a table is recommended.

Also, marking April 11th on your calendars now would be a wise move. See you then!
