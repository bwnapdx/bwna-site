Every year Grant High School holds a huge Book Fair, open to the public. Donate your used books now then mark your calendars for:

**November 22nd, 23rd and 24th**

to come shop the sale! 100% of the proceeds benefit the school’s library.

Any gently used books are appreciated, but please no magazines, textbooks or out-of-date reference books. We also happily accept movie DVDs and books on DVD or tape. To donate just drop your books in the yellow bin just inside the front doors of the school. For pickup, call Chelsea at 503.232.5042 or email cemohnike@aol.com or Kristen Jones at jones6@hevanet.com.

**The students thank you!**
