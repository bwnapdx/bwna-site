Bentley and our cocker spaniel, Bailey, have discovered another great new shop on Fremont, **Green Dog Pet Supply**. Mike and Christine Mallar opened their environmentally friendly supplies and gift shop for dogs, cats and their people on August 31st in the new Fremont Commons building at NE Fremont and 46th. They carry high quality natural and organic pet food plus durable pet toys and treats. Local artists and vendors supply products made from recycled materials or sustainable sources. That means you’ll find a great selection of products that have a lesser impact on our planet’s resources. Green Dog Pet Supply is open Tuesday – Saturday, 10-7 and Sunday, 10-3, 4605 NE Fremont, (503) 528-1800. www.greendogonline.com. Bring your dog in soon for a look around and a wonderful doggy treat. Please shop locally and support our neighborhood’s great businesses.
