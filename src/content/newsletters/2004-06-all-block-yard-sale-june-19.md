**One day only: Saturday, June 19th**
**9am – 3pm (please no early birds)**

Find bargains on **40th Avenue between Skidmore and Prescott** at the 2nd annual block yard sale.

Houses on the entire block will have items for sale. Some residents have been in their homes for 50+ years so just imagine the wonderful vintage treasures that’ll be available that day.
