**1,000-egg Easter Egg Hunt**

**Saturday, April 15**, 11 a.m. at Wilshire Park. Sponsored by Bethany Lutheran Church, this event is free to the public. Toddlers and small children are encouraged to bring a parent or guardian to help in their collecting of eggs. Ask for directions to the egg hunt site when you arrive at the park. We look forward to seeing your Easter egg hunters there!

Bethany Church will offer two services on Easter Sunday:

**Sunday, April 16**: 9:30 a.m. Traditional and 11:00 a.m. Contemporary. A free brunch will be served from 10:30 a.m. to 12 noon. Bethany is located at NE 37th and Skidmore
