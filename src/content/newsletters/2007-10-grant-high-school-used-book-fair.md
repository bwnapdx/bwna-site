The Grant High School Used Book Fair will be held during “Back-to-School” night on October 4, 2007 to benefit the school’s library. Last year we raised $1,100 and put nearly $3,000 worth of books right onto the shelves Please attend and buying a book or two, the cost ranges from $1-$3, and we take cash and checks.
