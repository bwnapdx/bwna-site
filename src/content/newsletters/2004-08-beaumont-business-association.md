The Beaumont Business Association (BBA) meets on the **first Tuesday of every month at 8:00am at the Alameda Brew Pub**. It is open to the public. If you would like to present information to the Association, please call Brandon Wooters at (503) 493-1390, and you will be added to the agenda for an upcoming meeting. The information should be relevant to business along Fremont.
