So many things to do, so little time…a common expression heard around Portland in the Summer time. But what is time?

“Time is an essential part of the measuring system used to understand events and to compare the durations of events and the intervals between them.” (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time).

Whether you agree or disagree with the information that Wikipedia provides, we can all agree that with more time we have the opportunity to create more experiences and thus more memories. With long summer days in our neighborhood (yes, there is sunshine in the forecast), beautiful parks, shops and trails for us to enjoy, I challenge you to create new experiences this summer with your friends and family. Please take a moment and review our calendar of upcoming events on this page and see what provokes your interest. Then, review the article associated with that event and put it on your calendar.

Another good activity which you can start during the summer is to help deliver this newsletter. It only takes 20-30 minutes every other month and is a great way to meet other people in your community (see page five). The Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association is a volunteer board (see page two) that works hard to help sustain a community which you and your family can enjoy. Just as the Board is a volunteer group, so is the Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Newsletter team. With over forty volunteers, we create, manage advertising and billing, and hand-deliver this newsletter to over 3000 residents. It is my goal as Editor to include information in this newsletter which helps you and your family to have a better quality of life. Don’t let time pass buy this summer without making some new memories!
