After a summer full of block parties and picnics, fall is here with cleansing rains, children back in school, and shorter, darker days. Riding my bike to and from work, I’ve noticed a large increase in traffic since school started. We all need to be aware of the traffic around us whether it’s children, pedestrians, bicyclists or motor vehicles.

At our upcoming general meeting in October, the main topic will be “Safety”. We will have our new Central Northeast Neighborhood safety representative, Preston Wong, speak about safety in our neighborhoods and how to set up a block watch on your street. Our Land-Use committee will bring some ideas regarding increasing safety along Fremont Street.

Enjoy the trees in our neighborhood as they change color with fall. Many of these trees were planted through BWNA volunteers during our bi-annual tree plantings with Friends of Trees. I hope to see you at our next general meeting on October 11 and at other volunteer opportunities. If everyone gives a little bit, we can keep our neighborhood a great place to live and raise our children!
