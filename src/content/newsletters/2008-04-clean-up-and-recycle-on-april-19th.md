*Cully Association of Neighbors Earth Day Spring Clean Up*

**Saturday, April 19, 2008
9 am to 3 pm at
Trinity Lutheran Church
5520 NE Killingsworth.**

*By Kathy Fuerstenau, Chair, Cully Association of Neighbors*

**Acceptable items:** Yard debris, furniture, appliances (no refrigerators or cooling units), mattresses, tires, plastics, Styrofoam, carpets, rubber, cell phones, computers, clothes, metal, electronics, printers, VCRs, DVDs and TV’s.

**NON Acceptable items:** Hazardous waste, gasoline, chemicals, oil, paint, tar, animal waste and liquids of any kind or under the sink garbage. Also these landscape and building materials: concrete, asphalt, dirt, sod, cement, sand, gravel, roofing and asbestos materials.

**Fees:** Car-$7, Van or SUV-$15, Pickup Truck-$20 and up with additional fees for oversized loads or trailers. Tires are $3 on rim and $2 off rim. Computer monitors and TV’s are $8 each (TVs over 37” are $20). This event is for residential vehicles only. No commercial vehicles allowed.

There will be a cell phone, clean bubble wrap and Styrofoam collection area and an electronics, TV and computer drop off area. The NE Emergency Food Program will be on hand to collect cases and cans of food (especially fruit) and toiletries or other personal items including toilet paper. Gently used clean clothing such as shoes, clothes, and hats will be gladly accepted. The “You Price It” area is where you can find donated reusable items that you price, pay for then take it home.

Sponsored by: Cully Association of Neighbors, Metro and Portland Office of Sustainable Development, SOLV, Trinity Lutheran Church and AGG Enterprises.
