Work officially started on the Hollywood Sewer repair on April 17th. As many of you have noticed, a part of the repair work has created the need to reduce traffic to one lane each direction between NE 41st and NE 45th Ave. Reduced traffic lanes on Sandy are scheduled to continue all hours and all days through September between 37th and 47th Avenues, although work locations will change. You will probably notice more traffic on Fremont St. during this construction period, as commuters look for other, more expedient ways to get to and from work. The potential increase in traffic will occur at the same time as four new crosswalks across Fremont St. are added and the speed limit on Fremont St. is lowered to 20 mph between 42nd and 52nd. So, we could have some confused drivers out there. Please be extra careful crossing Fremont St. and remember to look both ways, even if you have the right of way while crossing in one of the new crosswalks. Drivers are not used to the crosswalks and may not always yield the right of way to the pedestrian.
