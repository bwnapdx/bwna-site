Take a walkabout in our neighborhood and give your family a chance to enter a random drawing that might win you one of three $25 gift certificates from local businesses.

1. What animal is formed to make a unique flowerpot on the southeast corner of NE 40th and Shaver?

2. What MLK Jr quote is cited on the colorful landscape wall mural on NE Going Street between 33rd and 37th?

3. What street name is embossed in the curb at the southwest corner of NE 35th Place and Going?

4. What is the largest diameter of wood doweling carried by Beaumont Hardware and what is the price for a 3-ft-long piece?

5. At the southernmost point of our neighborhood boundary, what inscription is hand drawn on a curb? Also verify its intersection location. (Hint: Wisteria does not run east-west).

6. What is the posted speed limit on NE Wiberg Lane?

7. What item by the checkout counter at Folly custom boutique might inspire you to strike a John Travolta pose?

8. What is embossed on top of the fire hydrant at Siskiyou and 42nd? (Extra credit if you explain what it means).

Send your answers with your contact information to info@bwnapdx (scan as JPG or PDF) or drop off at 3425 NE Fremont. Entries must be received by Tuesday, August 2nd. Submissions with all eight questions answered correctly will be entered into the drawing. Happy hunting!
