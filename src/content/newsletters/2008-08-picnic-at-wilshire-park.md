Tuesday, August 5th
6:30 – 8:30 PM

The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association invites you to the annual BW picnic in celebration of National Night Out. Everyone is invited to this biggest neighborhood event of the year.

Special treat : massage therapist Johannes Venghaus will be on hand and will be offering free massages. For kids, there will be fire trucks to explore and lots of treats for everyone.

Bring a salad, side dish or dessert to share. Local businesses and the association will provide hamburgers, hot dogs, pizza, drinks, plates and utensils. There will be door prizes and surprises. Please mark your dishes on the bottom so they can be returned to you if they get left behind. Don’t miss this neighborhood get-together on Tuesday Aug. 5th from 6:30 to 8:30pm at Wilshire Park.
