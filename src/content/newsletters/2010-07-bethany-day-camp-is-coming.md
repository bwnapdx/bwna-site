6th Annual Day Camp ages 5-12 years will take place July 26th –30th. Trained camp counselors from Camp Lutherwood (www.lutherwood.org) combine with Bethany volunteers for an exciting week of camp from 9am-3pm (Friday till 11:30am). Cost is $50.00, family discounts and financial help available - includes snacks. Registration forms available on-line at www.bethanyportland.org or by calling 503.284.3836 or at the church. 4330 NE 37th and Skidmore.
