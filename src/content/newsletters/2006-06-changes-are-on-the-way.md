In May I noticed folks from PDOT were out painting the locations for the crosswalks across Fremont St. and striping for angled parking. Perhaps by June we should have brand new crosswalks at NE 50th, NE 48th, NE 46th and NE 44th. Striping for angled parking will be installed along NE 43rd, 44th, 45th, and 47th, and the new 20 mph speed limit signs should be posted along Fremont St at 52nd and 42nd. These improvements to our business district were brought to the district through the hard work of the folks on the BWNA transportation and land use committee and others. If you would like to get involved with the transportation and land use committee please join us at Fremont Coffee on the third Monday of each month. We meet from 7:30 to 8:30 pm to discuss important issues that affect the quality of life in our neighborhood.

—David Whitaker
