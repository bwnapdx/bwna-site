Late one afternoon I glanced out the window and saw "Pastor Jeff"—writing pad in hand— approaching our front porch. I wondered if he was walking around the neighborhood in his capacity as pastor at Bethany Lutheran Church or simply paying a visit to discuss neighborhood association business in his role as BWNA Secretary. It turned out that Jeff Bethke had come with a church survey--one that consisted of only one question: "What does 'community' mean to you?" What immediately crossed my mind was how germane this question is to BWNA's mission as well. The answer I gave Jeff was that a well-functioning community takes pride in its environment, feels secure in its communication, and is empowered to make local decisions that enhance livability. I'd like to think that our neighborhood is evolving into such a community, with BWNA facilitating the journey.

To that end, BWNA is now in the process of finalizing an "Action Plan" designed to bring about concrete results in seven general areas: BWNA member participation, community outreach, land use and transportation, parks and open spaces, cleanup, and crime prevention. At the October General meeting (Oct 13, see front page), specifics of the proposed plan will be reviewed, and public input will be encouraged. Come to the meeting and express your ideas. In a rough and tumble political year, this may be community at its democratic best.

–Al Ellis
President BWNA
