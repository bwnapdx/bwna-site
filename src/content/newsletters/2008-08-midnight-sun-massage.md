Dana Davis is the massage therapist and sole proprietor of Midnight Sun Massage located at 4020 NE Fremont St., at the intersection of 42nd and Fremont (next to Foot Traffic). Dana provides therapeutic bodywork in the forms of deep tissue, trigger point, and sports massage (specializing in walking, running, and biking). Her other services include Swedish, prenatal, Thai massage and reflexology.

Working as a yoga instructor, Dana became aware of the restrictions in her students’ bodies and wanted to help them become more limber. Since she could not provide such a service as a yoga instructor, she naturally progressed to studying and becoming a massage therapist. Dana instinctively knew that working the body’s tissue to help relax and lengthen muscles could help people be more pain-free.

When Dana was ready to open her own business, she chose the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood. She feels that her personality and professionalism blend well with this vibrant and lively community. Dana also chose this area because the residents are known for supporting local businesses, and she knew she would be able to work more closely with the many other health care providers located in the area.

Dana is especially recognized for her expertise in Trigger Point Therapy. A trigger point is a hyperirritable spot in a muscle that causes muscle spasm, tension, weakness, local and referred pain. Trigger Point Therapy is extremely effective in treating tension headaches, carpal tunnel, sciatica, and any area of chronic tension.
