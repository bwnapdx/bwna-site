**Monday, October 10, 7:30pm**
Bethany Lutheran Church
NE 37th and Skidmore

Beaumont Middle School is the only public school within the BW neighborhood boundaries. Many of us have kids who are going there now, or may be going there one day. **Sherie Knutsen**, the principal of Beaumont, will speak at our general meeting on Oct. 10. She became principal two years ago and plans to stay five years. She wants to share her five year plan to improve BMS and to address issues in three basic areas: achievement, expectations and challenges. She will speak about the ‘deficient’ classification BMS received recently in ‘No Child Left Behind’. Other topics of interest to her are maintenance and beautification of school grounds and funding for classroom and building needs. Please join us at this important meeting.

Also speaking at the meeting will be **Donna Bestwick**, the coordinator for the SUN (Schools United Neighborhoods) program at Beaumont. Her program offers fun and exciting things after school for students and parents.

Our last guest will be **Erika Wolfe Burr** of the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency team who will briefly speak on how we can better prepare our homes and families for catastrophic events such as earthquakes or ice storms.
