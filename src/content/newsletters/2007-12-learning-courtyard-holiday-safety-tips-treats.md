**BWNA General Meeting, Monday, Dec 10, 7:30 PM, Beaumont Middle School, Rm 140**

Join us on Monday, December 10th for the year-end holiday meeting of our neighborhood. Treats will be provided by the neighborhood association. Besides the usual update reports from the BW officers, we shall have the following speakers:

Jeff Holiman, Ankist Zadeyan and members of the planning committee for the Beaumont Learning Courtyard project will present latest site-plan details for the outdoor classroom feature for the courtyard, tentative schedule for implementation and upcoming opportunities for input on comprehensive campus plan (See p. 3 for more details).

Crime prevention coordinator, Stefanie Adams, will be there with copies of ‘Holiday Safety Tips’. We can all benefit from those as we shop for the holidays!

See you Monday Dec. 10 at 7:30 at Beaumont Middle School Room 140.
