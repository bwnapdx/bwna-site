Festivities kick-off at 10 AM with a kids and pet parade. Kids – decorate your wagons, trikes and bikes. Pets can dress up and Green Dog Pet Supply will award prizes. Parade participants will include members of the Beaumont Middle School Marching Band and members of the 7th annual ‘Cruisin on Fremont” sponsored by Alameda Café, Amalfi’s and Barrett Automotive. Parade participants will gather at the Beaumont Middle School parking lot at 9:30am Saturday morning. Decorating supplies will be provided by SCRAP.

Live local bands with a wide range of music styles will play on two stages throughout the day. Various eateries will also be hosting live music. There will also be retail sidewalk sales, artisan booths and horse-drawn carriage rides up and down the street. A Children’s Place will be hosting a children’s activity area on the Wallbeds of Oregon lawn. Don’t miss this all day event.
