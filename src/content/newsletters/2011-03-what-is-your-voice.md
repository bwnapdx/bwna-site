Now that American Idol has returned to television for another season, I began to wonder, “What is my voice”? As the show eliminates contestants each week in search of the next amazing undiscovered vocalist, it must first eliminate thousands to reach the top twelve. As the show highlights personal stories of the contestants, you obtain a better understanding of each unique voice. Some voices are loud, some soft. Some high, some low — and some sad. Each contestant has a voice, but not all voices sing well. I was told by a good friend many years ago that, “Everyone has a story to tell, the question is whether we are willing to listen.” So what is your voice, and what story do you have to tell?

With the Taste of Beaumont and the Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) annual elections coming up on April 11th (see page one), there is no better time to let your neighbors and friends hear your voice. The BWNA is the voice of this community. The volunteer board works to keep our neighborhood safe and vibrant. Just as you hear birds chirp and dogs bark when you walk down our sidewalks, your voice should be heard too. The annual elections on April 11th are how I became involved with the BWNA several years ago… and now it’s your turn. I’ll see you on April 11th, and together we’ll taste some great food from local restaurants and elect a few new board members who will continue to develop the BWNA and be the voice for our community.
