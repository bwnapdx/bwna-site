Bethany’s **Call to Community** – Bethany Lutheran, at 37th & Skidmore, is being called into its community to serve neighbors. In an attempt to build and strengthen community, Pastor Jeff is working toward a goal of 1,000 neighborhood contacts within the next few years. He will be connecting with neighbors in Beaumont Wilshire and asking: “What does community look like to you in your part of the neighborhood?” As a token of our appreciation for a moment or two of your time, we will offer a small gift card to a local business to say “Thanks!” We will base our contact to a time that is appropriate and convenient for you as our neighbor. If you are interested or have any questions, please leave a message for Pastor Jeff at 503-284-3836.

**Sept 11 – Benefit Concert** – Bethany will host “An Evening with Aivars and the Praise Team Band” at 7 PM. The concert will benefit the Portland Police Bureau’s Sunshine Division Food Bank. (Suggested donation – 2 cans of food or $10 per family.) Aivars, a native of Latvia, has been with Bethany and the Praise Team Band for over two years and brings a wealth of contemporary Christian songs to our contemporary service. As a song writer, he continues to inspire through his generous sharing and his boundless energy. There will be a reception after the concert. Call Bethany at 503-284-3836 for more information.
