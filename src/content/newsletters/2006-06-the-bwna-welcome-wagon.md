Want to help welcome our new neighbors? BWNA is looking for someone to bring back the new neighbor program. In the past, new neighbors were welcomed with a packet of information about our neighborhood and city, such as maps, bus schedules, information on parks, schools, and more. The program has been inactive and is ready to be revived. Want to get involved? Have some ideas? BWNA wants you! Please contact Jenny Lisk if interested.
