Not really, but you can! Writing for the Beaumont Wilshire Newsletter is a great way to hone your skills as you work on that next great American novel or prepare the piece that will win the Pulitzer Prize. Or it’s just a good way to get to know your neighbors and neighborhood. We welcome articles about events, people, and businesses in and around the BW neighborhood.

Deadlines for each issue are listed in the calendar section of the newsletter or on the neighborhood web page at: www.bwna.org /newsletter.html. The best way to submit your article is via email to the editors at mc-hk@comcast.net. Limit is 350 words per article. Don’t be shy.
