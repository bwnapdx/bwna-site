Our company August West and Company is involved with several trade organizations. The meetings we attend are usually some form of education pertaining to the trades, new eco products that are coming out, classes to attend and just general camaraderie.

The biggest news lately is the US Environmental Protection Agency’s new RRP Rule: Renovation, Repair and Paint for pre- 1978 homes and buildings which come into effect April 22, 2010.

We know there a lot of contractors out there that do not belong to trade organizations and are left in the dark when it comes these updates and issues. It is also very important that homeowners are aware of these changes as well. Contractors that are doing work on pre-1978 structures will need to be Certified Renovators by April 22, 2010.

The fines that the EPA will be handing out are STIFF, $32,500 per violation, per day. We know that a fine of $32,500 would put many contractors out of business and ruin lives and that is what we would like to express and help prevent.

We always use our lead tester before even thinking about doing any kind of remodeling. As most of us know lead based paint is extremely hazardous to our health and environment, but especially to our children. Children are most at risk. This is because contact with lead may cause seizures, learning disabilities and even death.

Below is the EPA’S web site and number with all the information needed to become certified.

www.epa.gov/lead/pubs/renovation.htm

National Lead Information Center 1.800.424.5323
