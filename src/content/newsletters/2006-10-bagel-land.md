If you love bagels, you’ll love Bagel Land, where you can choose from more than 20 varieties of bagels. Besides poppy seed, whole wheat, or just plain “plain.” you can tempt your palate with kalmata olive and rosemary, pumpkin raisin, or even pesto. “The number one seller is the Tillamook cheese bagel, with plenty of grated cheese inside and out,” says co-owner Jackie Ferere.

What is special about Bagel Land bagels? They are made from scratch on the premises. “There are no premixes or frozen dough shipped from headquarters,” says Jackie. They also water-boil the bagels, producing a traditional bagel with a hard, crusty outside and a dense, chewy center. They also don’t use oils, preservatives, sugars, or artificial colors or flavors.

Jackie and her husband Ben bought Bagel Land in 1992. Jackie, however, was not new to the business. She began working there as a night baker, 11 p.m. until 6 a.m., in 1982 while attending Portland State. After college Jackie served in the Peace Corps in Haiti, where she and Ben met.

Jackie returned home in 1989 and became manager at Bagel Land. Ben came for a visit on a three-month visa, but stayed to marry, raise two children, Sophia, ten, and Xander, eight, and become a business owner. “When we bought Bagel Land, we thought we’d do it for a few years, but here we are seventeen years later, still going strong.”

Originally, Bagel Land’s sales were mostly wholesale. Today, sales are mostly retail. “Wholesale isn’t who we are,” says Jackie. “We like people to know that what drives us is being part of the community. We have watched kids grow up here and now we’re hiring some of them. That is a wonderful benefit of owning a mom and pop store.”

Bagel Land, located at 4118 NE Fremont, is open seven days a week: 6 a.m.-3 p.m. Monday through Friday, and 7a.m.-3 p.m. Saturdays and Sundays. You can reach Jackie or Ben at 503.249.2848.
