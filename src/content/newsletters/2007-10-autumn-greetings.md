Hopefully, you have had a great summer!

As I write this message, we are still experiencing warm weather, flowers are in full bloom, and we see many people out on long walks in our beautiful BWNA neighborhood. School is back in session, and it will soon be autumn.

Special thanks to all who attended (approx. 175) and helped make the August 7 annual National Night Out picnic in Wilshire Park a success.

Our fall BWNA program begins with our General Meeting at Beaumont Middle School on October 8. You are invited to come and meet your neighbors and hear guest speakers on topics that include land use measures and the environment. Come and meet Stefanie Adams, our new Crime Prevention Coordinator.

Do you have a topic or issue to discuss, or a suggestion for speakers for our general meetings? Your neighborhood board wants to hear from you!

Looking forward to seeing you at the meeting!

*–Bill Markwart*
Acting President, BWNA
president@bwna.org
