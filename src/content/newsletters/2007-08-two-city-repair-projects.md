As you drive down NE Shaver St. and get to the stop sign at NE 37th *(top left photo)*, you cannot help but notice the street painting and the corner beautification which has appeared recently. Next time you are at Wilshire Park, check out the new bioswale just east of the pavilion *(top right photo)*. These projects were accomplished in May with the help of many neighbors and local businesses.

Thanks to everyone who came out to paint, dig and plant and especially thanks to Alameda Brewhouse, Papa Murphy’s, ChaChaCha and Parisi’s for supporting the Beaumont Wilshire Repair Neighborhood Projects on May 26. Their food made for a fantastic and varied lunch for the many dozens of people who came out on that Saturday to paint and plant. It really helped us get through the day.
