To some it is underground art, but to most of us, it is an unwelcome intrusion in our neighborhood. Graffiti in Beaumont Wilshire is mostly the “tagging” variety, meaning the vandal boldly signs his/her name or message. It appears on both private and public property but it is illegal. Unfortunately many choose to ignore it and forget that it detracts from the livability of Beaumont Wilshire.

You can do something about it: whenever possible, remove it yourself. I carry the following in my car ready to use: disposable gloves, Hillyard brand “Quick & Clean” graffiti remover, and ready-to-work towel wipes saturated with paint remover solution. These are great for removing tagging from signs, bus stops, mailboxes, etc. I also paint over graffiti on telephone poles with brown paint donated by Beaumont Hardware store.

If it is not possible to remove the graffiti yourself, report the exact location to the city’s Graffiti Hotline 503-823-4824. You can also report on-line at www.portlandonline.com. Go to the search section (on the left side of the home page) under City Web Sites/ Agencies/Police Bureau, click on “I want to: Report Graffiti online”. Attaching digital photos when reporting it online is very helpful to the police.

Traditionally more tagging appears in our neighborhood when school is out. Please be an active neighbor and help remove graffiti in our neighborhood.
