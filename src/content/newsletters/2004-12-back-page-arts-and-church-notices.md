Jenelle Kathan of Beaumont Artspace presents fused art glass along with jewelry design by Zale Palmer for two days at 3335 NE 49th Ave., Portland, OR on:

Friday, December 10, from 5 – 8 p.m.
Saturday, December 11, from 3 – 6 p.m.

## Caoile Art at Pacific Switchboard

Mario Caoile will be showing new small oil paintings on plexiglass and wood with three other artists at Pacific Switchboard located at 4637 N Albina during the month of January 2005. Show opens January 5th.

## Spaggiari Exhibits at Java Man

Suzie Spaggiari of Artspace will exhibit recent watercolor paintings at Java Man, 4727 NE Fremont from January 1–31, 2005. There will be a closing reception on Friday Jan. 28 from 7–9 pm. Everyone welcome!

## Christmas at Bethany

Bethany Lutheran Church will offer two services on Christmas Eve December 24th (Family 7:00 pm, and Candlelight 10:30 pm) and also a Thursday December 23rd service in Danish at 7:30pm. Visitors are welcome! Bethany is located at NE 37th and Skidmore. For more information, call the church office at 503-284-3836.
