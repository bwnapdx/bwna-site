The BWNA is looking for volunteers to help distribute the newsletter. This is a great way to meet your neighbors and get some exercise. The newsletter comes out 6 times a year. You will hand deliver newsletters to homes and businesses in a specific route, which only takes 1-2 hours every other month. If you are interested please contact Jennifer Francis at 503-459-8114 or jfrancis8@email.com.

If you are an existing volunteer please contact Jennifer Francis if you need to make any changes or will be on vacation. Thank you for your help and for ensuring the newsletter is distributed on time.

*—Jennifer Francis*
