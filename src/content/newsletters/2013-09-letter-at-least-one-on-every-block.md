Livability in our wonderful neighborhood means being good neighbors. Some of our neighbors are struggling either financially or physically. Why not offer to help a neighbor with some basic weeding or lawn mowing to help spruce up their yard this season? With the wind and birds' help, weed seeds don't stop at the property line so you're helping your own yard too. And your neighbor may not want to ask for help but would appreciate some.

If you take daily walks with or without your dog(s), carry an extra bag to pick up a few pieces of litter. Every little bit helps keep our sidewalks clear and may prevent someone from stumbling. Just a little extra help from those who can will benefit everyone in Beaumont Wilshire. Thank you!
