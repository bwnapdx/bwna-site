In our last issue, we forgot to acknowledge Umpqua Bank for bringing their ice cream truck to the annual picnic in Wilshire Park. Two of the bank’s staff, Jeff and Crystle, were there to hand out ice cream treats which were enjoyed by all. This community service by the bank is very much appreciated and a welcome sight at neighborhood events. Thanks Umpqua Bank!

–H. Koba, editor
