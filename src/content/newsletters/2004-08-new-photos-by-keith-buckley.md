Next time you check out Beaumont Wilshire’s Artspace you will find that it has a new look and new photos by Beaumont Wilshire photographer Keith Buckley.

The new color photos by Buckley are of familiar and exotic objects and scenes around Portland and Oregon, such as ‘breakfast jars’, bridges at sunset, old bikes, and lakes.

The redesign of Artspace includes a totally new look for the front page, a ‘News’ page that lists current and upcoming shows by artists, and a ‘Submissions’ page that lists who is eligible to join artspace and how to submit your work. Artspace artists are currently in the planning stages of organizing group and solo shows in the neighborhood businesses. Watch for those announcements in this newsletter and on the website.

Check out Buckley’s work and the work of the other Beaumont Wilshire artists at:
**www.bwna.org/artspace.html**
