A creative writing class open to all members of the community will be held at Bethany Lutheran Church on **Monday evenings, 6-8pm**, beginning **April 19**. Clara Renner will lead the six-week course. She has taught similar classes at University of Minnesota and Valencia Community College in Orlando, Florida. Course work is designed to help students preserve family stories and will cover: how to get started writing, exercises and suggestions to stimulate creative thinking, poetry, and letter writing. No experience necessary! Class size is limited. For more information, call the church office at 503-284-3836.
