In December of 2004 the YMCA of Columbia-Willamette announced the closure of its Northeast facility after 80 years of service. This brought forth an outpouring of support for the cherished institution that resulted in the formation of the non-profit Northeast Community Center (NECC). On February 1, 2005 the Northeast Community Center jumped into action and opened its doors - just three months after the announced closure.

The NECC offers youth programs including swimming lessons, basketball leagues, basketball conditioning and training, after school activities as well as unique programs such as circus camps and model plane flying classes. Senior classes include aerobics, water aerobics, circuit training, hikes and other activities.

The building has undergone several renovations to extend its useful life. The facility has three locker rooms: men, women, and a family locker room. Gym activities include aerobic step, kickboxing, total body conditioning, stretch and movement, freestyle aerobics, active older adults' aerobics, adult basketball, teen basketball, adult volleyball, Hapkido/Tai-Chi, dance, yoga and Pilates.

The NECC pool is heated to about 86° and provides activities such as lap swim, deep water aerobics, synchronized swimming, arthritis exercise, youth swim lessons, swim team, and open family swim.

NECC has no joining fees and no long-term contracts. Membership rates vary depending on age and are very reasonable for the whole family.

NECC also has a child care room with baby-sitting and a small meeting room available for members and community groups. This is truly a neighborhood community institution with a long history of active use. It's a gathering place for generations of people. NECC is located at 1630 NE 38th (near Sandy Blvd). 503-284-3377, www.necommunitycenter.org
