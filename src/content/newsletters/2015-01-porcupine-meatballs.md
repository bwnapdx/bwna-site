- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1/2 cup quick cooking rice (uncooked)
- 1 cup water
- 1/3 cup chopped onion
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/2 tsp. celery salt
- 1/8 tsp. garlic powder
- 1/8 tsp. pepper
- 1 15 oz. can tomato sauce
- 2 tsp. Worcestershire sauce

1. Heat oven to 350 degrees.
2. Mix meat, rice, 1/2 cup of the water, onions, salts, garlic powder, and pepper.
3. Shape into meatballs.
4. Place in ungreased baking dish.
5. Stir remaining ingredients together.
6. Pour over meatballs.
7. Cover with foil; bake 45 minutes.
8. Uncover and bake 15 minutes longer.

4-6 servings
