Uniquely prepared natural substances are used as medicines to assist the body in healing itself. Homeopathy has been used in Dr. Ananda’s family for three generations: by her grandmother, her mother and now herself. As a healing art, homeopathy has been around since the 1700’s. It reached its peak of popularity in the United States prior to World War II and before the discovery of penicillin and antibiotics when it successfully cured people during infectious disease epidemics.

Dr. Ananda’s interest and study of homeopathy intensified as she raised her own children and sought cures for many common childhood ailments without use of drugs. She felt empowered as a mother when she was able to use safe homeopathic medicines on her children. In recent years, the popularity of homeopathy is returning as young mothers and young grandmothers are seeking alternatives to mainstream medical and drug therapies. According to Dr. Ananda, it is never too early or too late to start.
