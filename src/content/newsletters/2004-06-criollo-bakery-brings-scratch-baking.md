“People ask me what my style is,” says Melissa McKinney, owner of the new Criollo Bakery in Beaumont Village. “It’s a little bit of everything, from rustic to home style to Parisian patisserie.”

Criollo (pronounced Cree-oh-lo) will have a full line of baked goods. Breakfast will feature muffins, scones, Danish, coffee cake, and gourmet donuts, all made from ‘scratch’. The bakery will carry organic artisan breads, made fresh every day by McKinney and her team. She has several unique ‘starters’—a key ingredient in bread—including one from a 150-year-old Paris bakery and one from a community bakery in Bahrain. At lunchtime, Criollo will have homemade soups and salads, sandwiches and panini made with the bakery’s specialty breads.

The desserts at Criollo –cakes, cookies, tarts, torts, pies, and candies– are sure to be a neighborhood favorite. Criollo will be open for dessert by candlelight until 11:00 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays, with tables both inside and outside for the enjoyment of the patrons. McKinney, plans to use lots of local fresh fruits in her desserts, and says, “The things that are very important to me are using local ingredients, supporting local farmers, growers and artisans, and using organic ingredients where I can.”

McKinney, who has lived within walking distance of her new shop for 10 years, looked all over town before deciding to open her bakery at 4727 NE Fremont.

She is particularly excited about the neighborhood and about being located next door to Java Man coffee. The French doors between Criollo and Java Man will remain open during business hours so that patrons can take full advantage of both shops. The two businesses are planning joint events for the summer, and people are welcome to sit in either shop to eat their pastries and sip their coffee.

Criollo Bakery will open the first week in June. They will open at 7:00 am every day, and close at 7:00 pm Monday to Thursday, 11:00 pm Friday to Saturday, and 4:00 pm Sunday, 4727 NE Fremont.
