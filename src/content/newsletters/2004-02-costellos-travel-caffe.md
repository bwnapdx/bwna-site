While many of us were celebrating the holidays and enjoying the snow, the Costello family, residents of the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood, were busy putting the finishing touches on their new establishment, Costello’s Travel Caffé. This unique café, now open at 2222 NE Broadway, is the creation of Chris Costello, graduate of the entrepreneurial program at the University of Portland, and David and Patti Costello, who have spent three years dedicated to the fulfillment of the project.

Since 2000, the Costellos have been traveling and filming in Europe, China, South America, Canada and the US. Their original footage features urban views, from Tiananmen Square to the bustling Champs-Elysees, and natural scenes, such as the Mediterranean coastline and the mountain peaks of Machu Picchu.

Two window-like plasma scenes showcase this footage inside the café, providing customers with an ever-changing, international “view.” Each day, different cities or regions are featured, complete with natural sounds and carefully selected global music. Other travel themed entertainment includes foreign films and international sporting events.

Costello’s Caffé offers a carefully selected array of food and drink. In the mornings, enjoy a drink from the espresso bar, pastries hot from the oven, fresh quiche and bottled juices. For lunch or dinner, choose from panini sandwiches, homemade soups, salads and cookies. During evening hours, indulge in a decadent dessert or sip tea or coffee. The café also features an assortment of rotating, internationally inspired appetizers. Be sure to check them out. In February, Costello’s will add microbrews, imports and wines from around the world.

Open six days a week (closed Mondays), Costello’s Travel Caffé offers a unique and welcome addition to the city’s dining scene.
