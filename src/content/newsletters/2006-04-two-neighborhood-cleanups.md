## Cully Neighborhood Cleanup

**Saturday, April 22, 9am – 4pm** at the Trinity Lutheran Church, 5520 NE Killingsworth. Accepting yard debris, bulky waste, appliances (no refrigerators), metal, and tires. No building materials or hazardous waste will be accepted including liquids of any kind or under the sink garbage. An ARC truck will be on site to collect usable clothing, bedding, towels etc. and Earth Share will be collecting cell phones to recycle or refurbish. Fees: $5.00/car, $15/suv/van, $20/pickup; oversized loads and tires will be charged extra. No commercial vehicles will be allowed.

## Roseway Neighborhood Cleanup

**Saturday, May 13, 9am – 2pm**, Gregory Heights Middle School parking lot - 7334 NE Siskiyou. Accepting yard debris, general trash and items that can be recycled such as wood, paper, metal. We will also have a truck from The Arc to accept re-usable items. The cost is $5/car, $10/pick-up and $15/larger truck.
