If shopping malls are not your ' cup of tea' for holiday shopping, bike or walk over to NE Fremont. You'll find everything from lawnmowers to clothing, from flowers to antiques, from books to baked goods, from doggie treats to wall beds. Many of the businesses are family owned and have been in the neighborhood for years. You will be surprised at the variety and quality of the items you'll find for everyone on your list. Relax and enjoy a cup of tea or coffee, a glass of beer, light lunch and dessert at one of the eateries.

Or if you prefer shopping from your home, browse **www.bwna.org/artspace.html**. This is the online gallery of works by fifteen neighborhood artists. Here you'll find paintings, sculptures, mosaics, jewelry, books, fairy furniture, clothing and more. You can contact the artist directly to make your purchase. Support your neighborhood businesses and artisans and shop in Beaumont Village this holiday season.
