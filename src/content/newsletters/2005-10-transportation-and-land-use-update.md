The Transportation and Land Use committee continues to meet at Fremont Coffee at 4727 NE Fremont St. at 7:30 p.m. on the third Monday of the month. All are welcome to attend the meeting and learn about transportation and land use related issues in the neighborhood. The committee is actively working on the two top neighborhood concerns that came to light in the neighborhood survey.

We are working on improving pedestrian safety and other issues in the Fremont business district by working with the Portland Department of Transportation (PDOT) and the Portland Bureau of Planning on the Fremont Main Street Study. A stakeholders group has met twice and has developed a list of recommendations that will be presented in a public meeting in October. The date of the public meeting has not been determined yet, but will be posted on the BWNA web site and in shop windows along Fremont St when a date is chosen.

There are two new commercial developments proposed for our neighborhood. The development proposed for 44th and Fremont (next door to Beaumont Hardware) recently received land use approval from the City of Portland. The transportation and land use committee is lobbying the developers to work with the City to install a pedestrian crossing at this location. The Beaumont Lofts development at NE 50th and Fremont received land use approval from the City, but the Rose City Park neighborhood is appealing that decision. After numerous conversations with the developer, our neighborhood board presented a letter of support for the project, which includes a pedestrian crossing at NE 50th.
