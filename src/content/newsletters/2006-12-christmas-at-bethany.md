Bethany Lutheran Church will offer three Christmas services this year: a Danish language service on **Saturday December 23rd at 7:30pm** and on **December 24th, a 10:00am Sunday** morning service and a **7:00pm Candlelight service.** Visitors are welcome! Bethany is located at NE 37th and Skidmore. For more information, call the church office at 503-284-3836.
