*Dear Neighbors:*

First, I want to thank you for your generous donation to our after-school programs here at Beaumont. I was surprised and thrilled with your generosity. *(BWNA donated $500 to Beaumont Middle School after-school program. –ed.)*

Secondly, I want to bring you up to date on our efforts to close the achievement gap that I described when I met with you last month. Since that time we have met with parents of thirty students who were excited to have their students be a part of this program. We are having our first parent training this Wednesday.

In addition, staff has trained 16 mentors from the community and assigned them to work with students who are currently below benchmark level. Another group of 6 mentors are being trained next week. We are still searching for 6 more people who can make a commitment of one hour a week for this school year to work with one student each. These mentors will help the students with reading, writing, and math as needed, as well as helping them organize their notebooks and homework. Perhaps more importantly, the mentors will form that much needed positive relationship with a caring adult that all students need to be successful. Please call me at 916-5610 if you want more information about how you can be involved in this project.

Please feel free to drop in to visit Beaumont. We are your neighborhood middle school. We would love to show you around the school and tell you more about our programs. Just give us a call to make an appointment.

Thanks again for your help.
Sherie Knutsen
Principal , Beaumont Middle School
Nov 10, 2005
