When John Lewis graduated from Indiana University in 1996 with a Doctorate in Optometry, his goal was to one day have his own practice. He achieved his goal last January 2007, when he opened his own private practice, Beaumont Vision at 4331 NE Fremont. Dr Lewis relocated to Portland in 2002 after a residency in New York City where he worked as an associate in an ophthalmology practice specializing in LASIK surgery. This procedure which corrects vision by laser surgery is the number one elective surgery preformed in this county, according to Dr. Lewis.

Dr. Lewis lives close to his practice and being an avid bicyclist, often rides his bike to work. He loves Portland, especially the weather! His office has received an award from the City of Portland Office of Sustainable Development. That award is the RecyleWorks Award (formerly known as Blue Works). To qualify, Dr. Lewis’s office practices the following waste prevention: purchase and use of recycled paper only, patient records are stored electronically to cut down on the use of paper, utensils and cups are not disposed of, but washed and reused, recycling for materials used is provided in the office and he bikes to work!

The services provided by his office include comprehensive eye exams, contact lens evaluation and medical eye care including pre- and post-operative care for patients who choose to have LASIK surgery. He also enjoys seeing young patients. A father of two small children himself, he likes to provide care for small children who he feels should be seen before first grade and even as early as age three to be sure that everything is going well with their vision. Vision problems in children may be one reason for delay in their learning abilities.

Dr. Lewis is also a Clinical Professor at the Pacific University Optometry School. To find out more about his services call or visit him at his office 4331 NE Fremont 503-331-3937, His office hours are Mon and Fri 9-6, Tues 10-7, Thurs 8-5 and Sat 9-5. Visit his website www.beaumontvision.com. We welcome Dr Lewis to our neighborhood and are glad to have his services so close by.
