Be prepared for an emergency - take the NET training

## What is NET?

Neighborhood Emergency Team (NET) members are citizens trained by the Portland Office of Emergency Management (POEM) and Portland Fire & Rescue to provide emergency disaster assistance within their own neighborhoods. Training is FREE. NET training is also known as Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) elsewhere.

## How to become a NET

The Portland Office of Emergency Management NET training is available to Portland residents and those who work in Portland. Individuals between the ages of 14 and 18 years old will need written and signed approval from your parent or guardian to take the NET training. To be certified you must complete all seven classes in the series and the Final Field Exercise.
