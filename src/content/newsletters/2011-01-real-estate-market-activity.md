## October Residential Highlights

Sales activity in Portland has shown some signs of slowing. The number of homes sold last month dropped by 35.7% when comparing October 2010 to October 2009. Pending sales also decreased by 21.5% and new listings dropped by 9.4%. In comparison to the previous month of September 2010, closed sales decreased by 7.7%, while pending sales grew by 3% and new listings fell by 12.1% for that same time period. There are currently 13,805 active residential listings in Portland and at the current pace of sales it would take approximately 10.7 months for the average home to sale.

## Sales Prices

The average price for a home in the Portland Metro area is 2.4% less then it was in October 2009. Current listings are around $276,800. While comparing the average sales price on a month-to-month basis, prices have dropped but only by 1.1%. The median sales price also decreased 2.7% from September 2010 and showed an annual decrease of 4.7%.

## Year-to-Date

Some good news appears while looking at January 2010 thru October 2010 and comparing it with the same time frame in 2009. Closed sales actually increased by 3.7%.  The number of pending sales went down by 3.4%, but the number of new listings went up by 4%.
