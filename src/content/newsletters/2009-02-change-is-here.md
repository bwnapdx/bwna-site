My husband Mario and I want to thank the Beaumont Wilshire board members and neighbors, especially Liz Johnston, for honoring us with a plaque and a donation to Oregon Food Bank in our name at the neighborhood meeting in December 2008. These were our ‘send off ’ gifts as we pass on the job of producing the neighborhood newsletter to Dan Johnson. Putting out the newsletter was challenging and rewarding, and we enjoyed ourselves. We could not have done it without all the people who wrote articles, contributed announcements, placed ads in each issue and delivered them to every neighbor and business in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood. We hope you all will continue to support Dan in his new endeavor. An endeavor that is completely voluntary, so I encourage you to get involved. We are not leaving Beaumont Wilshire, so we will still be around.
