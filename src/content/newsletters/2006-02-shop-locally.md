In 2005, as in previous years, the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood association has received tremendous support from the businesses located in our neighborhood. They have donated generously to the annual picnic, supported little league baseball, sponsored the Fremont Fest, assisted with the installation of bike racks along Fremont, donated treats to meetings and tree plantings, hosted committee meetings and advertised in the neighborhood newsletter.

We are lucky to have so many fine businesses and services so close to where we live. Many of us can walk or bike to the cafes, restaurants, specialty and grocery stores in BW. So next time you're thinking of something special to eat or buy, think Beaumont businesses.
