The Urban League of Portland, Adult & Senior Services Program is excited to announce opportunities for seniors age 60 and over to have fun with their peers by going on field trips, having lunch, playing games, going shopping, watching movies, volunteering, and participating in health and education classes every weekday at the Urban League Multicultural Senior Center. Studies show that seniors are healthier and happier when they engage in fun activities.

All of our activities are wheelchair accessible, open to those with disabilities and we offer transportation for seniors to and from activities. We also offer case management services that link seniors to other agencies for transportation, in-home and personal care services, food and nutrition services, respite for caregivers, grandparent support groups, tax and legal assistance, and utility assistance. Most of these services are at no cost to seniors.

The purpose of The Urban League of Portland, Adult & Senior Services Program is to promote the health, well-being, and economic security of seniors by providing opportunities and services that enable them to live independently and safely in their homes for as long as possible.

The Urban League Multicultural Senior Center is located at 5325 NE Martin Luther King, Jr. Blvd., 97211 and can be accessed by TriMet bus lines 6 and 72. You can also contact us to arrange transport by the Urban League van.

If you would like to obtain more information, please call our information and assistance line at 503-280-2632, or Arleta Ward, Activity Coordinator at the Senior Center at (503) 988-5470.
