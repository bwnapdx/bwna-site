Abundant sunshine, low humidity, minimum mosquitoes, clean drinking water, natural beauty, and recreational opportunities aplenty--that's summertime in our part of the country. Pretty hard to beat-especially in a lovely urban settings like ours. But along with the benefits come responsibilities. After all, the impressive quality of life that we enjoy today in the BW neighborhood didn't just "happen." It was a result of decades of community activism, perseverance, and stewardship--with BWNA at the vanguard of organization and changes every step of the way.

I vividly recall how different the neighborhood was when my family moved here almost 20 years ago. Drawn to the area's reputable schools, ethnic diversity, and natural beauty, we were nonetheless concerned about nearby Wilshire Park--a place that was dimly lit, with the consequent potential for crime. And while there were stores and cafes within walking distance, they were few in number and limited in variety. Fast-forward to 2009: Not only is the park lit up at night, but it's a family-friendly place for strollers, athletes, and dog owners alike. And the bad guys--they apparently prefer to go elsewhere--away from the nuisances of kids, dogs, joggers, and picnickers--a factor that has contributed to one of the lowest crime rates of any neighborhood in the area. And how about the growth of business over the past several years on 42nd Ave., and especially on Fremont Street, where the wide array of quality dining establishments, specialty shops, and health services attracts patrons from around the city and beyond!

From lights in the park to parallel parking and crosswalks adjacent to businesses, from the kids' play equipment and wall murals in the park to the slower speed limit on Fremont, and from the jogging path in the park to the enhancement of Beaumont Middle School building and grounds, BWNA citizen initiative got the ball rolling and oversaw projects to their completion. I encourage you to join us at BWNA in helping to shape the future of our evolving neighborhood.
