For those of you who’ve been shy about getting in the saddle, consider that bicycling provides many benefits to individuals and the community:

**Air that’s fit to breathe.** According to the EPA, about a pound of carbon dioxide is emitted for every mile driven. Imagine just how much air it takes to make a pound, much less having to breathe it. Seventy percent of car trips cover less than 3 miles, a perfect distance for bikes and one that’s probably faster, too, because cyclists don’t have to search for parking spaces.

**Bodies that are fit for life.** One in every two Americans is overweight or obese, with Type 2 diabetes on a scary rise among young people. Commuting to work avoids dreaded trips to the gym, makes exercise part of the daily routine, and shaves off the inches. Go ahead: Say you biked your butt off.

**A mood that’s fit for smiling.** Not only is cycling good for your body, it gives a boost to the mind. Studies show cycling relaxes the nervous system, improves the cyclist’s mood, and sharpens mental acuity. Another study demonstrated that car commuting elevates blood pressure, lowers tolerance for frustration (hello, road rage), and fosters negativity.

**Wallets fit for more fun.** A well-equipped commute bicycle will pay for itself within at least five months, saving on gasoline, car parking and maintenance. Many insurance companies also give credit to drivers who leave their cars at home.

It takes less than a half-hour to get downtown from Beaumont-Wilshire by bicycle. The Bicycle Transportation Alliance (http://www.bta4bikes.org/) has resources for bike commuters and holds talks on cyclists’ rights and the rules of the road. Once you start riding, you’ll wonder why you took so long.
