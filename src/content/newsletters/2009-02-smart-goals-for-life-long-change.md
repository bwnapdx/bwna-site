We all know diets rarely work for long term success; however, year after year we go back on them. Unfortunately, our society desires radical change and quick results, but many of us know first-hand, this can lead to equal if not greater weight regain. This is a hard pill to swallow, so we challenge you this year to go the way of the tortoise and not the hare – radical change replaced with slow and steady progress towards life-long change.

Careful SMART goal setting can help you do this

•Goals should be action-based versus weight based. Times you go to the gym, or number of glasses of water you drink, or number of vegetable you eat. Actions will help you lose weight without the intense focus on a number.

•Pick 1-3 small goals you are ready, willing and able to do NOW. Success will push you towards more success. For example, bringing your lunch 2 times per week versus bringing your own lunch every day. Every day is difficult as you adjust to a new behavior, but 2 times a week is more achievable NOW.

•Consider the 80/20 concept. Goals don’t need to be “always do without fail.” Instead, stick with it 80% of the time, allowing for unexpected events. Allow for reality without guilt of failure. Failure often leads to rash emotional decisions and undermines positive progress. Strive to be better, perfection is most often unrealistic.

Consider the SMART approach to your resolution making.

**Specific** – identify a specific action– break down large goals into baby steps versus an expansive over arching goal.

**Measurable** – your goal is something you can track and count.

**Attainable** – don’t overreach your bounds. Make sure it is within your ability to achieve. Is it doable? Is it an 80/20 behavior?

**Realistic** – you physically can meet your goal. Do you have the skills to achieve the goal?

**Time-based** – book end the time it takes to achieve your goal. This will give you a target to move towards. Don’t make it too short, like losing 20lbs in one week, but not too long that you lose focus over the time it takes you to achieve it.
