The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association and Friends of Trees are sponsoring a tree planting on **Feb. 17, 2007**. We are seeking homeowners who want trees and volunteers to help organize and plant. If you are interested in participating and haven’t submitted an interest form, please complete and send the form in this newsletter. All interest forms are due by **Nov. 1, 2006**. *(Form on page 4)*

Those who complete an interest form will receive a free inspection from the City’s Urban Forester who will advise on tree types and placement. Planting fees are $75 per tree, both street and yard trees will be available. The fee covers the cost of the tree, hole digging, assistance on planting day, stakes, twine, and follow-up monitoring. A slide show of available trees will be held in January – information to follow.

If you are able to help with outreach or on the day of the planting, please contact Colin Rowan at cmrowan1@yahoo.com or mail the form to Colin at 3305 NE 46th Ave. Portland OR 97213. We hope everyone gets involved in this year’s tree planting – whether you are receiving a tree or not. This is a great a way to help improve your neighborhood; hopefully you’ll jump in and help out!

For more information about Friends of Trees and the great work they are doing to increase the urban canopy in Portland, visit their website at www.friendsoftrees.org. See you on **Feb. 17, 2007**!
