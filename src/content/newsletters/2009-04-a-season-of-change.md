As a Financial Adviser with the John Hancock Financial Network, I am able to hear first-hand the anxiety and frustration that people are experiencing during these tough economic times. During difficult times, it is important to recognize and appreciate the people and events in our lives that bring us joy. Joy is contagious, and I encourage you to take action and share your appreciation for our community with others. In this issue, you will find many activities and events for little or no money that you and your family can enjoy. Your participation in community events builds strong relationships with others and also demonstrates the strength of our community. This strength is evident in the volunteer board members, committee members and entire newsletter staff who work so hard to sustain our community. Is it time for you to help? Join us on April 13th for the “Taste of Beaumont”, and find out more information on how you can make a difference. This is a perfect event to demonstrate your commitment to our community while you enjoy many free samples from restaurants in our area. The history of our community is a wonderful journey of individuals and their unique life paths, and I encourage you to share yours.
