A vehicle abandoned or stored on public right-of-way may be towed if one or more of the following conditions exist (City Code 16.90.005):

A. Parked over 24 hours

B. Expired tags

C. Vehicle inoperative or disabled

D. Vehicle wrecked, partially dismantled, or junked

The City of Portland operates an Abandoned Auto Hotline (503-823-7309). You may leave a confidential recorded message with the vehicle model, tag number, and location. They will investigate and place a warning on the vehicle. If stored, the owner is also notified by mail. If abandoned, it may be towed within 72 hours of notification.

More information is available at the Office of Transportation web site (http://www.trans.ci.portland.or.us/). Questions on abandoned autos? Call **503-823-6814**, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday.
