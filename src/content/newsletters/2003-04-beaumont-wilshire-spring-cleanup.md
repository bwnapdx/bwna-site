May 10, 2003, 8AM – 2PM
Wilshire United Methodist
NE 39th and Shaver

*We **WILL** accept:*
yard debris,scrap metal, tires, appliances, furniture, etc.

*We will **NOT** accept:*
glass, hazardous waste, garbage, wood or other house parts (sheet rock, roofing, etc).

The cost will be $10 per pickup/truck and $5 per car. Tires are $1 each or $2 if still on rim.
