> **BWNA General Meeting**
> **Monday, February 9, 7:30 PM**
> Bethany Lutheran Church, NE 37th & Skidmore
> All Beaumont Wilshire residents welcome!

The BWNA General Meeting for February will be augmented by two guest speakers who will share valuable information with our neighbors.

Pat Carroll, a Public Education Assistant with the Construction Contractors Board, will provide tips on remodeling your home, locating a licensed contractor, and avoiding construction fraud.

The Construction Contractors Board (CCB) is an agency that licenses and regulates construction contractors for the State of Oregon. They function as a consumer protection agency that provides free education for homeowners.

Typically, the CCB sees a rise in construction fraud in the spring and winter, especially after a winter storm. According to Caroll, con artists who operate during this time of year can be especially adept at targeting senior citizens.

You can access more information about the CCB at www.ccb.state.or.us.

Since most Multnomah County residents have now received the paperwork for the new County income tax enacted after passage of Ballot Measure 26-48 in May, we will also feature a presentation by employees of Multnomah County’s Finance Department who will be available to answer questions, provide forms and improve our understanding of the new tax, which is due on April 15th for the 2003 tax year.
