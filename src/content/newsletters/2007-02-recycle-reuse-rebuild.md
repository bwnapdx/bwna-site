**Monday, Feb 12th, 7:30 PM**
*Beaumont Middle School*
NE 42nd & Fremont

Come to the Feb 12th meeting and hear from Jennifer Jako about a unique and exciting resource in town called The ReBuilding Center. Whether you’re remodeling one room, re-doing a whole house, or putting in an urban strip mall, The ReBuilding Center is the place for you. Here you will find old moldings, doors, windows, tubs and other useful things, and this is where you can bring your ‘still usable but nor your style any longer’ building items.

Hear more about this non-profit organization, what it can do for you and what you can do for it. Also see the article on p. 7 for the history of The ReBuilding Center, the services it offers and its place in our community.
