Fremont Holiday Fest, proudly sponsored by the Beaumont Business Association (BBA), returns again to Beaumont Village on December 1st. Fremont Street will be bustling with entertainment, activities, and warm beverages. At 3:30 p.m. Santa and Mrs. Claus roar down Fremont in a fire truck and are available from 4:00 to 7:30 p.m. for photos. Reserved spots fill quickly, so visit www.beaumontvillagepdx.com to make reservations.

"It's always exciting to see neighbors come out and enjoy our business district. This is a great time to check out what's new on the street and get into the holiday spirit," said Andy Frazier, President of the BBA.

For more information and a schedule of activities, visit the BBA website listed above.
