According to comments from some of those who attended the annual national night picnic on August 1, this year was a huge success. Over 250 people showed up to enjoy great food brought by neighbors, and burgers and dogs prepared by board members and others. For the first time in years, the wading pool was filled with water for kids and adults to enjoy. Thanks to the hard work of board members, to Wilshire United Methodist Church for their tables and chairs, and to those who hauled them.
