We caught up with a few small business owners on NE Fremont Street to see how tariffs, the presence of ICE in Portland, or AI has affected their business.

Patrick of Curve CNC, a routing and wood fabrication studio at 3611 NE 50th Ave.

"Tariffs have not significantly impacted the business, and plywood prices remain stable compared to pandemic-era increases. As for AI, I haven't found an effective way to utilize it for research on competitors and other business."

Allyson of Pacific Holiday, a women's clothing consignment store at 4443 NE Fremont St.

"The shop has not been affected by tariffs or ICE-related disruptions, and I use AI as little as possible."

Lori from Body Image Fitness, a fitness and strength training business for all ages at 5046 NE Fremont St.

"Because the business relies on subcontractors rather than retail sales, tariffs have had little effect. I don't use AI, and ICE is not a relevant problem for me."

The BBA is a 501(c)(6) organization that advocates for its member businesses and works to promote the business district along NE Fremont Street from NE 38th Avenue to NE 50th. For more information about the BBA contact beaumontbusinesses@gmail.com.
