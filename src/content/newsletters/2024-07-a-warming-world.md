Even in a warming world
the heat is sometimes
a surprise

Of course, you say

We prefer to ignore what we can't control

Like sunspot surges on the sun

Or historic spikes in world temperatures

Still, when Portland's rain & grey
turn sunny suddenly

the battered spirit soars a bit

despite itself.
