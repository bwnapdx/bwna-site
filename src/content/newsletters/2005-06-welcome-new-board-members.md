At the April 11, 2005 general meeting, the following officers and board members were elected:
Vice president: Bill Markwart, secretary: Jim Karlock, members at large: Lew Evans, Debi Stanich, Heidi Zadeyan, Rev. Jeff Bethke, Al Ellis, and reelected were Liz Johnston and Mary Oldenburg.

Congratulations and thanks for volunteering to be part of the neighborhood association. The board will be having a retreat in June to plan for the coming year. All board members are listed on page two of the newsletter and on the neighborhood website, www.bwna.org/contact.html.
