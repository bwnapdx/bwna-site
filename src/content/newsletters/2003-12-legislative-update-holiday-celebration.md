These are three reasons to come to the Dec. 8 BWNA general meeting at 7:30 p.m. at the Bethany Lutheran Church, NE 37 at Skidmore. Rep. Jackie Dingfelder will update us on the achievements of the longest legislative session in Oregon history.

Neighbors are asked to bring cookies to share; homemade varieties are especially welcome. There will be an update on the upcoming Friends of Trees planting event in March 2004.

And a special election will be held to replace outgoing BWNA president Melissa Ritter who is moving out of state. All are welcome to this final meeting of 2003.

## BWNA General Meeting

Monday, December 8, 7:30 PM

Bethany Lutheran Church, NE 37th & Skidmore

All Beaumont Wilshire residents welcome!
