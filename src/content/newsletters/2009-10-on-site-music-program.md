My husband Chris and I are the owners of Sound Roots Music School in NE Portland as well as concerned neighborhood parents. Thankfully, these two worlds have recently collided to help save music education in Portland. As parents, we are deeply concerned about the budgetary cuts that are forcing our schools to cut music programs. As business people, we have created an innovative way to utilize our music school resources to do something about it.

Sound Roots On-Site is a brand new, pay-per-class program offering schools 8-24 week music programs in a wide variety of fun and interesting musical subjects, including Recycled Instrument Band, Musical Theater, and Electric Opera. The “a la carte” system allows schools with even the tightest budgets to afford a way to bring music to their classrooms. Classes are offered on location at each school both during the day and as an after school option so that no child is excluded.

The instructors of these classes are all professional teachers with a great deal of expertise in their field. Chris and I are highly selective in choosing our staff so that kids who are in these programs are not sacrificing a top notch musical education simply because their school does not have the funds to support a full or even part time teacher. The On-Site program is also a great opportunity for passionate teachers who are struggling to find work in this difficult economy.

Music has long been proven to help students in a variety of ways. In addition to helping to develop a well-rounded appreciation for the arts and culture, regular music education in a students’ curriculum has been proven to increase math and English scores. Sound Roots On-Site ensures that music is a part of every student’s education despite the current state of the economy- and that is something to sing about!
