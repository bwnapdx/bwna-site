Change is inevitable in most areas of life--no more so than with nonprofit organizations, which depend on volunteers for their very survival. One never knows when veteran volunteers will find it necessary to leave the organization for “personal reasons”--just as one never knows when new volunteers will make the decision to serve. But there’s something about the transition from the old year to the new that tips the balance for a lot of people. Priorities and time commitments are reexamined, with volunteerism often hanging in the balance.

Such was the situation for Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) last year between December and April, when six Board members resigned--including two officers (Secretary and Treasurer)--each citing job-related, family-related, and individual-interest-related reasons for their respective decisions. Additionally, we had one turnover after another in the position of BWNA Representative to the Central Northeast Neighbors Board (the umbrella organization that sets policy and assists with funding for eight different N.E. neighborhood associations). Suffice it to say, things seemed bleak at that point.

But as so often happens in life, just when everything appears to be going south, the pendulum abruptly changes direction--which brings to mind a classic song lyric from Crosby, Stills, Nash, & Young: “...And you know, the darkest hour is always just before the dawn...” Thankfully, that adage held true for BWNA last April when four neighbors volunteered to fill vacant Board positions and  three incumbent Board members did the same for the Secretary, Treasurer, and CNN Representative positions. Was it luck, fate, Beaumont-Wilshire (BW) “neighborhood spirit”....who knows? All that could be said for certain was that the bullet had been dodged for the time being—but for how long?

Answer: The pendulum has already swung. With two Board vacancies still remaining to be filled from last year, there is now a third, and it’s none other than our multi-talented Treasurer/Newsletter Editor Dan Johnson! A double whammy, it would appear, and pretty “dark” news for the neighborhood. But there’s a glimmer of the “dawn” in sight, I’m happy to report. While Dan’s increased time demands in his business are forcing him to resign from the Board, and thus from the Treasurer’s position, he believes it may still work out for him to continue on as Newsletter Editor, providing he gets some additional volunteer help.

Interested? I hope so. The Newsletter is not only one of the finest of its kind in the city, but one of BWNA’s primary outside revenue sources (from ad sales), and most importantly, BWNA’s most effective communication link with community residents. Volunteering to assist Dan and his ace publisher/graphic designer Debbie Schwing would be providing a huge service to our neighborhood, and you’d be walking into a well-organized system with expert direction. That was the pitch I made to those in attendance at December’s “Winter Holiday” General Meeting regarding the search for an “Assistant Editor”--but, frankly, I didn’t think much would come of it that night, figuring people would need some time to think it over. Then, out of the blue, BW resident Ethan Derner raised his hand and said simply, “I’ll do it.”

We’re looking for neighbors to follow Ethan’s example and step up to the BWNA plate. Board members are needed, a Treasurer is needed, and most immediately, more volunteers are needed to help Dan and his Newsletter staff. It’s a golden opportunity for you to become actively involved in the neighborhood association and give something back to our community.

Perhaps the “dawn” is once again just around the corner..
