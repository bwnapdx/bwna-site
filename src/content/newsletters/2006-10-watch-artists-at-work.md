October 14 and 15, artist studios west of the Willamette will open their doors. October 21 and 20, studios east of the Willamette welcome you in to their spaces.

Portland Open Studios 2006 is an opportunity to see how art is made. Ninety five artists in their own workplaces will share with you some of the secrets of their crafts. If you were ever curious to know what creative spaces look like or wanted to learn more about mediums such as glass, pottery, metal sculpture, painting and prints--here’s your chance!

In planning for the event, I’ve had the opportunity to meet some of these gifted artists and look forward to taking a peek into their studios, perhaps purchasing some art directly from the creator--a great experience! Some NE artists include: Lucia Johnson, Samir Sankari, Kelly Neidig, Rachelle Clark, Lauren Mantecon, Hillary Barsky, Jennifer Horning, Briana Linden and Judee Moonbeam

The official Portland Open Studios 2006 Tour Guide is available at Art Media Stores, New Seasons Market, Powell’s Books and other outlets listed on www.portlandopen-studios.com. It includes 2 adult tickets for all days (kids are free), a “Go by Bike” raffle ticket, a 2007 calendar, map to the studios and a complete artist directory guide, all for $15. Guides are available especially for Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood residents. For free delivery with purchase of your guide within the bounds of BWNA call: 503.285.1875
