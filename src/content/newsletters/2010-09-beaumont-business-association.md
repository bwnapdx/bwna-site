*Serving Business & Neighbors in NE Portland*

As I reflect on the 2010 Fremont Festival held Saturday, August 7th I can’t help feeling happy. This was my very first Fremont Festival but for many of you, it was your Best Fremont Festival ever! The Beaumont Business Association worked tirelessly this year to pull off some new things. We accomplished getting the street closed off which added so much to the day. Everyone seemed to enjoy strolling up and down the street, not dodging cars. The weather was just perfect and there was something for everyone. The day started off with a **5k Run sponsored by Foot Traffic** at 9:30 am. Next was the **Bike & Pet Parade.** Papa Murphy’s came through again to secure our favorite Fire Crew and their awesome Fire engine to lead the parade. Those kids really decked out their bikes… and the crazy outfits on the dogs! And the vintage cars joined in to make the parade perfectly complete.

Then, just as the song says…”let’s get this party started” the festivities began! Families filled the street with strollers, bikes, kids and pets. Vendors, food carts, bounce houses, face painting, petting zoo, horse & wagon. There was so much happening on the street end to end you had to walk it all to find out what you were missing. The **Main Stage,** sponsored by Doc George’s Jazz Kitchen, Bellamar Salon and Super Realty Group was the center hub. With back to back performers all day long… live music, fashion show, Salsa Dancers, Zumba Dancers and the Gypsy Dancers.

**Soluna Grill sponsored a Salsa Challenge** where 11 local residents entered their “Homemade Salsas” all vying for the $100 First Place Prize. Families & friends stopped by to taste the salsa’s and then cast their votes. John Holland took 1st Place with hi “SECRET SALSA”. Juli Miller of Trail Blazin Salsa took 2nd Place and Best Decorated Booth. Jeff Reya of Senior Jeffe’s Half Baked Salsa took 3rd Place.

Our **1st Annual Fremont Festival Pub Crawl** was awesome. The BBA had commemorative mugs, maps of participating establishments and tokens. The 7 establishments were McPeets, Pizzicato, Doc Georges, Soluna, Cha Cha Cha, Alameda Brewhouse and Bottles. Those holding the coveted green mug were fortunate enough to taste some of the best brews on tap.

The **Kid’s Zone** was another big hit this year. Sponsors of the Kids Zone, JMA Properties, Papa Murphy’s, Fremont Vet and Blackbird Wine Shop kept things rolling on the Bounce Houses. The Cake Walk, sponsored by Paper Jam Press and Bonnie’s Boutique was packed every game, with cakes donated by Sarah Behr of Soluna Grill.

Something’s we hope will never change and as in years past the **Horse & Wagon** was loved by all. Sponsored by Gazelle, Alameda Brew House and Fremont Veterinary Clinic, the horses, with their sweet temperament just meandered down the street waiting patiently as kids crossed.

**Behind the scenes** there was so much that went into planning and putting on this event. From executing the street closure, planning for trash, porta-potties to banners, advertising, printing and design. A lot of people spent their time and money to make it all happen. Besides those mentioned above I would like to thank these who worked behind the scenes. I’d like for you to know them, to frequent their business and say thank you. Albina Bank; Paper Jam Press; American Family Insurance; Found On Fremont; Songbird Freelance Design; Able Body Massage, Beaumont Market; Rosabode; MapClicks. These are the unsung heroes of the 2010 Fremont Festival! See you next year!
