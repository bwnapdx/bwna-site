This is the story of a family that has lived in our neighborhood for five generations. I met Howard (Howie) Boone while I was pruning trees at NE Shaver and 42nd for the Trelstad family. Howie told me he grew up in that very house. His grandparents, Fred and Mary Elsasser, escaped from Russia in the early 1900’s. Fred arrived in the US in 1907 and Mary in 1911. They married in the States and arrived in Portland in 1923.

They built a house on the southeast corner lot of Shaver and 42nd in 1924. This was mostly farmland and country, miles from downtown Portland. They raised seven children here. Helen, the youngest, married John Boone, a native Oregonian, whose family arrived in Oregon in 1918. After serving in World War II as a paratrooper, John and his wife Helen settled in the house now owned by the Trelstads, diagonally across from the Elsasser home.

Howie was the youngest of John and Helen’s three sons. The boys attended Beaumont and Grant, just as their mother had. When Howie went to Oregon State University in 1969, his parents moved to Lincoln City. In 1970 he married Sheri Jeffers, a friend from Grant High School, and they settled in the neighborhood. Their two children were the third generation of Boones to attend Beaumont and Grant.

Howie has held a variety of jobs. His favorite was in sales and he describes himself as a ‘jack of all trades’. When the children were grown, Sheri became office manager for Conklin Chiropractic (on Fremont). She is presently self-employed doing personal coaching. She has a degree in psychology from Marylhurst.

The property where Howie’s grandparents built their home stayed in the family and Howie’s uncle, Fred Elsasser, built apartments on the site in 1965. Helen and John Boone have since moved back from the coast and live in one of the apartments. Howie and Sheri live a few houses north on 42nd. Their two children and grandchildren live nearby making that five generations of the Elsasser/Boone family to live in NE Portland. That is some accomplishment!
