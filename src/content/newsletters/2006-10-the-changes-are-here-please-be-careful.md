We have brand new crosswalks at NE 50th, NE 48th, NE 46th and NE 44th; angled parking along NE 43rd, 44th, 45th, and 47th, and a reduced speed limit from 30 mph to 20 mph. These improvements to our business district were brought to the district through the hard work of the folks on the BWNA transportation and land use committee, the Beaumont Business Association, Debbie Bischoff from the Portland Planning Bureau and all the wonderful folks at PDOT.

I encourage all of you to obey the speed limit and drive 20 mph through the business district. I know it’s hard, but you can do it. Also, please stop for pedestrians when they are trying to cross the street. As for you pedestrians, you are much smaller than those cars, so look twice and be cautious before you cross the street. It doesn’t matter who is at fault, if a car hits you, you will definitely get the worse end of the deal.

Isn’t it great to live in such a wonderful neighborhood!
