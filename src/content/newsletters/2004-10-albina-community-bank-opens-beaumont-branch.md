Beaumont Village finally has its own bank – and it’s a special kind of bank. Albina Community Bank is one of only 55 certified community development banks in the country, and the only one in the Northwest. Now you can do your banking right here in the neighborhood and help your neighbors at the same time.

What is a community development bank? It’s a bank that has a special mission: to help local entrepreneurs create businesses and jobs, to help redevelop neighborhoods, and to help first-time buyers achieve their homeownership dreams. It’s a bank that gives back to the community. According to Yvonne Townsend, manager of the new Beaumont Branch, “it’s a bank for people who care about what happens with their money.”

Albina Bank is a bank that gives back to our community. For starters, the bank offers the “Scholastic Plastic” Visa card – 1% of all customer purchases is donated to the Portland Schools Foundation and the five public high schools in North and Northeast Portland. In addition, Albina employees donated nearly 4,000 volunteer hours in 2003 to local schools and nonprofits, such as the Community Cycling Center on NE Alberta, SOLV and Junior Achievement. The bank, through its commercial loans, business micro loans, and Small Business Administration loans, has been instrumental in the redevelopment of Martin Luther King Boulevard and Alberta Street. “All of the money stays here in Portland and supports the distressed areas of our community,” added Ms. Townsend.

The new Beaumont Branch has already shown its support for our own neighborhood with their Fremont Fest backpack giveaway. They gave away three backpacks full of school supplies, one each to an elementary, middle, and high school student. They have also made a contribution to Beaumont Middle School in the amount of $5 for each new account opened during Fremont Fest.

The new Albina Community Bank Beaumont branch opened June 14, 2004, and is located at 4020 NE Fremont.
