Each year, Solar Oregon presents more than 70 educational workshops throughout the state to empower homeowners, businesses and professionals to go solar. In Portland, Solar Oregon presents free, public workshops every month. This free workshop covers the basics of why solar is a smart choice for Oregon homeowners.

During the seminar, they will show you.

- How well solar works in Oregon’s climate
- Available solar technologies
- Financial incentives and tax credits
- How to choose a contractor

The next workshop is scheduled for:

- November 18, 2009 from 7:00pm to 8:00pm
- at Blackbird Wine Shop’s new location (4323 NE Fremont).
- Please RSVP at www.solaroregon.org.

Solar Oregon is also the resource for technical and financial information about going solar in Oregon. They provide public outreach at events, and offer numerous volunteer opportunities that allow you to connect with them to learn more about solar. They also partner with the Portland Office of Planning & Sustainability, Energy Trust of Oregon, Energy Trust New Homes Program, Oregon Department of Energy, Earth Advantage and many more to provide solar outreach and education across the state.

Solar Oregon’s website connects you to solar installers, architects, realtors and other professionals that can assist you with your project. They have the most comprehensive resource of solar related professionals in the state.

For more information, or to register for the upcoming seminar on November 18th, please visit www.solaroregon.org.
