Sales activity in the Portland area is continuing to bloom this spring. This April closed sales increased nearly 49% from the same time last year with close to 2,000 homes sold. Pending sales also increased by 60% with a total of 3,000 buyers entering into contract. The new listing numbers rose by 24% with a total of 4,700 homes for sale this April in comparison to last year, only 3,800 homes were available on the market. The month to month change in sales price in the Portland Metro area has been minimal with the average home price at $282,100 rising just 0.6% and the median value now at $240,000 up 0.5% from April to March of this year. It is taking on average about 4 months for new listings to sell.
