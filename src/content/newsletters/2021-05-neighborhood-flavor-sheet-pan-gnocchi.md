## Sheet Pan Roasted Gnocchi

**Ingredients:**

- 1 lb gnocchi (fresh, shelf-stable, or frozen)
- 1 lb mushrooms, sliced
- 2 shallots, chopped
- 3 Tbs olive oil
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- ¼ c grated parmesan cheese
- 1 lemon, zested

**Instructions:**

1. Mix (uncooked!) gnocchi with mushrooms, shallots, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
2. Spread out on a foil-lined sheet pan.
3. Bake at 400° for 20 minutes, stirring halfway through.
4. Garnish with parmesan, lemon zest, and more salt and pepper before serving.

Makes 4 servings. Enjoy!
