You are cordially invited to join your neighbors and plant trees in our neighborhood on February 11, 2006. Everyone in the community – all ages- is invited to this event – whether you are planting or receiving trees.

We will meet at 9am at the United Methodist and Native American Fellowship Church at 39th and Shaver. Joining us will be folks from the Cully and Concordia neighborhoods for this community-building event. There will be warm drinks and tasty treats to get everyone going and we hope to share a meal once the trees have been planted. There may be t-shirts for volunteers. Come – make new friends, show your child how to plant a tree and support the beautification of your neighborhood. By planting trees together we look into the future to see beyond our own lifetimes and share our positive vision with generations not yet born. And, we get our hands dirty.

For more information please contact albertkaufman@yahoo.com any hour of the night or day.
