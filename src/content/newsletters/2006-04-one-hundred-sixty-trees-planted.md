On February 11th the Cully, Concordia and Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhoods planted over 160 trees. We could not have done it without the 100 volunteers, Friends of Trees and the City of Portland. Also, many local businesses contributed culinary delights and monetary donations to make the day a wonderful community-building event. There was breakfast, lunch and free t-shirts for everyone.

If you didn’t get to plant a tree this time around, please plan to join us next time. And for those who did plant, there is information on how to keep your tree happy and healthy on the Friends of Trees website at:
http://www.friendsoftrees.org/tree_resources/maintenance.php

Photos from the tree planning can be viewed at
http://www.scn.org/neighbors/beacon/pdx/fot06/

Thanks to the many local businesses and individuals that contributed to this event. Too numerous to list here, please check www.bwna.ogs/events page for a complete list.
