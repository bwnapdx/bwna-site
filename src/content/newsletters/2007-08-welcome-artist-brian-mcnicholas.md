The newest member of Beaumont Artspace, BW’s online art gallery, is Beaumont Wilshire resident Brian McNicholas. Brian is a self-taught Oregon artist who enjoys drawing and painting animals, nature scenes and Native American portraits. Born in Portland, Oregon, he spent most of his childhood in Montana and has always been interested in Western history and particularly in the Plains Indians culture.

At an early age, he was exposed to the work of such great western artists as Charlie Russell, Olaf Carl Seltzer and Frederick Remington.

In his drawings and paintings of animals and people, he attempts to place them in authentic situations communicating a feeling for the subject itself. He studies his subjects closely and cares about the animals and people he draws. He feels that each has its own unique story and history.

Finally, he tries to present the animals and people he draws in a realistic style with the respect and attention to detail they deserve. See Brian’s work at http://www.bwna.org/artspace/brian_mcnicholas/index.htm
