Portland Public Schools has placed a temporary moratorium on the installation of rainwater infiltration features until they have in place a district wide maintenance policy. Because of this, the Beaumont Middle School courtyard project group decided to focus on another component of the transformation: seating for outdoor classroom activities.

With the professional assistance of *Ecoroofs Everywhere* the group is moving forward with design and installation of an outdoor seating facility situated under a small structure with an ecoroof. The engineering is in progress, material list is being compiled and permits were sought in late May. The construction is set for mid-June with installation of ecoroof and cob seating will occur in the fall.

The project group meets at Beaumont Middle School, Room #106 on Tuesdays at 5:45PM. All are welcome.
