At the April general meeting, those present elected Heidi Blischke as the new president of the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association. Heidi is active in neighborhood affairs, is on the Central Northeast Neighbors’ Board, and one of her goals is to revitalize the neighborhood watch program.

Also elected were David de la Rocha to the treasurer position, and the following residents to board positions at large: Jamal Baghdadi, Liz Johnston, Jenny Lisk, Wendy Martin, and Roger Price. Congratulations to all and thanks for committing time to the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood.
