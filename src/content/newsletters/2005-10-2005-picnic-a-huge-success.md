Thanks to all the board members and neighbors who worked so hard to make this year’s National Night Out Picnic a big success. About 250 BW residents enjoyed food and drinks donated by local business, and delicious home made treats brought by everyone who came. We want to especially acknowledge Liz Johnston and Heidi Blischke who ‘made it all happen’. But they were not alone!

David Friedman and Greg Schramm have been our chief grillers for the last few years; this year Erik Blischke and Keith Carlson assisted them. They also donated their grills and propane. Chairs and tables were donated by Wilshire United Methodist Church and Keith Carlson coordinated moving everything to the park on trucks provided by Jack Schrumm, Jim Schrumm and Erika Burr.

Jenny Lisk and Liz Johnston cut up onions, tomatoes and lettuce, Stanich’s restaurant helped with the bun orders. Treats were donated by: Beaumont Market, Safeway, Dollar Tree, Odwalla and Wild Oats. David Whitaker was responsible for soliciting door prizes. Thanks to him and the following businesses listed below.

Thanks again to everyone and see you next year at the best neighborhood picnic! See photos from the picnic taken by Helen Koba at:

http://www.bwna.org/photos.html
