**BWNA General Meeting**
Monday, October 11, 7:30 PM
Bethany Lutheran, NE 37th & Skidmore

Preston Wong, the new Central Northeast Neighborhood safety representative, will speak about safety issues in our neighborhood, homes, schools and park at the October BWNA general meeting. Also on the agenda is an update from the traffic/land use committee on areas of concern to neighbors as expressed at the picnic and in other meetings. Your questions, suggestions and input are welcome. The meeting takes place in the basement and the entry is on NE Skidmore.

## Proposed changes to bylaws

At the October meeting we will vote on an amendment to the BWNA bylaws. The proposed change adds a new item to Article 3, Section 1, as follows:

“E. Committee meeting decisions shall be determined by a majority vote of all committee members in attendance.”

As our committees become more active, it is necessary that our bylaws are clear and up-to-date. The purpose of the amendment is to clarify that general members vote at general meetings, board members vote at board meetings, and committee members vote at committee meetings. The first two are already clearly stated, but the last one is not.
