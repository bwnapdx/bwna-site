Have a speeding problem on your street? Know of an intersection that could use a couple stop signs? Concerned about the effects of bike boulevards, speed bumps, and curb extensions—or lack of such? Wonder how it’s all being financed? Well, here’s your opportunity to get some straight answers from people in the know. Come to Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association’s General Meeting on June 13th, beginning at 7:00pm, in the basement activity room (“Fellowship Hall”) of Bethany Lutheran Church (corner of NE 37th & Skidmore, across from Wilshire Park) to hear the latest from Portland Bureau of Transportation Project Manager Kyle Chisek and his team of experts on transportation projects affecting our neighborhood--followed by questions from the audience.

In addition to the focus on traffic calming concerns, the meeting will feature a presentation on “community engagement” by BW resident and community sustainability advocate Darcy Hitchcock. She has a success story to relate about the development of a strong community spirit along a 5-block stretch of Failing St. and will suggest steps that other groups of neighbors can take to achieve the same results.

Other topics for discussion will be upcoming summer festivals--including Movies in the Park on July 10th, All-Neighborhood National Night Out Picnic on August 2nd, Fremont Fest on August 6th, and the 42nd Street Fair at the end of August—and a neighborhood crime report from one of our local police officers.

Let your voice be heard, become informed, and help yourself to some light snacks and refreshments along the way.
