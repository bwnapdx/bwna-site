A sculpture by **Paul Arensmeyer** can be found at NE 45th and Fremont next to Kevin Cavenaugh’s building which houses the Fife restaurant on the main level and offices on the second level.

According to the artist, the piece is his reaction to the quiet repetitive architecture of the building and the man-made ‘natural’ beauty of the landscaping.

Arensmeyer is a Portland based artist who works exclusively with found objects. His work has been exhibited in the Northwest and around the US.
