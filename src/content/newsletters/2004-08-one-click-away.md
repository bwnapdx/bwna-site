The BWNA website is colorful, informative and up to date. The ‘Links’ page allows you to directly log onto other websites that may be of interest, such as: craigslist.org. (If you’re looking for work, trying to get rid of stuff or trying to get free stuff,) Oregon Zoo (upcoming Zoo concerts and events) PortlandMaps, TriMet (help plan your trip to downtown or Lloyd Center,) and others. On the same page you will find a list of handy phone numbers such as Water Main Breaks, Towing Information, Street Cleaning, Abandoned Cars, and others. All this information is in one place. Check it out next time you’re on the Internet: **www.bwna.org** —H. Koba
