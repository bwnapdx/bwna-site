Positive feedback from participants and onlookers alike regarding May’s “Neighborhood-Business Area Graffiti Cleanup” motivate Office of Neighborhood Involvement Graffiti Abatement Volunteer Coordinator Dennis Paul LoGiudice to collaborate in the organizing of a follow-up BW cleanup scheduled for July 30th. This is the Saturday preceding our neighborhood’s two biggest events--National Night Out Picnic in Wilshire Park and Fremont Fest. Typical of the expressions of appreciation by local businesses was an e-mail from Alameda Café owner Georjean Melonas thanking BWNA for “...your kind and generous efforts to contribute to our business community…” And the volunteers who systematically spread out in teams throughout the neighborhood to spray and scrub—including Graffiti Abatement Coordinator Marcia Dennis, North Service Zone Park Maintenance Supervisor Peggy Glascock, and City Commissioner Amanda Fritz—were equally appreciative for the opportunity to bond with neighbors in service to the community. Special thanks go out to Delphina’s Bakery and Starbucks for donating pastries and coffee, respectively, for the pre-cleanup meeting.

With characteristic enthusiasm, Neighborhood Emergency Team leader Liz Johnson summed up her graffiti abatement experience: “Had a great time!” Gazelle Natural Fibre Clothing owner Robin Ady expressed interest in the graffiti cleaner product being used, after which Liz made the suggestion that each business owner have his or her own bottle of the cleaner and instructions for “proactive” maintenance. The product, supplied by Marcia Dennis and her professional crew, is called “SoyClean Graffiti”—a soy-based mixture that is effective in removing graffiti paint while relatively gentle on the skin. BWNA is supportive of this idea and is spreading the word.

Volunteers are needed for the July 30th cleanup, as well as tips on where graffiti is located. As in May, participants will meet at 8:30am for pastries and coffee (or bottled water) at Bethany Lutheran Church (corner of 37th & Skidmore, in the basement activity room where BWNA general meetings are held), followed by instructions & cleaning supply distribution by Dennis LoGiudice and his professional crew. The cleanup will officially begin at 9:00am and end between 12:30 & 1:00pm If you would like to participate in the cleanup and/or report graffiti sites to be cleaned, please contact BWNA President Al Ellis at apelainjoy@aol.com. Also, if residents or storeowners want to have graffiti removed from their property by the professional crew, they need to phone Marcia Dennis at 503.823.5860 to make arrangements. (Although fire hydrants are off limits for our cleanup, if you send e-mail photos of hydrant graffiti along with street locations to Marcia Dennis at mdennis@ci.portland.or.us., she will forward the information to the Portland Water Bureau, which has responsibility for hydrant maintenance.)

**Join your neighbors in fighting back against graffiti vandalism!**
