Building on the success of the first cleanup last May, Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) once again collaborated with the Office of Neighborhood Involvement’s graffiti abatement staff in organizing a second cleanup for the last Saturday in July. Not coincidentally this follow-up was scheduled just prior to the National Night Out Picnic in Wilshire Park and Fremont Fest. With a handful of volunteers from the neighborhood and Graffiti Abatement Volunteer Coordinator Dennis LoGiudice providing supplies and advice, the morning began on a tasty note with pastries donated by Delphina’s and refreshments courtesy of neighbors and BWNA. Volunteers were assigned to different areas of the neighborhood based on graffiti sites reported to me during the previous two weeks. Most chose to work in small teams, some as individuals. The primary objectives were to use the soy-based liquid cleaner and scrubbers to dissolve and remove graffiti, razor blades to scrape off stickers, and paint to cover over graffiti on utility poles.

The team wore bright orange slipovers that identified them as “Graffiti Abatement Volunteers.” Thus, as they slowly made their way through their particular sections of the neighborhood, passers-by would take note and sometimes stop to say how much they appreciated this service to the neighborhood. One enthusiastic resident shouted out, “You are heroes!”

For information on how Graffiti Abatement can assist you in the fight against graffiti vandalism, contact Dennis LoGiudice at 503.823.9666 or Graffiti Abatement Coordinator Marcia Dennis at 503.823.5860. Although charged with the daunting task of serving all 95 of Portland’s neighborhood associations and their constituencies, they can be counted on as reliable partners. Ask any BWNA graffiti abatement volunteer!
