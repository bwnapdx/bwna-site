If you made a new year’s resolution to get more exercise in 2008 but have not acted on that resolution yet – this article may be for you! **Alameda Fitness Center** (AFC), located at 4016 NE Fremont, is a comfortable place to work out and get fit. And it is close enough for most of us to walk, run or ride our bikes to.

The facility opened in October 2006 and is owned by Kate Fischer and Dave Pietka. Alameda Fitness draws many of its members from the Beaumont Wilshire and surrounding neighborhoods, according to Kate. The main attraction, besides its easy to reach location, is the personalized services and its no pressure and non-threatening environment.

The main workout space is well equipped but not overbearing as are some bigger fitness places. AFC offers an array of services including:

- group fitness classes providing aerobic, core and strength training for all ages and abilities (Ex: pilates, yoga, preschool ballet, abs, spinning, etc.)
- individual workout sessions with one of three personal trainers OR one-on-one pilates or yoga instruction
- general workouts using cardio or strength equipment at your convenience
- nutrition consultations with Kate, who has a Master’s Degree in Exercise Physiology (OSU), and a Bachelor’s Degree in Dietetics (University of Wisconsin-Stevens Point).

Besides its convenient location, AFC has a low-key approach to membership. It offers member plans on a month to month basis. There is no joining fee or long term contract to sign. Class sizes are small, allowing for more individual attention. AFC is open seven days a week: M-F 6am-8pm. Sat 8am-4pm and Sun 10am-4pm. Rates are as follows: $49 per month for unlimited use of equipment and classes. $39 per month for senior (59 and over) or youth (through high school). Family members may join at an additional $20 per month. Visit AFC at 4016 NE Fremont or call 503-548-4011 for more information.
