The truth is in order to find a job or become promoted, you may need to become a better presenter and more confident team leader. You need the ability to talk and answer questions off the cuff. If you want to land a good position, you’ll need job interviewing skills. All of these skills can be learned in the supportive setting of a local Toastmasters.

Toastmasters is a nonprofit educational organization that helps you develop communication and leadership skills needed to reach your goals. Since its founding 85 years ago our organization has helped more than four million members worldwide. We meet Monday evenings 6:30pm at Concordia University. We look forward to seeing you.
