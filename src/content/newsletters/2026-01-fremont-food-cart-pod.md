**Fremont Garage**, the food cart pod under construction on NE Fremont Street at 44th, is scheduled to open in May, and the developer is inviting you to help with a few final decisions, like:

- Types of Cuisine
- Hours of Operation
- Specific Vendors You Recommend

The 3D renderings of Fremont Garage were created by Catherine Faye Interiors using Sketchup and Enscape, giving neighbors a preview of what the new food cart pod will look like when completed.

Take the survey to share your preferences and help shape this exciting new addition to the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood.
