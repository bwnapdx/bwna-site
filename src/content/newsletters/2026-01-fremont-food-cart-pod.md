**FREMONT GARAGE**, the food cart pod under construction on NE Fremont Street at 44th, is scheduled to open in May, and the developer is inviting you to help with a few final decisions, like:

- Types of Cuisine
- Hours of Operation
- Specific Vendors You Recommend

Take the brief survey at [surveymonkey.com/r/D2MVMV5](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/D2MVMV5).
