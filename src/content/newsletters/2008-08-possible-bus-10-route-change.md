Bus #10, which runs past Wilshire Park along 33rd Ave., is a popular line for neighborhood residents headed downtown. Tri-Met is studying the possibility of modifying that route in order to connect passengers with MAX light rail.

With an increased frequency of the MAX Red, Blue, and Green lines to every five minutes beginning in Sept. ‘09, Tri-Met believes Bus #10 passengers might benefit from a route linked directly to a MAX station. One of the proposals under consideration is to have the Bus #10 line routed to the Lloyd Center MAX stop, with passengers transferring to a MAX train for the fifteen minute ride into downtown.

Tri-Met spokespeople emphasize that this is far from being a done deal. In fact, the decision-making process has involved extensive surveying of Bus #10 passengers—both online and on the bus itself--to determine if they would be willing to transfer to MAX in exchange for improved service (i.e., enhanced frequency, later/earlier hours, etc.).

The final proposal from Tri-Met on a Bus #10 route change goes to the public in late September, ‘08, at which time a comment period will begin. An open house forum is also currently planned for late September.
