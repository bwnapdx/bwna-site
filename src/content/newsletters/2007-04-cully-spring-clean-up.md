**Saturday, April 28, 2007 from 9 AM – 3 PM**

Trinity Lutheran Church parking lot on 5520 NE Killingsworth.

**Acceptable items**: Yard debris, furniture, appliances without cooling units, mattresses, tires, plastics, carpets, rubber, cell phones, computers, clothes, metal and wood.

**NON Acceptable items**: Hazardous waste, gasoline, chemicals, oil, paint, tar, batteries, animal waste, liquids of any kind, under the sink garbage, also landscape and building materials

**Fees**: Car–$5, Van or SUV–$15, Pickup–$20; additional fees for oversized loads or trailers. Tires are $3 on rim and $2 off rim. Computer monitors $10 each.

This event is for residential vehicles only.
