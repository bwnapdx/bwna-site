BWNA board members will be working on the following neighborhood improvement projects this spring and summer and would love for you and your family to participate:

- a mural at Wilshire Park pavilion will be created and painted in April. This project needs people who like to draw and paint.
- the street painting at the intersection of NE 37th and Shaver will be repainted in May
- Beaumont Middle School “Learning Courtyard” is still in the works and needs interested neighbors to give their input and lend a hand.

Contact Ankist Zadeyan at 503-757-6360 to get involved.
