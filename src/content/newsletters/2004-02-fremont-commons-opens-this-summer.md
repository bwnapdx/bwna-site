Construction of a new development at the corner of NE 46th and Fremont St. commenced late this fall. The Fremont Commons Limited Liability Company (LLC) is constructing the Fremont Commons, which will be the newest addition to a thriving Beaumont Village. Tim Ray, owner of Sundance Restoration and Development, and Brad Malsin, owner of the recently refurbished Eastbank Commerce Center are partners in the venture.

As reported in the September newsletter, the plans for the Fremont Commons call for the construction of two 3,600 square foot buildings with a twenty-four foot wide courtyard between the buildings. The courtyard will face NE Fremont St, and will have a clock tower at the back of the courtyard. The tower will house an elevator. The storefront retail will have roll up glass garage doors that allow the patrons to spill out onto the sidewalks. A special drill rig will be on site in the next month to drill down to reach ground water that will be used in a heat pump to generate energy to heat and cool the buildings.

Tim Ray, who was interviewed for this article, expects the Fremont Commons to be open for business by June 2004. They have a number of tenants already signed on to move into the new facility. No names for the new shops were available, but Mr. Ray did share the type of tenants that will be moving in. The ground floor will be occupied by an ice cream store, a tea and spice purveyor, a pet supply store and dog training facility, an optician, and the Aveda Salon will be moving up the street to occupy a new space in the Commons. The one tenant that has leased space on the second floor will run a child-parent yoga and movement business.

With all these new businesses moving into the Village, there will be more people than ever strolling through our wonderful neighborhood. Please remember to drive slowly and safely along Fremont and on all other streets in the neighborhood.
