With summer warm weather, many people are outside in their yards or in the park, or biking or walking, which provides a great opportunity to meet neighbors. Please take a moment to greet someone new in our neighborhood! And better yet …

Join us for the annual neighborhood National Night Out (NNO) picnic in Wilshire Park on Tuesday, August 7 from 6:30 until 8:30pm. We’ll have a fire truck, local police, live music, raffle give-a-ways, and free hot dogs, hamburgers, gardenburgers and beverages. Bring a side dish or dessert. It’s always loads of fun and well attended. You can meet and visit with neighbors (typically 200-300 attend), city representatives and elected officials, your BWNA board members, local business people and others. Take a moment to talk to your neighborhood police officers and to pick up some tips for crime prevention, including ways to avoid being a victim of identity theft.

Special thanks to the BWNA board members, local businesses, and others who help make the NNO picnic possible.

Please watch the BWNA newsletter and website (www.bwna.org) for upcoming speakers and events. Do you have a topic or issue to discuss, or a suggested speaker for our general meetings? We would like to hear from you!

I look forward to seeing you at the picnic!

–*Bill Markwart*
Acting President, BWNA
president@bwna.org
