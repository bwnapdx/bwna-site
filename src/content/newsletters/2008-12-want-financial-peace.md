Financial Peace University (F.P.U.) is a life-changing program that teaches people how to make right decisions about money. It empowers with practical skills and confidence to achieve financial goals and experience true financial peace. Thirteen lessons deal with a different topic that will help change the way you think about personal finance. Dave Ramsey (daveramsey.com) is the instructor. Learn how to save money, live on a budget, communicate about money, eliminate debt, find bargains, and experience the joy of giving.

A free preview class is scheduled for Thursday, Dec. 11 at 7 PM at Bethany Lutheran Church (4330 NE 37th Ave) with no obligation. Classes are scheduled for Thursdays from 6:30-8:30 pm beginning January 8, 2009. Call the coordinator, Jeff Bethke, at 503.284.3836 for more information.
