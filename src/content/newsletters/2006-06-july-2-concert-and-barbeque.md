The Portland Community Wind Band, in conjunction with the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association presents an afternoon of Patriotic Wind Band Music. On **Sunday, July 2, at Wilshire Park**, please join us for a pot-luck style barbeque at noon and some great music at **2:00 p.m.** There is no charge for this event, and we welcome families and friends of all ages.

The Portland Community Wind Band rehearses at Beaumont Middle School on Wednesday evenings during the school year, and is currently seeking members for next year’s group. Advanced players under 18 are welcome with audition, grown-ups just e-mail for further details. PortlandCommunity.WindBand@comcast.net. Check out our website: www.PCWB.org
