Try scheduling an indoor meeting for July or August, and consider yourself fortunate to attract the minimum number necessary for a quorum. No surprise there. Portlanders understandably want to make the most of their rain-free, sunshiny days while they can, and Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) is on board. Thus, while General Meetings are normally held on even-numbered months, August has become the exception--with the General Meeting replaced by what is now one of the most popular events in our neighborhood: the **National Night Out Picnic in Wilshire Park**, held on the first Tuesday of August (this year falling on the 3rd), from 6:30pm-8:30pm

National Night Out celebrations are ostensibly a show of support by neighbors united against crime, and, as you might expect, are well attended by local police officers, fire fighters, and politicians. But what’s especially impressive about the Beaumont-Wilshire (BW) Picnic is the large number of families participating--usually swelling the crowd into the hundreds--and making this event, by far, the largest BWNA-sponsored community gathering of the year.

At the heart of BWNA’s National Night Out celebration is the all-neighborhood pot-luck picnic. Everyone attending is encouraged to bring a dish to share with neighbors--chips, dips, salads, entrees, desserts--whatever people feel like bringing. For its part, BWNA supplies tables, chairs, beverages, snacks, and all the hamburgers, veggieburgers, and hotdogs your family can eat! There will also be musical entertainers, special activities for the children (including a fire truck in the park to climb onto), free massages, a police dog unit, and much more. The evening culminates with the raffling of prizes donated by local businesses. (Raffle tickets are free, but you have to be present to claim your prize.) Local businesses also contribute ice cream and other treats to the pot-luck feast.

So mark **August 3rd, 6:30pm-8:30pm** on your calendar now and spread the word to your neighbors. It’s a neighborhood park experience unlike any other--guaranteed to satisfy the most demanding appetite and leave you smiling.

(Please mark your bowls and dishes on the bottom so they can be identified and returned if left behind. BWNA President Al Ellis will be in charge of lost and found items and can be reached at 503.287.0477.)
