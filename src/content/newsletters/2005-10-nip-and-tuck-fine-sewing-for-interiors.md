Love your sofa, but think it could use a ‘face lift’? Nancy Gilpin can transform your slightly worn but loved sofas and chairs into works of art. Nancy and a staff of five assistants work their ‘magic’ at her shop ‘Nip and Tuck’ which has been located at 4427 NE Fremont for the last 16 years. Nancy started her sewing career making clothes, but realized early on that her passion was in creating lovely things for people’s homes.

Nip and Tuck is best known for slipcovers, something that a lot of shops no longer do. When I visited the shop recently, she and her staff were busy creating lovely drapes for a ‘body and beauty salon’ in downtown Portland. There were yards of gorgeous dark blue and olive satin material that were being turned into dramatic, glorious drapes. Her customers come from near and far, some live in other states. One of the trends she has noticed is that people are ordering drapes for windows, where before they may have used shades or blinds. Also, she notices that as people are becoming more environmentally aware, they prefer to reuse old curtains, make them into pillow covers or something else, rather than just toss them.

Her work has been featured in six national magazines such as Home and Garden, House Beautiful, Home Interior and others. She does very little advertising and gets most of her customers by word of mouth. Personal references are very important in this kind of business. She loves her location on Fremont. Her client base is loyal; she gets many customers from the walk by traffic as people visit many of the adjoining businesses along this busy street. If you are looking for something special, unusual and well made to spruce up your home, visit Nip and Tuck 4427 NE Fremont 503-281-1781.
