A frozen yogurt shop, the first of its kind in the neighborhood, plans to open on Fremont Street in mid-July.

YoChoice Self-Serve Frozen Yogurt, located at 4941 NE Fremont, next to Jim and Patty’s Coffee, will feature half a dozen frozen yogurt flavors and over 40 toppings, including fresh fruit, granolas and dozens of other tasty treats.

“Our concept is to offer customers a nice variety of frozen yogurt flavors and literally dozens of toppings, letting them choose any flavor and topping combinations they’d like,” explained Andre LaGrande, owner of YoChoice. “After customizing their frozen yogurt, customers bring it to the check out counter to be sold by weight. It’s $.40 cents an ounce for all yogurt and toppings. People will have the option of sitting inside to enjoy their yogurt creation, or sitting outside to enjoy one of Portland’s many sunny days.”

LaGrande, who grew up in the neighborhood and graduated from Grant High School, said frozen yogurt is enjoying a renewed popularity because it is non-fat, delicious and healthful. He said self-serve is a new concept in frozen yogurt shops that originated in California and is now spreading rapidly across the country.

“One of the leading frozen yogurt suppliers in the nation is YoCream International, located in NE Portland,” LaGrande said. “It is a local company producing a high quality product and I’m proud to be offering YoCream in my shop.”

YoChoice Self-Serve Frozen Yogurt will rotate flavors frequently and invites customers to suggest flavors they’d like to try. The shop will also offer fun events like trivia night or possibly movie night. Furthermore, group activities like organized bike rides or fun runs are invited to begin or end their events at the shop.

To follow the progress of the shop and plans for the opening, go to www.yochoiceyogurt.com or contact Dan LaGrande at 503.784.9059.
