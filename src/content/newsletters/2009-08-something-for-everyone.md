The Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) invite you to the annual BWNA Wilshire Park picnic in celebration of National Night Out (neighbors united in crime prevention). It's the biggest neighborhood event of the year, featuring a veritable feast of picnic foods, entertainment for adults and kids, free massages from neighborhood massage therapist Johannes Venghaus, a door prize raffle, and a chance to interact with local political leaders, fire fighters, and police officers.

The fun begins at 6:30pm on Tuesday, August 4th, and concludes at 8:30pm. Everyone is encouraged to bring some kind of dish to share with your neighbors (e.g., chips, salad, side dish, entree, dessert). BWNA will provide barbequed burgers (both meat and veggie), hotdogs, condiments, bottled water, plates, utensils, & napkins. The ice cream man has been alerted as well!

**Please mark your dishes on the bottom so they can be returned to you if they get left behind.** BWNA President Al Ellis has volunteered to be in charge of lost and found items. You can reach him at 503.287.0477.

**So plan on bringing your family and spread the word to your neighbors. It's an evening in the park that is guaranteed to satisfy your appetite and make you smile.**
