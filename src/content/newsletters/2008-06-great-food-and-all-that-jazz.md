The Beaumont Student Jazz Band under the direction of Cynthia Pitts played at the April BWNA community meeting held in the cafeteria of Beaumont Middle School. Those who attended also got to taste some very tasty dishes from a few of the restaurants in the BW neighborhood. We want to thank the owners of the following businesses for bringing their treats to the meeting:

- Rose’s Ice Cream 5011 NE 42nd
- Bumblekiss 3517 NE 46th
- Ottoman Klasik 5015 NE Fremont
- Round Table Pizza 4831 NE Fremont
- Cha Cha Cha Mexican Taqueria 4727 NE Fremont

Say ‘thanks’ next time you stop at these places for a treat.
