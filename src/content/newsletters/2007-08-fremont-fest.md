Saturday, August 4th

The businesses along NE Fremont wish to thank the neighborhood residents by inviting them, their guests and all Portlanders to the annual fun event – the Fremont Fest! Businesses along NE Fremont St. from 40th to 50th will be participating in the all day event.

The event begins at **10:30am** with a kids and pets parade featuring the Beaumont Middle School Band. Arrive by 10am if you wish to participate in the parade! There will be free horse drawn carriage rides, live music and an artists’ market. Come join the fun, listen to the music, and sample great food and beverages from the restaurants along Fremont. There will be sales, giveaways and just lots of fun. See on the August 4th!
