Three years ago when Steve Burgus was seeking an insurance company to represent, he chose **American Family Insurance**. “I liked their customer service emphasis and model of providing comprehensive services,” says Burgus, who has spent fifteen years in the insurance field.

Although American Family, a Fortune 300 company, is one of the four largest insurance companies in the country, it provides insurance to families through independently owned neighborhood insurance centers. **The Steven Burgus Agency** employs three licensed agents, including Burgus, who sell auto, home, renter’s, business liability, disability, and life insurance. As a registered representative with American Family Securities LLC, Burgus also sells annuities and variable life insurance products. The American Family motto is “All Your Protection Under One Roof.”

“At our agency relationships with clients are everything,” says Burgus. “We don’t have an 800 number and guarantee a same-day call-back if an inquiry is received before 5 o’clock.” Recently the agency won a J.D. Power award for customer service. J.D. Power polls clients to rate a company’s customer service and reliability. To receive an award, a company must score 8-10 on a 10-point scale for each item.

Burgus and fellow agents write few insurance policies outside the area. “We specialize in insuring people in Beaumont Wilshire and 75 percent of our clients come from this area.” he says. His agency tailors policies to meet the specific needs of BW unlike agencies outside the area that are less familiar with the neighborhood and may characterize it as an area of older, rundown homes. “We know that isn’t true, so we are able to provide home owners with more coverage at better rates.”
