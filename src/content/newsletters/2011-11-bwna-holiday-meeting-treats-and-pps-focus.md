It seems a world away now—that pristine summer evening in early August when neighbors flocked to Wilshire Park with home-prepared picnic dishes for our National Night Out gathering. But take heart. The winter holiday season ushers in yet another opportunity for an all-neighborhood pot-luck gathering, this time at Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association’s (BWNA) Winter Holiday General Meeting. On the evening of Monday, December 12th, residents are encouraged to bring winter holiday desserts and snacks for sharing, with BWNA supplying refreshments, additional goodies to nosh on, and plates, utensils, etc. The festivities begin at 7:00 PM in Bethany Lutheran Church’s “Fellowship Hall” (a basement activity room on the Skidmore Street side of the church, located at the corner of 37th and Skidmore across from Wilshire Park).

Highlighting the meeting agenda will be an examination of the evolving redesign plan for Portland Public Schools, with an emphasis on what the future may hold for the neighborhood’s own Beaumont Middle School. Featured speakers will be Beaumont Middle School Principal Elizabeth Casson-Taylor and community organizer Mary Jaron Kelley. Principal Casson-Taylor will provide a “state of the schools” assessment, including the latest on proposed boundary changes, as well as personal commentary on achievements and challenges at Beaumont Middle School. Mrs. Kelley adds a parent’s perspective, providing information on volunteer opportunities at the middle school and ongoing PTA activities and service projects. The audience is encouraged to ask questions and share views following the presentations.

In addition to the “state of the schools” forum, local police officers will give their regularly-scheduled crime report and address residents’ concerns. Agenda time is also reserved for updates on local land use and traffic safety issues, as well as an opportunity to make suggestions and provide input to the Board regarding future speakers, activities, and projects.

So get into the holiday spirit and come participate in this tasty and informative evening. Bring an after-dinner treat to share, learn more about Portland’s educational scene from those closest to it, and contribute with your comments, questions, and ideas.
