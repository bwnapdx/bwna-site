Is there ever a time when you either don’t get or can’t find the current issue of the BW neighborhood newsletter? Do you ever want to re-read an article from a past issue but didn’t save it? The rain ate your newsletter before you could rescue it from the front porch and it is totally unreadable? Not to worry!

You can access each current issue as well as past issues of the BWNA newsletter online on the neighborhood website www.bwna.org. The current and most recent issue can be reached from the front page where there is a direct link to both, just click on the month you want. You can also click on the ‘newsletter’ icon and it will take you to the page which has links to every issue published since June/July 2000. Good for research or for just checking out what was going on in BW way back then. Happy reading.
