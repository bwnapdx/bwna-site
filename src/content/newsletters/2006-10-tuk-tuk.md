This spring, **Tuk Tuk** Thai Restaurant celebrated its third anniversary in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood. Tony Babpha moved his downtown restaurant, Bai Tong Express, to **4239 NE Fremont** where Kim Hong had been located and renamed it Tuk Tuk.

Tony is originally from Laos and lived in Thailand before coming to Portland in 1991. He also studied in Bratislava, Czechoslovakia in the 1980’s. He learned Thai cooking from his sister who owned a restaurant in Bangkok where Tony worked for a few years.

Tuk Tuk offers an extensive menu of regular Thai dishes such as Pad Thai, fried rice dishes and curries. The restaurant also offers specialties such as Pad Thai Curry, Pumpkin Curry, Sizzling Fried String Beans, Ginger Halibut, Sweet Sticky Rice with Mango and Pla Tod Rad Prik – a whole fish fried and topped with garlic and chili sauce. True to his roots, Tony also offers a green house salad Lao style, and a green papaya salad, Lao or Thai style. All dishes are prepared with fresh ingredients with no preservatives or MSG.

Neighborhood folks make up most of Tuk Tuk’s clientele, but its reputation has spread and draws regulars from Beaverton, Gresham and even Hood River. Tony and his wife welcomed son Newton to their family recently.

Tuk Tuk is located at 4239 NE Fremont 503-282-0456. It is open seven days a week from 11:30 am to 9:30 or 10:00pm. Check it out soon.
