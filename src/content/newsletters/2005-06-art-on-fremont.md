Thanks to the following businesses who exhibited works by BW artists and students during the month of May: **Jim and Patty’s, Alexander’s, Tri Level Fitness, Alameda Café, Java Man, Parisi’s, Criollo, Foxfire Tease, Fife, Red Fig, Bella Flora, Edward Jones Invest. Tuk Tuk, Cool Runnings and Albina Bank**. Many of these had opening receptions for the artists with live music and treats. A special thanks to Tim Ray and the businesses in the Fremont Commons for allowing the artists to display works in the courtyard. The artists are grateful for all the community and business support. To view photos from this event, check the neighborhood website, **www.bwna.org.**
