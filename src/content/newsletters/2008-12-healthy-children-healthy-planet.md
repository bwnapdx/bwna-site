Fremont United Methodist Church is hosting a Northwest Earth Institute discussion group, Healthy Children, Healthy Planet. The course goals are:

- To understand how the pervasive effects of advertising, media, and our consumer culture can influence a child’s view of the world.
- To discover ways to create meaningful family times and healthful environments for children.
- To explore ways to develop a child’s connection to nature, and to foster creativity.

Whether you have young children, older children, grandchildren, or even no children, this course will really make you think about how our consumer culture affects us all. Meetings will be held on Tuesday evenings, January 13 through February 24 at 7 pm. Books are $18; contact me, Michele Bernstein, at MicheleLB@fremontumc.org or 503.284.4647 to register. Fremont UMC is located at 2620 NE Fremont St. A more detailed description of the course can be located at http://nwei.org
