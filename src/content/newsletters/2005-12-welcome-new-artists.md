Artspace, part of the BWNA website, is dedicated to local artists. There is a “gallery” space with a sample of each artist’s work with contact information. Also listed are upcoming art events in which our members are involved.

Recently, we added four new members, Margaret Engel, Thomas Herring, Nancy Larson, and Robyn Waters.

*Margaret Engel*, a BW resident for 11 years, has studied art from the age of 12, continuing in high school and university. Busy with her career in the financial field, she only recently returned to painting, with encouragement from her husband. Margaret paints flowers and abstracts. She has work on display in Germany.

*Thomas J. Herring* did his first drawing, “Midnight” at 3. Cartoon characters were the inspiration for the developing, largely self-taught artist. For 20 years now Thomas has used the airbrush along with regular brushes to make his art. Thomas’ work has appeared in several airbrush publications. He has shown recently at Fife Restaurant on NE Fremont and Big Kahunas in St. Johns.

*Nancy Larson* holds a BA in art from California State University, Stanislaus and did one year post graduate work at the Accademia di Belle Arti, Florence, Italy. She has participated in group shows in California, Italy and Portland. Nancy works in watercolor and oil. Some of her pet portraits can be seen at Green Dog on Fremont. Nancy has lived in Portland for 10 years.

*Robyn Waters* works primarily with Caran d’ache (artist’s crayon) on black matte board. She is currently working on a series of acrylic paintings. Two years ago, Robyn went to rural Zimbabwe to work with local women artists, teaching and helping them use their art for income. Her work appears regularly in the We’moon Calendar. Upcoming events include: Quick Draw in Cannon Beach and a show at Dragon Fire Gallery.

Enjoy your neighborhood talent by going to http://bwna.org/artspace/.
