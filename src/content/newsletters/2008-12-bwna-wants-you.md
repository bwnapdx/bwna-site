The Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) board is looking for volunteers to serve in 3 important positions:

1.) There is a vacancy on the BWNA Board itself. The term of office for a Board member is two years, and the commitment includes attending monthly Monday evening meetings and participating actively in Association activities and decision-making.

2.) Central Northeast Neighbors (CNN) is a coalition of several neighborhood associations in the region. It provides each neighborhood association with its main funding, as well as expertise and services in a wide variety of areas. The CNN Board includes two representatives from each neighborhood association. BWNA began last summer with two CNN representatives but now only has one. Volunteering to serve as a CNN representative involves attending meetings on the first Wednesday of each month and reporting back to BWNA Board meetings on the second Monday of odd-numbered months.

3.) Friends of Trees has again invited BWNA to participate in the planting of trees in the neighborhood, but in order to participate, BWNA needs someone to volunteer to be its Neighborhood Coordinator. This position initially involves attending a one-time 90-minute training session and serving as the link between BWNA and Friends of Trees. The object of the program is to get homeowners excited about planting trees on their property and working with other Neighborhood Coordinators to get the trees planted. About 90 trees were planted in the area last year, and Neighborhood Coordinators averaged 70 hours of volunteer work throughout the planting season.

All three of these positions contribute greatly to the success of BWNA. If you are interested in being nominated or simply finding out more about the commitments involved, please contact BWNA President Al Ellis at 503-287-0477.
