The National Night Out Picnic at Wilshire Park last August 3rd was by far the most well-attended… Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) -sponsored event of the year--with an estimated crowd of between 300 and 350--and the well-traveled adage “It takes a village!” certainly applies to the teamwork needed to pull it off: First, there were the members of the BWNA Board, who provided the leadership-organizing logistics and dividing up responsibilities among themselves and neighborhood volunteers; second, there were the local businesses that contributed picnic prizes for the raffle and samples of their cuisine; third were the hundreds of neighbors who provided potluck picnic dishes to share with their fellow neighbors; and last, but certainly not least, were the always-prepared, can-do Boy Scouts and Scoutmasters from Troop 107, assisting with the transporting of tables and chairs to and from Wilshire United Methodist Church, setting up for the picnic, and cleaning up afterwards. Additionally, to the crowd’s approval, City Commissioner Amanda Fritz offered words of greeting, David De La Rocha and his funky band, “Kpanstk” (a.k.a.--the “Blunt Instruments of Justice League of America”) played traditional favorites, David’s daughter & friends performed on a trapeze suspended from a nearby tree, and students from the U.S. West Coast Taekwondo Association Family Martial Arts Center (in Hollywood) displayed their prowess in breaking blocks of wood. Meanwhile, neighborhood children delighted in the distribution of free pinwheels and bubble-blowing kits, courtesy of BWNA. The picnic concluded with scrumptious dessert--two beautifully decorated cakes from QFC and Whole Foods, respectively--and a free raffle of prizes provided by area businesses. What a great evening of fun and solidarity it was for the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood!

Special thanks go out to the following individuals and businesses who contributed mightily to the success of the picnic: the Troop 107 Boy Scouts and Troopmasters who provided the heavy lifting and even assisted with BWNA’s barbecuing; Wilshire United Methodist Church congregant Helen Stewart for facilitating the use of the church’s tables and chairs; Board members Bob McKibben and Corky Elsasser for transporting tables, chairs, and grills in their pickups; Beaumont-Wilshire resident Diane Storer and family for use of their grill; Board members Dan Johnson and Tom Melville as the primary barbecue cooks; and the following businesses for their generous donations (in alphabetical order)--Alameda Cafe, Amalfi’s Italian Restaurant, Beaumont Market, Green Dog Pet Supply, Marco Polo Designs, Papa Murphy’s Pizza, QFC (Hollywood), Umpqua Bank, Wilshire Market, and Whole Foods (Hollywood).
