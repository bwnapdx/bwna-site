Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood Association (BWNA) Bylaws require that a Board Meeting be held each odd-numbered month and that a General Meeting be held on each even-numbered month, except for August, when the National Night Out Wilshire Park Picnic takes precedence. But no specific day of the week or time is specified: “The meetings shall be convened at a time and day decided upon by the majority vote of the Board...” The longstanding BWNA tradition has been to hold meetings on the second Monday of the month, and Board members agree that consistency is the best policy. However, there’s a potential Catch-22: In order to conduct business, the Bylaws require a quorum (minimum of 12 at a General Meeting, simple majority at a Board Meeting), and if the second Monday of the month just happens to fall on an evening when most people choose not to attend due to a holiday or other special event conflict, then what we’re left with is “consistency for the sake of consistency”--and a meeting that ends up being postponed anyway. Well, for the first time that any veteran Board member can remember, such a circumstance has presented itself--not just once, but twice in a row!

The second Monday of January--the 10th--would normally be when our Board Meeting is held. But on that same evening, an unprecedented event takes place for Oregonians: U. of O. plays Auburn for the National Championship! Accordingly, the Board, anticipating the lack of a quorum, has rescheduled its meeting for the first Monday in January--the 3rd. Likewise, the February General Meeting has been rescheduled to the first Monday in February--the 7th--because the second Monday of the month is Valentine’s Day! So, in summary: Board Meeting is switched to January 3rd; General Meeting to February 7th. (These changes have been posted online on both the BWNA and Central Northeast Neighbors calendars).

Board meetings are open to the public, and we welcome your participation. The agenda for the January 3rd Board meeting will include preparation for the February General Meeting vote on proposed Bylaws revisions (details below), plans for this summer’s “Movies in the Park” event in Wilshire Park, and consideration of a grant proposal by Board member Colin O’Neill to enhance neighborhood communication via the BWNA Web site.

The agenda for the February 7th General Meeting will include a presentation on in-home energy-saving tips by Donald Shaw of Home Energy Solutions (postponed from December’s General Meeting due to Don coming down with a bad cold), a report from BWNA PPS Boundary Committee Representative Tracy Johnson on potential changes affecting Beaumont M.S., Alameda Elementary, and Sabin (K-8), a review of the incidence of crime in our neighborhood by local officers, planning for April’s Taste of Beaumont/BWNA Elections General Meeting, and a discussion and vote on proposed Bylaws revisions.

The entire text of the current Bylaws along with proposed revisions can be accessed on the Association’s Web site www.bwna.us. (Click on “About” at the top of the home page, then on “Bylaws of BWNA”) Here’s an overview of the main changes under consideration:

1.) Change the way decisions are made: At present, there’s no differentiation in the bylaws regarding voting on a proposal at a General or Special Meeting and voting on a proposal at a Board Meeting. The only stated requirement for passage is a majority vote of those in attendance, providing there’s a quorum (minimum of 12 residents 18 or older at a General Meeting and a majority of the Directors present at a Board meeting).

However, the Office of Neighborhood Involvement (which oversees all neighborhood associations in the city) provides the following guideline for Boards of Directors: “...The Board has the responsibility of acting in the best interest of the neighborhood but is not specifically bound to act according to the desire of the majority of members attending a particular meeting.” The proposed revision would add this language to the Bylaws and make votes taken at a General Meeting advisory, subject to final approval by the Board.

Commentary: Proponents point out that Board members are elected by the neighborhood to represent and make decisions for the neighborhood as a whole, not simply to rubber stamp proposals approved by residents who choose to attend a particular General or Special Meeting. Opponents counter that while representative democracy is a worthy goal for BWNA, the reality is much different, with Board members elected to their positions by a very small percentage of neighborhood residents; moreover, the prospect of effecting change through voting at General Meetings serves to motivate residents to become more engaged.

2.) Elimination of term limits: At present, Board members are limited to three consecutive two-year terms.

The proposed revision would remove this restriction from the Bylaws, thus affording Board members the opportunity to be reelected to their positions every two years for as long as they are willing to serve.

Commentary: Proponents maintain that the Board has had problems attracting candidates and can’t afford  to lose otherwise qualified Board members to term limits; moreover, membership (i.e., residents attending a General Meeting) can at any time vote to remove a board member with a two-thirds vote. Opponents suggest that rather than consolidating power and positions at the Board level, there should be greater emphasis placed on motivating neighbors to actively participate in BWNA; moreover, the Association is “re-energized” with each new addition to the Board.

3.) Housekeeping measures: These include everything from corrected punctuation and phrasing to overlooked or misplaced duties of officers and committees. (Go to the BWNA Web site for specifics.) There was no opposition voiced by Board members to any of these proposed revisions. Besides discussing and voting on the Bylaws revisions, issues facing the neighborhood will be addressed by a local police officer and by BWNA committees chairs, with the last part of the meeting devoted to suggestions from the audience on which neighborhood enhancement projects and activities to pursue in 2011.

Your ideas, suggestions, and votes at BWNA meetings help shape the direction of our neighborhood, and we hope to see you in attendance. Remember to mark the rescheduled dates (Board Meeting on Jan. 3rd, General Meeting on Feb. 7th) on your calendars. Meetings are held at Bethany Lutheran Church, corner of 37th and Skidmore--just across from Wilshire Park--with a 7:00 pm starting time.
