Sharon and Bob Smith moved from Southern California to be closer to their son and his family who live in the Beaumont Wilshire neighborhood. After settling into a new home only a block from their son, they focused on another goal – opening a children’s clothing store. Both left behind school-related careers in California, so a children’s store was a natural fit.

Having recently become a great aunt, I welcomed the opening of A Repeat Performance, Kid’s Boutique at 4803 NE Fremont in March. They carry a variety of brands you won’t find at the mall stores, such as Knuckleheads, a boys clothing brand manufactured across the river in Vancouver, and Young Color, a Colorado company.

Sharon and Bob carry infant, toddler and children clothes up to size 8 (soon to be extending that to size 10) for both boys and girls. In addition to regular clothes, they have a collection of fairy dresses, complete with wings that no little girl could resist!

Besides commercial brands, they are now carrying handmade garments including adorable hand knit sweaters, hand sewn dresses, pants and aprons. These are made by local designers including Sharon, who sews pants and skirts.

You will also find a stock of ‘pre-owned’ children’s clothes. Currently they are taking fall and winter clothes on consignment, so if your kids have outgrown their clothes, here is your chance to clean out the closets.

So all you moms, dads, grandpas, grandmas and aunts, be sure to visit A Repeat Performance soon at 4803 NE Fremont 503-922-2721, open Tues-Sat 10-6 and Sunday 11-5, closed Mondays.
