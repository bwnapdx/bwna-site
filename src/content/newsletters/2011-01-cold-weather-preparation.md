As we approach an expected cold winter, the Multnomah County Aging and Disability Services have prepared some basic information and resources to help prepare and guide everyone during inclement weather.

First, we have limited resources to assist clients during an extended cold weather spell. It is essential that we share information with clients about how to prepare for extended cold weather and work with neighbors and community organizations to help with keeping people warm and safe. Additionally, each Aging and Disability Services branch and district center has a plan for identifying and contacting high risk clients during inclement weather.

If the County is closed, Helpline/Gatekeeper/Adult Protective Services calls will be screened by Protocall (the afterhours call service) and triaged by an afterhours consultant. The Helpline Supervisor will identify callers with urgent needs and staff through our department emergency response. Additionally, the 211 website (http://www.211info.org/) will be updated daily with shelter and other cold weather information.

Clients who receive Meals on Wheels through Loaves and Fishes received two shelf stable meals in early October. These included instructions to “not eat the meals unless/until a call from the local meal-site advises that the center(s) are closed and Loaves and Fishes is unable to deliver regular meals.” Loaves and Fishes is currently in the process of identifying any “new starts” since October so they can ensure all Meals on Wheels clients have emergency meals on hand.

In addition, it is also important to have an emergency preparedness kit available.  For more information, please visit http://www.ready.gov/america/getakit/index.html.

For more information, please contact the Multnomah County Aging and Disability Services at 503.988.3620 x29463
