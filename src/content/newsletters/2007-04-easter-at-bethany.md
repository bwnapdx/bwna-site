Two services on Easter Sunday, April 8:
Traditional at 9:30AM
Contemporary at 11AM
10:30AM – 12PM, a continental brunch will be served. NE 37th and Skidmore

## 1,000-egg Easter Egg Hunt – Saturday, April 7th at 11AM

Bethany Lutheran Church will host a 1,000-egg Easter egg hunt at Wilshire Park beginning at 11AM. This event is free to the public. Toddlers and small children are encouraged to bring a parent or guardian to help in their collecting of eggs. Ask for directions to the egg hunt site when you arrive at the park. We look forward to seeing your Easter egg hunter there!

## 3rd Annual Day Camp – kids 5-12 yrs. old

Bethany will host Camp Lutherwood’s Day Camp Monday – Friday, July 30 to Aug. 3, from 9AM–3PM, Children ages 5–12 can experience, in an outdoor setting, what a Christian summer camping program is like without leaving home. Staff from Camp Lutherwood and adult volunteers from Bethany Church guide the children in many activities. Cost for the week is $40; which includes lunch, two snacks each day and a field trip to Grant Pool. Scholarships are available. Contact Bethany Lutheran Church for registration information at (503)284-3836 or go to our website at www.bethanyportland.org and download the registration form.
