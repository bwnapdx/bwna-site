Concerned about the speed of cars along Fremont Street? Worried about your children (or yourself) finding a safe place to cross Fremont? Interested in where and how more parking can fit within Beaumont Village?

At two public open houses in January, neighbors gave input and received some solutions to those questions. For the last year, the BWNA Transportation & Land Use Committee, Beaumont Business Association and City Planning and Transportation staffers have cooperated on a study of problems affecting the Fremont Street corridor through Beaumont Village.

Parking, pedestrian and driver safety, and the functioning of the intersection of Fremont and 41st/42nd streets were examined. Some potential short-term ideas to be implemented over the next 6-12 months include:

- Reducing the speed limit on Fremont to 20 mph from 30 mph in Beaumont Village between 39th and 52nd streets.
- Changing from parallel parking to angled parking on 43rd, 44th, 44th, and 47th streets north of Fremont, which will add 17 spaces near businesses on those streets.
- Adding marked crosswalks at 45th, 47th, 49th, and 50th streets and pedestrian warning signs at 41st and 52nd.

There are many other medium and long-term ideas that the City and the neighborhood are discussing, including curb extensions at several intersections along Fremont, as well as installing a roundabout at the intersection of Fremont and 41st and 42nd streets. These ideas will depend on input from neighbors, business owners, and funding availability. Based on the feedback received from neighbors at the open houses, City staffers will fine-tune the proposed ideas before offering a final version of the plan at another meeting with the neighborhood.
