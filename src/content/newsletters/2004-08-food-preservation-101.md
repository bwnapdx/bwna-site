The Interfaith Network for Earth Concerns, a program of Ecumenical Ministries of Oregon, is offering a free hands-on food preservation workshop. The goal of this program is to increase food security and community connections among neighbors and the earth.

This workshop will teach various methods of ‘putting up’ veggies grown in our own gardens.

**Saturday, August 21, 10am–2pm**
Bethany Lutheran Church, 4330 NE 37th Ave.
Free (donations $5-10 appreciated)
To register call: 503-221-1054 x210
