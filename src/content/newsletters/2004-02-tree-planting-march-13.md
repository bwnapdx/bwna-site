More than 40 Beaumont Wilshire residents have expressed interest in receiving street and yard trees this coming spring.

The annual Friends of Trees planting will take place on **Saturday, March 13, 2004,** in conjunction with the Alameda and Concordia neighborhood associations. **Volunteers are needed from 8am until noon** to serve on tree planting teams and to help with activities at the staging area, located at Wilshire United Methodist Church on the corner of NE Shaver and 39th Avenue.

Staging-area activities include setting up hot drinks and pastries for volunteers; working the registration table; checking in and assigning late arrivals; serving as a tool checker; and helping with the “job-well-done celebration” when plantings are completed. Someone with free time and a truck is also needed before the planting to pick up and deliver stakes and mulch and after the planting to return barricades.

If you would like to be a volunteer, please call or email Barbara Warren-Sams, 503.281.6094 or joieluna@msn.com.
