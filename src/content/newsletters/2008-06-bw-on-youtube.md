In the last issue, I reminded everyone that there are lots of photos from past neighborhood events on the BWNA website at www.bwna.org ‘photos’. This time I want to mention another feature of the website: the video section. There has been a lot of media attention given to the influence of sites such as YouTube on the internet on a whole generation of young people. So that BW is not left in the dust, M. Caoile, a BW resident, has created and posted fourteen videos of neighborhood events on YouTube.

These videos can be viewed from the BWNA website www.bwna.org on the ‘video’ link. Three are my favorite. The first is ‘Picnic at Wilshire Park’ which shows scenes from last year’s National Night Out picnic, where we were serenaded by the Fighting Instruments of Karma Marching Band! I hope seeing this video will inspire you to attend the upcoming picnic this August 5 at Wilshire Park. This video has been viewed by over 150 people.

My next favorite is ‘Fred’s Story #1’. This is an interview with Fred Elsasser, who has lived in BW since 1925. The best part is when he talks about getting his teeth fixed with braces at the age of 85! This video has had over 500 hits.

And last is ‘Tree planting #2 – to work’. Here we see the staff of Friends of Trees showing and helping BW residents plant and take care of new trees. The scenes in this video give me great hope and confirm in my mind that the tree planting torch (and shovel) has passed to the next generation. This video has been viewed over 1370 times!

Watch all 14 videos for yourself and share them with friends and relatives.

http://www.bwna.org/videos.html
