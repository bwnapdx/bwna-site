The Hollywood Farmers' Market is the premiere Saturday morning destination for the whole family. The market is located between 44th and 45th Ave. on NE Hancock St., 1 block South of Sandy Blvd. In addition to fresh produce, baked goods, fresh-cut flowers, meats, cheeses, and prepared foods, farmer market patrons can enjoy weekly live music, children's entertainment and monthly cooking demonstrations.

The market is run mostly by volunteers, so please contact the Volunteer Coordinator at volunteers@hollywoodfarmersmarket.org for more information on how to get involved. Visit the HFM online at www.hollywoodfarmersmarket.org to sign-up for a weekly e-newsletter or just to find out more.
