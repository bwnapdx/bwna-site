The Portland Bureau of Transportation received an American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA) grant as a part of the federal economic stimulus to improve the way finding pavement markings, way finding signage and arterial crossing signage for Portland’s existing and new Bicycle Boulevards. This project will install pavement markings across 53 miles of our new and existing bicycle boulevard system. Because some of the boulevards change streets in some areas, some boulevards have multiple street names associated with the project (for instance, the route change on Klickitat one block south at NE 17th Avenue to Siskiyou).

The Federal Highway Administration and the Oregon Department of Transportation are requiring a specific pavement marking, the “sharrow” pictured below for installation. Our way finding signage will be consistent with the existing way finding signage around the city and the crossing signage will have both pedestrian and cyclist symbols. The pavement markings will be installed in the travel lane – these are not bicycle lanes. The “sharrow” symbol indicates a shared-use roadway (motorist and cyclist). In January 2010, the City of Portland was approved to bid the project and Brown Contracting Inc. was awarded the project. Work will commence in April and extend through October of this year. The pavement marking installation is weather dependent and the dates below are estimated. Access will be maintained on the street during both sign installation and pavement marking installation. Each location will take approximately 30 minutes to install the pavement marking. Some road access will be limited while work is occurring in the street but access will be maintained through the duration of the construction. No complete road closure is anticipated.

Thank you for your patience The Bureau of Transportation is sensitive to the use of these local streets in the neighborhood and will endeavor to complete the work as quickly as possible to minimize the inconvenience these improvements may cause. We thank you for your patience. For questions about the project please contact the project manager assigned to the project, Kyle Chisek at 503.823.7041.
