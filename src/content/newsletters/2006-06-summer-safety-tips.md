Warmer weather, longer days and summer vacations are here. More of us will be out on the streets and in the parks. Being the victim of a crime can put a damper on your entire summer. Here are some tips for kids and adults on keeping safe:

1. Always let someone know where you are going, who you are with, and time of return.
2. Go places with friends and family. You are usually safer in a group.
3. Never go with someone you do not know, no matter what they say.
4. Be careful when approached by or talking to strangers; stay at least two arms length or two steps away.
5. Carry your cell phone with you so you can call for help quickly
6. Younger children should know their full name, parent’s names, and their address (a phone number is also helpful, but may be more information than a young child can handle). Carrying identification is helpful but should not be a substitute for knowing. Adults should ALWAYS carry identification.
7. If you are not in “your neighborhood” always know your street location such as NE Fremont and 47th.
8. If on a bicycle, always lock it and record and keep in a safe place the brand name and serial number of your bicycle. Also engrave you driver’s license number in several places on your bicycle.

For additional information or questions call Crime Prevention at Central Northeast Neighbors (CNN), 503-823-2781.
