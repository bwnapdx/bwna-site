Picture this, it’s a beautiful spring day and you have spent most of it at work, stuffed into a corner under fluorescent lights between cube walls. Finally, you make the break and fling yourself into the glorious afternoon sunshine. All you want to do is play and draw in the sweet scents of an Oregon spring evening. The last thing on your mind is trudging home to make dinner. Never fear, Sara of Leaf and Bean is here and her delicious offerings are just what you need!

Sara Lamadrid, owner of the Leaf and Bean Café at NE Fremont and 50th, has been serving delicious sandwiches, soups, salads and affordable dinners for 10 years. She is one of the pioneers of the Fremont business scene. Among the popular items on her menu are the Paso Doble, a hot sandwich combining shredded chicken breast, a dried fruit and green olive chutney, ham, marinated red onion and Dijon-tomato mustard, and the Mediterranean chicken sandwich which has poached Oregon-grown chicken breast, dressed with lemon, olive oil, garlic, herbs and capers, feta cheese, marinated red onion, roasted red pepper, cucumber, lettuce and an olive-caper mayo. It makes your mouth water just reading the ingredients.

The Leaf and Bean Café is like a second home for Sara. She fondly remembers how John Zweben, owner of the former L’etoile French restaurant, brought a bottle of sparkling cider to celebrate her first night of business on April 1, 1994. Fife chef, Marco Shaw, was a regular customer during the construction of his restaurant and loves the ‘La Cubana,’ house-smoked turkey, ham, onions and provolone. The green salads with homemade dressings, featuring cheeses and nuts are a favorite of many customers.

Fremont Street has been an excellent place for Sara’s business and it just keeps getting better. Jim and Patty’s coffee shop opened recently on Fremont at 51st, and the whole Fremont restaurant district has attracted local and citywide attention.

Sara takes pride in the fact that Leaf and Bean Café specializes in ‘made from scratch’ dishes using fresh ingredients. She offers vegetarian options, but also serves chicken, turkey and ham. There is a fine selection of beer and wine and new surprises are in store for the dinner menu in April. The café is open for lunch and dinner during the weekdays, and for brunch, lunch and dinner on the weekends. In addition, most items on the menu are available to go.

This brings me back to that spring evening. It would be so fine to hurry home, meet up with your best buddy, head on over to the Leaf and Bean Café for a couple of Paso Doble sandwiches to go, bike up to Mt Tabor and watch the sun set. Make it a point to visit Sara at the **Leaf and Bean Café, 4936 NE Fremont, (503) 281-1090**. Congratulations Leaf and Bean for 10 successful years!
