Brita Mae Gould is the newest artist on BWNA’s Artspace: http://bwna.org/artspace/ “Nature Spirits,” her current series displayed on the site, features miniature masks adorned with natural objects and mounted on dream catcher inspired backgrounds. Her process of working involves creating the masks from plaster gauze molds, backing them with epoxy, sealing, airbrushing, and lastly decorating them with organic materials.

Brita was a modern dancer and choreographer for many years and actively brings that experience and inspiration to her art. She considers her masks as “still life dances,” each one being carefully choreographed and created using her eye for shape, space, and movement. She has always been enamored with faces and several years ago a mask-making workshop provided her with a new medium in which to explore this fascination.

Brita’s latest series, “Tree Spirits,” features her miniature masks mounted on pieces of driftwood are also on Artspace.

Her work is in many private collections and she has exhibited at local galleries, including the Bonnie Kahn Gallery on NW 23rd and the former Graystone Gallery.
