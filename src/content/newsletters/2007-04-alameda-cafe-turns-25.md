“Maybe it’s in the blood,” quipped Georjean Melonas, as she pointed to the photo of her Grandfather’s restaurant on the wall in Alameda Cafe. Franklin Cafe in New Hampshire, operated into the early 1940’s.

Georjean began her career selling imported and domestic cheeses and meats to wholesale accounts, partnering with Brett Johanson. She found herself catering for friends at first, then continued catering as part of the wholesale business.

The next step was possibly opening a deli or small cafe. She knocked on doors in the neighborhood looking for the perfect space. In the storefront that housed the former Myers Typesetting, Georjean and Brett opened Johanson’s Cafe and Deli in July of 1982. Brett stayed with the wholesale business, but the cafe was Georjean’s “baby”. She offered great food and an easy charm that made folks feel at home.

Seven years later needing more room, Johanson’s moved two doors down to the current location at 4641 NE Fremont. Years ago I overheard Georjean commenting to a lunch guest, “Well, I’m continually reinventing myself.....!” What the context of that particular conversation was I’m not sure, but it stuck in my mind, and made sense when in 1995, Georjean renamed the restaurant “Alameda Cafe” and introduced her breakfast menu. In spring of 2006 she added liquor to her beer and wine menu. Neighboring Alameda Brewhouse supplies the beer! A facelift with a fresh new palette of warm hues, added around the same time, completed the next step into the 25th year.

I asked Georjean, “What’s next?” She suggested an interest in more special event functions. She says that participating in the Art on Fremont a few years back was a good experience. She’s open to art events, anniversary or wedding parties. “Any thoughts of retirement?” I couldn’t help asking. The spirited restaurateur winked at me and said, “Retirement? What’s that?” Watch for 25th anniversary celebrations. Stop by Alameda Café, 4641 NE Fremont, open Mon-Sat 8am-9pm Sun 8am-2pm and 3-9pm and say ‘Congrats’ to Georjean for 25 years!
