### Rylie's Chocolate Peppermint Delight Cookies

- 1 c butter, softened
- ¾ c sugar
- ¾ c brown sugar, packed
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 eggs
- ¾ c Hershey's cocoa powder
- 2¼ c flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 package of Hershey's peppermint kisses, crushed

1. Cream butter and sugars until light and mixed together.
2. Add vanilla, eggs, and cocoa powder.
3. Then add flour, soda, and salt.
4. Unwrap and put kisses in a food processor and crush into small chunks.
5. Lastly, add kisses to the dough and mix until the pieces are fully mixed in.
6. Bake at 350° for 10 minutes on an ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Let cool for at least 10 minutes on the pan. Take them off and enjoy.
