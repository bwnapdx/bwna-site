The Beaumont-Wilshire Newsletter is a bimonthly publication (6 issues a year) staffed by BWNA Board members and volunteers from the neighborhood. Black and white hard copies are distributed to over 2600 neighborhood residences and businesses, and a color version is available online at the BWNA Web site (www.bwna.us). Residents are encouraged to submit articles and announcements (limited to 300 words) or "Letter to the Board" editorials on topics of local concern (limited to 150 words), all of which are subject to editing. Local merchants and organizations may purchase ads in two different sizes for a minimum of 3 issues, with a discount given for 6 issues. The deadline for submitting articles, announcements, "Letter to the Editor" editorials, and ads is the second Friday of even-numbered months, with the deadline date publicized in each Newsletter under "Newsletter Calendar". Articles are requested to be typed in WORD format, if possible. All submissions——ads and articles——should be submitted the day of or prior to the deadline date and sent to BWNA President & Interim Newsletter Editor Al Ellis at president@bwna.us.

Size of ads and corresponding prices are as follows:

**Standard business card size ad (3.5 x 2 inches)**
\$40 per issue (minimum of 3 issues)
\$225 for 6 issues (which is a savings of \$15)

**Double-size ad (3.5 x 4 inches or 7 x 2 inches)**
\$60 per issue (minimum of 3 issues)
\$325 for 6 issues (which is a savings of \$35)

Payments by check may be made out to "BWNA" and should be **paid in full** prior to the upcoming submission deadline.
