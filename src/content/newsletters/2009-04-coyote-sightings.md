Over the past several months, many residents have expressed alarm about coyotes roaming the neighborhood. There have been a number of cats attacked, but thankfully, there have been no attacks on humans (a rarity in cities, based on USDA data). We have run a few articles about the problem, and following a presentation given by Kevin Christiansen and Mike Foster of the USDA about wildlife in urban settings at the BWNA General Meeting, we have posted a number to call on the BWNA Web site to report sightings and/or concerns. If you have questions about coyotes or other wildlife wandering the neighborhood, please call Wildlife Services at 503.326.2346. With spring coyote breeding season coming soon, we encourage residents to phone in sightings and/or stories of attacks immediately to Wildlife Services so that the situation can be professionally monitored.
