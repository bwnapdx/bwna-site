Wilshire United Methodist Native American Fellowship is planning a Soup, Supper, and Service for Maundy on Thursday, April 5, starting at 6:30pm. Bread and soup will be served, and then the group will move into the sanctuary for a time of remembrance of the Last Supper.

On Sunday, April 8, there will be a Sunrise Service at 7:30am with music and scripture reading. An Easter Sunday Celebration will start at 11:00am with more music and joy, and there will be a time of fellowship and snacks after the service. Please join us at 3917 NE Shaver Street for this special time!
