Vicki Hertel of SunGold Farms is willing to bring her fresh produce once a week to Wilshire Park this spring and summer. The cost is $16 a week for 4 months. If you are interested in this great service, contact Ankist at ankistz@comcast.net.

SunGold Farms uses no pesticides. Take advantage of this great opportunity to eat local produce brought to you directly from SunGold Farm, as part of the Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) program.
