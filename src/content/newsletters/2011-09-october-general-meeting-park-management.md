Northeast and North Portland residents love their parks. Take August’s National Night Out for example. Some of the largest park turnouts in the city were at Peninsula, Holladay, Irvington, Fernhill, and our own Wilshire Park. No doubt about it, whether it’s picnicking, jogging, walking, participating in athletics, dog running, or kids rollicking on play equipment, all kinds of people flock to our parks for any number of reasons.

Only fitting, then, that the main focus of BWNA’s October 10th General Meeting will be on utilization of parks, and in particular, on the multi-use nature of Wilshire Park. BWNA’s June 13th General Meeting featured “Transportation Projects” as the central topic, and the main speaker was a traffic calming planner from the Portland Bureau of Transportation. October’s “Parks” theme will be addressed by the North Zone Manager for Portland Parks and Recreation: Sue Glenn. Glenn comes highly recommended by her boss, City Commissioner Nick Fish (who was invited to attend but had to decline due to a schedule conflict). Sue will provide information about PP & R projects, priorities, and talk about the role that neighborhood associations can play in PP & R planning and decision-making processes. But perhaps most importantly, she will field questions and comments from the audience—be they about jogging paths, dogs, trees, picnics, facilities, or whatever. It’s an opportunity for park users to air their thoughts and concerns directly to city management.

The meeting will begin, as always, with a crime report from a local officer (or officers), followed by a question/answer session. The December “Winter Holiday Potluck Dessert” General Meeting will be discussed and committee reports given, including an update on the “Wistaria Curve” traffic calming project. Lastly, the audience will be given the opportunity to make suggestions regarding issues, activities, and speakers for the BWNA Board to consider in the coming months.

October is still weeks away, so best to mark your calendars now: BWNA General Meeting, second Monday of October, the 10th, at Bethany Lutheran Church (corner of NE Skidmore St. & 37th Ave., across from Wilshire Park), beginning at 7:00 pm. Enter down the walkway on the Skidmore side of the church. Light snacks and refreshments served. There’s no time like the present to get involved in your neighborhood.
