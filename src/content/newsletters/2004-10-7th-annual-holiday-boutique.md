**Sat. & Sun. Nov. 6th & 7th, 9 – 4**
**4336 NE 40th Avenue at Skidmore**

Eleanor Hunting, a great-grandmother who has lived here for 54 years, enjoys making crafts for family and friends. She and her daughter, Diane Charlton, invite the neighborhood to her annual holiday boutique. A portion of the sale proceeds will be donated to BWNA. Shop for autumn and holiday gifts. All items are handmade and include gifts, art, ornaments, all occasion note cards, baked goods, doggie treats and much more. If you don’t have time to make holiday gifts or just want to benefit the neighborhood, please mark Nov. 6 & 7 on your calendar to enjoy “unrushed” shopping and visiting with a long time neighbor.
