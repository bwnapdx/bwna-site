The Portland Community Wind Band presents its fourth and final concert of the season on **Sunday, July 1, 2007 at Wilshire Park at 2 PM**. This event is sponsored by the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association and is FREE & open to the public. Attendees are encouraged to bring a picnic or BBQ for themselves.

The Portland Community Wind Band performs contemporary classical music by various world composers. More information is available at pcwb.org.

Cynthia Plank, who founded the group in 2005, directs the Portland Community Wind Band. She is also Band Director for Beaumont Middle School. The Portland Community Wind Band is comprised of adult musicians playing music at an intermediate or advanced level.
