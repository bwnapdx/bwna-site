Sang Dae Lee and his wife, Heejoo Han, are the proprietors of Wilshire Market at the corner of Alameda and NE Fremont St. They bought the business (though not the building) in April of 2010 after its pending sale was pointed out to them by a friend from their church.

Wilshire Market is a general store, with its own small menu of freshly prepared food. The teriyaki chicken and spicy pork are delicious, each served with a small side salad topped with a zesty ginger dressing that is exceptional. One can find anything from fire logs, drain cleaner and batteries, to canned and packaged foods, local wine and beer – all at a reasonable price.

Upon entering the store one notices a contrast between the exterior of the building and lovingly crafted wooden furniture and decorations on the interior. This is because Sang Dae is a skilled carpenter and built the sushi bar, counters, low wall and artistic wooden sculptures by hand. Two months ago, Sang Dae also uncovered a vintage advertisement on the side of the building, which he took the time to frame - also in wood. As they are not the owners of the building they are limited in what they can do.

After first opening the business the couple had ambitious plans, and Sang Dae, having been a sushi chef for six years while living in Southern California, wanted to open a sushi bar in part of the store.  Unfortunately, business was not enough to sustain the necessary constant supply of fresh fish or their considerable efforts. Also, Sang Dae’s modest style will not permit him to use overly blatant advertising. The controversy over the proposed cell tower and resulting bad press also coincided with this launch. I stopped by one evening a few months ago for some of their delicious spicy pork and was treated to some extra unagi (roasted freshwater eel sushi) that they happened to have left over from their own dinner. It was as good as any that I have ever had in New York, San Francisco, or Portland. The family adores the neighborhood and would love to continue to improve the appearance and offerings of their business. They enjoy being friendly and allowing their reputation for great service, good prices and selection to spread.

Living nearby, I visit their store often, and enjoy the sense of community that comes from visiting a store where the owners are personally invested.
