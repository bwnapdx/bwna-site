In February, the Community Cycling Center located at 1700 NE Alberta was burglarized. The intruders ransacked desks, scattered files, and toppled displays. The shop was cleaned out of locks, lights, and parts. Three refurbished bikes were missing.

The loss was estimated at $12,000. This did not include security upgrades. More than 25 people donated over $1,500 upon hearing the news. Insurance will cover some costs too. However, the Center needs an additional $10,000 to recover losses and make upgrades to prevent thefts in the future. We can help by making a financial contribution to strengthen the Community Cycling Center or by donating a bike. The Center needed bikes before the break-in and could use them now more than ever. They’re looking for good quality adult bikes and kids bikes that they refurbish and use in their outreach programs.

Help the Center to continue their excellent work of reaching kids, recycling bikes and restoring community.
