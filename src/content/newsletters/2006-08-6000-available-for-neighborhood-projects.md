Are you working on a neighborhood-based project that needs a little extra cash to get off the ground? Would you like to see some great public art in Beaumont Village? If so, the Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Association wants to hear from you! The BWNA has accumulated over $6,000 in its coffers and the Board would like to get some ideas from neighbors about things they would like to see happen in the neighborhood. If you have ideas that the BWNA can help to fund and are willing to spend some time to make them happen, please contact me at dlisk@myuw.net and I will pass along all of your suggestions to the other BWNA Board members. Later this summer we will evaluate all of the ideas we receive from neighbors and decide on which projects the BWNA should assist.
