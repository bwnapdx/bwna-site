The annual National Night Out picnic held in early August was well attended. About 175 people attended and feasted on treats provided by the neighborhood associations, neighbors and local businesses. The association wants to thank the following businesses and individuals for their generous contributions:

- Alameda Cafe
- Alameda Brewhouse
- Beaumont Properties
- Papa Murphys
- A Repeat Performance
- Vestiges Storefront
- Alameda Fitness
- Green Dog Pet Supply
- Bella Wellness
- Andrine de la Rocha of Beaumont Health Care Clinic
- Strada
- Barrett Automotive

Also, thanks to:

Earth H2O - for donating 9 cases of bottled water

Thomas Egleston- Field Services Coordinator- Center for Urban Studies- PSU- for providing recycling receptacles

Portland Parks- providing extra trash cans

Several generous neighbors for donating grills and for cooking

Portland Fire Bureau for the trucks that were open to kids to explore

Wilshire United Methodist Church for tables and chairs and for the church ladies who helped to unload them

And thanks to the three members of the Fighting Instruments of Karma Marching Chamber Band/Orchestra for the fine music. You can see photos and a video of this year’s picnic at http://www.bwna.org and click on photos or video.
