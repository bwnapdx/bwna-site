Monday, Oct 14, 7:30pm
Bethany Lutheran Church
NE 37th & Skidmore

Kevin Cavenaugh is the guest speaker at the Oct 14 general meeting. Kevin will discuss his most recent building at NE Fremont and 45th and also talk about some other sites on Fremont which he may be developing.

The Board will also present the results of the neighborhood survey, which went out to residents in the June/July newsletter. Come and hear what is of the highest concern to residents and be ready to discuss what actions the neighborhood should take to implement solutions to these concerns.
