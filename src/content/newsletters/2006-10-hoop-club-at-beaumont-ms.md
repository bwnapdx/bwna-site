Hollywood Hoop Club will be held at Beaumont Middle School, **Mondays and Wednesdays from 4:00 to 5:30PM**. The Club will kick off on **October 4, 2006 at 7:00PM** with games, contests, trivia and much more. Coach Missy Smith will be there to meet with parents and answer questions about the Hollywood Hoop Club. The fee for this program is $40.00 a month. The Club provides young female athletes an opportunity to learn life skills through the medium of basketball. Coaching is done by knowledgeable and experienced staff members dedicated to promoting communication, academic achievement, and healthy nutrition and life choices. The after school program for girls in grades 4-8 is set up in a clinic format. No player will be turned away who can not meet the financial obligation. Scholarships are available on a need base. Players can sign up on a month to month basis if they so desire. If you wish to receive more information please contact Missy Smith at masmith01234@yahoo.com or call 503-709-4867. Missy Smith is Grant High School’s new Head Basketball Coach.
