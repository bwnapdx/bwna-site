**Looking for happiness? Look no further than the Blackbird.**

When a spot opened up on Fremont Street, Blackbird Wineshop owner Andy Diaz knew what he had to do – take a chance and fly. And it paid off. These days, Blackbird Wineshop is a bigger part of the community than ever, especially with the addition of Atomic Cheese. “Who doesn’t love cheese?” Diaz said. “It’s the perfect pairing with wine, plus it just makes people smile. It seemed like a natural choice.”

So, why Atomic Cheese? “The cheeses are small producer but pack a big punch,” Diaz said.”Plus it fits in with the philosophy of the entire shop – buy small, shop often.” Like a European market, you can now get affordable wine, cheese, specialty items and artisan bread – like Little T’s American Baker Salt Slab – right in the neighborhood.

It’s the perfect place to grab a friend, a bottle, an outdoor table (when it’s sunny) and a Blue Cheese Plate, a mix of blues plus honey, bread, fig jam and dates. Or try one of Andy’s newest creations, the Atomic Bite – a Caribe chocolate box filled with things like Forme d’Ambert, cherries, almonds and a drizzle of honey.

“To me, it’s more than just a  shop,” Diaz said. “It’s a part of the neighborhood: a place to gather with friends, learn more about small producer wines and cheese, get inspired by local ingredients and engage with art, music, and literary readings,” he said. “At the end of the day, I want this to be a place where people come to be happy. And if they can’t stay, I want them to bring the happy home.”

Join Andy and friends for the new Hours of Happiness featuring $4 glass pours and Atomic Bites, 3 for $5, Tuesday through Thursday. And look for the Saturday Morning French Toast Fest with Little T’s Breads, coming soon.
