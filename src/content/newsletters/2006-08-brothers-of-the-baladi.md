While war rages in Iraq and negative Arab stereotypes are formed from news reports, a lovely Iraqi melody, Foq Ennakhal, is played with gusto by a group of Americans in an eclectic music studio a short walk from NE Fremont. Musicians and artists always seem to find the beauty and good side to every culture.

Michael Beach, originally from Niagara Falls, NY, is admired by Middle Eastern people for his grasp of their languages in his vocal performances with his band, *Brothers of the Baladi*. And American fans love the “world music” sound. Michael started the band in Yuma, Arizona thirty years ago and continues to bring a unique blend of Middle East beats tweaked with American rock to offer a show that melts cultural borders and gets folks of all sorts up on their feet and dancing.

Brothers of the Baladi is a national touring band, so the opportunities to catch the show in Portland are limited. Most recently, their local line up included Portland Center Stage’s auction at the Portland Art Museum, The Crystal Ballroom, events for Tazo Tea and Nordstrom. Arab-American weddings are a good place to find Michael or the full group.

Michael has another band, *Arabesque*, which plays Wednesday evenings at ‘It’s a Beautiful Pizza’ on 34th and Hawthorne. Come and hear the music, see a few belly dancers, hit the dance floor yourself and munch some pizza. This family friendly event starts at 8:30 pm.

In addition to performing, Michael teaches drum workshops across the country, and also at his studio near Beaumont Village. He welcomes novice drummers as well as experienced, who want to refresh skills or learn the unique Middle Eastern rhythms. For more information view the website: www.baladi.com
