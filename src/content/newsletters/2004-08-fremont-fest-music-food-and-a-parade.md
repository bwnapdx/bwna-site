Fremont Fest will be held on **Saturday, August 7th** on NE Fremont between 42nd Ave and 57th Ave. This year’s festival promises to be bigger and better than ever. In addition to the local businesses, we have invited vendors from outside the area to participate. The Kids’ Parade will start at NE 42nd and Fremont. Kids who want to participate should show up at 9:30am. The parade starts at 10am.

Several bands with eclectic styles will perform at various times at various venues up and down the street. All of the area restaurants will feature specials. Other events include a classic car show, root beer floats, and an artists’ fair. The shops up and down Fremont will be open and many will display their wares outside. It should be a great event, so bring your friends and family to shop, dine, and play at Fremont Fest.
