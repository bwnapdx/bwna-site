One of the neighborhood’s busiest – and most hazardous – streets will be getting speed bumps.

City officials announced at a public meeting in July that 41st/Wistaria/42nd will get a total of six speed bumps between Klickitat and Stanton streets.

The traffic calming project is a result of neighbor complaints about serious accidents and a city study that showed high rates of speeding in the so-called “Wistaria curve” on Wistaria Drive between Cesar Chavez Boulevard (formerly 39th Avenue) and 42nd Avenue. The street is one of the main north-south routes in the area and carries the TriMet No. 75 bus.

The city will install 20-foot speed bumps, also known as speed tables, at the six locations. The tables are more gradual than the 14-foot bumps used in other locations and are used on bus routes. The bumps at Stanton, Alameda and Klickitat will be painted as raised crosswalks (per sidebar designation).

The majority of about 30 residents attending a July 19 meeting at Beaumont Middle School cheered the city’s decision.

Some residents raised concerns that the speed bumps would increase traffic noise. Will Stevens of the city’s Transportation Bureau said that might be the case until drivers get used to the bumps, but that it shouldn’t be an issue in the long run.

Others wondered whether the speed bumps would cause cut-through traffic on parallel streets. Stevens said he didn’t think that would happen because even with the bumps, other streets will take longer to travel. But the city will take before-and-after traffic counts to make sure, he said.

The city could install the bumps this year. But it might be delayed because of the replacement of a sanitary sewer on that section of Wistaria Drive. The city will likely excavate Wistaria in spring 2012, said David Allred of the city’s Bureau of Environmental Services. He expected the Transportation Bureau to wait until after that project is complete to install the speed bumps.

However, if the sewer project is delayed beyond next year, the speed bumps might go in before the sewer project. And Stevens said the city might install the bumps at Stanton, Alameda and Klickitat before the three on Wistaria Drive.
