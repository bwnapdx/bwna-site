Lynne Nesbitt is both a therapist and businesswoman. Five years ago, she realized a long-held dream and founded Wise Counsel and Comfort, a collective of 21 mental health professionals. "My goal," says Nesbitt, "was to create a space that brings mental health professionals and clients together in a comfortable, safe, and affordable setting."

Wise Counsel and Comfort is not a group practice, but a cooperative, shared practice space. "This allows mental health professionals to develop a private practice without the expense associated with opening or maintaining their own offices," says Nesbitt. This arrangement also allows for flexible hours, including evenings and weekends.

Colleagues must meet one requirement, however. "Everyone must be willing to adjust their fees or use a sliding-fee scale. In this way, clients can avail themselves of lower fees as well as have access to a wide range of therapists."

Are you looking to see a psychologist, counselor, social worker, psychiatric nurse practitioner, alternative therapy specialist, or vocational counselor? These specialists and more are available at Wise Counsel and Comfort. Not sure which professional to see? Nesbitt can help with that, directing you to an appropriate professional to meet your individual needs.

Two locations are available in the area: the newest location in Beaumont Wilshire has six professionals and is located at 4605 NE Fremont, Suite 209A; a second location in Irvington has 15 professionals and is located at 1305 NE Fremont.
