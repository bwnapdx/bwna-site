Fremont traffic is growing day by day and it’s getting harder to cross the street. I’d like to suggest the City install crosswalks every other block - some with blinking lights to alert cars to slow down. This may help to calm traffic some and make it possible for local residents to reach all of those great bakeries, coffee houses, yoga studios and other businesses we want to enjoy and support.

For a more pedestrian-friendly experience,

Albert Kaufman
NE 44th Ave./ May 23, 2003
