Fall's a great time to walk, for sure, and it's also a swell time to duck inside some neighborhood artists' studios for winter inspiration. The 2008 Portland Open Studios, a self-directed tour of artists demonstrating their work in their creative spaces, opens the door to 98 studios all over the city, seven of which are a stroll for Beaumont Wilshire residents.

I will be at work in my bookbinding studio October 11, 12, 18, and 19 along with near neighboring artists Samyak Yamauchi (mosaics), Tracy Taylor and Maggie Casey (fibers), and Gwenith Jones (canvas rugs).

A bit farther afield, painter Scott Conary and printmaker Nicole Rawlins also welcome visitors in Northeast neighborhoods adjoining ours.

To join the tour, purchase a tour guide ($15), which includes two tickets, maps, a calendar, and pictures of all artists' work, from Margaret Davis, 503-287-2419, or Art Media, New Seasons, and other stores listed at:

www.portlandopenstudios.com.

An artist-run organization, Portland Open Studios offers visitors the chance to go behind the scenes to see where, how, and why art is made in an up-close and personal way. Come out and see the creativity flowing.
