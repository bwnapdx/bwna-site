Ninety members of the Beaumont Middle School community celebrated a bold new adventure in school fundraising April 18th at the First Annual Un-Auction. The event raised over $20,000 to fund staffing at Beaumont next year.

The Un-Auction was held at the Irvington home of Beaumont parents Donna Wax and Jeff Jones, and was a wonderful time of mingling between staff, teachers and parents, and a great opportunity for the spirit of the Beaumont community to grow. Even incoming Alameda parents were in attendance! Fueling the lively discussions were great donated wine and delicious food. Beaumont Market, New Seasons and Fred Meyer donated to defray the cost of the event, and several Beaumont parents contributed wine and appetizers.

The success of the Un-Auction is a great beginning toward reaching the foundation’s goal of $100,000. “As school funding becomes more unpredictable and threatens the staff positions and the academic success of students, the Beaumont Un-Auction was a testament to parents sharing critical financial resources in a dire time,” said Teresa Osborne, Foundation Co-Chair. Contributing to the Beaumont Foundation is the only way parents and community members can help restore staffing at Beaumont. Until the Oregon legislature provides a final budget to PPS and it is sufficient to meet the needs and hopes of the Beaumont community, fundraising will continue.

The Beaumont Middle School Foundation was formed in 2007 and raises money to help the Beaumont Principal pay for school staffing needs. The focus is to raise money to fund key staff positions that support student preparation for high school.

Donations can be made at the Portland Schools Foundation website, www.thinkschools.org (be sure to specify that your money should be directed to Beaumont). For more information, contact the Beaumont Foundation by emailing BeaumontFoundation@gmail.com.
