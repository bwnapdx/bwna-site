At the April BWNA community meeting, we elected many new members to the board. Congratulations to newly elected president Al Ellis and treasurer Dan Johnson. Returning to currently held board positions are: Roger Price, Jeff Holiman and Ankist Zadeyan.

Newly elected board members include: Ana Naumoff, Tom Melville, Chris Osaka, and Kay Levis. Thanks to outgoing members David Whitaker and David de la Rocha, and to Bill Markwart for filling the position of acting president.

You may contact any of the board members with questions and suggestions. Their contact information can be found on the neighborhood website

http://www.bwna.org/contact.html

or on page 2 of this newsletter under *Officers & Board Members*.
