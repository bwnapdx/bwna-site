## Market Activity

While inventory continued to stay low in the Portland Metro area, sales activity saw both increases and declines. Closed sales increased 13.3% when comparing June 2010 with June 2009. However pending sales fell 25.2% and new listings fell 4.9%. Much of the decline in pending sales was a direct result of the buyer drop off due to the expiration of the First Time Buyer Tax Credit.

Comparing June 2010 with May 2010 closed sales dropped 1.9% and pending sales grew 8.4%, while new listings rose 16.3%

At this rate of sales, the 14,752 active residential listings would last approximately 7.3 months.

## Sales Price

The average sale price for June 2010 declined 3.3% compared to June 2009. The median sale price also fell 4%. On a month to month basis comparing June 2010 to May 2010 the average price went up 5.2% ($289,800 v. $275,500) and the median price grew a slight .4% ($240,000 v. $239,000). In NE Portland the average sale price is $294,000.

## Second Quarter Report

Comparing the second quarter of 2010 (April—June) with that of 2009, there are gains across the board. Closed sales rose 34% (6,191 v. 4,625). Pending sales and new listing also saw increases of 1% (5,842 v. 5,784) and 2.4% (12,456 v. 12,165), respectively.

## The Year in Beaumont-Wilshire

Looking back on the sales activity in Beaumont, 21 properties have sold in the last 12 months (August 2010 - August 2009). The average days on market is 47. The average List Price was $443,979. The average Sold Price was $436,005. The highest sale price was $710,000 and the lowest sale price was $320,000. The % of sold price to original list price is 94.66%.
