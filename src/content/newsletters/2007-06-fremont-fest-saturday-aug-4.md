How does the Beaumont neighborhood celebrate summer? We open our stores, our street, and our hearts to our neighbors! Please join us for our annual parade, shopping, music, food and fun as the Beaumont neighborhood celebrates **Fremont Fest on Saturday, August 4, 2007**. Parade begins 10:30am at the Beaumont Middle School. Vendors please contact Sue Mautz for space availability at 503-287-4440. Musicians please contact Nathan Edwards at 503-288-5640 or contact Apryl Strass at 503-445-8723 for more information.
