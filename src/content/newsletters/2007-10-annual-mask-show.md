If you can’t wait until Halloween to experience the color, humor, and mystery of masks, Quirks of Art is offering their first annual mask exhibition starting Wednesday, October 3. Showing through Halloween, Quirks of Art & Furniture will display an array of raku, stoneware, encaustic and mixed media masks by a group of local mask artists. Beaumont Wilshire artist Brita Gould will be showing along with Michelle Gallagher, Connie Trapp, Diane Trapp, Debbie Petricek, Monica Roxburg, and Todd Whitlock.

Quirks of Art & Furniture will host a wine and cheese reception on Saturday, October 13 from 3pm to 6 pm. Come nibble a cracker and chat with the artists on the origins of their ideas and the particulars of their craft. For gallery hours and to preview some work from the show, go to www.quirks-of-art.com. The gallery is located at 1602 N.E. 40th Ave (next to A-Boy parking lot) in the Hollywood District.
