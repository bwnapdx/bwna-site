After residential burglaries, car break-ins and thefts are currently the most frequently committed crimes in Portland. Vehicle related crimes are usually a crime of opportunity and committed by a lone individual.

Car thefts are committed for a number of reasons: to strip and resell the parts, for use in another crime, or for joy riding. Most stolen cars are recovered within two miles of where stolen. A large number of cars are stolen with the keys in the ignition. This is because the drivers were just going to “run in and run out”.

Car prowls are committed with the sole purpose of taking something out of the vehicle. The car prowler is a “window shopper”. He or she walks through a parking lot or down a street looking for value items inside cars. Once a car is targeted, a window is broken to gain entry. It takes the car thief and the car prowler less than 30 seconds to get into your car and take what they want.

**To Stop the Car Thief:**

1) Park in well-lighted, frequently traveled places. At home, park in a garage if possible, or under a streetlight, or in front of a window to your house.

2) Always keep your car doors locked.

3) Never leave your keys in the ignition, always take them with you.

4) Never leave your car running without you in it.

5) Install a car alarm or a ‘Club’.

**To Stop the Car Prowler:**

1) Follow steps 1, 2, and 5 above.

2) Keep the inside of your car “show room clean”, leave nothing inside your car for a thief to take.

3) Form a neighborhood block watch or a business watch program.

4) Report all incidents to the police. They need to know where the crimes are occurring.

Following these steps are not a guarantee that your car will never be stolen or broken into, but it will help reduce the chances considerably.
