**4th Annual Day Camp**: July 28-Aug 1, for kids ages 5 -12 years old. Four trained camp counselors from Camp Lutherwood (www.lutherwood.org) combine with Bethany volunteers for an exciting, engaging week of camp at Bethany from 9 AM - 3 PM M-Fri (over before Noon on Friday) where kids engage in crafts, fun activities, worship, and a Bible theme. The cost is $40 and includes 2 snacks, a lunch, and field trip to Grant Pool. Registration forms available on-line at www.bethanyportland.org or call (503)284-3836.

**PJ’s Purple Peddler** will be out on the streets on Tuesdays and Wednesdays during June and July in the early evenings bringing free frozen treats to the kids in your neighborhood as a way of connecting community together and sharing summer events at Bethany and in the community. Look for PJ’s (Pastor Jeff’s) purple bicycle in your neighborhood and listen for the bicycle bell being rung down your street. When you see him, wave at him to stop for a free frozen treat. This event is sponsored by the Bethany’s outreach team - connecting community together!

**No Experience Necessary** — a Bible study resource by Kelly Fryer and Rolf Jacobsen, which engages all levels of participants in a journey through the Bible. There will be something for brand new readers and for those with experience - but no previous experience is necessary in order to participate! Call 503-284-3836 and speak to Jeff about how he (or you) can help facilitate a small group at your choice location. Or, come to a No Experience Necessary! Bible Study already in progress at Bethany on Wednesdays, 11am-Noon. NE 37th at Skidmore.
