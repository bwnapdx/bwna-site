One of the more informative and creative websites by a neighborhood association is the Beaumont Wilshire website, **www.bwna.org**. Designed and maintained by Mario Caoile and hosted by Wink Jr of Sound-O-Mat, (both residents of BW), it is worthy of a shortcut on your computer desktop.

From the front page, which features a slide show of BW residents and events, you can link to eight subsections:

*Events* – calendar of upcoming meetings, picnics and other events – no excuses for missing the next general meeting because you forgot the date.

*Newsletter* – provides copies of all issues of the neighborhood newsletters back to June/July 2000, lists deadlines for submissions and ad rates.

*About* – mission of the organization and minutes from past meetings

*Transportation/Land Use* – information about traffic and development issues, committee members, and meeting times.

*Photos* – lots of images from events going back to 2001 such as past picnics, zoo visits, Taste of Beaumont and the 75th anniversary of Beaumont Middle School

*Artspace* – the online gallery of works by 21 artists who live in BW: whimsical, delicate and bold

*Links* – phone numbers and websites for such organizations as the library, zoo, bicycle program and others

*Contacts* – names, emails and phone numbers for BW board members who welcome your comments and suggestions.

Have look at www.bwna.org; send the link to relatives in other places so they will know what you mean when you say, ‘it’s a unique place, this neighborhood of mine.’
