Have you been on the Wilshire Park trail this winter? Just two years ago, it was high and dry—thanks to the Parks Department and a battalion of neighbors. Now it’s actually dangerous to run along the north section. If you stay on the path, you may need a rope to get out. If you go around—good luck staying upright on that clay!

Don’t despair. Solid ground is in sight. The Parks Department has agreed to take the matter seriously, and a group of neighbors is forming to help out. You too, can save a life—or keep a dog out of trouble! Call Dale Allen at **503-288-1780** to find out how.
