When word spread that our neighborhood was set to get an Italian gelato shop, one could hear the collective screech of delight from all who missed Rose’s Ice Cream, a landmark on Fremont for fifty years. Now there’s a new kid on the block, **PARISI’S** serving gelato, espresso, panini & fudge.

Last October, Mary and Mike Fonk (rhymes with “monk”) opened Parisi’s at 4605 NE Fremont. The red patent-leather booths are reminiscent of the 50’s.

PARISI’S serves Caffe Umbria, a classic Italian espresso blend. Pastries, such as the pear tart and croissants by “La Provence” make a nice accompaniment.

There are 24 flavors of gelato (Italian for ice cream) and sorbetto, made to order by artisan gelato maker Pete Palazzolo, which change on a rotating basis. French Chocolate Caramel Brandy Fudge, mmm, they had me at “French Chocolate”.

Soup of the day and panini sandwiches are also available. One can create a personal ‘make your own’ panini with a combination of meat, cheese, and different spreads like honey mustard, black olive tapenade or basil pesto, to name a few.

The real magic at PARISI’S happens at the hands of Mary, the master magician, who on weekends and evenings whips up fudge and classic cookies like Cinnamon, Chocolate-Chip, Nutmeg and Dutch Chocolate. All this, after putting in a full shift at her day job and being mom to Julia, 12 and Harrison, 8.

Seating is available both indoors and outdoors. PARISI’S is sure to become the new hub of our neighborhood where one can satisfy a hankering for a myriad of epicurean delights. Ciao!

Hours: M-Th 7:30AM–9PM, Fri 7:30AM–10PM, Sat. 8AM–1PM, Sun. 8AM–8PM. *Open one hour later in Summer.*
