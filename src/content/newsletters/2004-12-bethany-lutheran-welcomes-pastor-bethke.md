On October 17, Bethany Lutheran voted to call Pastor Jeff Bethke to be their pastor. Pastor Bethke hails from Texas and presently serves in Temple Hills, Maryland. Some of his ministry highlights include teaching, weekly preaching, and engaging in a redevelopment process with outreach to the community.

Bethany Lutheran Church invites the Beaumont Wilshire community to meet Pastor Jeff Bethke as he begins leading worship services on Sunday, December 5. Services are at 9:30 and 11:00 a.m. A potluck lunch and fellowship gathering will be served at noon.
