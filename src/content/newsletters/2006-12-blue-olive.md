Nestled in the heart of Beaumont Village lies a Mediterranean gem. Brothers Homayoon and Fazard Larki’s Blue Olive provides a warm atmosphere, reasonably priced wine and deliciously prepared Greek cuisine. The Larki brothers originate from Persia, however Fazard, who runs the kitchen alongside his mother, has worked and lived in Germany, Canada and the US. Homayoon, who has called Portland his home since the 1970’s, mans the bar and the front of the house, however it is not uncommon to be doted upon by both charming brothers. The restaurant offers a full and separate bar, a dining room and a beautiful and spacious garden in the back.

The menu consists of both warm and cold appetizers such as Baba Ghanouj, red pepper hummus, Garlic Prawns and Calamari averaging $6 apiece. Entrees consist of Grilled Lamb kabobs, Chicken Souvlaki, Mousaka and Dolmathes each costing around $15. Most of these dinners are served with roasted potatoes, rice, a Greek salad, and the fabulous Tzatziki sauce. The menu also offers several lavish platter options. We shared the Greek platter, and for $35 received a feast fit for a king. Fazard’s wife, Shiva is the pastry chef and we had her lemon flavored custard baked in filo, a pleasant ending indeed. They also offer chocolate baklava and crème caramel. So, whether you are looking for exceptional Greek food or just a really great meal in friendly family run business head to the Larki brothers Blue Olive. Lunch is served Monday thru Friday from 11 am to 2:30 pm and dinner is Monday thru Saturday from 5 pm until 10 pm. Blue Olive is located at 4627 NE Freemont. The phone number is (503) 528-2822. This month marks their one year anniversary.
