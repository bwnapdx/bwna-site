Tucked away in a little house at 4435 NE Fremont is a jewel of a beauty shop called Sparkle Beauty Bar. Sisters AnnSimone Mickelberry and Keelie Duncan opened the shop December 1, 2006. They offer an extensive array of services for men and women in their lovely, welcoming and well equipped space. Among  those services are: hair cuts and styling, wig services, hair color, waxing service and lash and brow tinting. Each of the sisters also has a specialty field. AnnSimone's is hair extensions and hair replacements and Keelie's is lash extensions and makeup. Also, Melissa Church is available for nail care.

When I asked them where most of their customers come from, their answer surprised me: from all over Portland and from places as far away as Kelso, Lincoln City and Seattle. And they range in ages between two to 89! So, what makes this place so unique and its client so loyal and far reaching? The answer is the specialized services that AnnSimone and Keelie offer, their backgrounds and the donation work they do. AnnSimone has a background in TV journalism and Keelie is a professional make up artist.

Their donation work reaches people far and near. Sparkle Beauty Bar partners with The American Cancer Society and provides 'Look Good Feel Better' classes at Providence Cancer Center. They provide free classes in make up and help patients with wig selection and wig care.

Another service they provide is partnering with Locks of Love (LOL). This is a non profit that provides hairpieces to low income children suffering hair loss. Sparkle offers a free hair cut to anyone who is cutting 10 inches of hair or more and donates the 'pony tail 'to LOL. It takes ten pony tails to make a wig. All hair left on the floor after cuts is donated to Matter of Trust who make hair clippings into mats used in oil spills.

Their interest in helping cancer patients started when their mother, a long time hair dresser, was diagnosed with terminal cancer 15 years ago. They worked in her salon and inspired by her courage and determination, decided to help others once they opened Sparkle.

Sparkle Beauty Bar is open Tues–Sat 10–5. Call 503-493-9440 or stop at 4435 NE Fremont to make an appointment. You will feel doubly blessed: you will look great and your old hair may help make a wig for a child or help mop up oil spills.
