Beaumont Wilshire NET had a community booth at the Hollywood Farmers Market on Saturday, July 7. The response to “Build a 72 Hour Kit” sign drew in many people with corresponding comments: “I have to do this”. “Where is the list?” It was a great opportunity for NET members to talk with neighbors who were shopping at the market. Team members from other NETs stopped by as well. Information about emergency preparedness, fire safety, and NET training was provided.

Beaumont Wilshire Neighborhood Emergency Team (NET) will have a community booth at Fremont Fest, Saturday, August 4, in the 4600 NE Fremont area, from 10 am to 1 pm. Stop by and get information about emergency preparedness and learn about the NET training.

Find out when the next Neighborhood Emergency Team (NET) training is by visiting: www.pdxprepared.net
