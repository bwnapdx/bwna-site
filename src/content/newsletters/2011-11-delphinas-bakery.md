Delphina’s Bakery began in the back kitchen of Delphina’s Restaurant in 1983 with bread being baked daily for its dinner customers. By 1985 they were making a push to make their product available to more customers. Carolyn Mistell came on board at this time and the bakery thrived. After some time away pursuing archeology and anthropology, Carolyn became the sole owner and president in 2004. Now she presides over a business that has grown leaps and bounds from it’s beginnings at the restaurant. Primarily a wholesale operation (although you can enjoy these delicacies at the café on 42nd), Delphina’s Bakery provides artisan breads and breakfast pastries to businesses all over the Portland area, baked and delivered fresh daily. Carolyn is particularly proud of her employees, stating that they have a very strong team in place. With clients as large as Trader Joe’s, they keep very busy getting their products where they need to be while maintaining the freshness, quality and flavor that have become synonymous with Delphina’s Bakery. This can be a tall order with a product that has only a one day shelf life. They are constantly developing new ideas and methods to get their goods from the oven to the customer as quickly as possible. As Carolyn says, “We want our customers to enjoy our products the way they are fresh out of the oven.” This can mean improvements in anything from packaging and transportation to the conditions of their fleet of vans they use to transport the items.

It is clear in talking with Carolyn that she is passionate not only about baking and her business, but also cares immensely about our community. She is an active participant in a number of projects that are helping improve 42nd Street including the recent street fair, working with a team of graduate students who are seeking out and applying for grants to improve the neighborhood, developing a mural and aesthetic improvements, and adorning the cafe’s walls with work from local artists to give their work exposure. Her eyes light up when discussing how far 42nd has come since Delphina’s first relocated and how excited she is to see what the future holds.
