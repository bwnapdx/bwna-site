Rigler School with the support of Principal Kathleen Kaczke has been chosen to be the 10th class of Dreamers in the Portland area. “I Have a Dream” was started in 1981. The first five classes of Oregon Dreamers have graduated from high school with double the expected high school graduation rate. Half of those students have gone on to college or other post secondary education. There are four on-going “I Have a Dream” classes in the Portland area. Targeted start date for the Rigler’s entire 3rd grade of 67 students is April 2007 if funding is in place.

The goal is that each child graduates from high school prepared for college or meaningful employment. This is done through a 14 year, year-round program of mentoring, tutoring, cultural enrichment and community service, with the promise of scholarship assistance for every child that graduates from high school. Volunteers are used extensively as one-on-one mentors, tutors and as one-time presenters.

About one half of the funds needed to start have been raised. All pledges and one time donations are welcome. A donation of $1000 sponsors one child for one year. For more information or to donate contact:
