Beaumont’s 7th graders have elective classes for sixth period. They are: Spanish, computers, and Community service class. Everyone is required to do them all, but we do them at different times over the course of the year.

The Community Service Class is about helping our neighborhood. Our teacher, Ms. Thoma helps us plan and arrange field trips. On the first day, we decide what field trips to go on, and how to help around our school. As a class, the kids do most of the planning and helping, except for arranging field trips, which Ms. Thoma does.

Some of the things we plan to do are: go to a homeless shelter to help serve food, raise money for our school, go to an animal shelter to help, and mentor kindergarteners.

After spring break, we went to Sabin School to read to the kindergarteners. The people who get the benefit of our work are homeless people, neighbors, and schoolmates. The kids that go to our school get the benefit of better gardens, not as much trash, and they learn about media news in our newspaper called the Beaumontian. It is called the Beaumontian because we combined Beaumont and the Oregonian.

Our class wants our neighborhood to know that we are helping the Beaumont area.
