Each Friday during the school year, we put lunch items in the backpacks of school children who are at risk of hunger on weekends when the federally-sponsored school lunch isn’t available.  Last year, we provided weekend lunches to 36 children at Woodlawn Elementary, but the need is much greater.  Our goal is to serve 100 children.  Through our food and monetary donations, we make a difference in the lives and futures of children in need.  The Backpack Program is a collaborative effort of four United Methodist Churches working with community friends to nurture vulnerable young students in Portland.  We invite you to help us expand our ability to feed hungry school children in our midst.

## The ways you can help

- Donate food for weekend lunches
- Volunteer to help pack or deliver lunch bags
- Help with food donation pickups
- Make a monetary donation

## Food items to donate

- cans of chili, soup, ravioli in meat sauce, cans/boxes of mac n’ cheese
- small juice boxes—100% juice only, no foil
- individual-size fruit cups
- small packages of crackers w/cheese, raisins or fruit snacks, granola bars (no peanuts)

## Monetary donations

The Backpack Lunch Program is an all volunteer program -- there are no paid staff.  Fremont United Methodist Church donates financial management services.  Make checks out to Fremont UMC.  Please write “Backpack Donation” on checks, and mail to Fremont UMC, 2620 NE Fremont Street, Portland OR 97212.  $100 sponsors a school-year of weekend lunches for one child.  Each donation, regardless of amount, is important.  Fremont UMC sends out documentation of donations for tax purposes annually.

This program could not exist without our volunteers!  To volunteer or learn more, contact Marilyn Mauch, 503/287-3014, Program Coordinator, Fremont UMC

We give thanks to our donors  -- Woodlawn Community Resource Center; Grigsby Bros. Paper Box; Fremont, Hughes Memorial, and Woodlawn United Methodist Churches; and Wilshire UMC/Native American Fellowship.  We are grateful to many individuals who have made this program possible through their donations.
