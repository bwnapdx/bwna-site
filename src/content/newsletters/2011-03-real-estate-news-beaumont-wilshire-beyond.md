Portland Metro market action

December Residential Highlights:

Home-buying activity is expected to pick up a little in 2011, when compared to 2010. High inventory, low mortgage rates, and a slowly improving job market will bring more buyers off the fence. The average sales price for 2010 dropped 2.7% and the median sales price is down 2.9%, however the number of days for a house to be on the market dropped 6.6% from last year, which is good!

Inventory for the Portland Metro area dipped down in December to 7.9 months from what it was in November at 10.2 months, which brings us close to the lowest we have been in the last 3 years at 7 months.

Although closed sales were down 2.9% when compared to last year in December, the number of pending offers on homes went up by 6%. New listings also dropped by 8.5% from 2009 to 2010.

When comparing the previous month of November to December 2010 closed sales made an increase by 14.3%. Although pending sales fell by -15.2%, the number of new homes on the market were fewer by -22.2%. For sellers with homes are on the market, this means less competition and more buyers.

The year to date market activity for closed and pending sales both dropped in 2010 from that of 2009. The numbers show 0.2% less homes that sold, and 2.1% less that went into contract. The number of new listings from year end 2009 to 2010 increased by 3%.
