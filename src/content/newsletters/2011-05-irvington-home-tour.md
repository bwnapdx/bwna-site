The 29th Irvington Home Tour will be held on Sunday, May 15th from 11am to 5pm. This year there will be 7 wonderful homes on the tour as well as the iconic White House Bed & Breakfast in this now Historic Portland Neighborhood. Ticket holders will also be able to hear a lecture by local architectural historian Jim Heuer on “Craftsman Style and the Great Boom: Building Portland’s Great Arts & Crafts Neighborhoods”. The architecture this year features Prairie, Arts & Crafts, Foursquare and Bungalow. One on a double lot shows off a fine vegetable and flower garden along with 6 prize chickens. (To the delight of the neighborhood children it was also the home of Mooshi the pot bellied pig until his recent demise.) The fee is $20 which is used by the Irvington Community Association to provide gifts and grants to non-profits servicing the region. No children under 7 are allowed with the exception of infants in front carriers. Tickets can be obtained online or from several local merchants. Visit Caffe Destino, Broadway Books, The Architectural Heritage Center or http://www.irvingtonhometour.com/TourInfo.shtml.
