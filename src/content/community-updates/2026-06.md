## Community News

### Public Plaza Update

In separate late-April votes, the Beaumont Business Association (BBA) and BWNA approved joint sponsorship of a public street plaza on NE 44th Avenue just north of Fremont for a three-month trial period beginning mid-June.

On the heels of these approvals, an ad hoc planning committee of BBA and BWNA board members, business representatives, and neighbors was convened and has been hard at work ensuring the plaza will be a wonderful neighborhood amenity with a range of activities and events to be enjoyed by all.

So far, key decisions include the selection of a plaza name — Beaumont Crossing — and opening dates. Street closure and plaza installations will occur on Saturday, June 13, and grand opening festivities will follow on Saturday, June 27.

Many more details on these events — including a call for volunteers of all ages and abilities to assist with street painting on June 13 — will be shared widely via BBA and BWNA communication channels. Find more information about Portland's public plaza program in [PBOT's 2025 Public Realm and Street Activation Report](https://www.portland.gov/transportation/planning/plazas/documents/2025-public-realm-and-street-activation-report/download).

*Note: the grand opening was later moved to [July 11](/events/2026-07-11-plaza-grand-opening). See the [Public Plaza](/public-plaza) page for the latest.*

### 2026 PDX World Naked Bike Ride to Launch from B-W; Transportation Routes Affected

Saturday, July 25, is the date scheduled for the 2026 PDX World Naked Bike Ride, which will start in Beaumont-Wilshire. Representatives of the city-permitted ride attended the BWNA board meeting on May 11, and while they asked that we keep the launch location quiet for now, they reported that thousands of riders will be participating, so streets will be significantly impacted.

Riders will start to assemble in B-W at 6:30 p.m. and begin departing at 8:00 p.m. to a final destination that will be announced at a later date. Look for more details in the July–August BWNA newsletter. For more information visit [pdxwnbr.org](http://pdxwnbr.org).

### An "Attractive Nuisance?" Flags on Fremont Stolen, Vandalized

It is with great disappointment that BWNA reports the loss of over 20 orange pedestrian flags in just a three-week period this spring. Adding to the disappointment, on May 12 BWNA board member Angela Frome found that flags had been thrown in the street at several locations on Fremont in Beaumont Village.

Although there are no eyewitnesses to the flag disappearances or vandalism, Fremont Flag coordinators Angela and Patti Koehler wonder if some of the mayhem may be the work of neighborhood young folks out on a lark, and they're asking parents to be on the lookout for orange safety flags on their premises. If you know something about the missing or badly treated flags, please email [info@bwnapdx.org](mailto:info@bwnapdx.org).

## Events in B-W and Nearby

### Free Stuff on the Lawn at Bethany Lutheran

**May 22–24 (Friday–Sunday) — 4330 NE 37th Ave.**

On the days listed, find a host of items on the Bethany Lutheran Church lawn, all free for the taking: kitchen supplies, office supplies, small furniture, concrete garden blocks, and other miscellaneous items. Any items remaining will be removed the morning of Monday, May 25.

With the disbanding of the Bethany Lutheran congregation over a year ago, the Oregon Synod has been getting closer to transitioning the property to its new owners; this weekend's event is the Synod's last step in clearing out the building. The [Native American Youth & Family Center (NAYA)](https://nayapdx.org/) will be assuming ownership of the property. More information about its plans will be forthcoming in the BWNA newsletter and future Community Updates.

### [Rose Festival Junior Parade](/events/2026-05-30-rose-festival-junior-parade)

**May 30 (Saturday), 12:00–2:00 p.m. — Begins at NE 52nd & Sandy Blvd.**

Come celebrate our community's children at the Rose Festival Junior Parade, a beloved tradition dating back to 1918, when six kids in the Hollywood District, sorely disappointed about the cancellation of the Rose Festival due to World War I, decided to hold their own parade. Today, children from all parts of Portland participate by dressing in costumes, creating floats, decorating bikes, and parading with friends and family. The parade travels down Sandy to NE 40th Avenue, then west onto NE Tillamook Street, ending at Grant High School. For details and how to participate, visit the [Rose Festival Junior Parade](https://www.rosefestival.org/events/2026/juniorparade2) page.

## Get Involved

**Meet people. Do good. Have fun!** For more information about ways to get involved, visit our [Get Involved](/get-involved) page.

### Outgoing & love the neighborhood and the newsletter?

Do you love to meet new people and would enjoy extolling the virtues of the BWNA newsletter to prospective advertisers? If this type of volunteer effort would hit your hot buttons, we'd love to have you on the newsletter team. Advertising revenue is the main fundraising source for BWNA's events, so it's an important role. Email [Editor@BWNApdx.org](mailto:editor@bwnapdx.org) if you're interested.

### Calling STEM/STEAM volunteers

Help bring back the Science Fair to Alameda Elementary, or a Family Science night. These events provide elementary students with an invaluable opportunity to nurture their curiosity, develop critical thinking skills, and get hands-on learning experience in a friendly, supportive environment. If interested and able, contact Julia Love at [vpcurriculum@alamedaschool.org](mailto:vpcurriculum@alamedaschool.org).

### Ideas for our Speakers Forum?

Birds, bees, seeds? History or herstory? City politics? Aging in place? Downsizing? In 2025, BWNA logged talks on neighborhood history, beneficial bugs, going all-electric, and the Cascadia Subduction Zone. What's in store for 2026? Send ideas to John Sandie at [SandieFam@gmail.com](mailto:SandieFam@gmail.com).

### Be a Trash Walker

Help keep the neighborhood clean by picking up trash and removing graffiti. BWNA holds two organized cleanup events per year, but regular attention keeps the problem in check. To receive free cleaning supplies, email [Angela Frome](mailto:angfrome@yahoo.com).

### Help with events

Looking to get involved, but not *too* involved? We'd love your occasional help with neighborhood events — many hands make light work. Email BWNA Events Chair [John Sandie](mailto:sandiefam@gmail.com) to be notified when help is needed.

### Newsletter distribution team

Get a walk in, meet new neighbors, and help bring news to the neighborhood. The distribution team is seeking people to hand-deliver the BWNA newsletter to a prescribed route every other month, or in a fill-in capacity. Email [John Sandie](mailto:SandieFam@gmail.com) or [Georgina Head](mailto:Georginafh@outlook.com) for more information.
