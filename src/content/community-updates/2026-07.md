## Community News

### Public Plaza Update

Barriers and outdoor furniture are being installed at the intersection of NE 44th Avenue and Fremont Street, kicking off the summer season with our new community plaza. Beaumont Crossing, the official name for the plaza, is the result of months of hard work by volunteers from the BWNA and the Beaumont Business Association. NE 44th Avenue will be closed to traffic at Fremont for the duration of the three-month plaza trial period.

### 2026 PDX World Naked Bike Ride to Launch from Wilshire Park

Thousands of riders are expected to descend upon our neighborhood on Saturday, July 25. Wilshire Park is likely to be entirely packed with celebrants as they converge beginning around 6:30 p.m., before the official ride starts at 8:00 p.m. Parking and transportation routes will be significantly affected — and the ride's final destination and route will not be announced until the ride begins. Note that riders will not return to the park at the conclusion of the event. For more information visit [pdxwnbr.org](https://pdxwnbr.org). [See on the calendar](/events/2026-07-25-naked-bike-ride).

### Bethany Lutheran's Future

Bethany Lutheran Church is being gifted to the Native American Youth and Family Center (NAYA), which plans to build elder housing at the site. Find out more at the BWNA board meeting on July 13, or [read more from OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/06/23/oregon-lutherans-church-land-return/).

### Play for Free at The Paladins League

The Paladins League co-owner Jess Monsey announced in a recent email that the board game-playing space on NE Fremont Street will no longer charge for access to its extensive board game library. "We hope this helps even more people to enjoy each other's good company," Monsey wrote. Look for more details in the July–August BWNA newsletter, or visit [thepaladinsleague.com](https://thepaladinsleague.com).

### Flags on Fremont Continue to Disappear

Over 35 of our orange pedestrian safety flags are missing — up from a count of 20 just last month. Some were found burned. BWNA is interested in tips regarding the missing flags. If you have any information, please email [info@bwnapdx.org](mailto:info@bwnapdx.org).

### [BWNA Neighborhood Yard Sale](/events/2026-07-11-yard-sale)

**July 11 (Saturday), 9:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. — Beaumont-Wilshire Neighborhood**

Whether you're ready to declutter and make some cash or want to hunt for one-of-a-kind treasures, you won't want to miss this community yard sale! BWNA will promote the event and produce an interactive map of participating households. No fee to participate.

### [BWNA Art & Garden Tour](/events/2026-07-18-art-garden-tour)

**July 18 (Saturday), 11:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. — Beaumont-Wilshire — Tickets: $17 donation, limited**

In its third year, this BWNA-sponsored event offers the opportunity to discover five private gardens in the Beaumont-Wilshire neighborhood, each with art for sale displayed in the gardens. Get ideas for your own garden, meet new people, connect with old friends, and simply partake in the community — all while helping BWNA raise funds for our many free events throughout the year. Thank you to our sponsor, local plant retailer Garden Fever.

### [Fremont Fest Marketplace: Through the Ages](/events/2026-08-01-fremont-fest)

**August 1 (Saturday), 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. — NE Fremont Street in Beaumont Village**

The Beaumont Business Association hosts this family-friendly festival on NE Fremont Street between NE 42nd and NE 51st avenues, with over 200 vendors offering a diverse mix of local goods, food, and artisan products. The theme is "Through the Ages," a celebration of four decades of community, small businesses, music, memories, and neighborhood tradition.

## Get Involved

**Meet people. Do good. Have fun!** For more information about ways to get involved, visit our [Get Involved](/get-involved) page.

### Outgoing & love the neighborhood and the newsletter?

Do you love to meet new people and would enjoy extolling the virtues of the BWNA newsletter to prospective advertisers? If this type of volunteer effort would hit your hot buttons, we'd love to have you on the newsletter team. Advertising revenue is the main fundraising source for BWNA's events, so it's an important role. Email [Editor@BWNApdx.org](mailto:editor@bwnapdx.org) if you're interested.

### Calling STEM/STEAM volunteers

Help bring back the Science Fair to Alameda Elementary, or a Family Science night. These events provide elementary students with an invaluable opportunity to nurture their curiosity, develop critical thinking skills, and get hands-on learning experience in a friendly, supportive environment. If interested and able, contact Julia Love at [vpcurriculum@alamedaschool.org](mailto:vpcurriculum@alamedaschool.org).

### Ideas for our Speakers Forum?

Birds, bees, seeds? History or herstory? City politics? Aging in place? Downsizing? In 2025, BWNA logged talks on neighborhood history, beneficial bugs, going all-electric, and the Cascadia Subduction Zone. What's in store for 2026? Send ideas to John Sandie at [SandieFam@gmail.com](mailto:SandieFam@gmail.com).

### Be a Trash Walker

Help keep the neighborhood clean by picking up trash and removing graffiti. BWNA holds two organized cleanup events per year, but regular attention keeps the problem in check. To receive free cleaning supplies, email Angela Frome.

### Help with events

Looking to get involved, but not *too* involved? We'd love your occasional help with neighborhood events — many hands make light work. Email BWNA Events Chair John Sandie to be notified when help is needed.

### Newsletter distribution team

Get a walk in, meet new neighbors, and help bring news to the neighborhood. The distribution team is seeking people to hand-deliver the BWNA newsletter to a prescribed route every other month, or in a fill-in capacity. Email John Sandie or Georgina Head for more information.

## Ongoing BWNA Events

**BWNA Board Meetings** are held the 2nd Monday of January, March, May, July, September, and November from 7:00–8:30 p.m. The public is welcome; time for public input is limited.

**BWNA General Meetings** are the 2nd Monday of February, April, June, August, October, and December from 7:00–8:30 p.m. The public is welcome to attend and participate.

Meeting locations change; most meetings are also held via Zoom. Look for current info in your monthly email from BWNA or check the [Upcoming Events](/events) page on the day of the meeting.
