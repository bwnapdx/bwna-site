## Public Plaza Update

At BWNA's April 13 general meeting, the board voted 13–2 to approve a summer 2026 public street plaza trial on NE 44th Avenue just north of Fremont. The votes were cast after the board considered all of PBOT's compromises to ameliorate stakeholder concerns surrounding the plaza trial. BWNA's vote was conditioned on Beaumont Business Association (BBA) approval and thus joint sponsorship. On April 20, the BBA board voted 8–1 to approve the plaza trial.

The BBA, BWNA, and PBOT will conduct extensive outreach and evaluation following the trial, and, if there is widespread support, full inclusion in PBOT's plaza program on a year-round basis will be considered. Look for more details about the vote in the May–June BWNA newsletter, and find more information about [Portland's public plaza program](https://www.portland.gov/transportation/plaza-program).

### [Rose Festival Junior Parade](/events/2026-05-30-rose-festival-junior-parade)

**May 30 (Saturday), Noon–2:00 p.m. — Parade begins at NE 52nd & Sandy Blvd.**

Celebrate the neighborhood's children at the Rose Festival Junior Parade, a beloved tradition dating back to 1918. Kids from across Portland dress in costumes, create floats, and decorate bikes. The parade travels down Sandy to NE 40th, then west on Tillamook, ending at Grant High School.

## Get Involved

**Meet people. Do good. Have fun!** For more information about ways to get involved, visit our [Get Involved](/get-involved) page.

### Outgoing & love the neighborhood and the newsletter?

Do you love to meet new people and would enjoy extolling the virtues of the BWNA newsletter to prospective advertisers? If this type of volunteer effort would hit your hot buttons, we'd love to have you on the newsletter team. Advertising revenue is the main fundraising source for BWNA's events, so it's an important role. Email [Editor@BWNApdx.org](mailto:editor@bwnapdx.org) if you're interested.

### Calling STEM/STEAM volunteers

Help bring back the Science Fair to Alameda Elementary, or a Family Science night. These events provide elementary students with an invaluable opportunity to nurture their curiosity, develop critical thinking skills, and get hands-on learning experience in a friendly, supportive environment. If interested and able, contact Julia Love at [vpcurriculum@alamedaschool.org](mailto:vpcurriculum@alamedaschool.org).

### Ideas for our Speakers Forum?

Birds, bees, seeds? History or herstory? City politics? Aging in place? Downsizing? In 2025, BWNA logged talks on neighborhood history, beneficial bugs, going all-electric, and the Cascadia Subduction Zone. What's in store for 2026? Send ideas to John Sandie at [SandieFam@gmail.com](mailto:SandieFam@gmail.com).

### Be a Trash Walker

Help keep the neighborhood clean by picking up trash and removing graffiti. BWNA holds two organized cleanup events per year, but regular attention keeps the problem in check. To receive free cleaning supplies, email Angela Frome.

### Help with events

Looking to get involved, but not *too* involved? We'd love your occasional help with neighborhood events — many hands make light work. Email BWNA Events Chair John Sandie to be notified when help is needed.

### Newsletter distribution team

Get a walk in, meet new neighbors, and help bring news to the neighborhood. The distribution team is seeking people to hand-deliver the BWNA newsletter to a prescribed route every other month, or in a fill-in capacity. Email John Sandie or Georgina Head for more information.

## Ongoing BWNA Events

**BWNA Board Meetings** are held the 2nd Monday of January, March, May, July, September, and November from 7:00–8:30 p.m. The public is welcome; time for public input is limited.

**BWNA General Meetings** are the 2nd Monday of February, April, June, August, October, and December from 7:00–8:30 p.m. The public is welcome to attend and participate.

Meeting locations change; most meetings are also held via Zoom. Look for current info in your monthly email from BWNA or check the [Upcoming Events](/events) page on the day of the meeting.
